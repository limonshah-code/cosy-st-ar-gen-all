Taste of the Wild dog food is manufactured in the United States. Its parent company, Diamond Pet Foods, operates several production facilities across the country. ### Does Taste of the Wild have any recalls?
Yes, Taste of the Wild has been subject to recalls in the past. A notable recall occurred in 2012 due to potential salmonella contamination. Diamond Pet Foods, its manufacturer, implemented stricter quality control and testing protocols after these incidents to enhance product safety.

### Is Taste of the Wild grain-free?

### Is Taste of the Wild dog food made by Purina?
No, Taste of the Wild dog food is not made by Purina. It is manufactured by Diamond Pet Foods. Diamond Pet Foods is a separate, family-owned company. They produce several pet food brands, including Taste of the Wild, Diamond Naturals, and Kirkland Signature Pet Food.

### Who owns Diamond Pet Foods?
Diamond Pet Foods is a privately held, family-owned and operated company. It was founded by Richard Kampeter and Gary Schell. The company has remained in the family since its inception in 1970. This ownership structure often allows for a focus on long-term goals and consistent values.
