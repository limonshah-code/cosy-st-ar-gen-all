#### The Origin Story of Nathan's Famous

Nathan Handwerker founded Nathan's Famous. He opened his hot dog stand in 1916. Legend says the eating contest began the same year. Four immigrants supposedly challenged each other. ### FAQ Section

#### When is the Hot Dog Eating Contest usually held?
The Nathan's Famous International Hot Dog Eating Contest takes place every year on July 4th. The women's competition typically starts in the late morning, followed by the men's competition around noon Eastern Time. It is a consistent holiday tradition for many.

#### Where does the contest take place?
The contest occurs at its iconic location in Coney Island, Brooklyn, New York. Specifically, it happens at the corner of Surf and Stillwell Avenues, right outside the original Nathan's Famous hot dog stand. This venue is part of its historic charm.

#### Can I watch the contest for free?
You can often watch the contest for free if you have an antenna and can pick up ESPN's broadcast over the air. Many streaming services offer free trials (e.g., Hulu + Live TV, Sling TV). You can use these trials to watch the event without cost, just remember to cancel afterwards.

#### Who are some famous hot dog eaters?
Joey Chestnut is the most famous male competitive eater, holding numerous records in hot dog eating. Miki Sudo is the most dominant female eater in the contest's history. Both are multiple-time champions. Other notable eaters include Matt Stonie, though he might focus on other competitive eating challenges.

#### How long does the contest last?
The main eating portion of both the men's and women's contests lasts exactly 10 minutes. The entire broadcast, including pre-show, commentary, and award ceremonies, usually spans one to two hours. This short, intense burst of competition is what makes it so thrilling.

#### Is competitive eating healthy?
Competitive eating is not considered a healthy practice. It puts extreme stress on the body and digestive system. Professional eaters train rigorously and often consult medical professionals. However, it is an extreme sport that comes with risks. It should not be attempted by untrained individuals.
