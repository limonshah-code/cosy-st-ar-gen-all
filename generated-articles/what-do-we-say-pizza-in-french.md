## The Simple Truth: "La Pizza" in French

Many words travel across languages. "Pizza" is a perfect example of this. The word "pizza" comes from Italian. French people adopted it directly into their language. They did not change the spelling. They kept "pizza" as it is.

When you use "pizza" in French, you must remember its gender. In French, every noun has a gender. "Pizza" is a feminine noun. This means you use "la" before it. So, you say "la pizza," not "le pizza." This is a key rule for speaking French correctly. Understanding the gender helps you use articles and adjectives properly. It shows you respect the language rules.

The adoption of "pizza" shows how food culture spreads. French cuisine is famous. Yet, French people widely embrace Italian dishes like pizza. It highlights the global love for good food. You might think French has its own word for everything. But for "pizza," they stick with the original. This makes your job easier. You do not need to learn a new, unfamiliar term. You simply use the word you already know. Just remember the feminine article "la" before it.
