### Understanding the Breed's History

The White Shepherd's history ties closely to the German Shepherd. German Shepherds first appeared in Germany in the late 19th century. White-coated German Shepherds existed from the start. They were part of the breed's original gene pool. However, breed standards later excluded white coats. Breeders thought the white color was a fault.

This decision led to a split. White German Shepherds still existed. They gained popularity in other regions. In North America, breeders worked to preserve the white coat. They developed the White Shepherd as a distinct line. This breed embodies the original German Shepherd traits. It simply has a beautiful white coat.

A white German Shepherd is simply a German Shepherd with a white coat. It is still considered a German Shepherd. A White Shepherd, however, is often considered a distinct breed by some kennel clubs.
