## How to Clean a Yellowed Print Safely

Finding a cherished old print turning yellow can feel disheartening. I know the feeling of wanting to bring back the crisp look of a vintage photo or a piece of art. When paper prints age, they often develop a yellowish tint. This discoloration happens due to acid content in the paper or exposure to environmental factors like light and humidity. Learning **how to clean a yellowed print** properly is crucial for its long-term survival. This guide will walk you through understanding why prints yellow, how to assess the damage, and the best methods for safe cleaning. We will cover gentler approaches and discuss when to seek professional help. My goal is to help you restore your precious prints carefully.

### Takeaway

*   Understand the cause of yellowing before you start cleaning.
*   Always test cleaning methods on a small, hidden area first.
*   Prioritize gentle, non-abrasive techniques for delicate prints.
*   Consider professional conservation for valuable or severely damaged items.
*   Store cleaned prints properly to prevent future yellowing.

### Clear, Concise Answer

To clean a yellowed print, gently remove surface dirt with a soft brush or eraser. For deeper yellowing, consider dry cleaning methods using art gum erasers or specialized dry cleaning sponges. For more stubborn stains, mild, non-acidic solutions or professional deacidification may be needed, always testing first.
