## Defining the Ideal French Bulldog for Competition

What makes a French Bulldog a standout in the show ring? It all comes down to the breed standard. This detailed document describes the ideal characteristics. Judges use it as their guide. A winning Frenchie must embody these traits.

Key physical traits include:

*   **Head:** A large, square head is essential. The skull should be flat between the ears. It should be slightly rounded on the forehead.
*   **Ears:** Bat ears are a defining feature. They are broad at the base, rounded at the top, and set high on the head. They stand erect.
*   **Body:** The body should be compact and muscular. It should have a heavy bone structure. A short, level back is important.
*   **Coat:** The coat is short, smooth, and fine. It has a soft feel.
*   **Tail:** The tail is short and either straight or corkscrew. It is set low.

Beyond physical appearance, temperament is also critical. A show Frenchie must be alert, active, and intelligent. They should have a charming and playful personality. They must be good-natured and show confidence in the ring. A dog’s health is also paramount. Judges look for a sound dog. This means good health and structure. These are dogs that move freely and breathe easily. These combined traits define a top-tier French Bulldog. They demonstrate the breed's unique appeal and quality.
