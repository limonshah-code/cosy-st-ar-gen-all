### How do you properly clean a Chilipad system?

You clean a Chilipad system by regularly changing the water in the control unit with distilled water. You also descale the control unit every one to three months using a specialized cleaner. For the mattress pad, you should spot clean it as needed or machine wash it if the label allows, then always air dry it completely.

## Understanding Your Chilipad: Key Components to Clean

Before you clean, you need to know what you are cleaning. Your Chilipad is a system with two main parts. First, there is the control unit. This unit holds the water. It heats or cools the water. It also pumps the water through tubes. Second, there is the mattress pad. This pad sits on your bed. It has thin tubes sewn inside it.

The control unit is where the action happens. It needs careful attention. Water passes through its internal parts. Minerals can build up inside. This is why descaling is so important for the control unit. The mattress pad also needs care. Sweat and skin cells can get on the pad. Stains can happen. Knowing these parts helps you target your cleaning efforts.
