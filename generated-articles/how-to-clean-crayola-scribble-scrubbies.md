## The Step-by-Step Washing Process for Your Pets

Cleaning your Scribble Scrubbies does not have to be a big chore. I find it quite satisfying to see the colors disappear. Follow these steps for a complete wash, bringing your pets back to their original state. This method is safe and effective for all Scribble Scrubbie figures. Let's get them sparkling clean for their next artistic transformation.

### Initial Rinse: Getting Started

Begin by holding your Scribble Scrubbie under cool running water. The water helps to loosen the marker ink. You will see many of the lighter colors start to wash away immediately. This initial rinse removes the surface layer of ink. It prepares the toy for a deeper clean. Let the water run over the toy for about 30 seconds. This ensures a good initial saturation.

While rinsing, gently squeeze the toy. This helps push water through its fuzzy fibers. Make sure the water runs clear before moving on. If colors still bleed, continue rinsing for a little longer. This step is important to remove as much loose ink as possible. It makes the scrubbing process much easier.

### Gentle Scrubbing: Removing the Color

Now it is time to use the scrub brush. Apply a tiny drop of mild dish soap directly onto the brush bristles. Alternatively, you can put a small amount of soap on the Scribble Scrubbie itself. Use the brush to gently scrub the colored areas. Work in small circular motions. You will see the remaining marker ink start to lift away.

Be careful not to scrub too hard. The goal is to loosen the ink, not to damage the fibers. If the color is stubborn, add a little more soap and continue gently scrubbing. I often find that some colors, like dark blues or reds, need a bit more attention. Take your time with this step to ensure all visible color is removed. Focus on one area at a time for best results.

### Rinsing Thoroughly: Washing Away Soap

After scrubbing, it is crucial to rinse your Scribble Scrubbie thoroughly. Hold the toy under cool running water again. Squeeze and rinse the toy repeatedly. This removes all traces of soap and remaining ink. Any soap left behind can make the fibers stiff. It might also attract dirt more quickly in the future.

Continue rinsing until no more suds appear. The water running off the toy should be completely clear. This step ensures a soft, clean finish. A clean rinse means your Scribble Scrubbie is truly ready. It will feel good to the touch and be ready for its next coloring session.

### Drying Your Scrubbies: Ready for Play

Once rinsed, gently squeeze out excess water from your Scribble Scrubbie. Do not twist or wring the toy too forcefully. This could distort its shape. You can also pat it dry with a clean, absorbent towel. Then, place your Scribble Scrubbie in a well-ventilated area to air dry completely.

Avoid using heat, such as a hair dryer, as this can damage the fibers or even set leftover stains. It usually takes several hours for the toy to dry completely. Make sure it is bone dry before storing it or letting your child re-color it. A damp toy can develop mildew or transfer moisture to new markers. Patience here ensures the best results.

## Tackling Stubborn Stains: Advanced Cleaning Methods

Sometimes, basic washing does not remove every last trace of color. This is especially true for markers left on too long or darker shades. When faced with stubborn stains on your Crayola Scribble Scrubbies, you need a few extra tricks. I have found these methods to be quite effective for those persistent marks. Remember to always proceed with caution and gentleness. Our goal is to clean, not to cause damage.

### The Magic Eraser Technique

A melamine foam eraser, widely known as a magic eraser, can be a powerful tool. This product works by acting like a very fine abrasive. It gently scours away tough stains. Dampen a small piece of the magic eraser with cool water. Then, gently rub it over the stained area on your Scribble Scrubbie. Use very light pressure. You will often see the leftover marker residue start to lift away.

Do not press too hard or rub vigorously. This can damage the toy's fuzzy surface. Rubbing too much can make the fibers thin or rough. Work in short, gentle strokes. After applying the magic eraser, rinse the area thoroughly with cool water. This removes any tiny eraser particles and leftover ink. This method is surprisingly effective for those ghost stains.

### Baking Soda Paste for Persistent Marks

Baking soda is a mild abrasive and a natural deodorizer. It can also help lift stubborn stains from fabrics. Mix a small amount of baking soda with just enough water to form a thick paste. Apply this paste directly onto the stained areas of your Scribble Scrubbie. Let it sit for about 10-15 minutes. This gives the baking soda time to work on the stain.

After the waiting period, use your soft scrub brush or a damp cloth to gently rub the paste into the stain. The mild abrasive action of the baking soda helps to lift the color. Rinse the Scribble Scrubbie thoroughly under cool running water afterward. Make sure all the baking soda paste is completely removed. This method is particularly useful for brightening slightly discolored areas. For general household cleaning, baking soda is a fantastic, versatile ingredient. When you clean items like the refrigerator, baking soda can absorb odors and gently scrub surfaces. This same gentle abrasive power makes it useful for our Scribble Scrubbies. [How to clean the refrigerator](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-refrigerator) often recommends baking soda for its mild yet effective cleaning properties.

### Spot Cleaning with Isopropyl Alcohol (with caution)

For extremely stubborn and deeply set stains, isopropyl alcohol (rubbing alcohol) can be a last resort. This method requires extreme caution. Alcohol can be harsh on materials and may even strip color if used improperly. I recommend trying this only if other methods fail. Dampen a cotton swab or a very small corner of a clean cloth with isopropyl alcohol.

Lightly dab the stained area with the alcohol-soaked swab. Do not rub vigorously. You should see the stain transfer to the cotton swab. As soon as the stain lifts, rinse the area immediately and thoroughly with cool water. Never soak the entire toy in alcohol. Use this method sparingly and always follow with a good rinse. This helps to minimize any potential damage to the Scribble Scrubbie's fibers. Always ensure good ventilation when using alcohol.

## Common Cleaning Mistakes to Avoid

While cleaning Crayola Scribble Scrubbies is generally straightforward, some common mistakes can hinder your efforts or even damage the toys. I want to help you steer clear of these pitfalls. Knowing what not to do is just as important as knowing what to do. Avoiding these errors ensures your Scribble Scrubbies remain in excellent condition. This lets your children enjoy them for many years.

One of the most frequent mistakes is using hot water for cleaning. It might seem intuitive that hot water cleans better, but for Scribble Scrubbies, this is not true. Hot water can actually set certain marker dyes into the fibers. This makes the stains much harder, if not impossible, to remove. Always stick to cool or lukewarm water for washing. This keeps the fibers open and helps the colors release easily.

Another common error is harsh scrubbing. When a stain is stubborn, there is a temptation to scrub harder. However, aggressive scrubbing can damage the toy's fuzzy texture. It can make the fibers frayed or matted. This affects how new colors adhere to the toy. Always use gentle, circular motions with the soft brush. Patience and a mild cleaning agent are more effective than brute force.

Leaving markers on the Scribble Scrubbies for too long is another common issue. The longer the ink sits on the fibers, the more it can seep in and dry. This makes it much more challenging to wash off completely. I always encourage cleaning the pets shortly after playtime. If your child has finished coloring, make a habit of washing them soon after. This prevents stains from setting. It keeps the toys ready for their next creative burst.

Using aggressive chemicals is a definite no-no. Bleach, harsh detergents, or industrial cleaners can severely damage the Scribble Scrubbies. These chemicals can discolor the fibers, break them down, or leave behind residues harmful to children. Stick to very mild dish soap or the gentle methods I described earlier. If you are unsure about a cleaning product, it is best to avoid it. Using the wrong products can cause irreversible damage. Just as you would not clean a delicate rug with harsh solvents, Scribble Scrubbies require gentle care. Understanding [what should you not clean carpet with](https://cosyhomecreation.com/what-should-you-not-clean-carpet-with) can help you avoid similar mistakes with other household items, including children's toys. The principle of using appropriate, mild cleaners extends across different cleaning tasks.

Improper drying is also a mistake. Rushing the drying process with high heat, like a hair dryer, can shrink or damage the toy's fibers. It can also cause any remaining faint stains to become more visible. Always allow your Scribble Scrubbies to air dry completely in a well-ventilated area. This preserves their shape and texture. It also ensures they are fully dry before the next play session.

By avoiding these common cleaning mistakes, you will ensure your Crayola Scribble Scrubbies stay soft, vibrant, and ready for countless coloring adventures. A little care goes a long way in preserving these cherished toys.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I put Scribble Scrubbies in the washing machine?

No, I do not recommend putting Scribble Scrubbies in a washing machine. The machine's agitation can damage the delicate fuzzy fibers. It might also distort the toy's shape. Handwashing under cool, running water with a gentle scrub brush is the safest and most effective method. This protects the toy's texture and ensures a thorough clean.

### What if the color does not come off completely?

It is common for some faint traces of color, especially darker shades, to remain after extensive use. Do not worry too much about it. Try the advanced cleaning methods mentioned, such as a gentle rub with a magic eraser or a baking soda paste. These can often lift those stubborn ghost stains. The toy will still function well and new colors will cover old ones.

### Are all markers safe for Scribble Scrubbies?

No, only Crayola Washable Markers are recommended for Scribble Scrubbies. These markers are specially formulated to wash off easily from the toy's fibers. Other types of markers, including permanent markers or even some non-Crayola washable markers, may not wash off completely. They can leave stubborn stains. Always use the markers included with the set or designated Scribble Scrubbies markers.

### How often should I clean them?

You should clean your Scribble Scrubbies after every play session. Or clean them whenever your child wants to change the design. Cleaning them promptly helps prevent marker stains from setting into the fibers. This makes the cleaning process much easier and more effective. Regular cleaning also keeps the toys fresh and hygienic.

### Can I use regular soap?

You should use a mild, unscented, and dye-free dish soap. Avoid harsh chemical soaps, laundry detergents, or hand soaps with strong dyes and fragrances. These can be too abrasive or leave residue. They might also irritate sensitive skin. A small drop of gentle dish soap is sufficient for most cleaning needs.
