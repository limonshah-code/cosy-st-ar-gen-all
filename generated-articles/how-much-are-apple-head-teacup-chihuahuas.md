## Common Health Issues in Apple Head Teacup Chihuahuas and Their Costs

Apple Head Teacup Chihuahuas, while charming, are prone to specific health issues. Their small size and unique head shape contribute to these vulnerabilities. Understanding these potential problems is crucial. It helps you prepare for possible medical expenses. Being aware can help you provide the best care for your tiny companion.

### Patellar Luxation

Patellar luxation is a common orthopedic problem in small breeds. This condition involves the kneecap slipping out of place. It can cause limping or pain. In severe cases, it requires surgery. The cost of surgery can range from $1,500 to $4,000 per knee. Physical therapy may also be needed. This adds to the overall expense.

Reputable breeders screen parent dogs for this condition. This helps reduce its occurrence in puppies. However, it can still develop. Regular vet check-ups can help detect it early. Early detection may lead to less invasive treatments.

### Hydrocephalus

Hydrocephalus is a neurological condition. It involves an excess of cerebrospinal fluid in the brain. This is more common in Apple Head Chihuahuas due to their domed skull. Symptoms include seizures, blindness, or behavioral changes. Diagnosis involves imaging like MRI, which is expensive. Treatment can involve medication or shunt surgery.

Diagnosis and treatment for hydrocephalus can be very costly. An MRI alone can cost $1,000-$3,000. Surgery, if an option, can reach $5,000-$10,000. This is a severe condition. It significantly impacts a dog's quality of life. Always choose a breeder who actively screens for and avoids breeding dogs with this predisposition.

### Hypoglycemia (Low Blood Sugar)

Teacup Chihuahuas are particularly susceptible to hypoglycemia. Their small bodies have less fat storage. They cannot regulate blood sugar as effectively. Symptoms include lethargy, trembling, and collapse. This condition can be life-threatening if not addressed quickly. It is especially common in young puppies.

Managing hypoglycemia involves frequent small meals. Owners must monitor their puppy's eating habits. Emergency vet visits for severe episodes can cost hundreds of dollars. Carrying a glucose source like Karo syrup is crucial. This helps stabilize blood sugar during an emergency. Education from your breeder about preventing and treating this condition is vital.

### Dental Problems

Small dog breeds, including Chihuahuas, are prone to dental issues. They often have overcrowded teeth. This leads to plaque buildup and gum disease. Retained puppy teeth are also common. These need removal by a vet. Regular dental cleanings under anesthesia are often necessary. These cleanings can cost $300-$800 per session.

Untreated dental disease can lead to more serious health problems. It can affect organs like the heart and kidneys. Daily tooth brushing helps prevent issues. Discuss dental care with your vet regularly. They can recommend the best routine.
