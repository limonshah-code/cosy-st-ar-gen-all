## Transitioning Your Puppy to Royal Canin and Feeding Guidelines

Once you decide on Royal Canin puppy food, a proper transition is essential. Switching foods too quickly can cause digestive upset. Puppies have sensitive stomachs. A gradual change helps their system adjust.

Understanding feeding guidelines is also important. Puppies need the right amount of food for their growth. Overfeeding can lead to obesity. Underfeeding can hinder development. Royal Canin provides clear feeding instructions on their packaging.

### How to Introduce New Food Safely

When switching your puppy to Royal Canin, do it slowly over 7-10 days. Start by mixing a small amount of the new food with their old food. Gradually increase the proportion of Royal Canin each day.

Here is a common transition schedule:

*   **Days 1-2:** 75% old food, 25% Royal Canin
*   **Days 3-4:** 50% old food, 50% Royal Canin
*   **Days 5-6:** 25% old food, 75% Royal Canin
*   **Day 7 onwards:** 100% Royal Canin

Watch your puppy for any signs of digestive upset during this time. These signs include diarrhea or vomiting. If problems occur, slow down the transition. Consult your vet if issues persist.
