## Keep Your Airwrap Sparkling: How To Clean Dyson Airwrap Attachments

The Dyson Airwrap creates stunning hairstyles. Many users love its versatile attachments. However, these tools accumulate hair product residue and natural oils over time. Learning how to clean Dyson Airwrap attachments properly is essential. Regular cleaning maintains optimal performance and hygiene. It also prolongs the lifespan of your valuable styling device. I will show you simple, effective ways to keep your Airwrap attachments in top condition. This guide covers everything from daily upkeep to deep cleaning. You will learn specific steps for barrels, brushes, and the pre-styling dryer.

### Takeaway

*   Regularly clean all Dyson Airwrap attachments to remove buildup.
*   Use gentle soap and water for barrels and the pre-styling dryer.
*   Carefully remove hair from brushes and wipe them clean.
*   Always ensure attachments are completely dry before reattaching.
*   Proper cleaning maintains performance and extends attachment life.

### Main Query Answer

To clean Dyson Airwrap attachments, gently wipe them with a damp cloth and mild soap. Remove hair from brushes with a comb. For barrels, soak briefly in warm soapy water, then scrub softly. Ensure all parts dry completely before reuse to prevent damage.

## Why Cleaning Your Dyson Airwrap Attachments Matters

You probably love your Dyson Airwrap for its ability to style hair quickly. But do you ever think about what builds up on those attachments? Hair products, natural oils from your scalp, and environmental dust all collect on the surfaces. This accumulation affects your Airwrap's performance and your hair's health. Dirty attachments can lead to less effective styling.

When attachments become clogged, the airflow can change. This means your curls might not hold as well, or your hair might not smooth effectively. The Coanda effect, which makes the Airwrap so special, relies on clear airflow. Blocked vents or bristles disrupt this effect significantly. This reduces the efficiency you expect from a premium styling tool.

Beyond performance, hygiene is a big concern. Dirty attachments can transfer old product residue or even bacteria to your clean hair. This can make your hair look dull or feel greasy faster. Regularly cleaning your Dyson Airwrap attachments prevents these issues. It keeps your hair looking its best and ensures a hygienic styling routine. Proper care means your Airwrap continues to deliver beautiful results every time.

Neglecting cleaning also shortens the life of your attachments. Product buildup can degrade the materials over time. Bristles on brushes might become stiff or break more easily. Metal components on barrels can suffer corrosion from residue. Investing a little time in cleaning saves you money in the long run. It prevents the need for early replacement of expensive parts. Just like any other valuable appliance, your Dyson Airwrap needs regular attention to stay in top shape.

## Tools You'll Need to Clean Dyson Airwrap Attachments

Before you begin the cleaning process, gather all necessary tools. Having everything ready saves time and makes the job easier. You do not need many specialized items. Most are common household supplies. I always make sure to have these items handy before I start.

First, you will need a soft, lint-free cloth or a microfiber towel. These are great for wiping surfaces without scratching them. They also absorb water well, which is important for drying. Avoid using rough cloths that could damage the delicate finishes. A gentle touch is key when dealing with high-quality styling tools.

Next, prepare a bowl of warm water. This water will be the base for your cleaning solution. Lukewarm water helps dissolve product buildup more effectively than cold water. Make sure the bowl is clean to avoid transferring dirt. This simple step prepares the stage for effective cleaning.

You will also need a mild soap or shampoo. Dish soap is usually too harsh. I recommend using a gentle hand soap or even a small amount of your regular hair shampoo. These are formulated to be gentle on hair and skin, making them safe for your attachments. A small amount goes a long way. Avoid strong chemicals or abrasive cleaners.

For removing stubborn hair from brushes, a small comb or a pair of tweezers will be useful. The Dyson Airwrap often comes with a small cleaning brush for the filter, but a regular fine-tooth comb also works for the brush attachments. These tools help disentangle hair strands without damaging the bristles. Be patient during this step to avoid pulling out the brush fibers.

Finally, you might want a soft-bristle brush, like an old toothbrush. This helps scrub away dried product buildup in tight spots or on the textured surfaces of the barrels. It reaches areas a cloth cannot. Just ensure the bristles are soft to prevent scratching. A clean, soft brush is perfect for gentle scrubbing.

## Cleaning Different Dyson Airwrap Barrels: Coanda Smoothing and Curling Barrels

The Coanda smoothing dryer and curling barrels are central to the Airwrap's magic. These attachments direct airflow to style your hair. Over time, hair product residue, natural oils, and even dust can accumulate on their surfaces. This buildup reduces efficiency and can leave a dull film. Learning how to clean these Dyson Airwrap attachments properly is simple.

Start by detaching the barrel from the main Airwrap device. Ensure the Airwrap is unplugged and completely cool. Never clean the attachments while they are hot or connected to power. Safety first is my motto, and this prevents any electrical mishaps. This separation allows you to clean each part thoroughly without risk.

Prepare a solution of warm water and a few drops of mild soap in a clean bowl. Submerge the barrels into this soapy water for about 10 to 15 minutes. This soaking helps to loosen any dried-on hair spray, mousse, or oil. The warm water acts as a gentle solvent, making removal easier. Avoid using very hot water, which could potentially warp plastic parts.

After soaking, use a soft-bristle brush, like an old toothbrush, to gently scrub the surfaces of the barrels. Pay close attention to the small vents and openings where air flows. Product buildup can often clog these tiny spaces. The soft bristles help dislodge any remaining residue without scratching the barrel's finish. Remember to be gentle; there is no need for harsh scrubbing.

Rinse the barrels thoroughly under cool, running water. Ensure all soap residue is completely washed away. Leftover soap can attract more dirt or leave a film on your hair. After rinsing, shake off excess water. Then, use a clean, lint-free cloth to wipe the barrels dry. It is crucial that the attachments are completely dry before you use them again or store them. Any moisture can lead to mold or damage.

## Maintaining Your Dyson Airwrap Brushes: Firm and Soft Smoothing Brushes

The Dyson Airwrap comes with various brush attachments. These include the firm smoothing brush and the soft smoothing brush. These brushes are fantastic for creating sleek styles and adding volume. However, they also collect a lot of hair, lint, and styling product buildup. Proper maintenance keeps them effective and hygienic. Cleaning these Dyson Airwrap attachments involves a few specific steps to protect their bristles.

First, remove all visible hair from the brush head. This is the most crucial initial step. Use a fine-tooth comb or your fingers to gently pull out tangled hair from the bristles. You can also use tweezers for smaller, more stubborn strands. Work slowly and carefully to avoid damaging or pulling out the bristles. I find this step oddly satisfying, seeing all the collected hair come off.

Once the bulk of the hair is gone, prepare a bowl of warm, soapy water. Use a very mild soap, such as a gentle hand soap or baby shampoo. Dip only the bristle head of the brush into the soapy water. Avoid submerging the entire attachment, especially the connector part. The electrical components should not get wet. This targeted approach protects the integrity of the attachment.

Gently agitate the bristles in the soapy water. You can use your fingers or a soft cloth to lightly rub the bristles and base. This helps loosen any accumulated product residue. For particularly stubborn buildup, you can use a clean, soft-bristle toothbrush to gently scrub between the bristles. Focus on removing the greasy film that can coat the brush. This ensures the bristles remain flexible and effective for styling.

After scrubbing, rinse the brush head under cool, running water. Hold the brush with the bristles facing down to prevent water from entering the main attachment body. Ensure all soap is thoroughly rinsed away. Shake off excess water. Finally, lay the brush flat on a clean towel or hang it upside down to air dry completely. It is very important that the bristles are fully dry before you store or use the brush again. Moisture can encourage mildew or damage the brush.

Regular care for these brushes extends their life significantly. It ensures they perform as intended, giving you smooth, beautiful hair every time. Ignoring this can lead to stiff, ineffective bristles and reduced styling power.

## Deep Cleaning the Pre-Styling Dryer Attachment

The pre-styling dryer is often the first attachment many people use with their Dyson Airwrap. It quickly takes hair from wet to damp, preparing it for styling. Because it handles wet hair, it can accumulate moisture, lint, and product residue. Deep cleaning this Dyson Airwrap attachment is straightforward. It ensures efficient drying and prevents any unpleasant odors.

Start by detaching the pre-styling dryer from the main Airwrap machine. As always, make sure the device is unplugged and cool. This separation allows safe and thorough cleaning. Inspect the nozzle and filter area for any visible lint or hair caught in the vents. Sometimes, small strands of hair can get pulled into the air intake during use. Removing these improves airflow.

Prepare a bowl with warm water and a small amount of mild liquid soap. Dip a clean, soft cloth into this soapy water. Wring out the cloth so it is damp, not dripping wet. Gently wipe down the entire outer surface of the pre-styling dryer attachment. Pay attention to the nozzle and the body where product residue might settle. The damp cloth effectively lifts away surface dirt and greasy films.

For the air intake area, which is typically a mesh or grate, use a soft-bristle brush (like an old, clean toothbrush) dipped lightly in the soapy water. Gently scrub this area to dislodge any trapped dust or lint. This ensures proper airflow. Be careful not to force water into any internal components. The goal is to clean the exterior and the immediate intake points.

After cleaning with soap, take another clean cloth, dampened only with plain water. Wipe down the attachment again to remove any soap residue. It is important to remove all traces of soap. Leftover soap can attract more dirt or leave a film when heated. This step ensures a clean, residue-free finish.

Finally, dry the pre-styling dryer attachment completely. Use a clean, dry, lint-free cloth to wipe all surfaces. You can also let it air dry for several hours, perhaps overnight, in a well-ventilated area. Ensure there is absolutely no moisture remaining inside the vents or on the surfaces before reattaching it to your Airwrap. Proper drying prevents mold growth and ensures safe operation. This deep cleaning keeps your pre-styling dryer ready for every styling session.

## Beyond the Attachments: Dyson Airwrap Filter Care

While the focus here is on how to clean Dyson Airwrap attachments, it is crucial to remember the device's main filter. The filter plays a vital role in your Airwrap's performance. It prevents hair, dust, and product particles from entering the motor. A clogged filter directly impacts airflow, reducing your Airwrap's efficiency and potentially causing it to overheat.

Dyson provides a filter cleaning brush with the Airwrap. This tool is specifically designed to clear debris from the filter mesh. To clean the filter, twist the filter cage counter-clockwise to remove it from the main unit. Use the provided brush to gently scrub the mesh. Rotate the brush to ensure all sides of the filter get cleaned. This helps remove trapped lint and dust.

For a more thorough clean, or if the filter is particularly dirty, you can rinse the filter cage under warm running water. After rinsing, shake off excess water. Allow the filter to air dry completely for at least 24 hours. It must be bone dry before reattaching it to your Airwrap. Using a wet filter can damage the motor and is a safety risk. I always plan my filter cleaning on a day I know I won't need my Airwrap for a while.

Regular filter cleaning is just as important as cleaning your attachments. It ensures consistent airflow. This helps your attachments work their best. It also protects the internal mechanics of your Airwrap. Neglecting the filter is a common reason for decreased performance and eventual device malfunction. A clean filter ensures your Airwrap continues to deliver powerful, efficient styling.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Airwrap Attachments

Cleaning your Dyson Airwrap attachments is straightforward. However, a few common mistakes can damage your valuable tools or reduce their lifespan. Being aware of these pitfalls helps you clean effectively and safely. I have learned these lessons over time and want to share them with you.

One major mistake is using harsh cleaning products. Avoid strong chemical cleaners, abrasive scrubs, or harsh dish soaps. These products can strip coatings, dull finishes, or even degrade the plastic and metal components of your attachments. Stick to mild soap or shampoo and warm water. Gentle cleaning ensures longevity and preserves the aesthetics of your Airwrap.

Another frequent error is submerging the entire attachment in water. While the barrels can handle some soaking, the brush attachments have electrical connection points. Getting water into these areas can cause severe damage or electrical hazards. Always aim to clean only the bristle or working part of the attachment. This careful approach prevents water damage to sensitive components.

Not drying attachments completely is another common oversight. Any residual moisture can lead to mold, mildew, or corrosion. It can also cause electrical issues if water seeps into connections. After cleaning, always air dry attachments thoroughly for several hours, or even overnight. Patience here prevents long-term problems. I always place them on a clean, dry towel in an open area.

Using abrasive tools for scrubbing can also be detrimental. Metal brushes, scourers, or anything that could scratch the surface should be avoided. The Dyson Airwrap attachments have a polished finish that you want to preserve. Stick to soft-bristle brushes, lint-free cloths, or old toothbrushes. Gentle tools do the job without causing cosmetic or functional damage.

Finally, neglecting regular cleaning is a mistake. Waiting until attachments are heavily caked with product buildup makes cleaning much harder. It also means you are styling with dirty tools for longer. A quick clean after every few uses is far better than a deep clean once a year. Consistent, light cleaning keeps your attachments in prime condition with minimal effort.

## Tips for Regular Dyson Airwrap Attachment Maintenance

Keeping your Dyson Airwrap attachments in top condition does not always require a full deep cleaning session. Regular, quick maintenance between uses can significantly extend their life and maintain performance. I have developed a few simple habits that make a big difference. These tips integrate easily into your routine.

After each styling session, take a moment to quickly wipe down your used attachments. A dry microfiber cloth is perfect for this. It removes surface dust and any light hair product residue before it has a chance to harden. This simple step prevents major buildup. It only takes a few seconds but saves a lot of effort later.

For your brush attachments, immediately remove any trapped hair after use. Use your fingers or a comb to gently pull out strands from the bristles. This prevents hair from becoming deeply embedded and making the brushes less effective. It also stops hair from clogging the airflow in the brush head. This quick detangling is crucial for maintaining brush efficacy.

Consider cleaning your attachments more frequently if you use a lot of hair products. Hairsprays, mousses, and gels leave more residue than styling with just heat. If you notice a sticky film or a change in airflow, it is a clear sign that cleaning is due. Adjust your cleaning frequency based on your product usage and hair type. My curly hair friends often clean theirs more frequently due to richer products.

Store your attachments properly to prevent dust and damage. The Dyson Airwrap often comes with a storage case. Using this case protects your attachments from environmental dust and accidental knocks. Storing them neatly also prevents them from getting dirty between uses. This keeps them cleaner for longer and ready for your next styling session.

Finally, make it a habit to inspect your attachments regularly. Look for any signs of wear, damage, or excessive buildup. Early detection of issues allows for prompt cleaning or care. This proactive approach helps you address minor problems before they become major ones. Regular inspection ensures your Dyson Airwrap attachments are always in perfect working order.

## FAQ Section

### Can I wash my Airwrap attachments with soap?
Yes, you can wash many Dyson Airwrap attachments with mild soap. Use a gentle hand soap or shampoo mixed with warm water. This is suitable for the barrels and the pre-styling dryer. Avoid harsh dish soaps or strong chemical cleaners. Always ensure soap residue is fully rinsed away.

### How often should I clean Airwrap attachments?
The cleaning frequency depends on your usage. If you use your Airwrap daily and apply many hair products, clean attachments weekly. For less frequent use or minimal product application, cleaning every 2-4 weeks is often sufficient. Regularly remove hair from brushes after each use.

### What if my Airwrap attachment smells burnt?
A burnt smell often indicates product buildup or trapped hair is overheating. Immediately stop using the attachment. Clean it thoroughly following the steps for barrels or brushes. Also, check the main Airwrap filter for clogs. If the smell persists after cleaning, contact Dyson support.

### Can I use alcohol to clean attachments?
No, avoid using alcohol-based cleaners on Dyson Airwrap attachments. Alcohol can strip protective coatings, damage plastic, or degrade the materials over time. Stick to gentle soap and water for safe and effective cleaning. Harsh chemicals are unnecessary and potentially harmful.

### How do I dry Airwrap attachments properly?
After cleaning, shake off excess water from the attachments. Use a clean, lint-free cloth to wipe them dry. Then, allow them to air dry completely for several hours, ideally overnight. Ensure there is no moisture left before reattaching or storing them. This prevents mold and damage.

### Can I put my Airwrap attachments in a dishwasher?
Absolutely not. Dyson Airwrap attachments are not dishwasher safe. The high heat, harsh detergents, and strong water jets in a dishwasher can cause severe damage. This includes warping, melting, and degrading the materials. Always hand wash them gently.
