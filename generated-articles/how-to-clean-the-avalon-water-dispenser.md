## How To Clean The Avalon Water Dispenser

Clean drinking water is vital for good health. An Avalon water dispenser provides convenient access to hydration. However, like any appliance that handles water, it needs regular cleaning. Mold, bacteria, and mineral deposits can build up over time. This article shows you how to clean the Avalon water dispenser thoroughly. We will cover why cleaning matters, what supplies you need, and a step-by-step cleaning process. You will also learn about routine maintenance and common troubleshooting tips.

### Takeaway

*   Regularly clean your Avalon water dispenser to prevent bacteria and mineral buildup.
*   Use a solution of white vinegar and water for effective sanitization.
*   Pay attention to both the interior tanks and exterior components like spigots and drip trays.
*   Always ensure the dispenser is unplugged and fully drained before starting any cleaning.
*   Rinse all parts thoroughly to remove any cleaning solution residue before use.

To clean your Avalon water dispenser, unplug it, drain all water, and use a vinegar-water solution to flush internal components. Scrub exterior parts like the drip tray and spigots with mild soap, then rinse thoroughly. Reassemble only when completely dry for fresh, clean water.

### Why Regular Cleaning of Your Avalon Water Dispenser Matters

Keeping your Avalon water dispenser clean is more important than you might think. Many people use these dispensers daily, assuming the water stays pristine. However, water dispensers can become breeding grounds for unwanted guests if not maintained. Mold and bacteria thrive in damp environments. They can easily develop inside the water lines and reservoirs of your dispenser.

Drinking water from a contaminated dispenser can lead to health issues. It might cause upset stomachs or other mild illnesses. Regular cleaning removes these harmful microorganisms. It ensures the water you drink is safe and clean. You will taste the difference with properly maintained equipment.

Beyond health, cleaning extends the life of your appliance. Mineral deposits, like limescale, build up over time. These deposits can clog lines and affect the dispenser's heating or cooling functions. Regular cleaning removes this buildup. This helps your Avalon dispenser work efficiently for many years. Neglecting cleaning can lead to costly repairs or even needing a new unit sooner than expected. Prioritizing cleanliness saves you money in the long run.

I consider regular cleaning an essential part of home maintenance. It protects my family's health and preserves our appliances. A clean water dispenser means better-tasting water and peace of mind. Taking the time to clean it properly is a small effort with big rewards. This commitment to hygiene ensures your water dispenser delivers pure refreshment every time you pour a glass.

### Essential Supplies for Cleaning Your Avalon Water Dispenser

Before you start cleaning your Avalon water dispenser, gather all the necessary supplies. Having everything ready makes the process smoother and more efficient. I always find it helpful to lay out all my tools. This prevents me from having to stop mid-task. The right supplies ensure a thorough and safe cleaning job.

Here is a list of what you will need:

*   **White Vinegar:** This is your primary cleaning and sanitizing agent. It is natural, effective, and safe for most water dispenser components. Vinegar helps break down mineral deposits and kills bacteria.
*   **Mild Dish Soap:** Use this for cleaning the exterior surfaces, drip tray, and spigots. Make sure it is a gentle, unscented soap.
*   **Soft Cloths or Sponges:** You will need a few of these. Microfiber cloths are great for wiping down surfaces without scratching them. They are also highly absorbent.
*   **Small Brushes:** A bottle brush or a pipe cleaner can reach inside spigots and small crevices. These areas often harbor hidden grime.
*   **Large Empty Bucket or Basin:** This is for draining water from the dispenser. It will catch any spills during the cleaning process.
*   **Clean Towels:** Use these for drying all parts thoroughly after rinsing. It is important to prevent moisture from remaining.
*   **Rubber Gloves:** Protect your hands from cleaning solutions and germs. I always wear gloves during cleaning tasks.
*   **Flashlight (Optional):** This can help you see inside the water reservoir. It ensures you do not miss any spots.

It is crucial to avoid harsh chemicals like bleach or abrasive cleaners. Bleach can leave a strong odor and residue. Abrasive cleaners might scratch the dispenser's surfaces. Sticking to vinegar and mild soap is the safest and most effective approach. For persistent mineral buildup, vinegar is often enough, similar to how one might clean calcium buildup on other water dispensing appliances. Proper preparation with these supplies ensures a successful cleaning session.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Your Avalon Water Dispenser's Exterior

Cleaning the exterior of your Avalon water dispenser is the first major step. This process focuses on the parts you see and touch most often. It prevents germs from spreading and keeps your dispenser looking great. I always start here because it prepares the unit for the deeper internal cleaning.

Follow these steps carefully:

1.  **Unplug the Dispenser:** Safety first. Always disconnect the power cord from the wall outlet. This prevents any electrical hazards. Ensure the unit is completely off before proceeding.
2.  **Remove the Water Bottle:** Lift the water bottle off the dispenser. Place it aside or replace it with a new one if it is empty. If your Avalon is a bottom-load model, open the door and slide out the empty bottle.
3.  **Drain Remaining Water:** Place your large bucket under the dispensing spigots. Open both the hot and cold water spigots. Let all the remaining water drain out of the internal tanks. This process can take a few minutes. Make sure the hot water switch is off if you have one. Then, let the water cool before draining it. You might need to press the hot water button to release the remaining hot water after it cools.
4.  **Remove the Drip Tray and Grille:** The drip tray catches spills and can get very dirty. Pull out the drip tray from its slot. Remove the grille on top of the tray. These parts often collect dust, debris, and water spots. They also might house mold.
    *   **Cleaning the Drip Tray and Grille:** Wash the drip tray and grille in warm, soapy water. Use your mild dish soap and a sponge. Scrub away any grime, mold, or mineral stains. For stubborn spots, let them soak for a few minutes. Rinse them thoroughly under running water. Ensure all soap residue is gone. Dry them completely with a clean towel.
5.  **Clean the Spigots/Paddles:** The spigots are high-touch areas. They need careful cleaning. Use a small brush or a cotton swab dipped in warm, soapy water. Scrub around and inside the spigots. Make sure to get into all the crevices. Rinse them by wiping with a clean, damp cloth. You want to remove all soap residue. This step is important for hygiene, similar to cleaning other dispenser spigots. If you have experience cleaning other types of dispensers, like a Primo, you will find this process familiar. More guidance can be found in a general guide on [how do you clean a Primo water dispenser](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-do-you-clean-a-primo-water-dispenser).
6.  **Wiping Down Exterior Surfaces:** Dampen a soft cloth with warm, soapy water. Wipe down the entire exterior of the dispenser. This includes the front, sides, and top. Pay attention to control panels and buttons. Remove any fingerprints, dust, or smudges. Follow up with a second cloth dampened with plain water to remove all soap residue. Finally, dry the entire exterior thoroughly with a clean, dry towel. This prevents water spots and mold growth. Ensuring all exterior parts are sparkling clean is satisfying. It makes the entire unit look fresh and inviting. This step also prepares the dispenser for the deep internal cleaning.

### Deep Cleaning the Interior of Your Avalon Water Dispenser

Cleaning the interior of your Avalon water dispenser is the most critical step. This is where water is stored and dispensed. It needs proper sanitization to ensure water quality. I always give this part my full attention. This internal cleaning prevents bacteria and biofilm buildup.

Here is how to deep clean the inside:

1.  **Prepare the Cleaning Solution:** For an effective and safe cleaning solution, mix white vinegar with clean water. A common ratio is one part white vinegar to three parts water. For example, if your dispenser's reservoir holds about a gallon, use one quart of vinegar and three quarts of water. This solution is powerful enough to kill bacteria and dissolve mineral deposits. It also leaves no harmful residue.
2.  **Pour Cleaning Solution into Reservoir:** Carefully pour the vinegar-water solution into the dispenser's top-loading reservoir. If you have a bottom-load dispenser, you might need to use a clean funnel or a small, clean pitcher to get the solution into the internal reservoir through the water intake port. Refer to your Avalon user manual for specific instructions on accessing the internal reservoir for bottom-load models.
3.  **Let the Solution Sit:** Allow the cleaning solution to sit inside the reservoir for at least 30 minutes to an hour. For heavy buildup or if it has been a long time since the last cleaning, you can let it sit for up to two hours. This soaking time allows the vinegar to work its magic. It breaks down deposits and sanitizes the surfaces. I usually set a timer to ensure adequate soaking.
4.  **Flush the Hot and Cold Tanks:**
    *   **Cold Water Tank:** Place your bucket under the cold water spigot. Open the spigot and allow several cups of the vinegar solution to flow out. Close the spigot. Repeat this process a few times until you have flushed a good amount of the solution through the cold line.
    *   **Hot Water Tank (Caution Recommended):** If your dispenser has a hot water tank, turn on the hot water switch. Wait a few minutes for the solution to heat up. Then, place your bucket under the hot water spigot. Carefully open the hot water spigot and let a few cups of the hot vinegar solution flow out. Be very careful as the water will be hot. Close the spigot. Allow the hot water switch to remain on for a short period to let the hot solution work. Turn off the hot water switch again before proceeding.
    5.  **Drain the Remaining Solution:** After flushing both tanks, open both the hot and cold spigots again. Let all the remaining vinegar solution drain completely into your bucket. This ensures the tanks are as empty as possible.
5.  **Final Rinsing Process:** This step is crucial to remove any vinegar taste or odor.
    *   Refill the dispenser's reservoir with fresh, clean water.
    *   Let this clean water sit for about 10-15 minutes.
    *   Drain several cups of water from both the hot and cold spigots, just as you did with the vinegar solution.
    *   Repeat this entire rinsing process (refill, sit, drain) at least two to three more times. The goal is to completely flush out any vinegar residue.
    *   Taste the water after the final rinse. If you still detect a vinegar taste, perform another rinse cycle. It is important to remove all traces of the cleaning solution. A thorough rinse ensures your water tastes pure and fresh. The steps here are similar to cleaning other types of water dispensers, for example, Vitapur models. ### Sanitizing and Reassembling Your Avalon Water Dispenser

After thoroughly cleaning the interior and exterior, the next steps are sanitization and careful reassembly. Proper drying and reassembly are key to preventing future issues. I always make sure everything is completely dry before putting it back together. This prevents mold and mildew from forming.

Here is how to sanitize and reassemble your dispenser:

1.  **Ensure Complete Drying:** After the final rinse, allow your dispenser to air dry as much as possible. Leave the spigots open and the top reservoir cap off for a few hours. This promotes air circulation. You can also use a clean, dry towel to wipe down any accessible internal surfaces. Ensuring everything is bone dry is critical. Any residual moisture can lead to mold growth.
2.  **Reattach Exterior Components:** Once dry, reattach the drip tray and its grille. Ensure they click securely into place. These parts are easy to forget, but they are essential for catching drips.
3.  **Install a New Water Bottle:** If you have a bottom-load dispenser, slide in a new, full water bottle. If it is a top-load model, carefully place a new water bottle onto the piercing valve. Make sure it seats correctly. Listen for the gurgling sound as water fills the internal reservoirs.
4.  **Prime the Dispenser:** Before plugging the unit back in, you need to prime it. Open both the hot and cold spigots. Let water flow out until it runs steadily, without any air bubbles. This ensures the tanks are full. It prevents the hot water tank from overheating and damaging its element. This step is vital for safety and proper function.
5.  **Plug In the Dispenser:** Once primed, plug the power cord back into the wall outlet. Turn on the hot and cold water switches if your model has them. Allow the dispenser to cool and heat the water to their set temperatures. This typically takes about 15-30 minutes.
6.  **Test the Dispenser:** Dispense a small amount of water from both the hot and cold spigots. Check for proper water flow and temperature. Taste the water to ensure there is no lingering vinegar taste. If there is, repeat the rinsing steps until the water tastes clean. This final check confirms your Avalon dispenser is ready for safe use. The process of drying and reassembly is consistent across many water dispenser brands. For instance, the steps for putting back together a Glacier Bay unit would be quite similar, emphasizing thorough drying.
