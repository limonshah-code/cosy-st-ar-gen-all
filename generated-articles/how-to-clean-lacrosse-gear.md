## Keep Your Lacrosse Gear Clean and Fresh

Playing lacrosse means sweat, dirt, and often, some pretty strong smells. Trust me, I know the feeling of opening a gear bag and being hit by that unique athletic aroma. Learning how to clean lacrosse gear properly is not just about smell, though. It protects your health, keeps your equipment in top shape, and even makes playing more enjoyable.

This guide will walk you through everything. We will cover cleaning helmets, pads, gloves, sticks, and even your gear bag. By the end, you will have a clear plan to maintain your lacrosse equipment. Get ready to banish the stink and prolong the life of your valuable gear!

**Takeaway:**
*   Regular cleaning prevents odors and bacterial growth.
*   Proper care extends the life of your lacrosse equipment.
*   Different gear parts need specific cleaning methods.
*   Drying completely is crucial to avoid mildew.

To clean lacrosse gear, you must regularly wash all components—pads, helmets, gloves, and sticks—using mild soap and water, then thoroughly air dry them. This process removes sweat, dirt, and bacteria, preventing odor and extending the life of your equipment.

### Why Cleaning Your Lacrosse Gear Matters for Players

Ignoring the dirt and grime on your lacrosse gear can lead to problems. First, there is the smell. That powerful odor comes from bacteria growing in sweat and moisture. This bacteria not only smells bad but can also cause skin infections like staph or ringworm. No one wants to deal with that after a game.

Beyond hygiene, clean gear lasts longer. Dirt and sweat break down materials over time. Pads lose their cushioning, straps weaken, and stitching can fray. Regular cleaning protects your investment. It helps your equipment stay sturdy and supportive, keeping you safe on the field. Also, fresh gear just feels better to wear. You can focus on your game when you are not distracted by discomfort or bad smells. My experience tells me that feeling good in your gear boosts confidence.

It is simple: clean gear means a healthier, safer, and more enjoyable lacrosse experience. Make cleaning a routine part of your post-game cool down. Your body and your wallet will thank you.

### Essential Cleaning Supplies for Your Lacrosse Equipment

Before you start cleaning, gather all your tools. Having everything ready makes the job much easier. You do not need fancy chemicals. Most items are already in your home. I keep a dedicated cleaning caddy for my sports gear.

Here is a list of what you will need:

*   **Mild Laundry Detergent or Sports Detergent:** Choose one without harsh dyes or perfumes. Specialized sports detergents work well against stubborn odors.
*   **White Vinegar:** This is a natural disinfectant and deodorizer. It helps kill bacteria and cut through tough smells.
*   **Baking Soda:** Another excellent natural deodorizer. It absorbs odors.
*   **Soft Brushes or Old Toothbrushes:** Perfect for scrubbing stubborn dirt from small areas.
*   **Clean Towels:** You will need these for wiping and drying.
*   **Spray Bottle:** Handy for mixing and applying cleaning solutions.
*   **Large Basin or Bathtub:** For hand washing larger items.
*   **Clothesline or Drying Rack:** Essential for proper air drying.
*   **Rubbing Alcohol (Optional):** For disinfecting helmet interiors.
*   **Disinfectant Wipes:** Quick clean-ups on non-absorbent surfaces.

Always read your gear’s care labels if they are still attached. Some items have specific washing instructions. Using the right supplies protects your gear and ensures a thorough clean.

### How to Clean Lacrosse Pads and Protective Gear

Lacrosse pads, including shoulder pads, arm guards, and rib pads, absorb a lot of sweat. This makes them prime spots for bacteria and odors. Cleaning them regularly is critical for hygiene and extending their life. I typically clean my pads after every few uses, or immediately if they get really soaked.

Here is how to effectively clean lacrosse pads:

1.  **Preparation:** Remove any removable liners or straps from the pads. Shake off loose dirt or grass.
2.  **Soak (Optional but Recommended):** Fill a large basin or bathtub with cool water. Add about half a cup of white vinegar and a tablespoon of mild laundry detergent. Submerge the pads completely. Let them soak for 30-60 minutes. This helps loosen grime and neutralize odors.
3.  **Hand Wash:** Drain the soaking water. Refill the basin with fresh, cool water and a small amount of detergent. Use a soft brush or cloth to gently scrub all surfaces of the pads. Pay extra attention to seams and areas where sweat collects. If the pads are machine washable (check the label!), put them in a mesh laundry bag on a delicate cycle with cold water.
4.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse the pads under cool running water until all soap residue is gone. Squeeze out as much excess water as possible. Do not twist or wring aggressively, as this can damage padding.
5.  **Dry Completely:** This step is the most important to prevent mildew. Hang the pads on a clothesline or drying rack in a well-ventilated area. Direct sunlight helps, but avoid extreme heat which can damage materials. Ensure air circulates around all parts of the pads. It can take 24-48 hours for pads to dry completely. Never put damp pads back into your gear bag.

Properly cleaned pads keep you feeling fresh and protected. Remember, neglecting your pads can lead to skin irritation and a much shorter lifespan for the gear. ### Cleaning Your Lacrosse Helmet and Face Mask

Your lacrosse helmet is your most important piece of protective gear. It also gets very sweaty. Keeping it clean is vital for hygiene and clear vision. My own helmet tends to get pretty grimy inside from all the sweat.

Follow these steps to clean your helmet:

1.  **Disassemble:** First, carefully remove any removable padding or liners from inside the helmet. You can usually detach the face mask by unscrewing small clips or screws.
2.  **Clean the Shell:** Wipe the outside of the helmet shell with a damp cloth and mild soap. You can use a gentle all-purpose cleaner too. Remove any dirt, grass stains, or scuff marks. Avoid abrasive cleaners that might scratch the finish.
3.  **Clean Internal Padding and Straps:** If the internal padding is removable, wash it by hand in cool water with mild soap. Rinse thoroughly. If padding is not removable, use a damp cloth with a small amount of soap to wipe down the interior surfaces. Pay close attention to the forehead area and chin strap. You can also use a solution of rubbing alcohol (50/50 with water) in a spray bottle to disinfect the interior. Spray lightly and wipe clean.
4.  **Clean the Face Mask:** Use a damp cloth to wipe down the face mask. For stubborn dirt or rust spots, a soft brush with soap and water works well. Make sure to clean all crevices. Rinse and dry completely to prevent rust.
5.  **Dry and Reassemble:** Let all helmet components air dry completely in a well-ventilated area. This might take several hours. Once everything is bone dry, reassemble your helmet. Ensure all padding is correctly reinserted and the face mask is securely attached.

A clean helmet protects your head and prevents skin issues. It also makes your vision clearer, helping you perform better. Never submerge the entire helmet in water, as this can damage the internal components and padding adhesive.

### Keeping Lacrosse Gloves Fresh and Odor-Free

Lacrosse gloves are notorious for harboring sweat and bacteria. They get wet, they stay wet, and then they smell. Trust me, I have thrown out a few pairs because the smell became too overwhelming! But with proper care, you can keep your gloves fresh.

Here is how to tackle those smelly lacrosse gloves:

1.  **Remove Loose Debris:** Before washing, shake out any grass, dirt, or debris from inside the gloves.
2.  **Hand Wash is Best:** Fill a basin or sink with cool water. Add a small amount of mild laundry detergent or a specialized sports detergent. Submerge your gloves. Gently squeeze and agitate the gloves to work the soapy water through all the material. Focus on the palms and finger areas where sweat collects most.
3.  **Soak for Tough Odors:** For extra stinky gloves, add a quarter cup of white vinegar to the wash water and let them soak for 30 minutes. The vinegar helps to kill odor-causing bacteria. You can also sprinkle baking soda inside the gloves and let it sit overnight before washing.
4.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse the gloves under cool running water until no soap bubbles appear. Squeeze out as much excess water as possible. Do not wring them aggressively, as this can damage the stitching or padding.
5.  **Dry Correctly:** This is the most crucial step for gloves. Hang your gloves to air dry. You can use clothespins or a drying rack. Point the fingers downwards to help water drain. Place them in a well-ventilated area, preferably with good airflow, like near a fan. It can take 24-48 hours for gloves to dry completely. Never put them in a dryer or expose them to direct heat, as this can shrink or crack the leather palms.

Regular cleaning and proper drying will save your gloves from early retirement. For more detailed instructions on keeping your hand protection pristine, check out our guide on [how to clean lacrosse gloves](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-lacrosse-gloves).

### Caring for Your Lacrosse Stick: Head, Mesh, and Shaft

Your lacrosse stick is an extension of you on the field. Keeping it clean maintains its performance and extends its life. While it doesn't get as sweaty as pads or gloves, it still gathers dirt and grime. I always give my stick a quick wipe-down after practices and games.

Here is how to care for each part of your lacrosse stick:

#### Cleaning the Lacrosse Head

1.  **Wipe Down:** Use a damp cloth to wipe the entire head. Remove dirt, mud, and grass stains.
2.  **Gentle Scrub:** For tougher marks, use a soft brush or old toothbrush with a bit of mild soap and water. Scrub gently.
3.  **Rinse and Dry:** Rinse the head under cool water to remove all soap. Wipe it dry with a towel. Ensure no water remains in any crevices.

#### Cleaning the Lacrosse Mesh (Pocket)

The mesh pocket is vital for performance. Dirt and debris can affect its feel and hold.

1.  **Remove Debris:** Pick out any grass, dirt, or small pebbles from the mesh.
2.  **Gentle Wash:** Mix a small amount of mild soap with cool water. Dip a soft brush or sponge into the solution and gently scrub the mesh. Avoid harsh scrubbing that could damage the fibers.
3.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse the mesh under cool water, ensuring all soap is gone. Residual soap can make the mesh stiff.
4.  **Air Dry:** Allow the mesh to air dry completely. Do not try to speed up drying with heat, as this can shrink or stiffen the mesh, affecting your pocket.

For specific guidance on keeping your mesh in top shape, refer to our article on [how to clean lacrosse mesh](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-lacrosse-mesh).

#### Cleaning the Lacrosse Shaft

The shaft gets handled constantly. It needs regular cleaning too.

1.  **Wipe Down:** Use a damp cloth with mild soap to wipe the entire length of the shaft. This removes dirt, sweat, and hand residue.
2.  **Rinse and Dry:** Rinse the shaft with clean water and dry it thoroughly with a towel.
3.  **Check Grip:** If your shaft has a special grip tape, check its manufacturer's cleaning instructions. Most can be wiped with a damp cloth.

Keeping your stick clean ensures a consistent feel and look. It also prevents dirt from transferring to your hands or other gear.

### Don't Forget the Lacrosse Bag and Accessories

It is easy to focus on the gear itself and forget about the bag that carries it all. However, your lacrosse bag is a breeding ground for bacteria and odors if not cleaned. Everything else you put in there will just get re-contaminated. I always make sure my bag gets some attention too.

Here is how to clean your lacrosse bag and other accessories:

#### Cleaning Your Lacrosse Bag

1.  **Empty Completely:** Take everything out of your bag. Turn it upside down and shake out all loose dirt, grass, and debris.
2.  **Vacuum (Optional):** If you have a vacuum cleaner with a hose attachment, vacuum the inside of the bag to remove any remaining small particles.
3.  **Spot Clean:** For small stains or dirty areas, use a damp cloth with mild soap and scrub the spot.
4.  **Full Wash (If Machine Washable):** Check the care label on your bag. Many fabric bags can be machine washed on a gentle cycle with cold water and mild detergent. Place it in a large mesh laundry bag if you have one to protect zippers and buckles.
5.  **Hand Wash (If Not Machine Washable):** Fill a tub with cool water and mild soap. Submerge the bag and scrub it with a brush, paying attention to straps and bottom. Rinse thoroughly.
6.  **Air Dry:** Hang the bag open in a well-ventilated area to air dry completely. Make sure all compartments are open. This can take a day or two.

#### Cleaning Lacrosse Balls

Lacrosse balls get incredibly dirty. They can carry bacteria too.

1.  **Bucket Wash:** Fill a bucket with warm, soapy water (using mild dish soap). Dump all your balls in.
2.  **Scrub or Soak:** You can scrub them with a stiff brush or let them soak for a while to loosen dirt.
3.  **Rinse and Dry:** Rinse them thoroughly and let them air dry on a towel or in a mesh bag.

#### Cleaning Mouthguards

Your mouthguard goes in your mouth, so it must be clean!

1.  **Rinse After Every Use:** Rinse it with cool water after each practice or game.
2.  **Brush and Soak:** At least once a week, brush it with an old toothbrush and toothpaste. You can also soak it in a denture cleaner solution or a diluted mouthwash solution for 15-30 minutes.
3.  **Rinse Well:** Always rinse it very well before putting it back in your mouth.

By cleaning your bag and accessories, you maintain a cleaner environment for all your freshly washed gear. This helps prevent cross-contamination and keeps everything smelling much better.

### FAQs About Cleaning Lacrosse Gear

Regular cleaning of your lacrosse gear keeps it fresh and extends its life. Here are answers to some common questions.

**How often should I clean my lacrosse gear?**
You should clean smaller items like mouthguards and base layers after every use. Pads, gloves, and helmets need cleaning at least once a week, or more often if you play daily or sweat heavily. Your gear bag can be cleaned monthly or whenever it starts to smell. Consistency prevents tough odors.

**Can I machine wash lacrosse pads and gloves?**
Some lacrosse pads and gloves are machine washable, but always check the care label first. If allowed, use a delicate cycle with cold water and mild detergent. Place items in a mesh laundry bag to protect them. Never use hot water, as it can damage materials. Hand washing is often safer for delicate items.

**How do I get the smell out of my lacrosse helmet?**
To remove helmet odor, first, clean the padding and straps with mild soap and water. For persistent smells, spray the interior with a diluted solution of white vinegar and water (50/50) or rubbing alcohol. Let it air dry completely. Ensure good ventilation during drying. Baking soda can also absorb odors if sprinkled inside overnight.

**What's the best way to dry lacrosse gear?**
Air drying is the best method for all lacrosse gear. Hang items on a clothesline or drying rack in a well-ventilated area. Point glove fingers downwards. Ensure good airflow around all sides. Avoid direct sunlight for extended periods and never use a machine dryer or direct heat, as this can damage materials and cause shrinkage. Complete dryness prevents mildew.

**Can I use bleach on lacrosse equipment?**
No, you should avoid using bleach on lacrosse equipment. Bleach is very harsh and can degrade the materials, weaken stitching, and damage protective coatings on your gear. It can also cause discoloration. Stick to mild detergents, white vinegar, or specialized sports cleaning solutions for effective and safe cleaning.

**How should I store my clean lacrosse gear?**
Store your clean and completely dry lacrosse gear in a well-ventilated area. Avoid stuffing it into a sealed bag or locker. A mesh bag allows air circulation. This prevents moisture buildup and keeps bacteria from growing. Proper storage ensures your gear stays fresh and ready for the next game.

Your body, your wallet, and your teammates will thank you. Get out there and play hard, knowing your gear is ready to go!
