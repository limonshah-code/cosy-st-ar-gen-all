## Clean Your Dyson Airwrap Filter for Peak Performance

Has your Dyson Airwrap started feeling a bit less powerful? Perhaps your hair is not drying or curling as efficiently as it once did. Many Airwrap users find themselves wondering why their favorite styling tool is not performing like new. Often, the answer lies in a simple yet critical component: the filter. A clean filter is essential for optimal function.

Learning how to clean your Dyson Airwrap filter is a key part of maintaining its performance. Dirt and debris can restrict airflow, making your device work harder and less effectively. Regular cleaning prevents these issues, ensuring your Airwrap continues to deliver beautiful results. This guide will walk you through each step. I will show you how to maintain your Airwrap, keeping it in top condition for years to come.

### Takeaway

*   Regularly clean your Dyson Airwrap filter to maintain strong airflow and peak styling performance.
*   Remove the outer filter cage and use the provided cleaning brush to clear trapped hair and dust.
*   Gently wipe the inner mesh with a dry cloth; avoid water on electrical components.
*   Reassemble the filter only when completely dry.
*   Make filter cleaning a routine task to extend your Airwrap's lifespan.

## Why Your Dyson Airwrap Filter Needs Cleaning

Your Dyson Airwrap is a marvel of engineering, creating beautiful hairstyles with its Coanda effect. However, its efficiency relies heavily on unobstructed airflow. The small filter at the base of your Airwrap plays a crucial role in this process. It acts as a protective barrier, stopping hair, dust, and product residue from entering the motor.

Over time, this filter collects a surprising amount of debris. Every time you use your Airwrap, tiny particles of hair, lint, and styling product get drawn into the filter mesh. This accumulation forms a barrier, blocking the air pathway. When the filter becomes clogged, the motor must work harder to pull air through, leading to several problems. Your Airwrap might lose suction or heat unevenly. It could even shut off unexpectedly during use.

Ignoring a dirty filter can lead to long-term damage. The motor can overheat, reducing the lifespan of your expensive device. A clogged filter makes the Airwrap less effective at styling, giving you dull results. Keeping this filter clean ensures consistent airflow, protecting your investment and guaranteeing a great hair day every time. Proper maintenance truly matters for device longevity and performance. ## Gather Your Cleaning Tools: What You Will Need

Before you begin the cleaning process, ensure you have the correct tools at hand. Having everything ready makes the job quicker and more efficient. Dyson designed the Airwrap with user-friendly maintenance in mind. They provide some specific accessories for this purpose. You will not need many items, but the right ones are important.

First and foremost, you will need the Dyson filter cleaning brush. This brush typically comes with your Airwrap when you purchase it. It is a small, cylindrical brush with stiff bristles specifically sized to fit inside the filter cage. If you have misplaced it, Dyson sells replacements, or you can use a similar small, soft-bristled brush. This brush is essential for dislodging trapped hair and dust.

Next, have a soft, dry cloth ready. A microfiber cloth works best because it can pick up fine dust particles without leaving lint behind. You will use this cloth for wiping down surfaces. A slightly damp cloth (damp, not wet) can be used for the outer casing, but never for the internal filter components. Finally, ensure you have a clean, well-lit workspace. ## Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Your Dyson Airwrap Filter

Cleaning your Dyson Airwrap filter is a straightforward process, but following each step carefully is important. This ensures thorough cleaning without causing damage. I find that breaking it down makes it much easier to manage. Let's get your Airwrap back to its optimal state.

**Step 1: Power Off and Unplug Your Airwrap**
Safety always comes first. Before you do anything, make sure your Dyson Airwrap is completely turned off and unplugged from the power outlet. This prevents any accidental activation during cleaning. It also protects you from electrical hazards. Never clean an appliance while it is plugged in.

**Step 2: Locate and Remove the Filter Cage**
The filter cage is at the bottom of your Airwrap handle. It looks like a mesh cylinder. To remove it, gently twist the filter cage anti-clockwise until it unlocks. You will feel a slight click. Then, pull it downwards and away from the main body of the device. It should slide off easily. Be careful not to force it.

**Step 3: Use the Filter Cleaning Brush**
Take your Dyson filter cleaning brush. Insert it into the outer filter cage. Rotate the brush several times to dislodge hair, dust, and lint that has built up on the mesh. You might be surprised by how much debris comes out. For the inner filter, place the brush over the main body of the Airwrap where the filter cage was removed. Twist the brush back and forth to clean the fine inner mesh filter.

**Step 4: Tap Out Debris**
After brushing, gently tap the filter cage and the main body of the Airwrap over a trash can. This helps release any remaining loose debris. You can also lightly brush any visible remaining particles away with your fingers or a dry cloth. Be thorough but gentle in this process.

**Step 5: Wipe Down Surfaces**
Use your soft, dry cloth to wipe both the inside and outside of the removed filter cage. Also, carefully wipe the fine inner mesh filter that remains on the Airwrap handle. Ensure all dust and lint are gone. Remember, do not use water on the internal electrical components. A dry wipe is sufficient for these parts. For a visual guide, refer to instructions on [how to clean the filter on Dyson Airwrap](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-filter-on-dyson-airwrap).

**Step 6: Reassemble the Filter**
Once both the filter cage and the inner mesh are clean and dry, it is time to put everything back together. Align the filter cage with the base of the Airwrap. Push it upwards, then twist it clockwise until it locks securely into place. You should hear or feel a click, indicating it is properly reattached. Ensure it is secure before plugging in your device.

## Cleaning the Outer Filter Cage and Mesh

The outer filter cage is the first line of defense for your Dyson Airwrap. It is the part most visible and often the most clogged with larger debris. Cleaning this component correctly ensures that your Airwrap can breathe freely. This step is usually quick and straightforward.

Once you have removed the filter cage from the Airwrap handle, you will notice a buildup of hair and dust. This is normal. Take your specialized Dyson filter cleaning brush. Insert the brush into the cylindrical cage. Rotate the brush firmly, scrubbing against the mesh from the inside. This action helps to loosen and pull out trapped hair and lint. You will see strands of hair and dust bunnies coming out. Repeat this action several times, making sure to cover all areas of the mesh.

After brushing thoroughly, hold the filter cage over a trash can. Gently tap it to dislodge any remaining loose debris. You can also use your fingers to pick off any stubborn clumps of hair. Follow up with a dry microfiber cloth to wipe the entire surface of the cage, both inside and out. This removes any fine dust that the brush might have missed. Make sure the cage is completely dry before reattaching it. This simple routine for the outer cage can significantly improve airflow. It also prepares your device for a deeper clean if needed. ## Deep Cleaning the Inner Filter: Beyond the Surface

While the outer filter cage catches most of the visible debris, the inner filter is just as important. This fine mesh is crucial for protecting the motor from smaller particles. It needs careful attention to ensure your Airwrap performs its best. This part of the cleaning process requires a gentle touch.

After you have removed the outer filter cage, you will see a fine mesh filter around the base of the Airwrap handle. This is the inner filter. Unlike the outer cage, you cannot remove this part. You will clean it while it is still attached to the device. Take your Dyson filter cleaning brush again. Carefully place the bristles over the inner mesh. Gently rotate the brush back and forth, moving it around the entire surface of the mesh. The goal here is to dislodge any fine dust or lint that has accumulated on this delicate screen.

Avoid pressing too hard or using any sharp objects. The inner mesh is delicate and crucial for device function. After brushing, use a clean, dry, soft cloth to gently wipe the inner mesh. Pay close attention to any areas where dust might still cling. Ensure no moisture touches this part, as it is close to the electrical components. The goal is to remove all visible debris without causing any damage. This deeper clean maintains optimal airflow directly to the motor. It also extends the life of your Dyson Airwrap. This attention to detail can make a big difference in performance.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Your Airwrap Filter

Cleaning your Dyson Airwrap filter is vital, but doing it incorrectly can cause more harm than good. I have learned from experience that certain mistakes are common. Avoiding them ensures your Airwrap remains in top condition. Let's look at what not to do.

First, never use water or any liquid cleaners directly on the inner filter or inside the Airwrap handle. The inner filter is close to sensitive electrical components. Introducing moisture can lead to short circuits, damage, or even a safety hazard. Always stick to dry cleaning for the internal parts. A soft, dry cloth is your best friend here.

Second, avoid using harsh brushes, sharp objects, or compressed air. While you want to remove debris, aggressive tools can damage the delicate filter mesh. This includes using toothbrushes with overly stiff bristles or metal tools. Compressed air can force debris further into the motor, creating worse blockages. Stick to the Dyson-provided brush or a similar soft-bristled tool.

Third, do not ignore regular cleaning. Letting the filter become heavily clogged makes cleaning harder and less effective. It also stresses the motor, leading to performance issues and a shorter device lifespan. Make filter cleaning a regular part of your Airwrap maintenance routine. Knowing [how often to clean the Dyson Airwrap filter](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-often-to-clean-the-dyson-airwrap-filter) is crucial here.

Lastly, do not force the filter cage back on if it resists. If the cage does not slide and lock easily, check for misalignment or trapped debris. Forcing it can damage the locking mechanism. Gently realign it and try again. Following these tips helps you maintain your Airwrap safely and effectively. ## How Often Should You Clean Your Dyson Airwrap Filter?

Establishing a regular cleaning schedule for your Dyson Airwrap filter is just as important as the cleaning itself. The frequency depends on several factors, including how often you use your device and your hair type. I have found a good routine makes all the difference in maintaining peak performance.

If you use your Dyson Airwrap daily or almost daily, I recommend cleaning the filter at least once a week. Frequent use means more hair and product residue passing through the filter. For those with long hair, thick hair, or who use many styling products, weekly cleaning is even more critical. These factors contribute to faster filter clogging.

For users who style their hair a few times a week, cleaning every two to three weeks should suffice. It is still important not to let too much time pass between cleanings. Even with less frequent use, dust and lint can accumulate. If you notice any signs of reduced airflow or your Airwrap is not performing as well, clean the filter immediately, regardless of your schedule. I also suggest a more thorough deep clean every one to two months, where you pay extra attention to ensuring the inner mesh is spotless. This extended cleaning ensures no fine particles are missed. Regular, consistent maintenance prevents major clogs. It keeps your Dyson Airwrap working efficiently and extends its overall lifespan. Consistency is key for optimal performance.

## FAQ Section

### Can I use water to clean the Dyson Airwrap filter?
No, you should not use water directly on the inner filter or inside the Airwrap. The internal components are electrical and sensitive to moisture. Only use a soft, dry cloth and the provided cleaning brush for the inner filter. You can gently wipe the *outer* filter cage with a slightly damp cloth if needed, but ensure it is completely dry before reattaching it.

### What happens if I don't clean my Dyson Airwrap filter?
If you neglect cleaning your Dyson Airwrap filter, debris will accumulate. This restricts airflow significantly. Your Airwrap will lose power, style less effectively, and may overheat or shut down during use. Over time, a persistently clogged filter can put excessive strain on the motor, leading to permanent damage and reducing the lifespan of your device.

### How do I know if my Dyson Airwrap filter is dirty?
You can tell your Dyson Airwrap filter is dirty by visual inspection. You will see visible lint, hair, or dust buildup on the mesh of the filter cage. Additionally, signs of reduced performance like weaker airflow, less effective styling, or the device shutting off unexpectedly are strong indicators that your filter needs cleaning.

### Can I clean the Dyson Airwrap filter without the brush?
Yes, you can clean the Dyson Airwrap filter without the specific Dyson brush if you have misplaced it. You can use a clean, dry, soft-bristled toothbrush or a similar small brush. Gently brush the outer and inner mesh to dislodge debris. A soft, dry microfiber cloth can also help wipe away surface dust and lint. For more details check this article on [how to clean the Dyson Airwrap filter without a brush](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-dyson-airwrap-filter-without-a-brush).

### How often should I clean my Dyson Airwrap filter?
The frequency of cleaning depends on your usage. For daily users, cleaning the filter weekly is recommended. If you use your Airwrap a few times a week, aim for every two to three weeks. If you have long hair, thick hair, or use many styling products, more frequent cleaning is advisable. Always clean it immediately if you notice reduced performance.
