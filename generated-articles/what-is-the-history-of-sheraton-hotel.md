### The Humble Beginnings: Founding and Early Expansion

Every big story starts somewhere small. The history of Sheraton Hotel begins in 1937. Ernest Henderson and Daniel Kearney were the founders. They bought the Stonehaven Hotel in Springfield, Massachusetts. This was their first step into the hotel world. They quickly found success.

Their next purchase was a hotel in Boston. It had a unique feature. A large, flashing sign on the roof was installed. It spelled out "Sheraton." This sign became a recognizable landmark. People started calling their hotels "Sheraton" because of this sign. This is how the name stuck.

Henderson and Kearney had a clear strategy. They bought existing properties. Many of these hotels were older. They focused on improving these hotels. They added modern amenities. They made sure guests felt comfortable. This approach allowed them to expand quickly without building from scratch.

By 1939, Sheraton owned three hotels. The company was growing. The approach was simple: acquire, improve, and provide consistent service. This foundation proved strong for future growth.

### Post-War Boom and Diversification: Growth and Innovation

The period after World War II brought big changes. The economy boomed. More people began to travel. This was a perfect time for Sheraton to grow even more. The company expanded rapidly across the United States. It became the first hotel chain listed on the New York Stock Exchange in 1947. This was a major milestone.

Sheraton did not just grow its number of hotels. It also looked at new ways to serve guests. The company was innovative. It was among the first to offer reservation systems. This made booking rooms much easier for travelers. It set a new standard for hotel operations.

In the 1950s, Sheraton began international expansion. Its first international hotel opened in Toronto, Canada, in 1949. This marked a new chapter. Sheraton properties soon appeared in various global destinations. The brand became known beyond U.S. borders. It offered consistent quality across different countries.

Sheraton also diversified its business. It started managing other properties. It developed its own brands. This era showed Sheraton's ambition. It wanted to be more than just a chain of hotels. It aimed to be a leader in the broader hospitality industry.

### ITT Sheraton: A Corporate Powerhouse Era

The 1960s brought a significant change for Sheraton. In 1968, International Telephone and Telegraph (ITT) acquired Sheraton. This marked the beginning of the ITT Sheraton era. ITT was a large conglomerate. This acquisition gave Sheraton access to more capital. It also provided global reach.

Under ITT, Sheraton continued to expand worldwide. The brand became even more prominent. ITT invested heavily in modernizing hotels. They also built new properties in key cities. This period saw Sheraton truly solidify its global presence.

However, being part of a large conglomerate also had challenges. Sheraton sometimes lost some of its independent identity. Decisions were made at a higher corporate level. Still, ITT's financial backing was crucial. It allowed Sheraton to compete with emerging global hotel giants.

This era was important for Sheraton's scale. It transformed the company into a truly international player. The focus remained on full-service hotels. These hotels provided a wide range of amenities. They catered to business travelers and tourists alike. This period prepared Sheraton for future industry shifts.

### Starwood Hotels & Resorts: A New Era for Sheraton

The late 1990s brought another big change. In 1998, Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide acquired ITT Corporation. This acquisition included Sheraton Hotels. This marked a new and exciting era for the brand. Starwood was a dynamic company. It had a strong focus on hotels and lifestyle brands.

Starwood invested heavily in revitalizing Sheraton. Many Sheraton properties needed updates. Starwood launched major renovation programs. The goal was to modernize the hotels. They wanted to improve the guest experience. This included upgrading rooms, lobbies, and dining areas.

One significant initiative was the "Sweet Sleeper Bed." This program introduced custom-designed beds across all Sheraton hotels. It became a signature amenity. Guests loved the comfort. It positioned Sheraton as a strong, reliable choice in the full-service segment. This period was crucial for bringing Sheraton into the 21st century.

### The Marriott Acquisition: Joining a Global Powerhouse

A truly monumental event happened in 2016. Marriott International acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide. This created the world's largest hotel company. Sheraton became part of this massive portfolio. It joined a family of over 30 distinct hotel brands.

This acquisition gave Sheraton unparalleled reach. It gained access to Marriott's vast loyalty program, Marriott Bonvoy. This program has millions of members. It meant more potential guests for Sheraton hotels. It also provided stronger marketing power.

Being part of Marriott brought new strategies. Marriott began a new revitalization plan for Sheraton. This plan focused on modernizing properties further. It aimed to improve communal spaces. The goal was to make lobbies more inviting. They wanted to create spaces for co-working and socializing.

Marriott's deep resources support Sheraton's growth. The brand benefits from Marriott's global sales network. It also gains from their technological advancements. This acquisition ensures Sheraton remains a major player. It can adapt to changing guest needs. It can compete effectively in the global market.

### FAQ Section

**Q1: Who founded Sheraton Hotels?**
Sheraton Hotels was founded by two enterprising individuals. Ernest Henderson and Daniel Kearney were the founders. They began their journey in the hotel business by purchasing their first hotel property in Massachusetts. Their vision quickly led to the expansion of their hotel portfolio. This laid the groundwork for the global brand we know today.

**Q2: When was Sheraton Hotel founded?**
Sheraton Hotel was founded in 1937. This year marks the acquisition of their first hotel property. It was located in Springfield, Massachusetts. From this single establishment, the brand began its remarkable expansion. It grew steadily over the decades. It eventually became one of the most recognizable names in the hospitality industry worldwide.

**Q3: What happened to Sheraton Hotels?**
Sheraton Hotels underwent several ownership changes. First, ITT acquired it in 1968. Then, Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide purchased ITT in 1998. Most recently, Marriott International acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts in 2016. Sheraton is now a major brand within the Marriott International portfolio. It continues to operate and expand globally.

**Q4: Is Sheraton considered a luxury brand?**
Sheraton is generally considered a premium or upper-upscale brand within the hospitality industry. While it offers high-quality services and amenities, it sits below true luxury brands like The Ritz-Carlton or St. Regis in the Marriott portfolio. However, its "Sheraton Grand" properties offer a more elevated and luxurious experience, often approaching luxury standards.

**Q5: What are some iconic Sheraton properties?**
Sheraton has several iconic properties around the world. These include the Sheraton Waikiki, known for its stunning oceanfront location in Hawaii. The Sheraton Grand Chicago is another notable example. It offers impressive city views and convenient access to downtown attractions. These properties often showcase the brand's commitment to prime locations and comprehensive services.

**Q6: How does Sheraton fit into Marriott's portfolio?**
Sheraton is a core brand within Marriott International's "Premium" tier. This tier includes full-service brands that offer a broad range of amenities and services. Sheraton complements other Marriott brands by providing a familiar and globally recognized option for business and leisure travelers. It helps Marriott maintain its vast global footprint and diverse brand offerings.
