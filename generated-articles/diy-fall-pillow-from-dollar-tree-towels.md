## Simple Steps to Prepare Your Dollar Tree Towels

Proper preparation of your Dollar Tree towels is a key first step. This ensures your finished **DIY Fall pillow** looks professional and lasts longer. Taking a few minutes to prepare the fabric makes a big difference. I always follow these steps for any fabric project. It helps avoid issues later on.

First, wash and dry your Dollar Tree towels. This step is important for a few reasons. Washing removes any sizing or chemicals from the factory. It also pre-shrinks the fabric. Towels can sometimes shrink a little after the first wash. Pre-shrinking prevents your pillow from changing shape after you make it. Use your usual laundry detergent and tumble dry them. Make sure they are completely dry before you move on.

Once dry, iron your towels. Even if they are just slightly wrinkled, ironing creates a smooth surface. This makes cutting and sewing much easier. A flat surface helps you achieve straight lines. It ensures your pillow pieces fit together perfectly. I iron on a medium heat setting, making sure there are no creases.

Now, measure and cut your towels. Decide on the size and shape of your pillow. A common size for a throw pillow is 16x16 inches. For this, you would cut two squares that are slightly larger, allowing for seam allowances. For instance, if you want a 14x14 inch finished pillow, cut two 15x15 inch squares. This gives you a half-inch seam allowance on each side. Place your towels face down and use a ruler and fabric marker to draw your squares. Cut slowly and carefully for straight edges. This precise cutting step is vital for a well-formed pillow.

## Assembling Your DIY Fall Pillow: Sewing Machine Method

Creating your **DIY Fall pillow from Dollar Tree towels** with a sewing machine is efficient. It produces a strong, durable seam. This method is great even for beginner sewers. I find that a sewing machine gives a neat, professional finish. It will make your homemade pillow look store-bought.

First, take your two cut fabric squares. Place them right sides together. This means the side of the towel you want to see on your finished pillow should be facing inward. Line up all the edges carefully. Use sewing pins to secure the edges. Pin every few inches along three of the four sides. Leave one side completely open for now. This open side will be where you insert the pillow stuffing.

Next, sew along the pinned edges. Use a straight stitch on your sewing machine. A half-inch seam allowance is a good standard. Start at one corner, backstitch a few stitches, and then sew all the way down. Stop at the next corner, lift your presser foot, pivot the fabric, and continue sewing the next side. Repeat for the third side. Remember to backstitch at the end of your seam. This locks the stitches in place. It prevents the seams from unraveling.

After sewing, trim the corners of your pillowcase. Cut diagonally across each corner, close to the seam, but be careful not to snip the stitches. This step reduces bulk in the corners. It creates sharper, more defined pillow corners when you turn it right side out. Now, turn your pillowcase right side out. Use a blunt object, like a chopstick or turning tool, to gently push out the corners. This helps them look crisp and square. You now have a pillow cover ready for stuffing. This method is similar to making a [DIY cozy plaid fall pillow from a Dollar Store scarf](https://cosyhomecreation.com/diy-cozy-plaid-fall-pillow-from-a-dollar-store-scarf), where precision in sewing creates a great result.

## No-Sew Option: Creating Your Pillow with Fabric Glue or Iron-On Hem Tape

Not everyone enjoys sewing, and that is perfectly fine. You can still create a beautiful **DIY Fall pillow from Dollar Tree towels** without a needle and thread. The no-sew method is quick, easy, and yields great results. I often use this technique for fast decor projects. It is perfect for beginners or anyone short on time.

One popular no-sew option is using fabric glue. After cutting your towel pieces, apply a thin line of fabric glue along three of the edges on the wrong side of one fabric piece. Carefully press the second fabric piece on top. Align the edges perfectly. Ensure you leave one side completely open for stuffing. Press firmly along the glued edges. You can use clips or heavy books to hold the edges together while the glue dries. Read the glue instructions for drying times. Some glues dry quickly, others need several hours. Make sure the glue is completely dry before moving on. This ensures a strong bond.

Another fantastic no-sew option involves iron-on hem tape. This tape creates a very strong and durable bond. Lay one towel piece right side down. Place a strip of hem tape along three of its edges, again leaving one side open. Lay the second towel piece right side down on top. Ensure the edges line up perfectly. Place a pressing cloth over the hem tape areas. Press down with a hot iron, following the hem tape instructions. The heat melts the adhesive, bonding the fabrics together. Move the iron slowly and evenly over the tape. This creates a secure seam.

Once your chosen adhesive is fully cured or cooled, turn your pillow cover right side out. Use your fingers or a blunt tool to push out the corners. You now have a sturdy, no-sew pillow cover. It is ready for you to add the stuffing. This method is great for a quick and simple fall accent. It gives you a lot of flexibility without the need for sewing skills.

## Stuffing Your Pillow and Final Touches

After you have decorated your **DIY Fall pillow from Dollar Tree towels**, the next step is to stuff it. Proper stuffing makes your pillow look full and inviting. This is where your pillow truly comes to life. I focus on making it plush and comfortable.

Carefully insert your chosen stuffing material into the pillow cover. Fiberfill works wonderfully. It is soft, fluffy, and holds its shape well. Start by pushing small handfuls of fiberfill into the corners first. This ensures they are fully filled and square. Then, continue filling the rest of the pillow, adding fiberfill evenly. Avoid overstuffing, which can make the pillow too hard. Also, avoid understuffing, which makes it look flat. You want a firm yet squishy feel. I usually gently pat and shape the pillow as I go. This helps distribute the stuffing evenly.

Once your pillow is stuffed to your liking, it is time to close the opening. If you sewed your pillow, you will hand-stitch the opening closed. Use a needle and thread in a matching color. A ladder stitch (or invisible stitch) works best. This stitch hides the thread, making the seam almost invisible. If you used fabric glue or iron-on hem tape, apply more adhesive to the opening. Press it firmly together. Allow it to dry completely. Ensure the closure is secure. You do not want stuffing popping out later.

Finally, give your pillow a good fluff and adjust its shape. You might need to gently massage the stuffing to ensure it is perfectly distributed. Stand back and admire your handiwork. Your new **DIY Fall pillow** is ready to adorn your home. This final step is rewarding. You created a cozy piece of decor.

## Care and Maintenance for Your Homemade Dollar Tree Pillow

Taking care of your **DIY Fall pillow from Dollar Tree towels** helps it stay beautiful for many seasons. Proper maintenance ensures your hard work lasts. I always make sure to follow care instructions for handmade items. This extends their life and keeps them looking fresh.

For light cleaning, a quick spot clean is often enough. If you get a small spill or mark, use a damp cloth with mild soap. Gently dab the stained area. Avoid rubbing vigorously, as this can spread the stain or damage the fabric. Blot the area dry with a clean towel. Let the pillow air dry completely. This method is safe for both sewn and no-sew pillows. It addresses minor mishaps quickly.

If your pillow needs a more thorough cleaning, consider its construction. For sewn pillows, you might be able to hand wash or machine wash on a delicate cycle. Use cold water and a mild detergent. If the fabric is delicate or if there are embellishments, hand washing is safer. Gently squeeze out excess water. Reshape the pillow and lay it flat to air dry. Avoid putting it in a dryer, as high heat can damage the fabric or stuffing. It can also cause shrinkage.

For no-sew pillows made with fabric glue or hem tape, washing should be done with caution. Spot cleaning is generally recommended. If a full wash is necessary, hand washing is the safest option. Be gentle with the glued seams. Excessive soaking or agitation might weaken the adhesive. Always allow your pillow to air dry fully. Ensuring the pillow is completely dry prevents mildew and keeps the stuffing fresh. By following these simple care tips, your Dollar Tree towel pillow will remain a cherished part of your fall decor for years to come.

## FAQ Section

### Can I use any type of Dollar Tree towel for a DIY Fall pillow?
Yes, you can use various types of Dollar Tree towels. Hand towels or dish towels work best due to their size. Look for different textures like terry cloth or waffle weave. Consider the towel's thickness and softness. Thicker towels might be harder to sew, but they offer more durability.

### How do I stuff my DIY Fall pillow effectively?
Stuff your pillow by pushing small handfuls of fiberfill into the corners first. This ensures a full and square shape. Continue adding fiberfill evenly throughout the pillow. Avoid overstuffing, which makes the pillow too firm. Ensure the pillow has a plump and comfortable feel.

### Is a no-sew option for the pillow really durable?
A no-sew option using quality fabric glue or iron-on hem tape can be surprisingly durable. When applied correctly, these adhesives create strong bonds. Follow product instructions carefully for the best results. While not as robust as machine sewing, it holds up well for decorative use.

### How much does it cost to make one of these pillows?
Making a DIY Fall pillow from Dollar Tree towels is very budget-friendly. Each towel costs $1.25. You will need two towels. Stuffing can cost a few dollars, or you can use repurposed materials. The total cost is often under $10, making it an affordable decor project.

### Can I wash my Dollar Tree towel pillow?
Washing your Dollar Tree towel pillow depends on its construction. For sewn pillows, hand washing or a delicate machine cycle with cold water is usually safe. For no-sew pillows, spot cleaning is recommended. Hand washing with care is an option if needed. Always air dry your pillow to maintain its shape.

### How can I make my DIY Fall pillow unique?
You can make your DIY Fall pillow unique by adding various embellishments. Use stencils and fabric paint for custom designs like leaves or fall phrases. Attach felt appliques, rustic buttons, or decorative ribbons. You can also mix and match different colored or textured Dollar Tree towels.
