## The Westminster Best in Show Judging Process

The Westminster Dog Show culminates in the Best in Show competition. This final round features the seven Group winners. These dogs represent each of the seven AKC groups. The judge evaluates each dog based on several criteria. First, they check how well the dog fits its breed standard. This means looking at the dog's structure, coat, and overall appearance. The dog must present itself well to the judge. Its handler must also work seamlessly with the dog. The dog's temperament should be confident and alert.

The judge does not compare one breed to another directly. Instead, they look for the best example of each breed. Then, they select the dog that is the best among those seven excellent examples.
