## Understanding the "Cost" Per Service Dog: Where Funds Go

When people ask, "How much does K9 For Warriors make?", they are often trying to understand the investment required. It is better to think of this as "How much does it cost K9 For Warriors to make a service dog and support a veteran?" The financial commitment per service dog team is substantial. Estimates often place the cost between $25,000 to $50,000 or even more, depending on the specific program elements included. This figure covers a wide array of expenses necessary to transform a shelter dog into a highly trained service animal and support a veteran through the entire process.

First, there is the cost of acquiring the dogs. K9 For Warriors primarily rescues dogs from shelters, which saves lives and reduces acquisition costs compared to purchasing from breeders. However, rescue still involves transport, initial veterinary checks, and providing immediate care. Once acquired, the dogs enter a rigorous training program. This involves professional dog trainers, specialized equipment, and dedicated training facilities. The training is intensive, focusing on specific tasks like mitigating PTSD symptoms, alerting to anxiety attacks, or providing stability. It is not just about teaching basic commands; it is about cultivating a deep, intuitive partnership.

Veterinary care is an ongoing and significant expense. Each dog receives comprehensive health checks, vaccinations, spaying/neutering, microchipping, and any necessary medical treatments throughout their training and placement. Ensuring the health and well-being of these animals is paramount. Beyond the dog's needs, the program also covers the veteran's expenses during their two- to three-week residential training program at the K9 For Warriors campus. This includes housing, meals, and specialized therapy sessions. The organization also provides follow-up support to ensure the continued success of each veteran-dog team.

Finally, there are the operational overheads that allow the entire mission to run. This includes salaries for skilled staff (trainers, veteran support specialists, administrative personnel), facility maintenance, utilities, insurance, and outreach programs.
