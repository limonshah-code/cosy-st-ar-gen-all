## FAQ Section

### Are French Bulldogs aggressive?
No, French Bulldogs are not typically aggressive. They are known for their gentle and loving temper. They prefer companionship and generally get along well with people and other pets. Aggression is rare and often a result of poor socialization, fear, or pain.

### Do French Bulldogs bark a lot?
French Bulldogs are not excessive barkers. They may bark to alert you to strangers, or when excited. However, they are not a noisy breed. Excessive barking could signal separation anxiety or boredom, indicating a need for more attention or training.

### How much exercise does a French Bulldog need?
French Bulldogs have low to moderate exercise needs. They require about 20-30 minutes of daily activity. This can include short walks and indoor playtime. Due to their brachycephalic nature, avoid strenuous exercise or hot weather to prevent overheating.

### Are French Bulldogs good with kids?
Yes, French Bulldogs are generally excellent with children. Their patient and gentle temper makes them good family pets. Their sturdy build can handle playful interactions. Always supervise interactions between dogs and young children to ensure safety for both.

### Can French Bulldogs be left alone?
French Bulldogs prefer companionship and do not do well when left alone for extended periods. They are prone to separation anxiety. If you must leave them, keep it brief. Crate training and puzzle toys can help manage their anxiety.

### Is a French Bulldog a good first dog?
Yes, a French Bulldog can be a good first dog. Their adaptable nature, relatively low exercise needs, and affectionate temper make them suitable. However, new owners must be aware of their potential stubbornness and specific health concerns.
