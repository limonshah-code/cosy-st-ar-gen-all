## Deep Clean Your Bestop Twill Soft Top

Having a Bestop twill soft top on your vehicle offers a unique blend of style and open-air freedom. This premium fabric, known for its durability and luxurious feel, enhances your driving experience. I understand the desire to maintain your vehicle's condition, especially when it features a high-quality accessory like a Bestop twill soft top. A clean top not only looks good but also resists wear and tear more effectively. This guide will show you how to clean Bestop twill soft top thoroughly. ### Understanding Your Bestop Twill Soft Top Material

Before you start cleaning, it helps to know what your Bestop twill soft top is made of. Bestop twill fabric is a specific type of material. It features a woven construction that gives it a distinct look and feel. This fabric is durable and resists stretching and shrinking.

The twill weave also provides good water resistance. However, it is still a fabric and can absorb dirt and stains. Understanding these properties helps you choose the right cleaning products and methods. Harsh chemicals can damage the fabric fibers and coatings.

Using gentle, pH-balanced cleaners is important. These cleaners remove grime without stripping essential protections. Regular care keeps the twill looking new and performing well. I always advise people to treat their soft tops with care.

Ignoring proper cleaning can lead to dullness and premature wear. Small particles of dirt can act like sandpaper on the fabric. This accelerates the aging process of the material.

### Essential Tools and Products for Soft Top Cleaning

Gathering the right tools and products makes the cleaning process easier and more effective. You do not need a lot of items, but specialized products yield the best results. Using the wrong products can harm your Bestop twill soft top. This can shorten its life and diminish its appearance.

Here is a list of what you will need for a successful cleaning session. Each item plays an important role in protecting your fabric. I have found these items to be indispensable for proper soft top care. Make sure you have everything ready before you start.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Your Bestop Twill Soft Top

Cleaning your Bestop twill soft top is a straightforward process. Following these steps ensures a thorough clean without causing damage. I recommend setting aside a few hours for this task. This allows ample time for cleaning and drying.

Always work in a shaded area. Direct sunlight can dry the cleaner too quickly. This leaves streaks or spots on the fabric. Start early in the morning or late in the afternoon.

#### Step 1: Prepare the Soft Top

Begin by removing any loose dirt or debris from the surface of your top. You can use a soft-bristle brush or a vacuum cleaner with a brush attachment. Gently brush or vacuum the entire top. Pay attention to seams and crevices where dirt often collects.

This initial step prevents larger particles from scratching the fabric. It also makes the washing process more efficient. Ensure all windows and doors are fully closed. This prevents water from entering the vehicle.

#### Step 2: Rinse Thoroughly

Using your garden hose with a low-pressure nozzle, thoroughly rinse the entire soft top. Start from the front and work your way to the back. This removes surface dirt, dust, and any remaining loose debris. Make sure to wet the fabric completely.

A good initial rinse prepares the fabric for the cleaner. It also prevents the cleaner from drying too fast. Avoid using high-pressure sprayers. They can force water into seams or damage the fabric weave.

#### Step 3: Apply Soft Top Cleaner

Dilute your chosen soft top cleaner according to the manufacturer's instructions. Pour the solution into one of your buckets. Dip your soft-bristle brush into the solution.

Work on a small section of the top at a time, perhaps a 2x2 foot area. Gently scrub the wet fabric in circular motions. Focus on areas with visible stains or heavy dirt buildup. The cleaner should create a light foam.

Do not let the cleaner dry on the fabric. Keep the area wet with solution as you scrub. For tougher spots, you might need to apply a bit more cleaner. Repeat this process until you have scrubbed the entire top.

#### Step 4: Rinse Again

After scrubbing a section, immediately rinse it thoroughly with clean water from your hose. This removes the cleaner and all the lifted dirt. Ensure no soap residue remains on the fabric. Soap residue can attract new dirt quickly.

Move to the next section and repeat the scrubbing and rinsing steps. Continue this process until you have cleaned and rinsed the entire Bestop twill soft top. Pay close attention to all seams and edges.

#### Step 5: Final Rinse and Inspection

Once you have cleaned all sections, perform a final, comprehensive rinse of the entire top. Make sure no suds or cleaner residue is left anywhere. Visually inspect the top for any missed spots or remaining stains. If you find any, repeat the cleaning process on those specific areas.

A clean, residue-free top is important for the next step. It ensures that the protectant can adhere properly.

### Drying Your Bestop Twill Soft Top

Proper drying is as important as the cleaning itself. Incorrect drying can lead to water spots or mildew. Always allow your Bestop twill soft top to air dry completely. This is the best method to ensure all moisture is gone.

#### Air Drying Method

After the final rinse, gently shake off excess water from the top if possible. Then, leave your vehicle in a shaded area. Allow the top to air dry naturally. This can take several hours depending on weather conditions.

Avoid using heat guns or direct sunlight to speed up drying. These can cause the fabric to shrink or fade. Do not put the top down until it is completely dry. Closing a wet top traps moisture. This can lead to mildew growth and an unpleasant smell inside your vehicle.

You can use clean microfiber towels to blot away any standing water. This can help speed up the drying process slightly. However, air drying is still essential. This ensures deep fabric dryness.

### Protecting and Conditioning Your Twill Soft Top

Cleaning your Bestop twill soft top is only half the battle. Protecting it after cleaning is crucial for its longevity and appearance. A good fabric protectant creates a barrier. This barrier repels water, resists stains, and blocks harmful UV rays.

Think of it as sunscreen for your soft top. It keeps the fabric looking newer for longer. This also makes future cleanings easier. I always include this step in my cleaning routine. #### Applying Fabric Protectant

Once your Bestop twill soft top is completely dry, you can apply the protectant. Make sure the top is cool to the touch. Work in a shaded, well-ventilated area.

1.  **Read Instructions:** Always read the specific instructions on your chosen fabric protectant. Different products may have different application methods.
2.  **Prepare for Application:** Mask off any surrounding paint, glass, or plastic trim with painter's tape or plastic sheeting. This prevents overspray from getting on unwanted surfaces. Some protectants can stain or leave residue on these areas.
3.  **Apply Evenly:** Spray the protectant evenly over the entire fabric surface of the soft top. Hold the can or bottle at the recommended distance. Work in overlapping passes to ensure full coverage. Do not saturate the fabric. A light, even coat is better.
4.  **Allow to Cure:** Let the protectant dry and cure according to the product's instructions. This usually takes several hours. Avoid rain or dew during the curing process. Some products recommend a second coat after the first has dried.
5.  **Remove Masking:** Once fully cured, carefully remove any masking tape or plastic. You will notice water beading up on the fabric. This indicates the protectant is working.

Applying a protectant regularly extends the life of your top. It also maintains its factory-fresh appearance. This is a small investment of time for a big return in protection.

### Dealing with Stubborn Stains and Mildew

Even with regular cleaning, stubborn stains or mildew can appear on your Bestop twill soft top. These issues require a more focused approach. Acting quickly is important when you notice a new stain.

#### Removing Stubborn Stains

For specific stains like bird droppings, tree sap, or grease, a targeted cleaner might be necessary. Some soft top cleaner brands offer spot removers. These are designed for tougher stains.

1.  **Test First:** Always test any stain remover on an inconspicuous area of the fabric. This ensures it does not cause discoloration or damage.
2.  **Apply Spot Cleaner:** Apply the spot cleaner directly to the stain. Let it dwell for the recommended time.
3.  **Gently Scrub:** Use a soft brush or microfiber cloth to gently scrub the stain. Work from the outside of the stain inwards. This prevents spreading the stain.
4.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse the area completely with water. Make sure all cleaner residue is gone.
5.  **Repeat if Needed:** You might need to repeat the process for very stubborn stains. Be patient and gentle.

#### Tackling Mildew

Mildew is a common problem in humid climates or when a top stays wet for too long. It appears as black spots or a fuzzy growth. Special mildew removers for fabric are available. Do not use bleach, as it can damage the fabric and stitching.

1.  **Brush Off Loose Mildew:** Use a dry, soft brush to gently brush off any loose mildew spores. Do this in a well-ventilated area.
2.  **Apply Mildew Remover:** Spray or apply a fabric-safe mildew remover to the affected areas. Follow the product's instructions carefully.
3.  **Light Scrubbing:** Gently scrub with a soft brush. The mildew should start to lift away.
4.  **Rinse and Dry:** Rinse the area completely. Allow the top to air dry in the sun, if possible, to help kill remaining spores.
5.  **Prevent Recurrence:** After treatment, ensure your top dries fully after rain or washing. Consider a fabric protectant with mildew inhibitors.

Addressing these issues promptly prevents them from spreading. It also preserves the fabric's integrity.

### Ongoing Maintenance and Care Tips

Keeping your Bestop twill soft top in top condition requires more than just occasional deep cleaning. Regular, simple maintenance practices will significantly extend its life. These small efforts prevent major cleaning challenges later.

#### Regular Brushing and Wiping

Make it a habit to lightly brush or wipe your soft top regularly. Use a soft brush or a damp microfiber cloth. This removes dust, pollen, and light debris before it can bond to the fabric. Doing this once a week can make a big difference. It prevents dirt buildup.

#### Spot Cleaning Small Spills

If you spill something on your soft top, clean it immediately. Use a damp cloth and a mild soap solution. Blot the spill, do not rub. Quick action prevents stains from setting into the twill fabric.

#### Parking Practices

Where you park your vehicle affects your soft top's condition. Parking under trees can lead to sap and bird droppings. Parking in direct sunlight for long periods exposes the top to harmful UV rays. Whenever possible, park in a garage or shaded area. If outdoor parking is necessary, consider a car cover. This provides an extra layer of protection.

#### Avoid Harsh Chemicals

As mentioned before, never use harsh detergents, abrasive cleaners, or household chemicals. These products can strip the protective coatings. They can also degrade the fabric. Stick to cleaners specifically made for soft tops.

#### Window Care

Your Bestop soft top likely has clear vinyl windows. These require separate care. Clean them with a dedicated vinyl window cleaner and a soft microfiber cloth. Always wipe them gently in one direction. This prevents scratches. Never use abrasive cloths or paper towels on vinyl windows.

#### Frequency of Cleaning

How often you need to deep clean depends on your environment and usage. For most people, a deep clean every 3-6 months is sufficient. If you drive often in dusty areas or harsh weather, you might need to clean it more often. Regularly check your top. Clean it when you notice dirt buildup or a dull appearance. ### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Your Soft Top

Cleaning your Bestop twill soft top might seem simple. However, some common mistakes can actually cause damage. Knowing what to avoid is as important as knowing what to do. I have seen these errors cause unnecessary wear and tear.

#### Using a High-Pressure Washer

High-pressure washers are too aggressive for soft top fabrics. They can force water under seams, lifting the fabric or damaging the stitching. The powerful stream can also abrade the twill weave. Always use a garden hose with a gentle flow.

#### Cleaning in Direct Sunlight

As mentioned, direct sunlight can dry cleaners too quickly. This leaves streaks and makes rinsing difficult. The heat can also make the fabric more sensitive to scrubbing. Always work in a shaded area.

#### Using Harsh Brushes or Abrasive Cloths

Stiff brushes or abrasive sponges can scratch and damage the delicate twill fibers. They can also wear down the protective coating on the fabric. Stick to soft-bristle brushes and microfiber towels.

#### Forgetting to Rinse Thoroughly

Leaving cleaner residue on your top can be detrimental. It attracts dirt and grime more quickly. It can also leave unsightly spots or dull the fabric. Rinse every section completely. Ensure all soap is gone.

#### Not Allowing the Top to Dry Completely

Putting your soft top down while it is still damp invites mildew and mold. This creates unpleasant odors and damages the fabric over time. Always allow the top to air dry fully before folding or storing it.

#### Neglecting Protection

Skipping the protectant step after cleaning leaves your top vulnerable. UV rays, water, and environmental pollutants will degrade the fabric faster. A good protectant is an essential part of the cleaning process.

Avoiding these common pitfalls helps you maintain your Bestop twill soft top. It ensures its beauty and functionality for years to come. Your careful approach saves you time and money.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How often should I clean my Bestop twill soft top?**
You should deep clean your Bestop twill soft top every 3 to 6 months. This depends on your driving conditions and local environment. If you drive in dusty areas or face harsh weather, cleaning it more often is a good idea. Regular light cleaning, like brushing off debris, should be done weekly. This prevents dirt buildup.

**Q2: Can I use regular car wash soap on my soft top?**
No, you should avoid using regular car wash soap on your Bestop twill soft top. These soaps can contain waxes, detergents, or other chemicals not suitable for fabric. They can strip the fabric's protective coatings. Always use a cleaner specifically formulated for fabric soft tops.

**Q3: What if I get bird droppings on my soft top?**
Clean bird droppings immediately. They are acidic and can quickly stain and damage the fabric. Gently remove the solid material. Then, use a damp cloth with a mild soft top cleaner to blot the area. Rinse thoroughly and let it air dry. Quick action prevents permanent stains.

**Q4: How do I protect my soft top from UV damage?**
Protect your soft top from UV damage by regularly applying a specialized fabric protectant after cleaning. These products create a barrier that blocks harmful UV rays. Parking in a garage or shaded area also greatly reduces UV exposure. A car cover can provide additional protection.

**Q5: Can I put my Bestop twill soft top through an automatic car wash?**
It is strongly recommended not to put your Bestop twill soft top through an automatic car wash. The harsh brushes, high-pressure water jets, and strong detergents used in these washes can severely damage the fabric, stitching, and clear vinyl windows. Always hand wash your soft top.

**Q6: My soft top has a cloudy film on the windows. How do I clean it?**
The cloudy film is on your vinyl windows, not the fabric. Use a dedicated vinyl window cleaner. Apply it with a soft microfiber cloth. Wipe gently in one direction to avoid scratching. Do not use ammonia-based cleaners or abrasive cloths, as these will damage the vinyl.
