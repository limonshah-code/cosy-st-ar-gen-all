## How To Clean Jordan 11: Restore Your Prized Sneakers

Do you own a pair of iconic Jordan 11s? These sneakers are true collector's items. Their unique design features patent leather, mesh, and often an icy sole. Keeping them clean is important for their value and appearance. I understand the pride you feel when wearing a fresh pair.

However, dirt and grime can quickly diminish their shine. Learning how to clean Jordan 11s properly is key. This guide will walk you through every step. We will cover necessary tools, techniques for different materials, and advanced stain removal. Get ready to make your Jordan 11s look new again.

### Takeaway: Keep Your Jordan 11s Pristine
*   Gather the right tools: soft brushes, microfiber cloths, gentle cleaner.
*   Clean patent leather gently to avoid scratches and streaks.
*   Address mesh uppers and laces carefully with a soft brush.
*   Focus on soles to remove dirt and reduce yellowing.
*   Always air dry your sneakers away from direct heat or sunlight.
*   Regular maintenance helps prevent deep stains and material damage.

Cleaning Jordan 11s requires attention to detail. You must use appropriate methods for their distinct materials. This process ensures their lasting appeal. Your sneakers will thank you for this care.

### How do I effectively clean my Jordan 11 sneakers?

To effectively clean Jordan 11 sneakers, you should remove laces and insoles first. Then, gently scrub the patent leather and mesh uppers using a soft brush and a mild cleaning solution. Focus on the soles with a stiffer brush to remove deep dirt. Rinse with a damp cloth and air dry completely.

## Understanding Jordan 11 Materials for Effective Cleaning

Jordan 11 sneakers are famous for their unique material composition. This includes patent leather, ballistic mesh, and a clear or translucent rubber sole. Each material needs a specific cleaning approach. Understanding these differences helps you clean your Jordan 11s without causing damage. It also ensures a thorough restoration.

The patent leather on the Jordan 11 is its most recognizable feature. This glossy material looks sleek but can attract fingerprints and scuffs easily. It requires gentle cleaning to maintain its shine. Harsh scrubbing or abrasive cleaners can dull the finish or even cause irreversible scratches. We want to protect this signature look.

The ballistic mesh upper provides breathability and structure. This material is strong but can trap dirt and dust within its weave. Cleaning the mesh needs a soft touch. Using a soft brush helps dislodge debris without damaging the fabric. Proper technique keeps the mesh looking clean and fresh.

Finally, the Jordan 11's outsole often features a translucent or "icy" rubber. This part is prone to yellowing over time due to oxidation and dirt. The midsole is typically foam, which also collects grime. Both require specific methods to clean and maintain their original appearance. Knowing your materials is the first step to a successful clean.

### Patent Leather Care: Keeping the Shine

Patent leather gives the Jordan 11 its premium appeal. It has a high-gloss, reflective surface. This surface is also fairly delicate. You must approach cleaning patent leather with care.

Start with a soft, damp microfiber cloth. Gently wipe down the entire patent leather area. This removes loose dirt and dust. For more stubborn marks, use a small amount of mild soap mixed with water. Apply it to the cloth, not directly to the shoe.

Rub the area lightly in small, circular motions. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive sponges. These can easily scratch the glossy finish. After cleaning, wipe the area with a clean, damp cloth to remove any soap residue. Then, buff it dry with another clean microfiber cloth. This brings back the natural shine.

### Mesh Upper and Carbon Fiber Shank

The ballistic mesh upper is durable but can trap dirt. For the mesh, you will need a soft-bristled brush. A toothbrush works well for this part. Mix a small amount of mild cleaning solution with water.

Dip your brush into the solution. Gently scrub the mesh in one direction. This helps lift dirt from the fibers. Avoid soaking the mesh, as this can lead to longer drying times. Be gentle, but firm enough to remove grime.

The carbon fiber shank is usually visible on the midfoot of the outsole. This area is mostly decorative but can collect dirt. Use a soft brush or a damp cloth to clean it. Wipe away any grime that has accumulated. These parts contribute to the overall clean look of your sneakers.

### Icy Soles and Midsoles

Jordan 11 icy soles are a defining characteristic. They also present one of the biggest cleaning challenges. They tend to yellow over time due to oxidation and dirt. The midsoles, usually white foam, also show dirt easily. [Cleaning Jordan 11 soles](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-jordan-11-soles) is crucial for a complete restoration.

For general dirt on both the icy sole and midsole, use a medium-bristle brush. Apply your cleaning solution directly to the brush. Scrub the soles vigorously but carefully. Pay attention to the grooves and traction patterns. These areas hold a lot of dirt.

To combat yellowing on icy soles, you might need a specialized product. There are various sole-whitening solutions available. These products often require sunlight exposure to work effectively. We will discuss [how to clean Jordan 11 yellow sole](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-jordan-11-yellow-sole) more later. Always follow product instructions carefully.

## Gathering Your Essential Jordan 11 Cleaning Supplies

Having the right tools is half the battle when cleaning your Jordan 11s. You do not need expensive, specialized kits to get great results. Many common household items can work wonders. I like to be prepared before I start any cleaning project. This ensures a smooth and efficient process.

First, you need a good cleaning solution. A mild dish soap mixed with water is often sufficient. You can also use a dedicated sneaker cleaning solution. These are designed specifically for different shoe materials. Choose one that is gentle but effective.

Next, brushes are essential for different parts of the shoe. A soft-bristled brush is perfect for the delicate patent leather and mesh. A medium or stiff-bristled brush will handle the stubborn dirt on the soles. Having a few different brushes allows for precise cleaning.

Finally, you need a good drying method and some protective items. Microfiber cloths are great for wiping and drying. Shoe trees or paper towels help maintain the shoe's shape during drying. Gloves can protect your hands from cleaning solutions. Gather everything before you start to make the process easier.

### Gentle Cleaning Solutions

Choosing the right cleaning solution is very important. For Jordan 11s, a gentle approach is always best. You can create an effective solution with simple ingredients. My go-to is often a mix of warm water and a few drops of mild dish soap. This mixture cleans without damaging materials.

Some people prefer to use specialized sneaker cleaners. These products are formulated for various shoe fabrics. They often come with specific instructions. Read these instructions carefully before use. Avoid harsh chemicals like bleach or strong degreasers. These can discolor materials or dry them out.

If you are looking for [how to clean Jordan 11 with household items](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-jordan-11-with-household-items), a baking soda paste can work wonders on midsoles. A mixture of equal parts baking soda and water forms a gentle abrasive. This helps lift stains. Always test any new solution on a small, inconspicuous area first. This ensures no adverse reactions occur.

### Brushes and Cloths

Different parts of your Jordan 11s need different brushes. For the patent leather and mesh, a soft-bristled brush is crucial. An old, soft toothbrush works perfectly. This allows you to gently scrub dirt without scratching the delicate surfaces. A soft cloth also helps remove surface dust.

For the outsoles and midsoles, you will need a stiffer brush. A medium-bristle utility brush or a specialized sole brush is ideal. These brushes can tackle tough grime and dirt embedded in the sole's grooves. Make sure the bristles are not too harsh. You do not want to damage the rubber.

Microfiber cloths are indispensable for wiping and drying. They absorb water efficiently and do not leave lint behind. Have at least two on hand: one for cleaning and one for drying. A clean sponge can also be useful for applying solutions and wiping.

### Protective Gear and Drying Aids

Protecting your hands is a good idea when cleaning. Wear rubber gloves to prevent skin irritation from cleaning solutions. This also keeps your hands clean during the process. It is a small step that makes a big difference.

When it comes to drying, never use direct heat. This includes hair dryers or direct sunlight. High heat can damage the patent leather, melt adhesives, or yellow the icy soles further. Air drying is the safest method.

To help the shoes maintain their shape while drying, insert shoe trees. If you do not have shoe trees, crumple up some paper towels or newspaper. Stuff them inside the shoes. They will absorb moisture from the inside and prevent creasing. Replace them if they become too damp.

## Step-by-Step: Cleaning Jordan 11 Uppers and Laces

Now that you have gathered your supplies, it is time to get started. Cleaning the upper part of your Jordan 11s requires a systematic approach. We will tackle the laces, then the patent leather, and finally the mesh. Each step aims to gently remove dirt and restore freshness.

First, you need to prepare the shoes. Remove the laces from both sneakers. Take out the insoles as well, if they are removable. This allows you to clean each part individually and thoroughly. It also prevents the laces from getting tangled or the insoles from getting wet unnecessarily.

Once the shoes are disassembled, you can begin cleaning the laces. Laces often accumulate a lot of dirt and grime. I like to soak them separately. This helps loosen stubborn dirt before scrubbing.

After the laces are soaking, you can focus on the main body of the shoe. We will move from the cleanest parts to the dirtiest. This prevents spreading dirt around. Following these steps ensures your Jordan 11 uppers look sharp and clean.

### Laces and Interior

Start by giving your laces a good wash. Place them in a small bowl with warm water and a few drops of mild soap. Let them soak for about 15-20 minutes. This will loosen up any ingrained dirt. After soaking, you can gently scrub them with a soft brush or simply rub them together. Rinse them thoroughly under running water. Squeeze out excess water and set them aside to air dry.

While the laces soak, clean the interior of your shoes. If your insoles are removable, take them out. You can gently wipe them down with a damp cloth and a small amount of cleaning solution. For the inside of the shoe, use a damp cloth to wipe away any dust or debris. If there is a noticeable odor, sprinkle some baking soda inside. Let it sit for a few hours, then vacuum or shake it out. This helps to absorb odors.

### Patent Leather Cleaning

Cleaning the patent leather is a delicate task. This material is prone to scratches. Begin by wiping the patent leather areas with a clean, dry microfiber cloth. This removes any loose dust and debris. Always start with the least aggressive method.

Next, prepare your gentle cleaning solution. Mix a small amount of mild dish soap with warm water. Dip a clean microfiber cloth into the solution. Wring it out well so it is only damp, not dripping wet. Gently wipe the patent leather in smooth, even strokes. Do not scrub hard.

For any stubborn scuffs or marks, you can try a small amount of rubbing alcohol on a cotton swab. Apply it very sparingly and only to the mark itself. Rub gently, then immediately wipe the area with a damp cloth. Finish by buffing the patent leather with a dry microfiber cloth. This will restore its brilliant shine.

### Mesh Upper Cleaning

The mesh upper of your Jordan 11s also requires gentle attention. This material can trap dirt in its weave. Use a soft-bristled brush, like an old toothbrush, for this part. Dip the brush into your mild cleaning solution. Ensure the brush is damp, not saturated.

Gently scrub the mesh upper in small, circular motions. Work on one section at a time. This helps lift the dirt from the fibers. Be careful not to press too hard. You want to clean the mesh, not damage it.

After scrubbing, use a clean, damp microfiber cloth to wipe away the suds and dirt. Make sure to remove all soap residue. Repeat this process if necessary for heavily soiled areas. The goal is to leave the mesh clean and free of any cleaning solution residue.

## Restoring Your Jordan 11 Soles: Outsole to Icy Finish

The soles of Jordan 11s often take the most beating. They accumulate dirt, grime, and show signs of wear. Restoring them can significantly improve the overall look of your sneakers. This section focuses on effective techniques for cleaning the outsole, midsole, and tackling the notorious yellowing of icy soles. My goal is to bring back that fresh-from-the-box look.

The outsole, with its various traction patterns, traps a lot of dirt. This requires a more aggressive approach than the delicate upper materials. You need brushes that can get into those nooks and crannies. The midsole, usually white, often shows scuffs and dirt very clearly. A good cleaning can make it pop again.

However, the most challenging part is often the icy sole. Over time, these translucent soles oxidize and turn yellow. This oxidation is a natural process. But, with the right products and methods, you can significantly reduce or even reverse the yellowing. It takes patience, but the results are worth it.

So, let's dive into bringing your Jordan 11 soles back to life. We will cover general cleaning, specific treatments for yellowing, and steps to maintain that coveted icy look. Your sneakers will soon be stepping out with confidence.

### Cleaning the Outsole and Traction

The outsole is the part of your Jordan 11s that touches the ground. It picks up a lot of dirt, mud, and debris. For this section, you need a stiffer brush. A medium-bristle brush or even a plastic utility brush works well. Prepare your cleaning solution in a bowl.

Dip the brush into the solution. Start scrubbing the outsole vigorously. Pay close attention to the grooves and traction patterns. Dirt loves to hide in these areas. Do not be afraid to apply some pressure here. The rubber is durable.

Rinse the brush frequently to remove accumulated dirt. Continue scrubbing until all visible dirt is gone. After scrubbing, wipe the outsole thoroughly with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. Make sure no cleaning solution residue remains. This step makes a huge difference in the shoe's overall cleanliness.

### Tackling Yellowed Soles

Yellowing icy soles are a common problem for Jordan 11 owners. This is primarily due to oxidation. Sunlight and air exposure cause the clear rubber to yellow over time. Regular cleaning helps, but often a specialized treatment is needed for [how to clean Jordan 11 yellow sole](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-jordan-11-yellow-sole).

Many sneaker care brands offer "sole sauce" or de-yellowing creams. These products usually contain peroxide. They work by reversing the oxidation process. To use them, apply a thin layer of the cream to the yellowed areas of the icy sole. Make sure to cover the entire yellowed surface evenly.

Wrap the sole in plastic cling wrap. This helps activate the product and prevents it from drying out. Place the shoes in direct sunlight for several hours. The UV rays from the sun activate the peroxide. Check the progress periodically. Repeat the process if necessary until the desired whiteness is achieved. Always wear gloves when handling these products.

### Restoring Icy Soles

Beyond de-yellowing, you can take steps to maintain and restore the overall appearance of your icy soles. Once you have tackled any yellowing, focus on general clarity. After using a de-yellowing product, thoroughly rinse the soles. Use a soft brush and water to remove all product residue.

For ongoing maintenance, use a soft brush and a gentle cleaning solution. This removes surface dirt before it can embed itself. Regular cleaning is the best prevention against future yellowing. You are proactive in this care.

Consider applying a clear sole protector. These products form a barrier on the icy sole. They help slow down the oxidation process. This keeps your soles looking clear and fresh for longer. Always follow the product instructions carefully for the best results.

## Tackling Tough Stains and Deep Cleaning Jordan 11s

Sometimes, regular cleaning is not enough for your Jordan 11s. Stubborn stains, scuffs, or lingering odors might require a deeper approach. Do not worry, there are effective methods to deal with these tougher challenges. I have faced these issues myself, and knowing the right techniques can save your sneakers.

Deep cleaning goes beyond surface-level dirt. It targets embedded grime and specific types of stains that do not come off with a simple wipe. This involves understanding the nature of the stain. Different stains require different removal methods. You need to be patient and precise with your application.

Odor elimination is another key part of deep cleaning. Sneakers can develop unpleasant smells over time. This happens due to bacteria buildup from sweat. Addressing these odors makes your Jordan 11s pleasant to wear again. It is not just about looks; it is about comfort too.

Always remember to test any new product or technique on an inconspicuous area first. This prevents accidental damage or discoloration. We want to clean, not harm, our valuable Jordan 11s. Let's explore how to tackle those persistent problems.

### Addressing Stubborn Scuffs and Marks

Stubborn scuffs on patent leather can be tricky. For light scuffs, sometimes the gentle cleaning solution and microfiber cloth are enough. If a scuff remains, try a small amount of non-gel toothpaste. Apply a tiny dot to a clean cloth. Gently rub the scuff in a circular motion. The mild abrasive in toothpaste can buff out minor marks. Wipe away residue with a damp cloth.

For deeper scuffs or marks that look like they might have transferred color, a magic eraser can be effective. Dampen a small piece of a magic eraser. Gently rub the scuff mark. Use very light pressure. Magic erasers are abrasive, so excessive force can damage the patent leather's finish. Use sparingly and carefully. Always follow up by wiping with a clean, damp cloth.

For tougher marks on the midsole or outsole, a stronger cleaning solution might be needed. Some sneaker cleaning kits include specialized stain removers. Always read the instructions carefully. Apply the product directly to the stain. Let it sit for the recommended time. Then, scrub with a medium-bristle brush. Rinse thoroughly.

### Odor Elimination

Lingering odors in your Jordan 11s are unpleasant. They come from bacteria that thrive in damp, enclosed environments. Regular cleaning helps, but sometimes a deeper treatment is needed. My favorite method involves baking soda.

After cleaning your shoes, make sure they are completely dry. Sprinkle a generous amount of baking soda inside each shoe. Make sure it covers the entire footbed. Let the baking soda sit overnight, or even for 24-48 hours. Baking soda is a natural odor absorber. It helps neutralize smells.

After the waiting period, shake out the baking soda. You can also use a small vacuum cleaner with an attachment to remove all residue. For a more intense treatment, you can also place dryer sheets inside the shoes overnight. These add a fresh scent. Another option is a sneaker deodorizer spray. These sprays often contain antibacterial agents. They can kill odor-causing bacteria.

## Proper Drying and Long-Term Care for Jordan 11s

After all your hard work cleaning, proper drying is a critical final step. Incorrect drying can undo your efforts or even damage your Jordan 11s. Once your sneakers are clean and fresh, maintaining that condition becomes the focus. Long-term care ensures their longevity and keeps them looking great. I always make sure my sneakers dry correctly.

The most important rule for drying is to avoid direct heat. High temperatures can cause materials to warp or adhesives to weaken. This applies to both the patent leather and the icy soles. Patience is key during the drying process. Rushing it can lead to problems.

Beyond drying, thinking about storage and ongoing prevention is crucial. How you store your Jordan 11s when not wearing them impacts their condition. Protecting them from dust and harsh environments helps preserve their materials. Regular small actions can prevent bigger cleaning tasks later.

Let's ensure your freshly cleaned Jordan 11s stay in top shape. We will cover the best drying methods and essential tips for their long-term care. Your sneakers deserve this final attention.

### Proper Drying Methods

Air drying is the only safe method for Jordan 11s. Never use a tumble dryer, hair dryer, or place them near a radiator. High heat can cause the patent leather to crack or stiffen. It can also melt the glue that holds the shoe together. This can also yellow the icy soles further.

After cleaning, gently pat down the shoes with a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Remove as much surface moisture as possible. Then, unlace the shoes completely. Open them up as much as you can. This allows for maximum airflow.

Stuff the shoes with crumpled paper towels or newspaper. These absorb moisture from the inside. They also help maintain the shoe's shape as it dries. Replace the paper if it becomes saturated. Place the shoes in a well-ventilated area, away from direct sunlight. A cool, dry room is ideal. Depending on humidity, drying can take 24-48 hours. Make sure they are completely dry before wearing or storing.

### Storage and Prevention

Proper storage protects your Jordan 11s from environmental damage. Store them in a cool, dry place. Avoid basements or attics where temperatures and humidity can fluctuate wildly. Extreme conditions can damage materials. A closet or a dedicated shoe rack is usually best.

Consider using sneaker storage boxes. These protect your shoes from dust and light exposure. Clear plastic boxes allow you to see your collection easily. If you have the original box, that works well too. Placing silica gel packets in the box can help absorb moisture. This prevents mildew.

To prevent future heavy cleaning, practice regular maintenance. Wipe down your Jordan 11s after each wear with a damp cloth. This removes surface dirt before it sets in. Avoid wearing them in very harsh weather conditions like heavy rain or mud. A protective spray designed for sneakers can add an invisible barrier against water and stains. This proactive approach saves time and effort in the long run.

## Everyday Maintenance Tips to Keep Your Jordan 11s Fresh

Keeping your Jordan 11s looking great does not just happen after a deep clean. Consistent, everyday maintenance plays a significant role. Small habits can prevent major cleaning headaches. I find that a little bit of care after each wear goes a long way. It extends the life and appearance of your prized sneakers.

Think of it as routine upkeep, like brushing your teeth. You would not wait until you have a cavity to clean them. Similarly, you should not wait until your Jordan 11s are heavily soiled to address dirt. These simple practices are easy to integrate into your routine. They save you time and effort compared to tackling deep-set stains.

These tips focus on quick actions you can take. They help protect the patent leather, mesh, and soles from daily wear and tear. You are preserving their iconic look. It is about being proactive rather than reactive.

Let's explore how you can keep your Jordan 11s looking fresh every day. These small steps make a big difference in maintaining their appeal. Your sneakers will thank you for this continuous attention.

### Quick Wipe-Down After Each Wear

One of the easiest and most effective maintenance tips is a quick wipe-down. After every time you wear your Jordan 11s, take a minute to clean them. Use a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Wipe away any dust or loose dirt from the patent leather and mesh. This prevents dirt from settling and embedding into the materials.

For any light scuffs or marks on the patent leather, a slightly damp cloth can be used. Gently rub the area to remove the fresh mark. This immediate action is crucial. It keeps the patent leather looking shiny and new. Waiting too long allows marks to set in.

Do not forget the soles. Use a damp cloth to wipe off any visible dirt from the outsole and midsole. This quick cleaning prevents grime buildup. It also helps preserve the clarity of your icy soles. This simple habit keeps your Jordan 11s looking presentable.

### Protecting Your Sneakers from Elements

Prevention is better than cure, especially with expensive sneakers. Consider using a sneaker protector spray. These sprays create a water- and stain-repellent barrier. Apply it to your Jordan 11s according to the product instructions. This adds an invisible layer of defense.

Avoid wearing your Jordan 11s in harsh weather conditions. Heavy rain, snow, or muddy environments are not ideal. Water and dirt can quickly damage the materials and cause deep stains. If you must wear them in light rain, ensure they are protected.

Be mindful of where you step. Try to avoid puddles and excessively dusty or dirty areas. This reduces the amount of grime your shoes pick up. This protective mindset helps preserve their condition. It is about making smart choices for your sneakers.

### Regular De-Lacing and Deep Dusting

Even with regular wipes, dust and small debris can accumulate. Periodically, maybe once a month or every few weeks, de-lace your Jordan 11s. This allows you to access areas usually covered. Use a soft brush or a dry cloth to dust out these hidden spots.

Pay attention to the eyelets and the area around the tongue. Dust often settles here. While the laces are out, you can also give them a quick wash if needed. This keeps all parts of your shoe fresh. It prevents dust buildup from dulling the overall appearance.

This occasional deeper dusting helps prevent long-term grime accumulation. It also allows you to inspect your sneakers closely. You can spot any new marks or issues early. Early detection means easier treatment. This proactive approach extends the pristine look of your Jordan 11s.
