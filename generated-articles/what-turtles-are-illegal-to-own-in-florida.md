### Are Florida box turtles illegal to own?

Most native Florida box turtles are illegal to collect from the wild for personal ownership. They are protected species. You can only own them with specific permits for educational or scientific purposes. However, some non-native box turtle species, typically those sold by licensed breeders outside of Florida and brought in, may be legal without special permits, but always verify with FWC first.

If caught with an illegal turtle in Florida, you can face significant penalties. ### Do I need a permit for any turtle in Florida?

Yes, for many turtles, you need a permit from the Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission (FWC). ### Are snapping turtles illegal to own in Florida?
