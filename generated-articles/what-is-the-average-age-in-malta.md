### What is the Difference Between Median and Average Age?

The terms "median age" and "average age" are sometimes used interchangeably, but they mean different things. The average age, also known as the mean, is calculated by adding up everyone's age and dividing by the total number of people. This can be skewed by a few very old or very young individuals. On the other hand, the median age is the middle value in a dataset when all ages are arranged in order. It gives a more representative value for the "typical" age of a population.
