Astro is primarily depicted as a Great Dane. His animated design gives him a shaggy, brown coat and a large build. However, his human-like qualities and unique vocalizations make him a highly stylized version of the breed. He often walks on two legs.

Astro was voiced by the legendary voice actor Don Messick. Messick was famous for many Hanna-Barbera characters. He also voiced Scooby-Doo and Boo-Boo Bear. His distinct voice acting gave Astro his iconic garbled speech and "Ruh-roh!" sound.

### Is Astro related to Scooby-Doo?
Astro is not related to Scooby-Doo in terms of character lore or family. However, both characters were voiced by the same actor, Don Messick. This shared voice actor led to similar vocalizations, like the "Ruh-roh!" phrase. This similarity often creates a connection in fans' minds.
