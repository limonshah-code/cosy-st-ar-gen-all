## Clean Your Pit Boss Vertical Smoker Auger

Maintaining your Pit Boss vertical smoker keeps it working great. A clean auger ensures consistent performance and delicious food. Many owners overlook this important part. I know because I did too, once.

Learning how to clean auger on Pit Boss vertical smoker is a simple task that improves your cooking. It prevents common problems like pellet jams and uneven temperatures. This guide covers why auger cleaning matters and offers clear, step-by-step instructions. You will learn about the tools you need and how to reassemble your smoker. Let's make sure your vertical smoker delivers perfect results every time.

### Takeaway

*   **Unplug and Cool Down:** Always disconnect power and ensure the smoker is completely cool before starting.
*   **Empty Hopper:** Remove all pellets from the hopper to prevent new feed during cleaning.
*   **Access Auger:** Disassemble the fire pot and heat deflector to reach the auger assembly.
*   **Clean Thoroughly:** Use a brush and vacuum to remove all ash, dust, and debris from the auger and its housing.
*   **Reassemble Carefully:** Put all components back correctly, making sure they fit snugly.
*   **Prevent Future Jams:** Use dry, good-quality pellets and store your smoker properly.

To clean the auger on a Pit Boss vertical smoker, first empty the hopper and ensure the unit is unplugged and cool. Access the auger by removing the internal components like the grates, water pan, and fire pot. Then, use a vacuum and stiff brush to meticulously clear all pellet dust, ash, and any blockages from the auger screw and its tube, ensuring smooth pellet flow.

## Why Auger Cleaning Is Essential for Your Vertical Smoker

The auger is the heart of your Pit Boss vertical smoker. It moves wood pellets from the hopper to the fire pot. This steady movement provides the fuel for smoke and heat. If the auger stops working right, your smoker cannot perform well.

A dirty auger causes many problems. Pellets might not feed smoothly, leading to temperature swings. You could experience a complete auger jam, stopping your cook cold. This also impacts the quality of your smoked food. Uneven temperatures prevent proper cooking and smoke absorption.

Regular cleaning keeps the auger turning freely. It removes built-up dust, ash, and pellet fragments. This prevents blockages and ensures a consistent pellet supply. My smoker always performs better after a good auger clean. It holds temperature better and produces more even smoke. This small effort makes a big difference in your smoking results.

Ignoring auger maintenance can shorten your smoker's life. The motor works harder when the auger is dirty. This can lead to premature wear and costly repairs. A clean auger means a happy smoker and better BBQ. It is part of taking good care of your grilling equipment. For more general Pit Boss cleaning routines, you can check out this guide on [overall pellet grill maintenance](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-a-pit-boss-pro-series-pellet-grill).

## Necessary Tools and Safety Measures

Before you start cleaning your Pit Boss vertical smoker's auger, gather your tools. Having everything ready makes the job easier and quicker. You do not want to stop in the middle to look for something. I always lay out my tools beforehand.

Here is a list of what you will need:

*   **Shop-Vac or Handheld Vacuum:** This helps remove loose pellets, ash, and dust.
*   **Stiff-Bristle Brush:** A grill brush or a small wire brush works well for scraping.
*   **Screwdriver (Phillips and/or Flathead):** You will need this to remove screws holding components.
*   **Bucket or Container:** For collecting old pellets.
*   **Gloves:** Protect your hands from ash and grime.
*   **Flashlight (Optional but Recommended):** Helps you see inside dark areas of the smoker.

Safety is very important during this process. Always unplug your Pit Boss vertical smoker from the power outlet. This prevents accidental startup. Ensure the smoker is completely cool before you touch any internal parts. Hot components can cause burns. I learned this the hard way, so trust me on this step. Working on a cool smoker is much safer. Wear gloves to keep your hands clean and protect them from sharp edges. Always work in a well-lit area to see what you are doing.

## Preparing Your Pit Boss for Auger Access

Getting to the auger on your Pit Boss vertical smoker requires some preparation. You need to clear out the cooking area first. This step ensures you have a clear path to the auger assembly. It also helps keep your workspace clean.

Start by emptying the hopper completely. Remove any remaining wood pellets. You can scoop them out or use a shop-vac. Store usable pellets in an airtight container to protect them from moisture. Moisture causes pellets to swell and break down, which can lead to auger jams. Never leave pellets in the hopper for extended periods, especially if your smoker is stored outdoors.

Next, remove all internal cooking components. These usually include:

*   **Cooking Grates:** Lift them out and set them aside.
*   **Water Pan:** Carefully remove the water pan. If it has water, empty it first.
*   **Heat Deflector/Baffle:** This metal plate sits above the fire pot. It directs heat and smoke. You will need to remove it to access the fire pot and auger below. Some models might have screws holding it in place.

Once these parts are out, you should see the fire pot. The auger feeds pellets directly into this component. Getting these out of the way creates a clear view and working space. This allows you to inspect the area better. It also prepares the smoker for the next disassembly steps. Removing these parts now saves time later.

## Step-by-Step Auger Disassembly

Now that your Pit Boss vertical smoker is ready, it is time to access the auger itself. This involves carefully removing the fire pot. The fire pot usually sits at the bottom of the cooking chamber. It holds the igniter rod and receives pellets from the auger. This part of the process requires a bit of patience and a screwdriver.

First, locate the fire pot. It is a small, metal cup-shaped component at the base. You will see wires connected to it for the igniter. Make sure these wires are clear before you try to remove the pot. Some Pit Boss models use screws to hold the fire pot in place. Other models might have a simple clip or just sit in a cradle. Look for any visible screws around the base or sides of the fire pot. Use your screwdriver to carefully loosen and remove them. Keep track of these screws; you will need them for reassembly.

Once the screws are out, gently lift the fire pot. You might need to wiggle it slightly to free it. Be careful not to pull on the igniter wires. These wires are delicate. After removing the fire pot, you will see the end of the auger tube. The auger screw itself should be visible inside this tube. This is the part that moves the pellets. This step opens up the area for a thorough cleaning.

For some models, you might need to remove a small access panel to get a better view of the auger motor and shaft. This is less common for simple auger cleaning but good to know for deeper maintenance. Always refer to your Pit Boss vertical smoker's specific manual if you are unsure about removing any component. This ensures you do not damage any parts. This detailed auger cleaning guide helps ensure a smooth operation.

## Thoroughly Cleaning the Auger and Housing

With the auger finally exposed, it is time for the main cleaning. This step removes all the debris that causes jams and poor performance. A thorough cleaning ensures pellets move freely. I always take my time here.

Start by using your shop-vac or handheld vacuum. Vacuum out all loose ash, pellet dust, and small pellet fragments from the fire pot area. Get into all the corners and crevices. Make sure to vacuum inside the auger tube as much as you can. This gets rid of the majority of the dry buildup.

Next, use your stiff-bristle brush. Scrub the auger screw itself. Look for any caked-on residue, burnt pellets, or stubborn ash. Sometimes, pellets can break down into a sticky substance if they get wet or are left too long. Scrape off any hard deposits from the auger flights (the spiral part). Also, scrub the inside walls of the auger tube. Ensure the entire length of the visible auger screw is clean. You want it to be smooth to the touch.

Use your flashlight to inspect the auger tube. Check for any remaining blockages or obstructions deep inside. If you see any, try to dislodge them with a smaller brush or a long, thin tool. Be gentle not to damage the auger screw. I once found a small piece of wood that broke off a pellet, which was causing a partial blockage. Removing it made a huge difference.

After brushing, vacuum the area again to pick up any loosened debris. Make sure the auger can turn freely. You might even manually turn the auger motor slightly (if accessible and safe) to expose more of the screw for cleaning. This comprehensive auger maintenance offers benefits you will appreciate.

## Reassembly and Post-Cleaning Checks

After a thorough cleaning, it is time to put your Pit Boss vertical smoker back together. This process is essentially the reverse of disassembly. Making sure everything is reassembled correctly is important. Incorrect assembly can lead to performance issues or safety hazards.

First, carefully place the fire pot back into its position. Ensure the igniter wires are not pinched or crimped. If there were screws, reinsert and tighten them. Do not overtighten, as this can strip the threads. The fire pot should sit snugly and securely.

Next, replace the heat deflector/baffle. Ensure it sits correctly over the fire pot. If it had screws, put those back in. Then, carefully place the water pan back into its slot. Finally, put the cooking grates back inside the smoker. Double-check that all components are in their proper places. They should not wobble or shift easily.
