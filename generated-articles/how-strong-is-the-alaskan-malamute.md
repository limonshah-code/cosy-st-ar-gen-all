### FAQ Section

#### Are Alaskan Malamutes stronger than German Shepherds?
Alaskan Malamutes are generally considered stronger in terms of brute pulling power and endurance for heavy loads. German Shepherds are very strong dogs too, excelling in agility, speed, and bite force for protection work. Their strength types differ based on their original breeding purposes.

#### How much weight can an Alaskan Malamute pull?
An adult Alaskan Malamute can pull significant weight. In formal weight pulling competitions, well-trained Malamutes can pull several times their own body weight. Some have pulled over 1,000 to 2,000 pounds on a wheeled cart. This showcases their incredible power.

#### Is a male or female Alaskan Malamute stronger?
Male Alaskan Malamutes are typically larger and heavier than females. This often translates to greater physical strength and pulling power. Females are still very strong dogs, but males generally have a more robust build. The difference is usually consistent with most dog breeds where males are larger.

#### Do Alaskan Malamutes have a high pain tolerance?
Alaskan Malamutes possess a good pain tolerance, likely inherited from their history as working dogs in harsh Arctic conditions. They had to endure cold and minor injuries while performing their duties. This resilience makes them stoic, but owners should still watch for signs of discomfort or illness.

#### Can an Alaskan Malamute be a good guard dog due to its strength?
While an Alaskan Malamute is a large and strong dog, they are not typically ideal guard dogs. Their strength can deter intruders, but they are often friendly and lack the protective instincts of true guard breeds. They are more likely to greet a stranger than attack.

#### What makes an Alaskan Malamute a "strong" dog?
An Alaskan Malamute is a strong dog due to several factors. These include their large, muscular build, dense bone structure, high endurance for heavy work, and powerful jaws. Their historical role as heavy freight pullers shaped these physical and mental attributes.
