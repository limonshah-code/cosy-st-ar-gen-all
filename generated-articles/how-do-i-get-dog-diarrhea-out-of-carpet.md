### Step-by-Step Guide: How to Clean Dog Diarrhea from Carpet

Cleaning dog diarrhea from carpet follows a clear process. Each step builds on the last for effective removal. Following these steps helps you eliminate the mess completely. I rely on this method every time.

#### 1. Prepare the Area and Remove Solids

First, put on your gloves. This protects your hands from bacteria. Next, use a dull knife, spoon, or stiff piece of cardboard. Gently scrape all solid material from the carpet. Work from the outside edges of the mess towards the center. This prevents spreading the stain further. Place the collected solids onto several layers of paper towels. Immediately dispose of these paper towels in a plastic bag. Seal the bag tightly to contain odors and bacteria.

#### 2. Blot, Do Not Rub

After removing solids, the carpet will still be wet. Grab a stack of clean paper towels or an absorbent cloth. Press firmly onto the stained area. Blot the liquid up. Do not rub the carpet. Rubbing pushes the stain deeper into the fibers. It can also damage the carpet pile. Lift the paper towel and discard it when it becomes soiled. Continue blotting with fresh towels until no more liquid transfers from the carpet. You want the area as dry as possible before applying cleaner.

#### 3. Apply Your Chosen Cleaning Solution

Now, it is time for the cleaning solution. For enzymatic cleaners, follow the product instructions carefully. Typically, you spray or pour the cleaner onto the stained area. Ensure the entire soiled area is saturated. The enzymes need to penetrate the carpet fibers to work effectively. For DIY solutions like vinegar and water, saturate the spot with the mixture. If using hydrogen peroxide, apply it sparingly and blot immediately. Remember to test any solution in an hidden spot first.

#### 4. Let the Cleaner Work

After applying the cleaner, let it sit. Enzymatic cleaners need time to break down organic matter. Check the product label for the recommended dwell time. This is usually 10-15 minutes, sometimes longer. During this time, the enzymes are actively working. They are dissolving the stain and odor molecules. Do not rush this step. Allowing sufficient time for the cleaner to work is crucial.

#### 5. Blot Again and Rinse

Once the dwell time passes, blot the area again. Use clean, dry paper towels or a white cloth. Press firmly to absorb the cleaner and loosened stain particles. Continue blotting until no more residue comes up. Then, rinse the area. You can do this by lightly dampening a clean cloth with plain water. Blot the area with the damp cloth. This removes any cleaning solution residue. Repeat this rinsing and blotting process several times. This ensures all cleaner is gone.

#### 6. Dry the Carpet Thoroughly

Proper drying prevents mold and mildew growth. After rinsing, place several layers of dry paper towels over the cleaned spot. You can weigh them down with a heavy book. This helps absorb remaining moisture. Replace wet towels with dry ones as needed. Alternatively, use a wet/dry vacuum cleaner. This device can extract a lot of moisture quickly. Aim a fan at the area to speed up drying. Ensure the carpet is completely dry before walking on it regularly.
