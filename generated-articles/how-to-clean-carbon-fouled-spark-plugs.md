## Restore Engine Power: Clean Carbon Fouled Spark Plugs

Have you noticed your engine losing its usual pep? Perhaps your fuel economy is dropping, or the car idles roughly. These issues often point to one common culprit: carbon fouled spark plugs. When carbon builds up on your spark plugs, it can seriously hinder your engine's ability to create a strong, consistent spark. This directly impacts performance and efficiency. I have experienced this firsthand and know the frustration of a sluggish engine. This guide will show you precisely how to clean carbon fouled spark plugs, helping you restore your engine's power and improve overall vehicle health. We will cover identifying the problem, gathering tools, the actual cleaning process, and proper reinstallation.

### Takeaway:
*   Identify carbon fouling by inspecting spark plug tips.
*   Gather correct tools and cleaning agents for effective carbon removal.
*   Follow a step-by-step cleaning process for each spark plug.
*   Ensure correct gapping and reinstallation for optimal engine performance.

**To clean carbon fouled spark plugs, you must first remove them carefully, then use a wire brush or fine-grit sandpaper to scrape away carbon deposits. Follow this with a non-flammable cleaner to remove residues, ensuring the electrode and insulator are pristine. Finally, re-gap and reinstall the spark plugs.**
