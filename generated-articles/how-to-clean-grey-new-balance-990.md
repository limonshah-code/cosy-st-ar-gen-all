## Clean Your Grey New Balance 990: A Complete Guide

Your New Balance 990 sneakers are more than just shoes; they are a statement of comfort and style. The iconic grey color is a favorite for many. However, daily wear can leave them looking dull and dirty. Keeping your **Grey New Balance 990** clean helps them last longer. It also maintains their sharp appearance. A fresh pair of sneakers makes you feel good.

Cleaning your **Grey New Balance 990** requires special care. Their premium materials, like suede and mesh, need gentle treatment. I have seen many people damage their shoes with harsh methods. ### Takeaway

*   Gather the right, gentle cleaning tools before you start.
*   Always dry brush your **Grey New Balance 990** to remove loose dirt first.
*   Treat suede with specific cleaners and a soft brush for best results.
*   Use a mild solution and soft brush for mesh uppers.
*   Clean midsoles and outsoles with a stiffer brush and shoe cleaner.

### Understanding Your Grey New Balance 990 Materials

The **Grey New Balance 990** is famous for its comfort. It is also known for its classic design. This shoe uses a mix of materials. These materials make it durable and stylish. Each material needs specific care. Knowing these parts helps you clean them correctly.

The upper part often features suede overlays. Suede gives the shoe a premium look and feel. It is a soft leather material. Suede can easily attract dirt and stains. Water can also mark suede permanently. This means you must be careful when cleaning it. Use a specialized suede brush and cleaner.

Mesh sections are also common on the 990. Mesh is a breathable fabric. It keeps your feet cool and comfortable. Dirt and dust can get trapped in the mesh. This can make your grey shoes look dull. Mesh is generally easier to clean than suede. However, it still needs gentle handling. A soft brush works well for mesh.

The midsole is usually made of EVA foam or similar cushioning. This part provides comfort and support. It often gets dirty from stepping on various surfaces. Scuffs and grime are common on the white or off-white midsoles. The outsole is the bottom part of the shoe. It is made of rubber. The outsole has a tread pattern for grip. It picks up the most dirt and mud.

Side panels and other accents might use synthetic leather. These materials are generally easier to wipe clean. They are less porous than suede or mesh. Understanding these different parts is the first step. It ensures you use the right cleaning method for each. Proper care prevents damage to your valuable **Grey New Balance 990**.

### Gathering Your Cleaning Tools and Products

Having the right tools makes cleaning your **Grey New Balance 990** much easier. It also helps you avoid damage. Do not just grab any household cleaner. Specific shoe cleaning products exist for a reason. They are formulated to be safe for delicate materials. I always prepare my tools beforehand. This saves time and ensures a smooth cleaning process.

First, you need a good shoe cleaning solution. Many brands offer dedicated sneaker cleaners. Look for one that is safe for suede and mesh. Mild soap, like dish soap, can work in a pinch. However, use it very sparingly and dilute it well. Avoid harsh chemicals or detergents. These can strip color or damage materials.

Next, gather a few brushes. A soft-bristle brush is essential for mesh and general scrubbing. An old toothbrush can work for this too. You will need a medium-bristle brush for midsoles. This helps to remove stubborn grime. A stiff-bristle brush is great for outsoles. This can get into deep treads. For suede, a specialized suede brush with rubber bristles is ideal. It helps lift dirt without damaging the nap. A suede eraser is also useful for small scuffs.

Microfiber cloths are very important. You will need a few clean cloths. They are good for wiping and drying. Make sure they are soft and absorbent. A bowl of clean water is also needed for rinsing brushes. Paper towels can help absorb excess moisture. Cotton swabs can reach tight spots.

Finally, consider a shoe protector spray. This is applied after cleaning. It helps repel water and stains. This makes future clean-ups easier. This is especially good for your **Grey New Balance 990** suede. Having these items ready will help you effectively clean your shoes. It will keep them looking their best.

### Pre-Cleaning Steps: Preparing Your 990s for a Deep Clean

Before you start scrubbing, some preparation is key. These first steps are crucial. They remove loose dirt and protect your shoes. I never skip these initial actions. They make the deep cleaning process much more effective. Proper preparation prevents spreading dirt around.

First, remove the shoelaces. Untie them completely. You will clean these separately later. Removing laces lets you access all parts of the shoe. It also keeps them from getting tangled or excessively wet. Set them aside in a small bowl if they are very dirty.

Next, take out the insoles. Most New Balance 990s have removable insoles. These can harbor sweat and odor. Cleaning them separately is important. If they are not removable, you can still clean them inside the shoe. Just be careful not to oversaturate the shoe's interior.

Now, it is time for the dry brushing. This is a very important step. Use a soft-bristle brush for the upper. Gently brush off any loose dirt, dust, or mud. Work in small sections. Pay extra attention to the suede parts. Dry brushing prevents dirt from turning into mud when you add water. This makes the cleaning solution more effective. For the midsole and outsole, use a stiffer brush. Scrape off any caked-on mud or debris. You can tap the shoes together to dislodge stubborn bits.

Shake out any sand or small stones from inside the shoes. You can also use a small vacuum cleaner hose for this. Make sure the shoes are completely free of loose debris. This pre-cleaning stage sets the foundation. It ensures a thorough and safe clean for your **Grey New Balance 990**. Just as you might take time to prepare for [new carpet expenses](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-much-will-new-carpet-cost) by planning your budget, investing time in these preliminary cleaning steps helps prevent future problems and maintains the longevity of your footwear.

### Cleaning the Suede and Mesh Uppers

The upper part of your **Grey New Balance 990** needs the most careful attention. This is where the suede and mesh meet. These materials are delicate. They can be easily damaged if not cleaned correctly. I approach this step with patience. My goal is to lift dirt without harming the fabric.

Start with the suede sections. Suede is sensitive to water. Avoid saturating it. If you have a suede eraser, use it first. Gently rub the eraser over any scuff marks or light stains. This often lifts dirt without liquid. For deeper stains, use a specialized suede cleaner. Apply a small amount of cleaner to a soft cloth or brush. Gently rub the suede in one direction. Do not scrub back and forth vigorously. This can damage the nap of the suede. Wipe off excess cleaner with a dry microfiber cloth. After cleaning, use a suede brush to restore the nap. Brush in a consistent direction to make the fibers stand up. This brings back the soft texture of your grey shoes.

Now, move to the mesh areas. Mesh is more forgiving than suede. Mix your shoe cleaning solution with water in a bowl. Follow the product's instructions for dilution. Dip your soft-bristle brush into the solution. Gently scrub the mesh in small circular motions. Do not press too hard. The goal is to create a light foam. This foam lifts the dirt from the mesh fibers.

After scrubbing, wipe the mesh with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. Make sure to remove all soap residue. Change the cloth or rinse it often. You do not want to reapply dirty water. Repeat this process until the mesh looks clean. Be careful not to let the suede get too wet during this step. If water accidentally gets on the suede, blot it immediately. This helps prevent water stains. ### Tackling Midsoles and Outsoles

The midsoles and outsoles of your **Grey New Balance 990** see the most action. They collect dirt, mud, and scuff marks. These parts are usually made of rubber or foam. They are more durable than the upper materials. This means you can use a bit more pressure here. Still, approach cleaning with care to preserve your shoes.

For the midsoles, which are often white or light-colored, scuffs are common. Dip a medium-bristle brush into your shoe cleaning solution. Apply a small amount of cleaner directly to the midsole. Scrub in small, firm circles. Pay extra attention to any scuff marks. The bristles will help lift the grime. If some stains are persistent, you can try a magic eraser. Gently rub it over the scuff. Be careful not to rub too hard, especially near the fabric.

After scrubbing, wipe the midsole with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. Ensure all cleaning solution is removed. Residue can attract more dirt over time. Rinse your cloth frequently. You want a fresh wipe each time. Repeat this until the midsole is clean. The contrast of a clean midsole makes your whole **Grey New Balance 990** look much better.

The outsoles, the very bottom of your shoes, get the dirtiest. They also have deep grooves. These grooves trap small stones and mud. Use a stiff-bristle brush for the outsole. Apply the cleaning solution directly to the outsole. Scrub vigorously. Get into all the nooks and crannies. An old toothbrush can help reach tight spots in the tread. You can also use a small pick or toothpick to dislodge stubborn debris.

Rinse the outsole thoroughly. You can run it under tap water. Make sure to not get the upper parts too wet. Shake off excess water. A clean outsole provides better grip. It also completes the overall clean look of your **Grey New Balance 990**. Maintaining the right [balance in cleaning care](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-balance-lg-front-load-washing-machine) for each part of your shoe is key to a successful clean.

### Lace and Insole Refresh

While your **Grey New Balance 990** uppers and soles are getting cleaned, do not forget the laces and insoles. These small parts also contribute to the overall freshness of your shoes. Cleaning them separately is important. It ensures a complete clean.

Let us start with the laces. Shoelaces can get surprisingly dirty. They pick up dust and grime. They also absorb sweat. You have a few options for cleaning them. The easiest way is to hand wash them. Put them in a bowl with warm water and a small amount of mild detergent. Rub them together vigorously. This helps release the dirt. If they are very dirty, you can let them soak for about 15-30 minutes.

Alternatively, you can put laces in a mesh laundry bag. Then, machine wash them on a gentle cycle. Use cold water and mild detergent. This method is quicker. However, ensure they are secured in the bag. This prevents them from tangling. After washing, rinse them thoroughly. Hang them to air dry. Never put laces in a dryer. High heat can shrink or damage them. Clean, fresh laces make a big difference.

Next, address the insoles. Insoles absorb sweat and can start to smell. If they are removable, take them out. You can hand wash them in warm, soapy water. Use a soft brush to gently scrub them. Focus on areas that contact your foot. Rinse them well to remove all soap. Squeeze out excess water. Lay them flat to air dry. Make sure they are completely dry before putting them back into your shoes. This prevents mildew and odors.

If your insoles are not removable, you can still freshen them. Sprinkle baking soda inside the shoes. Let it sit overnight. Baking soda absorbs odors. Then, vacuum or shake out the baking soda. You can also lightly spray them with an odor-eliminating spray. Ensure the spray is safe for shoe materials. Taking care of these details ensures your whole **Grey New Balance 990** smells and looks clean. ### Drying and Post-Cleaning Care

The cleaning process does not end with scrubbing. Proper drying is just as important. Incorrect drying can damage your **Grey New Balance 990**. It can cause shrinking, warping, or material breakdown. Post-cleaning care also protects your shoes. It keeps them looking good for longer.

After cleaning, gently press excess water from your shoes. You can use a clean, dry microfiber cloth for this. Blot the suede and mesh sections carefully. Do not twist or wring the shoes. This can distort their shape. You want to remove as much surface moisture as possible.

The most crucial step is air drying. Always air dry your **Grey New Balance 990**. Find a cool, dry place. Keep them away from direct sunlight. Direct sun can fade the grey color. It can also dry out and crack the materials. Avoid using heat sources like radiators or hair dryers. High heat can shrink the shoes. It can also damage the glue and cause parts to separate.

To help maintain the shoe's shape during drying, stuff them with paper towels or shoe trees. Paper towels absorb internal moisture. They also help the shoes keep their form. Change the paper towels every few hours if they get very damp. Let the shoes dry for at least 24-48 hours. Ensure they are completely dry before wearing them again. Wearing damp shoes can cause odors and mildew.

Once your shoes are thoroughly dry, reinsert the insoles. Lace them up with your freshly cleaned laces. For an extra layer of protection, apply a shoe protector spray. These sprays create a barrier against water and stains. Follow the product instructions for application. This step is especially beneficial for the suede on your **Grey New Balance 990**. A protector helps repel future dirt. It makes subsequent cleaning easier. Regular cleaning and protection extend the life of your sneakers. They keep your cherished 990s looking sharp.

### FAQ Section

#### Q1: Can I put Grey New Balance 990s in the washing machine?
No, it is not recommended to put your Grey New Balance 990s in a washing machine. The harsh cycles and water saturation can damage the delicate suede and mesh materials. It can also degrade the shoe's structure and glues. Hand cleaning or spot cleaning is always the safest method to protect your sneakers.

#### Q2: How do I clean suede on my 990s without water?
For light dirt or scuffs on suede, use a specialized suede eraser. Gently rub the eraser over the affected area to lift the dirt. Follow up with a suede brush to restore the nap. This method is effective for dry stains and helps avoid water marks on the delicate material.

#### Q3: What is the best cleaner for grey mesh on New Balance 990s?
The best cleaner for grey mesh is a mild shoe cleaning solution diluted with water. Use a soft-bristle brush, like an old toothbrush, to gently scrub the mesh. Avoid harsh chemicals. Wipe off the solution with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. This approach is effective and safe for the fabric.

#### Q4: How often should I clean my New Balance 990s?
The frequency of cleaning depends on how often you wear your shoes and where you wear them. For daily wear, a quick dry brush after each use is good. A deep clean every 2-4 weeks is usually sufficient to keep your Grey New Balance 990s looking fresh. Clean them immediately after any significant dirt or stain.

#### Q5: How do I prevent my grey 990s from getting dirty quickly?
To prevent your grey 990s from getting dirty quickly, apply a good quality water and stain repellent spray after cleaning. Avoid wearing them in heavy rain or muddy conditions. Store them in a dust bag or shoe box when not in use. Regular dry brushing after each wear also helps.

#### Q6: Can I use household items to clean my 990s?
Some household items can be used, but with caution. A mild dish soap diluted with water can be a substitute for shoe cleaner. An old toothbrush works for scrubbing. Baking soda helps with odors. However, avoid strong household cleaners like bleach. Always test any product on a small, inconspicuous area first.
