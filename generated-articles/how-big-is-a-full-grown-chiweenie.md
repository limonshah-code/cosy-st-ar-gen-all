### Growth Stages: When Does a Chiweenie Reach Full Size?

Chiweenies grow quite rapidly during their first few months. Most Chiweenies reach their full adult size between 9 and 12 months of age. This growth timeline is typical for small dog breeds. They gain most of their adult height and a good portion of their adult weight during this period. After six months, their growth slows down. By around 9 months, many Chiweenies have reached their full height. They may continue to gain a little weight and muscle density until they are 12 months old. Some Chiweenies, especially those leaning towards the larger end of the spectrum, might take a little longer to fully mature, perhaps until 15 months. However, this is less common.
