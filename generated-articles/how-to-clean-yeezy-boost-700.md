## Understanding Your Yeezy Boost 700 Materials

The Yeezy Boost 700 features a mix of materials. Each material needs a specific cleaning approach. Knowing these components helps you clean your shoes safely. Mesh, suede, leather, and the Boost sole are the main parts.

The shoe’s upper often includes breathable mesh sections. Mesh is durable but can trap dirt easily. It needs gentle cleaning to avoid damage. Too much pressure can stretch or tear the fabric.

Suede overlays are a key design element on the Yeezy Boost 700. Suede is a delicate material that can stain easily. Water can leave marks, so always use specialized suede brushes and cleaners. Rough cleaning can damage its soft texture.

Some Yeezy Boost 700 models also feature leather or synthetic leather parts. These sections are generally more resilient. They can withstand light scrubbing. However, harsh chemicals can dry out or crack the material.

The Boost sole provides comfort and a distinct look. It is made from expanded thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) pellets. This material is great for cushioning but can get dirty quickly. It also yellows over time, which requires specific attention.

The outsole is rubber and offers traction. This part is very durable. You can scrub it with more force. Knowing each material helps you choose the right cleaning tools and techniques.

## Gathering Your Cleaning Arsenal

Having the right tools makes cleaning your Yeezy Boost 700 much easier. Using incorrect products can damage your valuable sneakers. You need a few basic items to start your cleaning process. I always recommend using sneaker-specific cleaning products for best results.

First, you need a soft-bristled brush. This brush is perfect for the delicate mesh and suede parts. A medium-bristled brush works well for the Boost sole and outsole. Some people use old toothbrushes for tight spots.

Second, a mild cleaning solution is essential. You can buy specialized sneaker cleaners. Many brands offer effective and safe formulas. If you do not have a specific cleaner, a mix of mild soap and water can work. Always test any solution on a small, hidden area first.

Third, grab a few microfiber cloths. These cloths are highly absorbent and lint-free. They are good for wiping away dirt and excess solution. You will also use them to blot dry the shoes.

Fourth, a bowl of clean water is necessary for rinsing your brushes and cloths. This keeps dirt from spreading back onto the shoe. You might need two bowls: one for soapy water and one for clean water.

Fifth, consider a suede eraser and brush if your Yeezy 700s have prominent suede parts. These tools help remove marks without wetting the suede. ## Step-by-Step Guide: Cleaning the Yeezy Boost 700 Upper

Cleaning the upper part of your Yeezy Boost 700 requires gentle care. This section often has the most visible dirt. Follow these steps to clean it effectively without causing harm. My process focuses on safety and thoroughness.

### Pre-Clean Preparation

Start by removing the shoelaces. This allows you to clean all areas of the upper easily. You can clean the laces separately by hand washing them or placing them in a mesh bag for a gentle machine wash. Use a dry, soft-bristled brush to remove any loose dirt or debris from the entire shoe. Pay attention to crevices where dust collects.

### Cleaning the Mesh and Leather Sections

Mix a small amount of mild sneaker cleaning solution with water in a bowl. Dip your soft-bristled brush into the solution. Gently scrub the mesh and leather parts of the upper. Use small, circular motions. Do not press too hard. The goal is to lift dirt, not push it deeper.

After scrubbing, wipe the area with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. This removes the cleaning solution and lifted dirt. Repeat this process if necessary for heavily soiled areas. For other 700 models, like the Yeezy 700 Blue Tint, these steps are also effective. ### Treating Suede Overlays

Suede needs special attention. If your Yeezy Boost 700 has suede, use a suede eraser first. Gently rub the eraser over any dirty spots to lift surface stains. After using the eraser, brush the suede with a dry suede brush. Brush in one direction to restore the nap.

If water or a cleaning solution is absolutely necessary for tough suede stains, use it sparingly. Apply a tiny amount of specialized suede cleaner to a microfiber cloth. Dab the stain lightly, then immediately blot dry with another clean, dry cloth. Always avoid saturating suede with liquid. ## Revitalizing the Boost Sole and Outsole

The Boost sole is a signature feature of your Yeezy Boost 700. It provides comfort but can also attract dirt and show yellowing. Cleaning this part needs a different approach than the upper. I focus on removing grime and restoring its bright appearance.

Start by removing any loose dirt from the Boost sole. Use a medium-bristled brush or an old toothbrush. Scrape off dried mud or debris. This makes the deep cleaning process much easier. Pay close attention to the textured areas.

Next, prepare your cleaning solution. Mix a sneaker cleaner with water according to the product instructions. Dip your medium-bristled brush into the solution. Scrub the Boost sole with firm, but not aggressive, pressure. The Boost material can handle more scrubbing than the upper.

Work in small sections. Focus on getting into the small crevices of the Boost foam. The aim is to lift all the embedded dirt. For very stubborn marks, you might need a bit more cleaner or a slightly stiffer brush. This approach also applies to other Yeezy models with similar sole structures. The outsole, the rubber part on the bottom, is the most durable. Use your medium-bristled brush and cleaning solution to scrub it thoroughly. You can apply more pressure here. Rinse the outsole by wiping it with a damp cloth until clean. This final step brings back the sole's original grip and look.

## Tackling Stubborn Stains and Deep Cleaning

Sometimes, standard cleaning methods are not enough for tough stains. Your Yeezy Boost 700 can pick up challenging marks. This section provides solutions for those harder-to-remove spots. I focus on targeted treatments to restore your shoes.

For oil-based stains on mesh or leather, act quickly. Sprinkle a small amount of cornstarch or talcum powder directly onto the stain. Let it sit for a few hours or overnight. The powder helps absorb the oil. Then, gently brush off the powder. Follow up with your regular cleaning solution.

Mud stains can be tricky. Let the mud dry completely first. Then, gently brush off as much dry mud as possible. Do not rub wet mud, as this can spread the stain. After brushing, proceed with the standard cleaning steps. You might need to repeat the process a few times.

Discoloration or yellowing on the Boost sole is a common issue. You can try a mild whitening solution specifically designed for sneaker soles. Apply a thin layer of the solution to the yellowed Boost area. Wrap the sole in plastic wrap and place the shoe in direct sunlight for a few hours. The UV light activates the solution. Monitor it closely to avoid over-exposure. Rinse thoroughly afterward.

For very deep dirt, especially on mesh, a gentle spot cleaner can help. Apply a small amount directly to the stained area. Use a soft brush to work it in. Blot thoroughly with a clean, damp cloth. Always patch test any new product in an inconspicuous area first.

Remember that patience is key with stubborn stains. Sometimes, multiple light treatments are better than one aggressive attempt. Always prioritize the integrity of your shoe materials. ## Drying and Post-Cleaning Care

Proper drying is as important as cleaning for your Yeezy Boost 700. Incorrect drying can lead to damage or an unpleasant smell. After cleaning, you want to ensure your shoes dry completely and safely. My tips will help you avoid common mistakes.

First, remove any excess water. Gently blot the entire shoe with a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Press down firmly but carefully to absorb as much moisture as possible. Pay extra attention to the mesh and suede parts.

Next, stuff your Yeezy Boost 700s with paper towels or shoe trees. Paper towels help absorb internal moisture and maintain the shoe's shape. Change the paper towels every few hours if they become damp. Avoid using newspaper, as the ink can transfer to the shoe's interior.

Place your shoes in a well-ventilated area to air dry. A room with good airflow is ideal. Avoid direct sunlight, as strong UV rays can fade colors or yellow the Boost sole. Keep them away from direct heat sources like heaters or radiators. High heat can warp materials or damage adhesives.

Allow your shoes to dry for at least 24 hours. Depending on the humidity and how wet they got, it might take longer. Make sure they are completely dry before wearing them again. Wearing damp shoes can lead to mildew and odors.

Once dry, you can apply a protective spray. A water and stain repellent spray creates an invisible barrier. This helps protect the materials from future dirt and liquids. Spray evenly from a distance, then let them dry again. This step is a proactive measure for keeping your shoes clean longer. This process is good for most Yeezy styles, including slides. ## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Your Yeezy 700s

Cleaning your Yeezy Boost 700s incorrectly can do more harm than good. Many people make simple errors that damage their expensive sneakers. I want to highlight these common mistakes. Avoiding them ensures your shoes stay in great condition.

One major mistake is putting your Yeezy 700s in the washing machine. While some sneakers can handle it, the delicate materials and construction of the 700s are not suited for machine washing. It can cause materials to shrink, glue to weaken, and colors to bleed. Always hand wash these shoes.

Using harsh chemicals like bleach or strong detergents is another big no. These products can strip colors, dry out materials, and cause irreversible damage. Stick to mild sneaker-specific cleaners or gentle soap. Always test a small, hidden area first.

Scrubbing too aggressively, especially on suede and mesh, can ruin the material. Suede can become matted or discolored. Mesh can stretch or fray. Use soft brushes and gentle, circular motions. Let the cleaning solution do most of the work.

Not removing laces before cleaning is a common oversight. Leaving laces in makes it difficult to clean the tongue and eyelets thoroughly. It can also lead to uneven cleaning results. Always take them out first.

Improper drying is a frequent issue. Placing shoes in direct sunlight or using a hairdryer can damage the materials. Heat can warp the Boost sole or cause glue to fail. Always air dry your Yeezys in a cool, well-ventilated spot.

Ignoring manufacturer care instructions is also a mistake. While this guide offers general advice, always check any specific care tags that came with your shoes. These provide details for your exact model. By avoiding these common errors, you protect your investment and keep your Yeezys looking new.

## Maintaining Your Yeezy Boost 700s for Longevity

Keeping your Yeezy Boost 700s clean is an ongoing effort, not a one-time task. Regular maintenance helps extend their lifespan. By taking a few extra steps, you can keep them looking fresh between deep cleans. I believe consistent care is the key to longevity.

After each wear, quickly wipe down your shoes. Use a dry microfiber cloth to remove surface dust and light dirt. This prevents grime from setting in and becoming harder to remove later. A quick wipe takes only a minute.

If you encounter a small scuff or smudge, address it immediately. Do not let minor marks become major stains. A quick spot clean with a damp cloth can often do the trick. For suede, use a suede eraser right away.

Store your Yeezy Boost 700s properly when not wearing them. Keep them in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Using shoe trees helps maintain their shape and absorbs moisture. Original boxes can also protect them from dust.

Consider using a waterproof and stain repellent spray regularly. Reapply the spray every few weeks or months, depending on how often you wear your shoes. This creates a protective barrier against spills and dirt. It makes future cleaning much easier.

Rotate your sneakers. Wearing the same pair of shoes every day causes faster wear and tear. Give your Yeezys a break. This allows them to air out and recover, extending their life.

For persistent odors, use sneaker deodorizers or cedar shoe inserts. These absorb moisture and neutralize smells. This keeps your shoes fresh inside and out. Regular small efforts add up to big results for your Yeezy Boost 700s.

## FAQ Section

### Can I put Yeezy Boost 700s in the washing machine?
No, you should not put Yeezy Boost 700s in the washing machine. The machine's agitation can damage the delicate mesh, suede, and leather components. It can also weaken the adhesives and cause the Boost sole to warp. Hand washing is the safest and most effective method for cleaning these sneakers.

### How often should I clean my Yeezy Boost 700?
Clean your Yeezy Boost 700s as needed, depending on how often you wear them and where. For light dirt, a quick wipe down after each wear is good. A more thorough cleaning should be done every few weeks or months. This depends on how dirty they get. Spot clean stains immediately.

### What is the best cleaner for the Boost sole?
For the Boost sole, a mild sneaker cleaning solution mixed with water works best. You can also use a mixture of dish soap and water. Use a medium-bristled brush to scrub the textured surface. For yellowing, specific sole whitening solutions can help restore its original color.

### How do I protect my Yeezy 700s after cleaning?
After cleaning and drying your Yeezy 700s, apply a high-quality water and stain repellent spray. This creates a protective barrier against liquids and dirt. Reapply the spray periodically. Store your shoes in a cool, dry place with shoe trees or paper towels to maintain shape and absorb moisture.

### Can I use household cleaners on Yeezy 700s?
You should avoid using harsh household cleaners like bleach or strong detergents on your Yeezy 700s. These can damage materials, cause discoloration, or weaken adhesives. Stick to mild soaps, specialized sneaker cleaners, or natural alternatives like a baking soda paste for specific stains. Always patch test first.
