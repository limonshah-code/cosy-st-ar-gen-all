### Essential Dollar Tree Haul for Your Village

The first step in building your **elegant DIY Dollar Tree Christmas village** is shopping. Dollar Tree provides many treasures for this project. You need to know what to look for. Start with the miniature houses. They come in various styles like churches, homes, and shops. Pick a few that appeal to you. Do not worry about their original color. We will change that later. Next, search for small figurines. These might include people, animals, or even tiny cars.

Look for miniature trees. Dollar Tree often carries bottle brush trees or small plastic pine trees. These add height and texture to your village. You will also need "snow" elements. Consider cotton batting, iridescent shred, or even white glitter. These materials create a magical winter ground. Lighting is crucial for ambiance. Search for battery-operated fairy lights or tea lights. These will illuminate your village beautifully. Remember to grab craft supplies like paintbrushes, sponges, and glue.

Here is a quick shopping list to get you started:

*   **Miniature Houses:** Choose 4-6 diverse structures.
*   **Figurines:** People, animals, or small vehicles.
*   **Mini Trees:** Various sizes for depth.
*   **Faux Snow:** Cotton batting, glitter, or artificial snow.
*   **Lighting:** Battery-operated fairy lights, LED tea lights.
*   **Craft Supplies:**
    *   Acrylic craft paint (white, silver, gold, black, light gray).
    *   Paintbrushes and sponges.
    *   Hot glue gun and glue sticks.
    *   Mod Podge or clear sealant.
    *   Glitter
