## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Does Chihuahua Airport shut down overnight?
No, Chihuahua Airport (CUU) does not shut down overnight for aircraft operations. Its runways, air traffic control, and essential personnel operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. This ensures planes can land and take off at any time.

### Are all services at Chihuahua Airport open 24/7?
No, not all services are open 24/7. While the airport itself is always operational for flights, passenger services like airline check-in counters, retail stores, and most restaurants have specific operating hours. These hours typically align with scheduled flight times, opening a few hours before the first flight and closing after the last departure.

### What time do airline counters open at CUU?
Airline counters at Chihuahua Airport usually open two to three hours before a scheduled flight's departure. This allows ample time for passenger check-in and baggage drop-off. It is always best to verify the exact opening time with your specific airline, as this can vary.

### Can I stay overnight at Chihuahua Airport?
You can technically stay inside Chihuahua Airport overnight, as it operates 24/7. However, services are very limited during late-night and early-morning hours. Most shops and restaurants will be closed. Consider bringing snacks and a blanket if you plan to have an overnight layover.

### What should I do if my flight arrives very late or departs very early?
If your flight arrives very late or departs very early from Chihuahua Airport, plan your ground transportation in advance. Pre-book a taxi or arrange a pick-up, as public transport options may be limited. Also, consider bringing snacks or drinks, as most airport amenities will be closed during these off-peak hours.

### Is security open 24 hours at Chihuahua Airport?
Security checkpoints at Chihuahua Airport operate in conjunction with flight schedules. They open before the first scheduled flights of the day and close after the last flight has passed through. While not strictly 24/7 in terms of open lanes, security is always available when flights are active.
