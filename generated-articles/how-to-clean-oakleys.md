## How To Clean Oakleys: A Comprehensive Guide

My Oakley sunglasses are more than just eyewear. They protect my eyes and complete my style. Keeping them clean is essential. Dirty lenses can ruin your vision and scratch easily. You need the right methods to preserve their clarity and extend their life.

This guide helps you master the art of proper Oakley cleaning. We will cover the tools you need. We will walk through a step-by-step process for lenses and frames. We will also look at special care for different lens types. You will learn common mistakes to avoid. This article ensures your Oakleys always look and perform their best.

### Takeaway

*   Always rinse Oakleys with lukewarm water before wiping to remove loose debris.
*   Use Oakley-specific lens cleaner or mild dish soap with a microfiber cloth.
*   Avoid harsh chemicals, paper towels, clothing, or abrasive materials.
*   Clean frames and ear socks separately with gentle soap and water.
*   Store your Oakleys in their case when not in use to prevent damage.

### The Direct Answer

To clean Oakleys, first rinse them under lukewarm water to remove loose particles. Then, apply a small amount of Oakley lens cleaning solution or mild dish soap. Gently wipe the lenses and frames with a clean microfiber cloth. Rinse thoroughly and air dry or lightly dab with a fresh microfiber cloth.

### Essential Tools for Cleaning Your Oakleys

Having the right tools is the first step. You need gentle and effective supplies. These tools will clean your Oakleys without causing damage. I always keep these items handy. They make my cleaning routine simple and safe.

The most important tool is a good microfiber cloth. Oakley often provides one with your sunglasses. These cloths are soft. They are designed to pick up dust and oils. They do this without scratching delicate lens surfaces. Avoid using any other type of cloth. Paper towels, shirt sleeves, or tissues are too rough. They can leave tiny scratches. These scratches build up over time.

Next, you need a cleaning solution. Oakley makes its own lens cleaning spray. This spray is safe for all Oakley lenses and coatings. You can also use a very mild dish soap. Use only one drop of soap diluted in lukewarm water. Never use harsh household cleaners. Products like window cleaner contain ammonia. Ammonia can damage lens coatings and frame materials. It can make your lenses foggy or peel their protective layers.

Finally, access to lukewarm running water is crucial. Water helps rinse away loose debris. It also helps dilute your cleaning solution. Distilled water is ideal if your tap water is hard. Hard water can leave mineral spots. These spots are hard to remove later. Always use clean water for rinsing. These simple tools ensure your Oakleys stay pristine.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Oakley Lenses

Cleaning your Oakley lenses properly is a precise task. Follow these steps carefully to achieve streak-free clarity. This method protects your lenses from scratches and damage. I follow these steps every time I clean my sunglasses.

#### Initial Rinse to Remove Loose Debris

First, hold your Oakleys under lukewarm running water. Let the water flow over both sides of the lenses. This step is vital. It washes away dust, dirt, and sand particles. These particles are often invisible. If you rub them while dry, they will scratch your lenses. Ensure you rinse the entire lens surface thoroughly. Do not use hot water. Hot water can damage certain lens coatings or frame materials.

#### Applying the Cleaning Solution

After rinsing, you can apply your cleaner. If using Oakley lens spray, mist a small amount on each lens surface. If using mild dish soap, put one tiny drop on your finger. Then, gently rub it onto a wet lens. A single drop is enough for both lenses. Spread it evenly across the lens with your wet fingertip. Do not use too much soap. Too much soap can leave a residue.

#### Gentle Wiping with a Microfiber Cloth

Now, it is time to wipe the lenses. Take your clean, dry microfiber cloth. Gently wipe the lenses in a circular motion. Start from the center and move outwards. Apply light pressure only. Heavy pressure can spread dirt. It can also push particles into the lens. This might cause scratches. Flip the cloth to a clean section as needed. Ensure you remove all smudges and fingerprints.

#### Final Rinse and Drying

Rinse your lenses thoroughly again under lukewarm water. Make sure all cleaning solution is gone. Any leftover soap will leave streaks. Shake off excess water from the frames. You can let your Oakleys air dry completely. Or, you can gently dab them with a fresh, dry section of your microfiber cloth. Do not rub them hard. Avoid paper towels or tissues. They can scratch the lens or leave lint. Your lenses should now be perfectly clean and clear.

### Cleaning Oakley Frames and Ear Socks

Start by detaching any removable parts, if possible. Some Oakley models have removable nose pads or ear socks. This makes cleaning easier. If not, proceed with the parts attached. Use lukewarm water and a small amount of mild dish soap. Create a soapy water solution in a small bowl. Do not use harsh detergents or abrasive scrubbers. ### Specific Care for Different Oakley Lens Technologies

Oakley sunglasses feature many advanced lens technologies. Each type offers unique benefits. Understanding how to clean each one is important. Special coatings and materials require specific care. This ensures their performance and longevity. I always consider the lens type before cleaning.

#### Prizm Lenses

Prizm lenses are engineered to enhance color and contrast. They are delicate. Prizm technology often involves specific dyes embedded in the lens material. These lenses have various coatings. You must use the gentlest cleaning methods. Always rinse Prizm lenses with water first. Use an Oakley lens cleaner or mild soap. Wipe them with a clean microfiber cloth. Never use alcohol or ammonia-based cleaners. These chemicals can degrade the Prizm optics. They can also damage the specialized coatings. Treat Prizm lenses with extreme care.

#### Polarized Lenses

Polarized lenses reduce glare from reflective surfaces. They contain a special filter layer. This layer is very sensitive. Harsh chemicals can delaminate this filter. This means the layers can separate. This will ruin the lens. Stick to water and Oakley-approved cleaners. Avoid very hot water. High temperatures can also damage the polarizing film. Gentle wiping is key. Ensure no solvents come into contact with the lens edge where the layers meet. Protecting this filter is crucial for glare reduction.

#### Iridium Coatings

Many Oakley lenses feature an Iridium coating. This is a mirrored layer applied to the lens surface. Iridium coatings are quite durable. However, they can still scratch. They can also be damaged by improper cleaning. Abrasive materials will scratch the mirror finish. Alcohol or ammonia can dissolve the coating over time. Always use the recommended Oakley microfiber cloth. Use only water or Oakley lens cleaner. When wiping, apply minimal pressure. This preserves the reflective quality and aesthetic of your Iridium lenses. Consistent gentle care keeps these lenses looking vibrant.

#### Photochromic (Transitions) Lenses

Photochromic lenses adjust their tint based on UV light. They have a special chemical process within the lens. Like other Oakley lenses, they require gentle care. Avoid harsh chemicals. These can interfere with the photochromic properties. This might stop the lenses from changing tint correctly. Always rinse thoroughly. Use a soft microfiber cloth for drying. Proper cleaning ensures the lenses continue to react to light as intended. This maintains their versatility and performance.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Oakleys

Many people unknowingly damage their Oakleys during cleaning. Avoiding these common mistakes is crucial. Incorrect methods can scratch lenses. They can also strip coatings. This reduces the lifespan and performance of your expensive eyewear. I have learned to steer clear of these errors myself.

#### Using Your Shirt or Paper Towels

This is perhaps the most common mistake. Your shirt, even if it feels soft, contains fibers. These fibers are coarser than a microfiber cloth. They can carry dust and tiny debris. Rubbing your lenses with your shirt can create micro-scratches. Over time, these scratches accumulate. They make your lenses appear cloudy. Paper towels are even worse. They are made from wood pulp. This material is very abrasive. It will scratch your lenses easily. Always use a dedicated microfiber cloth for lens wiping. This prevents irreversible damage.

#### Employing Harsh Chemicals

Household cleaners are not suitable for Oakley lenses. Window cleaners, ammonia-based sprays, or solvents contain harsh chemicals. These chemicals can dissolve or degrade specialized lens coatings. They can cause hazing, peeling, or a rainbow effect. This damage is permanent. Alcohol, though sometimes used for cleaning, can also be too strong for certain Oakley coatings. Stick to Oakley-specific lens cleaner. Or use a very small amount of mild dish soap diluted in water. Read the labels carefully. Ensure the cleaner is safe for coated optics.

#### Using Hot Water or Steaming Hot Environments

Extreme temperatures are bad for Oakley lenses and frames. Hot water can cause certain lens coatings to delaminate. It can also warp plastic frames. Steaming hot environments, like a car dashboard on a sunny day, can have a similar effect. Always use lukewarm or cool water for rinsing. Never leave your Oakleys in places with high heat. This includes saunas, hot tubs, or direct sunlight in a closed car. Such heat exposure is a surefire way to damage your glasses.

#### Wiping Dry Lenses

Always rinse your Oakleys before wiping them. Dust and dirt particles sit on dry lenses. Wiping these dry lenses causes the particles to scrape against the surface. This is a guaranteed way to create scratches. A quick rinse under lukewarm water washes these particles away. This prepares the lens for a safe wipe. This simple step prevents significant damage. Make it a habit to rinse first.

#### Ignoring Frame and Hinge Cleaning

Focusing only on lenses is another mistake. Frames, ear socks, and hinges also collect grime. Sweat, skin oils, and dirt can build up. This buildup can degrade frame materials. It can make ear socks sticky. It can also stiffen hinges. Regular, gentle cleaning of the entire frame prevents this. Use a soft cloth and mild soapy water for frames. Pay attention to hinges and nose pads. This ensures your entire pair of Oakleys stays in top condition.

It seems obvious, but it is easy to forget. Never place your Oakleys face down on any surface. The lenses are the most vulnerable part. Even a seemingly clean table can have tiny abrasive particles. ### Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning Oakleys

#### Q1: Can I use Windex or glass cleaner on my Oakley lenses?
No, never use Windex or other glass cleaners. These products often contain ammonia. Ammonia can severely damage Oakley's specialized lens coatings. It can cause hazing, peeling, or even permanently distort your vision. Stick to Oakley-approved cleaners or mild dish soap.

#### Q2: How often should I clean my Oakley sunglasses?
The frequency depends on your usage. If you wear them daily, a quick rinse and wipe might be needed every day. A thorough cleaning, including frames, should happen once a week or whenever they appear visibly dirty. Consistent gentle cleaning is better than infrequent harsh cleaning.

#### Q3: What if I get scratches on my Oakley lenses? Can I remove them?
Unfortunately, most scratches on Oakley lenses are permanent. They cannot be polished out without damaging the lens coatings. Prevention is key. If scratches are significant and impair vision, replacing the lenses is often the best option. Oakley sells replacement lenses for many models.

#### Q4: Is it safe to wash my Oakley lenses under tap water?
Yes, it is safe and recommended to rinse your Oakley lenses under lukewarm tap water. This removes loose dirt and debris before wiping. Always use lukewarm water, not hot water. Hard water might leave mineral spots, so distilled water is ideal if you have very hard tap water.

#### Q5: What is the best type of cloth to use for cleaning Oakley lenses?
The best cloth to use is a clean, lint-free microfiber cloth. Oakley usually provides one with your sunglasses. These cloths are specially designed to clean delicate lens surfaces effectively without scratching. Avoid using paper towels, shirt sleeves, or tissues, as they can cause damage.

#### Q6: Can I use rubbing alcohol to disinfect my Oakley frames?
While rubbing alcohol can disinfect, it's generally best to avoid it, especially near lenses or rubberized frame parts. Alcohol can degrade some frame finishes and can be too harsh for lens coatings. For frames, mild soap and water are safer and equally effective for cleaning.
