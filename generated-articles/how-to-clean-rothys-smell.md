## Clean Rothys Smell: The Definitive Guide to Freshness

We all love Rothy's for their style and comfort. These shoes are perfect for everyday wear. However, even the best shoes can develop an unpleasant odor over time. That distinctive Rothy's smell can be a real issue.

You are not alone if you wonder how to clean Rothys smell. This guide gives you proven strategies to restore your shoes to fresh perfection. We cover everything from understanding the causes of shoe odor to deep cleaning methods and daily maintenance. You will learn the best ways to keep your Rothy's smelling clean and feeling great.

### Takeaway

*   Machine wash your Rothy's in cold water with mild detergent for a deep clean.
*   Always air dry your Rothy's completely to prevent new odors.
*   Use natural odor absorbers like baking soda between washes.
*   Wear socks and rotate your shoes to prevent smells from building up.

### How to Clean Rothys Smell: Quick Answer

To effectively clean Rothys smell, machine wash them on a cold, gentle cycle with a mild detergent and a laundry bag. Remove insoles before washing. Air dry your Rothy's completely in a well-ventilated area, away from direct heat.

## Understanding Why Your Rothy's Develop a Smell

Rothy's shoes are fantastic for daily life. They are made from recycled plastic bottles, giving them a unique knit fabric. This material is comfortable and flexible. However, these features also make them prone to developing a certain smell. Understanding the root causes helps in effective cleaning.

Our feet sweat, producing moisture inside the shoes. This warm, damp environment becomes a breeding ground for bacteria. Bacteria feed on dead skin cells and sweat. Their metabolic byproducts create the unpleasant odors we notice. This process happens in all shoes, but Rothy's unique construction can sometimes trap moisture more effectively. The dense knit fabric, while breathable, still holds onto some moisture. This can make the Rothy's smell more noticeable after extended wear.

Many people wear Rothy's without socks. This direct skin contact with the shoe lining increases sweat and bacteria transfer. Over time, this builds up, contributing to a persistent smell. Frequent wear without proper drying time also compounds the issue. The shoes never fully dry out between uses. Dampness promotes more bacterial growth. It is a cycle that needs interruption to maintain freshness.

Proper care starts with knowing why the smell occurs. Preventing moisture buildup and regular cleaning are key. We must address both the existing smell and the conditions that cause it. This keeps your Rothy's pleasant to wear for a long time.

## Preparing Your Rothy's for an Odor-Busting Wash

Before you toss your Rothy's into the washing machine, a little preparation goes a long way. This ensures a more effective clean and better odor removal. Taking these steps seriously makes the cleaning process easier. It also helps your shoes last longer. We want to clean Rothys smell completely.

First, remove the insoles from your Rothy's. Most Rothy's insoles are also machine washable, but separating them helps both parts dry faster. It also allows water and detergent to reach all surfaces inside the shoe. This targets odor-causing bacteria more directly. I find this step crucial for a deep clean.

Next, gently shake out any loose dirt, debris, or small pebbles from inside the shoes. You can also use a soft brush or a dry cloth to wipe away any visible grime on the outside. This prevents dirt from redepositing during the wash cycle. It also keeps your washing machine cleaner. If you have light-colored Rothy's, like white ones, this initial step is especially important to prevent staining during the wash. You can find more tips on keeping light-colored Rothy's clean by reading our guide on [how to clean white Rothys](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-white-rothys).

For any stubborn spots or stains, consider a quick spot treatment. Use a small amount of mild detergent or a stain remover diluted with water. Gently rub the area with a soft cloth or toothbrush. Let it sit for a few minutes before washing. This pre-treatment helps break down tough dirt. It ensures your shoes come out looking their best. Following these steps helps prepare your shoes for the wash. This makes the odor removal process much more successful.

## The Best Way to Machine Wash Rothy's for Smell Removal

Machine washing is the most effective method to clean Rothys smell deeply. Rothy's are designed to be machine washable, which is a huge convenience. However, using the correct technique is vital to protect the shoes and ensure thorough cleaning. Do not just throw them in with your regular laundry.

Start by placing your Rothy's into a mesh laundry bag. This bag protects the shoes from harsh tumbling against the washing machine drum. It also prevents them from getting tangled with other clothes. This gentle handling preserves the shape and knit of your shoes. This step is a small effort for a big benefit.

Select a cold water cycle on your washing machine. Cold water is gentler on the fabric. It helps prevent any potential shrinking or color fading. Avoid hot water, as it can damage the recycled plastic fibers. Choose a gentle cycle setting. This mimics hand washing, giving your Rothy's a thorough but delicate clean. A mild, eco-friendly detergent is also important. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, or fabric softeners. These can break down the fabric or leave residues that attract more dirt. For detailed instructions on gentle cleaning, you can refer to our guide on [how to clean Rothys by hand](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-rothys-by-hand), which shares principles applicable to gentle machine washing.

Once the cycle finishes, immediately remove your Rothy's from the washing machine. Leaving them in a damp environment can encourage mildew growth and new odors. The key to truly clean Rothys smell is not just the wash, but also the drying process. Prepare them for air drying right away. This quick action prevents any further odor development.

## Effective Air Drying Techniques to Prevent Odor Return

Proper drying is just as crucial as washing when you clean Rothys smell. If your Rothy's do not dry completely, residual moisture creates an ideal environment for bacteria and mildew to thrive. This causes the unpleasant smell to return quickly. You want your shoes to be bone dry.

Always air dry your Rothy's. Never put them in the dryer. The high heat from a dryer can warp the shoe's shape. It can also damage the recycled plastic fibers. This irreversible damage would ruin your favorite shoes. Patience during the drying process is key.

Find a well-ventilated area for drying. A spot with good airflow, away from direct sunlight or intense heat, is ideal. You can place them on a drying rack or hang them up. Make sure air circulates around and inside the shoes. This helps moisture evaporate evenly from all surfaces. Direct sunlight can fade colors over time, so keep them in a shaded, airy spot.

To speed up the drying process and absorb internal moisture, you can stuff your Rothy's with plain white paper towels or newspaper. Change these absorbent materials every few hours. They draw out dampness from the shoe's interior. This helps dry the shoes from the inside out. Ensure the paper you use is plain to avoid ink transfer. Allow at least 24-48 hours for your Rothy's to dry completely. This might seem like a long time, but it ensures no hidden moisture remains. Checking that they are completely dry before wearing prevents new odors.

## Hand Washing Rothy's for Stubborn Smells and Delicates

Sometimes, hand washing is a better option for your Rothy's. This might be true for very delicate styles or if you have specific stains to target. It is also an excellent choice if you prefer a more hands-on approach. Hand washing gives you precise control over the cleaning process. This helps you clean Rothys smell without risking machine damage.

First, gather your supplies. You will need a basin or sink, cool water, a mild detergent, and a soft brush or cloth. Remove the insoles, as you would for machine washing. Gently tap the shoes together to remove loose dirt. This prepares them for a thorough clean. Taking your time here makes a big difference.

Next, fill your basin with cool water and add a small amount of mild detergent. Submerge your Rothy's and their insoles in the soapy water. Let them soak for about 15-20 minutes. This helps loosen dirt and start breaking down odor-causing bacteria. For tough smells, you might add a tablespoon of white vinegar to the water. Vinegar is a natural deodorizer and disinfectant. It can work wonders against persistent shoe odors. For general shoe odor concerns, similar methods can be effective, as discussed in our guide on [how to clean boots that smell](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-boots-that-smell).

After soaking, use your soft brush or cloth to gently scrub the shoes. Pay special attention to any stained areas and the inside lining. Work the soapy water into the fabric. Rinse the shoes and insoles thoroughly under cool running water. Squeeze out excess water without twisting or wringing the shoes. You want to remove all soap residue. Any remaining soap can attract more dirt later. Once rinsed, follow the air-drying steps described in the previous section. This ensures your hand-washed Rothy's dry completely and stay fresh. ## Natural Odor Eliminators and Quick Fixes for Rothy's

Sometimes you need a quick fix between washes, or your Rothy's have a persistent odor. Natural solutions can be very effective at neutralizing smells. These methods are safe for your shoes and environmentally friendly. They help you maintain fresh Rothy's without constant washing.

Baking soda is a powerful natural deodorizer. It absorbs odors rather than just masking them. Sprinkle a tablespoon or two of baking soda inside each shoe. Let it sit overnight, or even for 24-48 hours. The longer it sits, the more odor it absorbs. After the time passes, simply shake out the baking soda. You can also use a small vacuum cleaner attachment to remove any residue. This simple trick makes a big difference.

White vinegar is another excellent odor combatant. Its acetic acid kills bacteria and neutralizes odors. Mix equal parts white vinegar and water in a spray bottle. Lightly mist the inside of your Rothy's. Do not oversaturate them. Let the shoes air dry completely afterward. The vinegar smell dissipates as it dries, leaving your shoes fresh. This method is great for a quick refresh.

Other options include activated charcoal inserts or cedar shoe inserts. Activated charcoal is highly porous and absorbs odors and moisture. You can buy small charcoal sachets made for shoes. Cedar wood also has natural deodorizing and moisture-absorbing properties. Placing these inserts into your Rothy's when you are not wearing them helps maintain freshness. Even used, dry tea bags (especially black tea) can absorb odors and leave a faint, pleasant scent. Place a few in each shoe overnight. These quick fixes help you manage the Rothy's smell effectively. They keep your shoes wearable and pleasant.

## Long-Term Strategies to Keep Your Rothy's Smelling Fresh

Preventing the Rothys smell from developing is always better than cleaning it. Good shoe hygiene and regular maintenance keep your Rothy's fresh for a longer time. Incorporate these habits into your routine. This will extend the life of your shoes and ensure they always smell good.

One of the simplest strategies is to wear socks with your Rothy's. Socks absorb sweat and reduce direct skin contact with the shoe lining. This significantly lessens the amount of moisture and bacteria that build up inside. Even thin, no-show socks make a big difference. They act as a barrier. This small change can prevent much of the odor.

Rotate your Rothy's with other shoes. Do not wear the same pair day after day. Giving your Rothy's at least 24 hours to air out between wears is crucial. This allows them to dry completely, even if you do not wash them. Moisture is the enemy of freshness. A fully dry shoe is a less smelly shoe. Having a few pairs to rotate means each pair gets the rest it needs.

Proper storage also plays a role in preventing odors. Store your Rothy's in a cool, dry, and well-ventilated area. Avoid stuffing them into a dark, damp closet immediately after wearing. You can use shoe trees made of cedar wood. Cedar naturally absorbs moisture and imparts a fresh scent. Odor-absorbing sachets or baking soda packets can also be placed in shoes when stored. Regularly inspecting your Rothy's for any signs of odor and addressing them quickly helps. This proactive approach ensures your Rothy's remain a joy to wear, without any unwelcome smells.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning Rothy's Smell

### Can I put Rothy's in the dryer?

No, you must never put Rothy's in the dryer. The high heat can warp the shoe's shape and damage the recycled plastic fibers. Always air dry your Rothy's completely in a well-ventilated area. This preserves their structure and prevents damage.

### How often should I wash my Rothy's to prevent smell?

The frequency depends on how often you wear them and your activity level. For regular wear, washing them every few weeks or once a month is a good general guideline. If you wear them daily or without socks, more frequent washing, perhaps every 1-2 weeks, is better.

### Are Rothy's insoles machine washable?

Yes, Rothy's insoles are typically machine washable. It is best to remove them from the shoes and wash them separately in the same mesh laundry bag. This ensures both the shoes and insoles get a thorough cleaning. Allow them to air dry completely.

### What if the smell persists after washing?

If the smell persists, try a more aggressive approach with natural deodorizers. Soak the shoes in a white vinegar and water solution before washing. Alternatively, use generous amounts of baking soda and let it sit for 24-48 hours. Ensure they dry completely.

### Can I use bleach on white Rothy's?

Avoid using bleach on any Rothy's, including white ones. Bleach can damage the fabric and cause discoloration. Instead, use a mild, color-safe detergent. For stubborn stains on white Rothy's, a baking soda paste can often work wonders as a gentle brightener.

### Does wearing socks help prevent Rothy's smell?

Yes, wearing socks significantly helps prevent Rothy's from smelling. Socks absorb sweat and create a barrier between your foot and the shoe lining. This reduces moisture buildup and the growth of odor-causing bacteria. Even thin, no-show socks are effective.
