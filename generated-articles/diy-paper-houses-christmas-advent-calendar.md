## Assembling Your Charming Paper Village

Once you have your designs or templates printed, assembly begins. Carefully cut out each house template. Use sharp scissors for clean edges. A craft knife and a ruler help with straight lines and precise cuts. Precision makes folding easier and results in neater houses. Take your time with this step to avoid mistakes.

Score along all fold lines before folding. Scoring creates a crease in the paper. This allows for sharp, crisp folds. A scoring tool or the back of a butter knife works well. Run the tool along the fold lines using a ruler as a guide. This step is essential for professional-looking paper houses. Without scoring, your houses might look messy or uneven.

Apply glue to the designated tabs on your template. Use a strong, fast-drying craft glue. A glue stick is also effective for smaller tabs. Press the tabs firmly together to secure the house shape. Hold them in place for a few seconds until the glue sets. Repeat this process for each side of the house. You can also use different techniques to [make DIY outdoor Christmas planters](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-make-diy-outdoor-christmas-planters), adding more DIY flair to your holiday decor.

Assemble all 24 (or desired number) houses. Let them dry completely before moving to the next step. This prevents them from falling apart. Set them aside on a flat surface. You want to ensure they hold their shape perfectly. Building your paper village takes patience, but the result is truly rewarding.

## Making It Reusable and Sustainable

Making your Diy Paper Houses Christmas Advent Calendar reusable is smart. This saves time and resources in future years. To ensure durability, use sturdy cardstock from the start. Thicker paper withstands more handling. Reinforce corners and folds with extra glue if needed. A little extra care during assembly makes a big difference.

Consider how you will open the houses. Small flaps or removable roofs work well. Avoid designs that require tearing. Tearing damages the house and makes it unusable next year. You can use tiny magnets or Velcro dots to secure the openings. This allows for easy access and re-closing. Ensure these closures are strong enough to hold treats.

Proper storage is key for reuse. Once Christmas is over, empty your houses. Carefully fold down any flexible parts. Store the houses in a sturdy box or container. Keep them in a dry, safe place. Label the box clearly so you find them easily next year. A dedicated storage box prevents crushing or damage.

Repair any minor damages before storing them away. A small tear can be fixed with tape or glue. Repainting faded areas helps maintain their fresh look. Taking care of repairs ensures your advent calendar looks great for many holidays. This sustainable approach helps reduce waste. You can enjoy your beautiful creation year after year.

## FAQ Section

### How long does it take to make a DIY paper house advent calendar?
Making a Diy Paper Houses Christmas Advent Calendar generally takes several hours. The exact time depends on the complexity of your designs. It also depends on your crafting speed. Cutting and folding 24 houses precisely takes patience. You can spread the project over a few days. This makes it a relaxing, enjoyable holiday craft.

### What is the best type of paper for advent calendar houses?
The best paper for advent calendar houses is heavy cardstock. Cardstock provides durability and structure. It prevents the houses from collapsing easily. Look for paper weights between 65 lb and 110 lb. Patterned cardstock adds visual interest. Plain white cardstock allows for more personal decoration.

### Can kids help make a paper house advent calendar?
Yes, kids can definitely help make a Diy Paper Houses Christmas Advent Calendar. Younger children can help with decorating. They can color, glue glitter, or add stickers. Older children can help with cutting and folding. Supervise them with scissors or craft knives. Making the calendar together creates fun family memories.

### What are some unique fillers besides candy for the houses?
Beyond candy, unique fillers include small notes with positive affirmations or jokes. You can add tiny toy figures, stickers, or temporary tattoos. Small craft supplies, like mini erasers or beads, are also popular. Activity prompts, like "bake cookies" or "read a Christmas story," create lasting experiences.

### How can I make my paper houses stand upright securely?
To make paper houses stand upright securely, use sturdy cardstock. Ensure all glue tabs are firmly sealed during assembly. You can add a small weight inside the base of each house, like a coin. Arranging them closely together on a flat, stable surface also helps. A rigid display base prevents wobbling.

### Is it possible to use a cutting machine for this project?
Yes, using a cutting machine like a Cricut or Silhouette is perfect for this project. Digital cutting machines cut paper house templates quickly and precisely. You can download or design SVG files for the house shapes. This saves significant time and effort compared to hand-cutting. It ensures uniform houses with clean edges.
