## Understanding Bil Jac's Unique Production Process

Bil Jac stands out in the pet food market due to its unique manufacturing process. Most dry dog foods use high-temperature extrusion. This process can degrade some nutrients. Bil Jac uses a different method. They call it their "fresh, slow-cooked" process. This approach is central to their brand identity.

The process begins with fresh chicken. This fresh meat is the first ingredient in many of their formulas. They then combine it with other ingredients. This mixture cooks slowly at low temperatures. After cooking, they vacuum dry the food. This special drying method removes moisture gently. It helps to preserve the delicate nutrients in the ingredients.

This gentle handling aims to maintain the integrity of vitamins, amino acids, and fats. The company believes this method makes the food more digestible. It also aims to improve the food's taste. Dogs often find vacuum-dried kibble very appealing. This could be why many picky eaters seem to enjoy Bil Jac.

The result is a soft, crumbly kibble. It is different from the hard, crunchy kibble of many other brands. This texture is another benefit of their unique process. It may be easier for some dogs to chew and swallow. Older dogs or those with dental issues might find this texture more comfortable. This distinct production method is a key selling point for Bil Jac. It sets them apart from typical dry dog food manufacturers.
