## Sew A DIY Reusable Snack Bag: Your Guide to Eco-Friendly Snacking

Do you pack lunches or snacks often? Single-use plastic bags create a lot of waste. Imagine cutting down on that waste while saving money. Learning to **sew a DIY reusable snack bag** lets you do exactly that.

These handmade bags are better for the planet. They are also gentle on your wallet. Plus, you get to choose fun fabrics. This guide will show you how to create your own reusable snack bags. You will learn about materials, simple steps, and care tips. Join me in making a positive change, one stitch at a time.

### Takeaway

*   Making your own reusable snack bags reduces plastic waste.
*   DIY snack bags save money over time compared to disposable options.
*   You can customize your bags with unique fabrics and designs.
*   Sewing a reusable snack bag is a simple project for all skill levels.

### Main Query Answer

You can sew a DIY reusable snack bag by selecting food-safe fabric, cutting two rectangles for the outer and lining layers, and stitching them together with a closure like a zipper or hook-and-loop tape. This simple process creates a custom, eco-friendly container for your snacks.

## Essential Materials for Your DIY Snack Bag

Before you **sew a DIY reusable snack bag**, gather your materials. Having everything ready makes the process smooth. I always lay out my supplies before I begin any project.

You will need two main types of fabric. The outer fabric can be cotton, canvas, or even a fun quilting fabric. Choose something durable and washable. This fabric will be the visible part of your bag. It should withstand regular use and washing.

The lining fabric is very important. It needs to be food-safe and water-resistant. PUL fabric (polyurethane laminate) is a popular choice. It is waterproof and thin. Another option is food-safe laminated cotton. Waxed canvas also works, but it needs different care. Ensure your chosen lining is free of harmful chemicals. You can learn more about specific fabric properties when looking into materials like those used for [best fabric for reusable paper towels](https://cosyhomecreation.com/best-fabric-for-reusable-paper-towels).

For the closure, you have options. A zipper is very secure. Hook-and-loop tape (like Velcro) is easy for children to use. Snap buttons or even a simple fold-over flap with a button can also work. Pick a closure that fits your needs. I usually prefer zippers for maximum freshness.

Basic sewing supplies are also necessary. You will need a sewing machine, thread that matches your fabric, sharp fabric scissors, and pins or fabric clips. An iron and ironing board are also helpful for crisp seams. Having these tools makes sewing easier and more accurate.

## Preparing Your Fabric and Pattern Pieces

Proper preparation ensures a successful sewing project. This step is crucial before you **sew a DIY reusable snack bag**. Taking your time here will lead to a better finished product.

First, always pre-wash and iron your fabrics. Fabric can shrink when washed for the first time. Pre-washing prevents your finished bag from shrinking unevenly later. Ironing removes wrinkles. It gives you a smooth surface to cut and sew. This step is a small effort that makes a big difference. If you need tips on ironing, consider checking out how to [sew an easy DIY ironing board cover](https://cosyhomecreation.com/sew-an-easy-diy-ironing-board-cover).

Next, decide on your bag's dimensions. A common snack bag size is about 7x7 inches. A sandwich bag might be 7x9 inches. Consider what snacks you will carry most often. You can make a small bag for nuts or a larger one for a sandwich. I usually make a few different sizes to fit various needs.

Creating a pattern is simple. You can draw rectangles directly onto your fabric. Alternatively, make a paper template. This ensures consistent sizes if you plan to make multiple bags. Remember to add a seam allowance of about 3/8 to 1/2 inch around all edges. This extra fabric gives you room for sewing the seams.

Finally, cut your outer and lining fabrics. You will need two pieces for the outer bag and two for the lining. Cut them precisely according to your chosen dimensions. Accuracy here helps the pieces fit together well. Use sharp scissors or a rotary cutter for clean edges.

## Step-by-Step Sewing Your Snack Bag

Now comes the fun part: sewing your bag. Follow these steps carefully to **sew a DIY reusable snack bag**. This process is straightforward, even for beginners. I break it down into manageable parts.

### Attaching the Closure

Start by attaching your chosen closure to the top edge of your outer fabric pieces. If using a zipper, place the zipper right side down along the top edge of one outer fabric piece. Align the edges. Pin it in place. Then, place one lining fabric piece right side down on top of the zipper, aligning all raw edges. Sew these layers together using a zipper foot. Repeat this for the other side of the zipper with the remaining outer and lining pieces. Make sure your fabric is straight.

If you are using hook-and-loop tape, attach one side of the tape to the top edge of each outer fabric piece. Place the tape about 1/2 inch from the edge. Stitch it firmly in place. Repeat this for both outer pieces. For more general sewing tips that can help, consider looking at a guide on [how to sew a reversible tote bag](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-sew-a-reversible-tote-bag).

### Sewing Outer and Lining Layers

Once your closure is attached, open up your fabric. Press the seams flat away from the zipper or hook-and-loop tape. Topstitch along the zipper edge on both sides. This makes the bag look neat. It also prevents fabric from getting caught in the zipper.

Next, you will sew the outer fabric pieces together. Place the two outer fabric pieces right sides together. Pin along the three unsewn sides (bottom and two sides). Sew these edges, leaving the top open. Repeat this process for the lining fabric pieces. Make sure to leave a 3-inch opening along the bottom edge of the *lining* fabric. This opening is for turning the bag right side out later.

### Creating the Boxed Corners (Optional)

Boxed corners give your snack bag more depth. This step is optional but adds a nice professional finish. To create boxed corners, flatten each bottom corner of your outer bag. Line up the side seam with the bottom seam. This forms a triangle. Measure about 1 inch from the tip of the triangle and draw a line. Sew along this line. Trim the excess fabric. Repeat this for all four corners (two outer, two lining).

### Turning and Finishing

Now, turn the outer bag right side out. Keep the lining bag inside out. Place the outer bag inside the lining bag, right sides together. Match up the top raw edges where the closure is. Pin all around the top. Sew around this opening, securing the outer bag to the lining bag. Leave a small gap if your closure wasn't fully attached.

Reach into the opening you left in the lining fabric. Pull the entire bag right side out through this opening. Push out all the corners. Press the seams flat. Hand stitch or machine stitch the opening in the lining closed. Finally, topstitch around the entire top edge of your snack bag. This gives it a clean finish.

## Care and Maintenance for Longevity

To ensure your DIY reusable snack bags last, proper care is essential. Knowing how to clean them keeps them hygienic and extends their life. I always make sure to follow care instructions for my handmade items.

Washing instructions depend on your lining material. For PUL fabric linings, most bags can be machine washed on a gentle cycle. Use cold water. Hot water can damage the PUL's waterproof layer over time. Avoid harsh detergents or bleach. These can also degrade the fabric.

If you used waxed canvas for your lining, machine washing is not recommended. Waxed canvas loses its wax coating in the wash. Instead, wipe waxed canvas bags clean with a damp cloth. For tougher spots, use a mild soap and a brush. Re-waxing might be needed eventually.

Drying tips are also important. Air drying is best for most reusable snack bags. Hang them open to dry completely. This prevents mold or mildew from forming, especially inside the lining. If you use a dryer, use a low heat setting. High heat can damage PUL and other delicate linings.

Troubleshooting common issues helps too. If stains appear, treat them quickly. For food stains, a little dish soap and water often work. For bags that start to smell, a sprinkle of baking soda inside overnight can absorb odors. Then, just shake it out or rinse. Keeping your bags clean and dry will ensure they are ready for their next use. ## FAQ Section

### What fabric is best for the lining of a reusable snack bag?

PUL fabric is best for the lining of a reusable snack bag. It is waterproof, food-safe, and easy to clean. Food-safe laminated cotton is another good option. These fabrics create a barrier that keeps food fresh. They prevent leaks. Always check that the fabric is certified food-safe.

### Can I put my DIY snack bag in the washing machine?

Yes, you can machine wash most DIY snack bags. Use a gentle cycle with cold water. Avoid bleach and harsh detergents. Air drying is recommended. This helps preserve the waterproof lining. High heat in a dryer can damage the fabric's integrity over time.

### How do I make a snack bag waterproof?

To make a snack bag waterproof, use a water-resistant fabric for the lining. PUL fabric is an excellent choice for this. It has a laminated layer that repels water. Food-safe laminated cotton also provides a waterproof barrier. Ensure all seams are stitched well to prevent leaks.

### What size should I make my reusable snack bag?

The size depends on what snacks you carry. A common snack bag size is 7x7 inches. For sandwiches, 7x9 inches works well. You can make smaller bags for nuts or larger ones for multiple items. Measure your typical snacks to determine ideal dimensions.

### Are these bags safe for all types of food?

Yes, DIY reusable snack bags are safe for most foods. Use food-safe lining fabrics like PUL or laminated cotton. Avoid direct contact with very hot foods. Hot foods can degrade certain linings. Also, be sure to clean your bags regularly to maintain hygiene and freshness.

### Can beginners sew a reusable snack bag?

Absolutely, beginners can easily sew a reusable snack bag. The basic shape is a simple rectangle. The steps involve straight stitching. Instructions for zipper or hook-and-loop tape closures are easy to follow. This project is a great way to practice basic sewing skills.
