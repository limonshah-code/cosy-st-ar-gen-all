### Slow and Steady Transition Tips

Switching your dog's food should be a gradual process. This minimizes digestive upset. A typical transition period is 7 to 10 days.

Here is a simple plan:
*   **Days 1-2:** Mix 25% new food with 75% old food.
*   **Days 3-4:** Use a 50/50 mix of new and old food.
*   **Days 5-6:** Mix 75% new food with 25% old food.
*   **Days 7-10:** Feed 100% new food.

Monitor your Chihuahua during this time. Watch for any changes in appetite or stool consistency. If you notice digestive upset, slow down the transition. Extend each stage for a few more days. Some Chihuahuas are picky; you might find [adding a little water to dry dog food](https://cosyhomecreation.com/is-it-good-to-add-a-little-water-to-dry-dog-food) can help with palatability during transition.
