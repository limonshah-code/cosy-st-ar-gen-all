## Storage and Preparation of Nom Nom Meals

Proper storage and preparation are essential for Nom Nom dog food. Since it is fresh, it requires refrigeration or freezing. This ensures the food remains safe and palatable for your dog. Understanding these steps helps maintain the food's quality.

When your Nom Nom order arrives, immediately transfer the food to your refrigerator or freezer. Each meal comes in individual, pre-portioned packages. This makes storage simple. If stored in the refrigerator, the food stays fresh for up to seven days. For longer storage, freeze the unopened packages. Frozen meals remain good for up to six months.

To prepare a meal, simply thaw a package if it's frozen. You can do this in the refrigerator overnight. For quicker thawing, place the sealed package in a bowl of cold water for a few hours. Once thawed, the food is ready to serve. Just open the package and pour it into your dog's bowl. There is no need for cooking or additional steps.

The pre-portioned design removes any guesswork. Each package contains one meal or a daily portion, depending on your dog's specific plan. This helps ensure your dog receives the exact amount of food calculated for their needs. This level of convenience makes feeding fresh food much easier. It also helps manage your dog's weight effectively.
