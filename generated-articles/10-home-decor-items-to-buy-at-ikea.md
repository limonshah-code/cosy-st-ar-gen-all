### 4. LOTS / LANGNES Mirrors: Expand Your Space

Mirrors are essential home decor items. They do more than just show reflections. Mirrors reflect light, making rooms feel brighter and more open. This is especially helpful in smaller spaces or rooms with limited windows. Placing a large mirror strategically can dramatically change a room's perception.

Ikea offers various mirror styles. LOTS mirrors are frameless and come in packs. You can arrange them in a grid pattern to create a large mirror wall. This modern approach adds geometric interest. LANGNES mirrors feature traditional frames. They can serve as elegant focal points in a bedroom or entryway. Choose a style that complements your existing decor.

Proper placement of mirrors is important. Consider what the mirror will reflect. Aim to reflect a window, a piece of art, or another attractive area. Avoid reflecting clutter or blank walls. A well-placed mirror can highlight your favorite parts of the room. It visually doubles their presence. ### 8. VOLLERSLEV / STOENSE Rugs: Anchor and Define Spaces

Rugs are powerful decor elements. They can define zones in an open-plan room. They add warmth and comfort underfoot. Ikea offers many rug options, including the plush VOLLERSLEV and the modern STOENSE. A well-chosen rug can completely transform a room's feel.

The VOLLERSLEV rug is shaggy and soft. It adds a luxurious, cozy texture. Place it in a living room or bedroom for ultimate comfort. This rug makes a space feel inviting and relaxed. The STOENSE rug has a low pile and a sleek design. It works well in contemporary or minimalist settings. This rug defines an area without overwhelming it.

Rugs also introduce color and pattern. Choose a rug that complements your existing furniture and wall colors. A bold rug can be a statement piece. A subtle rug can provide a calm foundation. Always consider the size of the rug. It should be large enough to anchor your furniture. For example, all front legs of a sofa should sit on the rug. Proper sizing ensures balance and harmony in your room.
