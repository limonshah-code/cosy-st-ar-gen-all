### What is the simplest way to make Saskatoon Lemon Cheesecake Jars?

The simplest way to make Saskatoon Lemon Cheesecake Jars involves a no-bake method. You crush graham crackers for the crust. Then, you mix cream cheese, lemon juice, and sugar for the filling. Layer these components with fresh or thawed Saskatoon berries in small jars. Chill the jars until firm. This creates a quick, delicious dessert.
