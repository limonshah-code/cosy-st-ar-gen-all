## What A 4-Month-Old Dog Should Master For Success

Welcoming a puppy into your home brings joy and challenges. Your puppy grows fast. At 4 months old, your dog reaches a vital stage. This period shapes their future behavior. It is a time for significant learning. I want to help you understand what a dog should know at 4 months. We will cover key milestones and training goals. This includes basic obedience, house manners, and important social skills. Getting this stage right sets your dog up for a happy life. Let us explore the important knowledge and skills your young dog needs to develop. I will guide you through each step for a confident and well-behaved companion.

### Takeaway

At 4 months, your dog should:

*   Respond to their name.
*   Understand basic commands like 'sit,' 'stay,' and 'come.'
*   Show progress in house training
