## Designing Your Unicorn in Cricut Design Space

Start by finding a basic unicorn shape in Design Space. You can search for "unicorn" in the Images library. Choose a simple silhouette that you like. This will be the main body of your pillow. Next, think about the unicorn's features.

### Preparing Fabric Pieces for Cutting

Once your unicorn design is complete, it is time to prepare it for cutting. First, select all the elements that will be cut from the same fabric color. Use the 'Attach' tool to group them together. This ensures they cut on the same mat and keep their relative positions.

Next, send your project to the 'Make It' screen. Here, you will see how your pieces are arranged on the mats. Make sure to choose the correct material setting for your fabric. ## Precision Cutting with Your Cricut Explore Air

Now comes the moment of truth: cutting your fabric pieces. The Cricut Explore Air excels at this, turning your digital design into tangible components. This step is where your careful design work pays off. I always feel a sense of accomplishment watching my Cricut bring designs to life.

### Loading Fabric and Running the Cut

Before loading your fabric, make sure your FabricGrip mat is clean and sticky. If your mat is not sticky enough, your fabric might shift during cutting. Gently place your chosen fabric onto the mat. Smooth out any wrinkles or bubbles. Make sure the fabric adheres well to the entire cutting area.

Next, load the mat into your Cricut Explore Air machine. Double-check your material settings in Design Space. Press the 'Go' button on your machine. Watch as your Cricut precisely cuts out all your unicorn pieces. Once the cut is complete, carefully peel the fabric off the mat. Remove any tiny threads or lingering fabric pieces. Be gentle to avoid stretching your fabric. Sometimes, if the mat is dirty, the fabric won't stick well. ## Assembling Your Magical Unicorn Plush Pillow

With all your fabric pieces neatly cut, the real fun begins. Now you get to bring your unicorn to life through sewing. This step transforms flat fabric into a three-dimensional, huggable pillow. I find sewing very meditative, watching the pieces come together.

Take your time with each seam, ensuring they are straight and strong. A well-sewn pillow will last for many years of cuddles. Even if you are new to sewing, remember that practice makes perfect. Your first unicorn plush pillow will be a cherished creation. If you're looking for inspiration for other pillow projects, you might enjoy our article on [how do I make a pillow for my dog](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-do-i-make-a-pillow-for-my-dog).

### Sewing the Pillow Body

First, place the two main body pieces of your unicorn right sides together. Use pins or fabric clips to hold the edges in place. Leave a gap of about 3-4 inches on one straight edge (like the bottom of a foot or tail) for stuffing later. This opening is crucial.

Sew around the perimeter of the unicorn shape using a straight stitch. Maintain a consistent seam allowance, usually 1/4 inch or 3/8 inch. When you reach curves, slow down your sewing. Clip the curves after sewing, before turning right side out. This helps the edges lay flat and smooth. Turn your unicorn body right side out through the opening. Gently poke out all seams with a blunt tool.

### Attaching the Unicorn's Features

Now, attach the decorative elements you cut. For the horn and ears, you will likely have two pieces for each. Sew them together, right sides facing, leaving the bottom open. Clip curves, turn them right side out, and stuff them lightly if desired. Pin these to the main unicorn body at the desired locations. Stitch them in place.

For the mane and tail, you can create fabric strips or use yarn. If using fabric strips, fold them and stitch them onto the unicorn's back and rear. For eyes and cheeks, you can stitch them on with matching thread, use fabric glue, or even embroider them. Safety eyes are an option if the pillow is for older children. Ensure all features are securely attached. Consider using strong fabric glue for small details that are not sewn.

## Stuffing and Finishing Your Plush Pillow

The moment you start stuffing your unicorn plush pillow is incredibly satisfying. It transforms from a flat shape into a plump, cuddly companion. This step gives your pillow its final form and softness. I always enjoy seeing the character emerge as the pillow fills out.

Proper stuffing makes a huge difference in the pillow's appearance and feel. Take your time to distribute the fiberfill evenly. The finishing touches are like adding the sprinkles to a magical cupcake. They make your unicorn truly unique.

### Stuffing Your Unicorn Pillow Evenly

Begin by pushing small handfuls of polyester fiberfill into the unicorn pillow. Start with the extremities first, like the horn, ears, and legs. Use a blunt stick or a chopstick to push the stuffing into tight spots. This helps avoid lumpy areas.

Work your way through the entire pillow, adding more stuffing as you go. Make sure to fill every part evenly, creating a smooth, firm, yet huggable shape. Add more stuffing until the pillow feels full and holds its shape well. Do not overstuff, or the seams might strain. Do not understuff, or the pillow will feel flimsy. It is a delicate balance.

### Closing the Opening and Adding Embellishments

Once the unicorn plush pillow is stuffed to your liking, it is time to close the opening. Fold the raw edges of the opening inward. Use a ladder stitch or an invisible stitch to sew the opening closed by hand. This stitch is almost invisible, making your pillow look professionally finished.

Now, for the fun part: adding extra embellishments! You can use embroidery floss to add details like eyelashes, nostrils, or even small stars. Fabric paint can add blush to the cheeks or glitter accents. Ribbons or bows can be tied around the horn. Remember to ensure all embellishments are secure, especially if the pillow is for a child. This is where your unicorn truly gets its personality. ## Care and Maintenance of Your Unicorn Plush Pillow

You have poured your creativity and effort into making this beautiful unicorn plush pillow. Now, it is important to know how to care for it. Proper maintenance will keep your pillow looking fresh and magical for a long time. I always want my handmade items to last.

Knowing how to clean and store your pillow ensures it remains a cherished item. This section provides simple tips to help you keep your unicorn pillow in top condition. A little care goes a long way. This ensures your creation brings joy for many years.

### Gentle Cleaning for Longevity

Most plush pillows, especially those made with Minky or fleece, require gentle cleaning. Spot cleaning is often the best method for small spills or marks. Use a mild soap and a damp cloth to gently blot the affected area. Avoid rubbing harshly, as this can damage the fabric.

For a full wash, check the care instructions for your chosen fabric. Generally, machine wash on a delicate cycle with cold water. Use a gentle detergent. Placing the pillow in a mesh laundry bag can offer extra protection. Air dry your unicorn pillow completely to prevent mildew. Avoid high heat from a dryer, as it can melt synthetic fibers or cause shrinking.

### Protecting Your Handmade Treasure

To protect your unicorn plush pillow, consider where you display or store it. Keep it out of direct sunlight to prevent fabric fading. If it is a decorative pillow, consider using it in areas with less wear and tear. For children's pillows, regular gentle cleaning is important.

If you need to store the pillow for a longer period, place it in a breathable storage bag. This protects it from dust and pests. Avoid compressing it too much, as this can flatten the stuffing. Taking these small steps helps maintain the pillow's shape, color, and softness. Your handmade unicorn deserves to be preserved.

## FAQ Section

### Can the Cricut Explore Air really cut fabric for plush toys?
Yes, the Cricut Explore Air can effectively cut fabric, which is perfect for plush toys. You need to use the Cricut FabricGrip Mat and either a Bonded-Fabric Blade or a Rotary Blade for best results. Ensure your fabric is prepared correctly and adhered firmly to the mat.

### What type of fabric is best for a unicorn plush pillow?
Minky fabric and fleece are excellent choices for a unicorn plush pillow. They are soft, cuddly, and relatively easy to work with. Cotton blends can also be used, especially for smaller details. Always pre-wash and dry your fabric to prevent shrinking after your pillow is made.

### How do I make sure my Cricut fabric cuts are precise?
To ensure precise fabric cuts, use a clean and sticky FabricGrip mat. Press your fabric down firmly without wrinkles. Select the correct fabric setting in Cricut Design Space. Using a sharp blade, like the Bonded-Fabric Blade, also helps achieve clean and accurate cuts.

### What kind of stuffing should I use for a plush pillow?
Polyester fiberfill is the most common and recommended stuffing for plush pillows and toys. It is soft, washable, and holds its shape well. Make sure to use enough to create a firm, evenly stuffed pillow without making it too hard or too soft.

### Can I personalize my unicorn pillow design?
Absolutely! Personalizing your unicorn pillow is a key part of the fun. You can choose unique fabric colors, add embroidered details, use fabric paint for blush or glitter, or even add ribbons and bows. Cricut Design Space allows endless customization options for shapes and text.

### How do I clean my handmade unicorn plush pillow?
For general cleaning, spot clean with mild soap and a damp cloth. For a full wash, machine wash on a delicate cycle with cold water and gentle detergent. Air dry completely to maintain the pillow's shape and prevent damage. Avoid high heat from a dryer.
