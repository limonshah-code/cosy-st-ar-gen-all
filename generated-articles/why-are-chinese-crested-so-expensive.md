### The Unique Allure and Rarity of Chinese Crested Dogs

Chinese Crested dogs are truly one-of-a-kind. They capture attention with their striking appearance. Some people fall in love with their unique look right away. The breed comes in two varieties: hairless and Powderpuff. The hairless type has soft skin and tufts of fur on its head, feet, and tail. The Powderpuff variety boasts a full, silky coat. Both types are recognized, but the hairless is often more widely known. This distinct appearance is a major factor in why Chinese Crested dogs are so expensive.

Their rarity also plays a role in their cost. You do not see Chinese Crested dogs as commonly as other breeds. This scarcity naturally increases their market value. When something is harder to find, it becomes more desirable. Many people seek out these dogs because they want a pet that stands out. This desire for something different drives up their price. They are not just pets; they are conversation starters.

People often want a dog with a unique personality too. Chinese Crested dogs are known for being affectionate and playful. They form strong bonds with their families. These traits make them wonderful companions. Their gentle nature and loving disposition add to their appeal. They are not just expensive because of their looks, but also for the joy they bring. Their unique combination of looks and temperament truly sets them apart.

### High Breeding Standards and Costs for Chinese Crested

This is a risk no responsible owner wants to take.

### Extensive Health Care and Genetic Screening

Health is a top priority for any good breeder. Chinese Crested dogs, like many purebreds, have specific health concerns. Reputable breeders take proactive steps to minimize these risks. This means they invest heavily in various health screenings. These tests are essential for ensuring a healthy bloodline. I always look for breeders who prioritize health.

One common concern for Chinese Crested dogs is dental health. The hairless variety can have missing or misaligned teeth. This issue requires regular dental care and sometimes interventions. Eye conditions like glaucoma and progressive retinal atrophy are also prevalent. Breeders perform eye exams annually to detect these problems early. This protects the puppies from inheriting these conditions.

Patellar luxation, a knee problem, is another health issue. Hip and elbow dysplasia can also occur. Breeders screen for these orthopedic problems using X-rays. They aim to breed only from dogs with healthy joints. This reduces the chances of puppies developing painful mobility issues later in life. These tests are not cheap, and they are performed on both parent dogs.

Responsible breeders also test for genetic disorders through DNA panels. This includes conditions like Primary Lens Luxation (PLL) and Degenerative Myelopathy (DM). These tests identify carriers or affected dogs. Breeders then make informed decisions about breeding pairs. This prevents the passing on of severe genetic diseases. The cost of these comprehensive screenings is a major component of why Chinese Crested dogs are so expensive. It is an investment in the health of the entire breed.

### The Investment in Puppy Rearing and Early Care

Raising a litter of Chinese Crested puppies is a round-the-clock commitment. It involves much more than just feeding them. Breeders dedicate immense time and resources to each puppy. This intensive early care significantly contributes to their price. I have seen the hard work involved in raising healthy puppies.

Before birth, the mother dog receives excellent prenatal care. This includes special nutrition and regular vet visits. Proper care during pregnancy ensures a healthy start for the puppies. Once the puppies are born, the breeder watches them constantly. They ensure each puppy is nursing well and gaining weight. Newborns are very delicate and need constant supervision.

As the puppies grow, they need specific vaccinations and deworming. These veterinary services protect them from common diseases. Each puppy gets its initial health check-ups. The vet ensures they are developing properly. These medical expenses add up quickly, especially for a litter. This early veterinary care is crucial for their well-being.

Socialization is another critical aspect of puppy rearing. Breeders expose puppies to different sounds, sights, and people. They handle them gently and begin basic training. This helps puppies become well-adjusted and confident adults. A well-socialized puppy is less likely to develop behavioral problems. This investment in their early development is vital. ### Specialized Grooming and Ongoing Maintenance

Owning a Chinese Crested dog means committing to specific grooming routines. Both the hairless and Powderpuff varieties have unique needs. These ongoing maintenance requirements add to their lifetime cost. This specialized care is another reason why Chinese Crested dogs are so expensive, even after the initial purchase. I can tell you, their grooming is an art.

The hairless Chinese Crested needs regular skin care. Their skin can get sunburned, just like human skin. Owners must apply sunscreen when they go outside. They also need frequent baths to prevent skin issues. Moisturizers help keep their skin soft and healthy. Some hairless dogs may develop acne or blackheads. This requires extra cleaning and sometimes veterinary attention. These skin care products and baths are a recurring expense.

The Powderpuff Chinese Crested has a full, flowing coat. This coat requires daily brushing to prevent mats and tangles. Without proper brushing, their fur can become matted, which is painful for the dog. Regular professional grooming appointments are often necessary. These sessions include bathing, conditioning, and trimming. A professional groomer understands how to care for their unique coat. These grooming services can be quite expensive, depending on location and frequency.

Beyond grooming, these dogs have other needs. Their unique temperament means they thrive on companionship. They do not like being left alone for long periods. This might require dog walkers or pet sitters if you work long hours. Providing proper nutrition, toys, and a warm environment are also ongoing costs. All these factors contribute to the total cost of owning a Chinese Crested dog, making it a significant financial commitment beyond the purchase price. ### FAQ Section

#### How much does a Chinese Crested puppy typically cost?
A Chinese Crested puppy from a reputable breeder typically costs between $2,000 and $4,000. Prices can vary based on the breeder's reputation, the puppy's lineage, location, and whether it's a hairless or Powderpuff variety. Show-quality puppies or those with exceptional pedigrees might be even more expensive.

#### Are Chinese Crested dogs considered a rare breed?
Yes, Chinese Crested dogs are considered a relatively rare breed compared to more common household pets. Their unique appearance and specific breeding requirements mean they are not as widely available. This rarity contributes to their higher price point and desirability among enthusiasts.

#### Do Chinese Crested dogs have many health problems?
Chinese Crested dogs can be prone to certain genetic health issues, including dental problems, eye conditions like glaucoma, patellar luxation, and some skin sensitivities in the hairless variety. Reputable breeders conduct extensive health screenings to minimize these risks in their litters.

#### What are the ongoing costs of owning a Chinese Crested?
Ongoing costs for a Chinese Crested include premium dog food, regular veterinary check-ups, and vaccinations. Specialized grooming for both hairless (skin care, sunscreen) and Powderpuff (daily brushing, professional grooming) varieties is also a significant expense. Pet insurance, toys, and accessories also add to the overall cost.

#### Is a Chinese Crested a good family pet?
Yes, Chinese Crested dogs can be excellent family pets. They are known for being affectionate, loyal, and playful companions. They bond strongly with their families and generally get along well with children and other pets. Their small size makes them suitable for various living situations, including apartments.

#### Why is the hairless variety often more expensive than the Powderpuff?
The hairless variety is often perceived as more unique and iconic for the breed, which can drive up its demand and price. Additionally, breeding hairless dogs can involve specific genetic challenges, and their specialized skin care needs might be seen as requiring a higher initial investment in a puppy.
