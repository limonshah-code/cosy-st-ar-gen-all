## The Allure of the Lilac Tan French Bulldog: A Distinctive Hue

The Lilac Tan French Bulldog stands out among French Bulldogs. Its coat color is truly special. This color results from specific genetic combinations. It needs two copies of the dilute gene (dd) and two copies of the chocolate gene (bb). The dog also carries the tan points gene (at/at or At/a). This genetic blueprint creates a beautiful, iridescent gray-purple hue with tan markings. The tan points appear on the eyebrows, cheeks, chest, and legs.
