## Caring for Your Squishmallow: Tips for Longevity

Squishmallows are incredibly soft and comforting. Proper care ensures they remain in excellent condition. This helps them stay squishy and vibrant for a long time. Caring for your Chihuahua Squishmallow, Rosie, is simple. It just requires a few easy steps.

First, always check the care tag on your Squishmallow. Most advise spot cleaning for small messes. Use a damp cloth and mild soap. Gently blot the soiled area. Avoid harsh scrubbing, as this can damage the fabric.

For a deeper clean, many Squishmallows can be machine washed. Use a gentle cycle with cold water. Place your Squishmallow inside a pillowcase or laundry bag. This protects it during the wash. Use a small amount of mild detergent. Avoid bleach or fabric softener, as these can affect the plushness.

After washing, air drying is best. Tumble drying on low heat is also an option. Ensure your Squishmallow is completely dry before storing it. This prevents mildew. You can gently fluff it by hand to restore its shape. Following these steps helps keep your beloved Rosie feeling new.
