## Cleaning Your Aluminum Pool Cage Frame

Is your pool cage looking less than its best? Over time, dirt, grime, and algae can build up on aluminum pool cage frames. This makes your outdoor space appear neglected. Cleaning your aluminum pool cage frame regularly improves its look and extends its life. I will show you how to restore its shine.

This guide provides clear steps for a thorough clean. We will cover everything from gathering supplies to applying protective measures. You will learn about safe cleaning solutions and techniques for stubborn stains. Get ready to make your pool enclosure sparkle again. A clean pool cage enhances your entire backyard.

### Takeaway

*   Gather essential tools and safety gear before you start.
*   Use gentle cleaning solutions for regular dirt and grime.
*   Address tough stains like mold, mildew, and rust with targeted methods.
*   Clean the aluminum frame first, then move to the screens carefully.
*   Rinse thoroughly to prevent streaks and residue buildup.
*   Apply a protective coating to extend cleanliness and protect the frame.
*   Consider professional help for severe issues or large structures.

### Main Query Answer

To clean an aluminum pool cage frame, first remove loose debris. Then, apply a gentle cleaning solution like mild dish soap and water. Scrub the frame using a soft brush. Rinse thoroughly with a garden hose. For tough stains like mold or mildew, use a diluted bleach solution or an appropriate aluminum cleaner. Always test cleaners in an inconspicuous spot first.
