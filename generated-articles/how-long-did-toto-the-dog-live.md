## Health Issues Common in Cairn Terriers

While Cairn Terriers are generally healthy, they can face specific health challenges. Knowing about these issues helps owners provide better care. One common concern is patellar luxation. This condition affects the kneecap. The kneecap slips out of place. It can cause limping or pain. Vets can diagnose this with an examination. Sometimes, surgery helps.

Another issue is cataracts. This affects a dog's eyes. The lens of the eye becomes cloudy. It can lead to impaired vision or blindness. Cataracts can be hereditary. They can also develop with age. Surgery may correct the problem in some cases. Regular eye exams are important for older dogs.

Cairn Terriers are also prone to certain skin allergies. These can cause itching, redness, and discomfort. Allergies can stem from food or environmental factors. Identifying the allergen is key to treatment. Special diets or medications can help manage symptoms. This requires careful observation by the owner.
