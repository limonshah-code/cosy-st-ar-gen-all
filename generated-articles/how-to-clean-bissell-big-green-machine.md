## Post-Use Routine: Essential First Steps to Clean Your Bissell Big Green Machine

You just finished cleaning your carpets, and they look great. The work is not over yet, though. The most important step to keeping your machine clean happens right after you use it. Then, do the same for the clean water tank. Even though it held clean solution, a quick rinse is good practice. This removes any leftover cleaning solution that could dry and build up. ## Deep Cleaning the Water Tanks: A Critical Step for Bissell Big Green Maintenance

The water tanks are workhorses in your Bissell Big Green Machine. They hold the cleaning solution and collect the dirty water. Keeping them truly clean is vital for the machine's hygiene and performance. A simple rinse is a start, but a deep clean ensures no residue or odors remain. This prevents issues like mold growth and unpleasant smells during future use.

I make sure to give these tanks extra attention. Regular deep cleaning ensures they function properly. It also keeps your home environment healthy. You do not want a cleaner that adds smells instead of removing them.

### Cleaning the Dirty Water Tank

The dirty water tank often holds a mix of grime, pet hair, and old carpet fibers. These can stick to the tank walls and internal components. After emptying the tank, fill it halfway with warm water. Add a small amount of mild dish soap or a dedicated Bissell tank cleaning solution. Close the cap and shake the tank vigorously for about 30 seconds. This action helps dislodge stubborn dirt.

Next, open the tank and use a soft brush or sponge to scrub the inside. Pay close attention to corners and edges. Rinse the tank thoroughly with clean, warm water until all soap bubbles and debris are gone. Allow the tank to air dry completely upside down. This prevents mildew and ensures proper hygiene. For more specific guidance, review how to [clean the dirty tank on Bissell Little Green](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-dirty-tank-on-bissell-little-green). This process is very similar for both machines.

### Caring for the Clean Water Tank

The clean water tank usually stays cleaner. However, cleaning solution residue can still build up. This buildup might affect the machine's water flow over time. Empty any leftover solution from the tank. Rinse it thoroughly with warm water. If you notice any film or residue, use a gentle brush. You can also use a mixture of equal parts water and white vinegar to break down mineral deposits.

Let this mixture sit in the tank for 15-20 minutes. Then, scrub and rinse thoroughly. Always rinse several times to ensure no vinegar smell remains. Make sure the tank dries completely before you store it. This prevents any unwanted growth. Proper care of both tanks extends their life. It also keeps your Bissell Big Green Machine running efficiently.

## Maintaining the Brush Roll and Nozzle: Key to Effective Carpet Cleaning

The brush roll and nozzle are the parts of your Bissell Big Green Machine that do the actual scrubbing and suction. If these components are dirty or clogged, your machine cannot clean effectively. Hair, fibers, and dirt can get tangled in the brush or block the nozzle. This reduces performance significantly. I always check these parts carefully after each use.

Keeping them clean ensures powerful scrubbing and strong suction. It is an essential step for top cleaning results. Neglecting these areas can lead to streaks, poor dirt extraction, and a shorter machine lifespan.

### Cleaning the Brush Roll

The brush roll is the part that agitates the carpet fibers. It helps lift dirt. Hair, lint, and string often wrap around it. First, turn off and unplug your machine. Carefully remove the brush roll cover according to your model's instructions. This usually involves unlatching a few clips. Take out the brush roll. Use scissors or a seam ripper to cut away any tangled hair or fibers.

Once freed, rinse the brush roll under warm, running water. You can use a soft brush to scrub away any caked-on dirt. Ensure all bristles are free of debris. Let the brush roll air dry completely before putting it back into the machine. A dry brush prevents mold and mildew. This step is similar to how you would [clean the Bissell Little Green brush head](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-bissell-little-green-brush-head).

### Caring for the Suction Nozzle

The suction nozzle is where the dirty water and lifted grime get sucked back into the machine. This part can easily get clogged with larger debris or matted dirt. Remove the nozzle from the machine. Check for any visible blockages. Often, pet hair clumps or carpet fibers get stuck here. Use your fingers or a small brush to clear them out.

Rinse the nozzle under warm water. Make sure water flows freely through all openings. If you have stubborn clogs, a pipe cleaner or a small, stiff brush can help. Ensure the nozzle is completely clear of any obstructions. Allow it to air dry fully before reattaching it. A clean nozzle ensures maximum suction power. This provides better drying times for your carpets. Properly maintaining this component is crucial for your machine's efficiency. ## Flushing and Cleaning the Hose and Attachments for Your Bissell Big Green

The hose and various attachments allow your Bissell Big Green Machine to clean stairs, upholstery, and tight spots. These parts are just as important as the main unit. They can harbor dirty water, cleaning solution residue, and even mildew if not cleaned correctly. Many users overlook these components. However, I know that ignoring them can lead to bad odors and reduced performance.

Proper cleaning of the hose and attachments ensures that every part of your machine is ready to deliver powerful cleaning. It stops dirt from transferring back onto surfaces. This also extends the life of these flexible tools.

### Cleaning the Main Hose

The internal part of the main hose can hold onto dirty water. This creates an environment for bacteria. To clean the hose, fill the clean water tank with warm water, without any cleaning solution. Attach a tool, like the upholstery tool, to the end of the hose. Turn on your machine. Spray the clean water through the hose into a sink or bucket for a few minutes. This flushes out any residual dirty water and grime.

Repeat this process until the water coming out of the hose runs clear. After flushing, disconnect the hose from the machine. Hang it vertically if possible. This allows any remaining water to drain out completely. Ensure it is fully dry before storing it. Drying prevents mildew and unpleasant smells. This step is critical for maintaining overall machine hygiene.

### Washing Attachments and Tools

Your Bissell Big Green Machine comes with various attachments. Each one requires attention. After using an attachment, disconnect it from the hose. Rinse each tool thoroughly under warm, running water. Use a small brush, like an old toothbrush, to scrub away any visible dirt, hair, or residue. Pay attention to crevices and nozzle openings.

If any attachment has stubborn buildup, you can let it soak in warm, soapy water for a few minutes. Rinse again until all soap is gone. Ensure all parts are completely dry before storing them. You can place them on a towel or a drying rack. Store them in a well-ventilated area. This prevents mold and ensures they are ready for the next task. Keeping these parts clean maintains your machine's versatility. It also extends the life of your entire cleaning system. ## Addressing Odors and Mineral Buildup in Your Bissell Big Green Cleaner

Even with regular cleaning, your Bissell Big Green Machine might develop peculiar odors or show signs of mineral buildup. These issues can impact the machine's efficiency and the freshness of your carpets. Odors usually come from bacteria growing in residual dirty water or cleaning solution. Mineral buildup, on the other hand, is common in areas with hard water. It can clog spray nozzles and restrict water flow.

I have found that specific treatments are needed for these problems. Ignoring them can lead to a less effective cleaner. It can also cause a less pleasant cleaning experience. These specialized steps ensure your machine stays hygienic and powerful.

### Eliminating Unpleasant Odors

If your Bissell Big Green Machine has a stale or musty smell, it usually means mildew or bacteria are present. This often happens in the dirty water tank, hose, or brush roll if they were not dried thoroughly. To combat this, perform an extra deep clean. Fill the clean water tank with warm water and one cup of white vinegar. Run this solution through the machine as if you were cleaning a carpet. Spray it into a bucket or sink. This helps sanitize the internal pathways.

After running the vinegar solution, run plain warm water through the machine to rinse thoroughly. Disassemble all removable parts: tanks, brush roll, nozzle, and attachments. Wash them with warm, soapy water. Then, soak them in a solution of one part white vinegar to two parts water for 30 minutes. Rinse everything extremely well. Ensure all parts are completely air-dried in a well-ventilated area before reassembling. This eliminates the source of the odor. It leaves your machine smelling fresh.

### Removing Mineral Buildup

Mineral buildup, often called limescale, looks like white or crusty deposits. It typically forms in the clean water tank, spray nozzles, and internal lines. Hard water is the cause. This buildup can reduce spray pressure and clog the machine. To remove it, create a descaling solution. Mix equal parts white vinegar and warm water.

Fill your clean water tank with this vinegar solution. Let it sit for 30-60 minutes without turning on the machine. This allows the vinegar to dissolve the minerals. Then, turn on the machine. Spray the solution through the hose and nozzle into a sink or bucket. Continue until the tank is empty. Follow this by running two full tanks of plain warm water through the machine to rinse away all vinegar and loosened mineral deposits. For stubborn clogs in spray nozzles, use a pin or a toothpick to gently clear the openings. Regular descaling prevents long-term damage. It ensures consistent spray performance.

## FAQ Section: Common Questions About Cleaning Your Bissell Big Green Machine

Here are some frequent questions users ask about keeping their Bissell Big Green Machine in top condition.

### How often should I clean my Bissell Big Green Machine?

You should perform basic cleaning tasks after every use. This includes emptying and rinsing tanks, flushing the hose, and wiping the exterior. A deeper clean, focusing on the brush roll and nozzle, is good every few uses or monthly. An annual descaling and comprehensive check-up maintains peak performance.

### Can I use vinegar to clean my Bissell Big Green Machine?

Yes, white vinegar is safe and effective for cleaning and descaling your Bissell Big Green Machine. It helps remove mineral buildup and eliminate odors. Always dilute it with water, typically 1:1, for descaling. Rinse the machine thoroughly with plain water afterward to remove any vinegar smell.

### What causes a bad smell in my Bissell Big Green?

Bad smells usually come from mildew or bacteria. These grow in residual dirty water or cleaning solution left in tanks, the hose, or the brush roll. Not drying parts completely after cleaning is a common cause. Regular rinsing and thorough drying of all components prevent this.

### How do I prevent clogs in my Bissell Big Green hose?

To prevent hose clogs, always flush the hose with clean water after each use. Attach a cleaning tool and spray plain warm water into a sink or bucket until it runs clear. Also, try to vacuum heavily soiled areas with a regular vacuum first. This removes loose debris before deep cleaning.

### Is it okay to leave water in the tanks of my Bissell Big Green?

No, it is not okay to leave water in the tanks. Stagnant water, especially dirty water, promotes mold and bacterial growth. This leads to unpleasant odors and can damage machine parts over time. Always empty and rinse both tanks immediately after each cleaning session.

### How do I store my Bissell Big Green Machine long-term?

For long-term storage, ensure the machine is completely clean and dry, inside and out. Disassemble and dry all removable parts. Store the machine in a cool, dry, well-ventilated area, away from direct sunlight or extreme temperatures. Coil the power cord loosely to prevent damage.
