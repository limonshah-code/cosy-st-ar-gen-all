## What Dog Is Bigger Than A Bernese Mountain Dog? Unveiling Giant Breeds

Do you love large dogs? Perhaps you admire the gentle nature and impressive size of a Bernese Mountain Dog. These dogs are truly magnificent, known for their loyal hearts and striking tricolor coats. Yet, as big as they are, many people wonder: what dog is bigger than a Bernese Mountain Dog? You might be surprised to learn that several dog breeds significantly surpass the Bernese in both height and weight.

This article explores the world of truly giant dog breeds. We will compare their impressive dimensions to the Bernese Mountain Dog. You will learn about their unique temperaments and the special care these colossal canines require. Understanding these differences helps you choose the right companion if you are considering a very large dog. Join us to discover the giants among dogs.

### Takeaway

*   **Many Breeds Outsize Bernese:** Several dog breeds are larger than a Bernese Mountain Dog in both height and weight.
*   **Giants Have Unique Needs:** Very large dogs require specific care regarding diet, exercise, space, and health.
*   **Temperament Varies:** Despite their size, many giant breeds are gentle and calm, but early training is vital.
*   **Lifespan Considerations:** Giant breeds generally have shorter lifespans compared to smaller dogs.

### Clear, Concise Answer

Several dog breeds are larger than a Bernese Mountain Dog. These include the Great Dane, Mastiff breeds (English Mastiff, Neapolitan Mastiff), Saint Bernard, Irish Wolfhound, Newfoundland, and Leonberger. These dogs typically exceed the Bernese Mountain Dog's average weight of 70-115 pounds and height of 23-27.5 inches.

## Understanding the Bernese Mountain Dog's Size

The Bernese Mountain Dog is a large, sturdy dog. They have a powerful build. Males typically stand 25 to 27.5 inches tall at the shoulder. They weigh between 80 and 115 pounds. Females are slightly smaller, standing 23 to 26 inches tall. They weigh 70 to 95 pounds. This makes them a substantial dog, certainly not small. Their broad chests and muscular bodies add to their overall impressive appearance.

Many dog owners find the Bernese Mountain Dog's size to be ideal. They are big enough to be imposing but often fit well into family life. My own experience with a Bernese showed me their gentle giants nature. They mature slower than smaller dogs. A Bernese Mountain Dog is usually fully grown by [18 to 24 months of age](https://cosyhomecreation.com/at-what-age-is-a-bernese-mountain-dog-fully-grown). This means their growth plates are closed. It is important to feed them a specific diet during their puppy stages. This prevents rapid growth issues. These issues can affect their bones and joints later in life. Their size requires space, even indoors. They enjoy sprawling out on cool floors.

Despite their size, Bernese Mountain Dogs are agile. They enjoy activities like hiking or carting. Their heavy coats and thick bones contribute to their significant weight. When thinking about their size, it's not just height. Weight plays a big part in classifying a dog as "bigger." This distinction becomes clear when we look at breeds that truly dwarf the Bernese Mountain Dog. Their weight distribution and bone structure are different from lean, tall breeds. This contributes to their stocky appearance.

## The Mighty Mastiff: A True Giant Among Dogs

When considering what dog is bigger than a Bernese Mountain Dog, Mastiff breeds quickly come to mind. The English Mastiff is often cited as one of the largest dog breeds. They are true giants. Males can stand 27 to 30 inches tall. They frequently weigh 150 to 250 pounds or even more. Some exceptional males have reached over 300 pounds. This makes them significantly heavier than a Bernese Mountain Dog. Their massive heads and broad bodies give them an imposing presence.

Other Mastiff types also surpass the Bernese in size. The Neapolitan Mastiff is another contender. They are known for their loose skin and powerful build. Males typically weigh 150 to 200 pounds. Their height ranges from 26 to 31 inches. The Bullmastiff, while smaller than the English Mastiff, can still be larger than a Bernese. They weigh 100 to 130 pounds. All these Mastiff breeds share a common ancestry of powerful guardian dogs. They were historically used for protection.

Despite their intimidating size, many Mastiffs are gentle giants at home. They often have calm and watchful temperaments. They are generally loyal to their families. My interactions with Mastiffs show they can be surprisingly mellow indoors. They love to lounge. Their immense size means they need specific care. This includes plenty of space. It also means large food portions. They need strong training from a young age. Their sheer strength makes them hard to manage without proper training. Owners must consider the [pros and cons of a Bernese Mountain Dog](https://cosyhomecreation.com/what-are-the-pros-and-cons-of-a-bernese-mountain-dog), and amplify them for a Mastiff.

## The Great Dane: The Tallest Dog Breed

The Great Dane stands out when we talk about dog height. They are often called the "Apollo of Dogs." This breed consistently holds the record for the tallest dog. Male Great Danes typically stand 30 to 34 inches tall at the shoulder. Some can be even taller. Their weight ranges from 120 to 175 pounds. While they might not always be heavier than the heaviest Bernese, their height is undeniable. A Great Dane towers over a Bernese Mountain Dog.

The Great Dane's appearance is elegant and muscular. They have a regal posture. Their long legs and graceful stride are distinctive. Despite their imposing height, they are known for being gentle. They can be affectionate companions. They are often very good with children. However, their size means they need a lot of space. They require special attention to their growing bones as puppies. Over-exercising a young Great Dane can cause serious joint problems.

Owning a Great Dane means preparing for a truly large animal. Their reach can clear countertops with ease. Their wagging tail can knock items off tables. My experience shows that proper training and socialization are key. This helps them be well-behaved giants. Their size means higher costs for food, vet care, and supplies. ## Saint Bernard: A Bear-like Companion

The Saint Bernard is a legendary giant. This breed originated in the Swiss Alps. They are famous for their rescue work. They are truly massive dogs. Males stand 28 to 30 inches tall. They weigh between 140 and 180 pounds. Some individuals can weigh over 200 pounds. This makes them considerably heavier and often taller than a Bernese Mountain Dog. Their thick coats and large bone structure contribute to their bear-like appearance.

Saint Bernards have a calm and friendly temperament. They are often very patient. This makes them excellent family dogs. They are known for their loyalty and gentle nature. However, their sheer size requires a lot of space. They are not suited for small living areas. They also produce a fair amount of drool. This is something potential owners should know. Like other giant breeds, they grow quickly as puppies. This demands careful feeding and exercise.

They need proper socialization from a young age. This helps them develop into well-adjusted adults. Their thick double coat requires regular grooming. This prevents matting. Their lifespan is typically shorter. Many giant breeds share this characteristic. Health issues common to large breeds, like hip and elbow dysplasia, are a concern. Owners need to be prepared for the care these large dogs require. This includes special food bowls and larger vehicles for transport.

## Irish Wolfhound: The Tallest of All

The Irish Wolfhound is remarkable for its incredible height. They are often considered the tallest dog breed in the world. Males stand at least 32 inches tall. They often reach 34 to 35 inches. While they may not always surpass the heaviest Mastiffs in weight, their height is unmatched. They truly dwarf a Bernese Mountain Dog.

These dogs were originally bred for hunting wolves. They possess a rugged yet graceful build. Despite their hunting history, Irish Wolfhounds are known for their gentle and dignified nature. It is important to know that their size impacts every aspect of their care, from food bills to vet visits.

## Health and Lifespan of Giant Breeds

Understanding the health and lifespan of giant dog breeds is very important for potential owners. While their massive size is impressive, it often comes with certain health predispositions and a generally shorter lifespan compared to smaller dogs. For example, a Bernese Mountain Dog typically lives 7-10 years. Many giant breeds live even less. [The most common cause of death in Bernese Mountain Dogs](https://cosyhomecreation.com/what-is-the-most-common-cause-of-death-in-bernese-mountain-dogs) is often cancer, a sad truth shared by many large breeds.

Common health issues in giant breeds include:
*   **Hip and Elbow Dysplasia:** This is a genetic condition. It affects the proper formation of joints. It can cause pain and arthritis. Screening tests are available for breeding dogs.
*   **Bloat (Gastric Dilatation-Volvulus - GDV):** This is a life-threatening condition. The stomach twists. It often happens after eating or drinking too quickly. It is an emergency. Deep-chested breeds are more susceptible.
*   **Cardiomyopathy:** This is a heart muscle disease. It can lead to heart failure. Regular veterinary check-ups are vital for early detection.
*   **Osteosarcoma (Bone Cancer):** Large and giant breeds have a higher risk of developing this aggressive cancer.
*   **Arthritis:** Due to their heavy weight, joints can experience more wear and tear. This leads to early onset arthritis.

A specialized diet is critical for giant breed puppies. It helps them grow at a slow, controlled rate. This supports proper bone and joint development. Too much protein or calcium can lead to growth problems. Regular, moderate exercise is also important. This builds muscle without stressing developing joints. Avoiding strenuous activities until they are fully mature helps prevent injuries. Responsible breeders screen their dogs for common genetic health problems. This helps ensure healthier puppies.
