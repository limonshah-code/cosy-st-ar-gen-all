### Checking Expiration Dates

Dog food, like human food, has an expiration date. This date indicates when the product is best used by. After this date, the nutrients may degrade. The food might also become rancid or unsafe. Always check the "Best By" or "Expiration Date" on the dog food bag. This date is usually stamped near the top or bottom of the bag. It might be on a seam.

When buying online, check the product description for expiration date information. Some retailers state that they only ship products with a minimum number of months before expiration. If you are unsure, contact customer service before ordering. When picking up in-store, make it a habit to physically check the date. Ensure it gives you enough time to use the food before it expires. For example, if you buy a large bag, make sure the expiration date is several months away. This gives your dog ample time to consume it.

### Proper Storage After Purchase

Proper storage after purchase is just as important as buying fresh. Once you open a bag of Nutrisource dog food, its freshness begins to decline. Exposure to air, moisture, and pests can spoil the food quickly. Always transfer the opened bag into an airtight container. This container should be made of food-grade plastic or metal.

Store the container in a cool, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight or extreme temperatures. A pantry or a utility room is often suitable. Do not store dog food in the garage if it gets too hot or humid. Heat and humidity speed up spoilage. Keeping the food fresh helps maintain its nutritional value and palatability. It also prevents your dog from getting sick.
