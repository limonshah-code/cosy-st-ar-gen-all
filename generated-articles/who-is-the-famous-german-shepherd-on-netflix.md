### How many different dogs played Rex in the series?
Over the long run of "Kommissar Rex," several German Shepherds portrayed the iconic role of Rex. The most famous and first dog was Reginald von Ravenhorst (Santo vom Haus Ziegelmayer). Later, Rhett Butler took over for a significant period. Other dogs also stepped into the role for different seasons and productions.

### Are German Shepherds good family pets?
German Shepherds can be excellent family pets with proper training and socialization. They are loyal, protective, and form strong bonds with their families. They require consistent guidance, ample exercise, and mental stimulation. Their protective nature means early socialization is key to ensure they are well-behaved around strangers and other animals.

### What makes German Shepherds good police dogs?
German Shepherds are ideal police dogs due to their high intelligence, strong work drive, trainability, and courage. Their keen sense of smell is crucial for detection work. Their physical strength and protective instincts make them effective in suspect apprehension and crowd control. They form a strong bond with their human handlers.

### Are there other famous German Shepherds in movies or TV?
Yes, many German Shepherds have achieved fame in media. Historically, Rin Tin Tin and Strongheart were early cinema stars. More recently, dogs in films like "K-9" and "I Am Legend" (though the dog in "I Am Legend" was a Belgian Malinois for part of the role, a breed often confused with GSDs) have also captivated audiences. Their versatility makes them popular choices.

### What is the typical lifespan of a German Shepherd?
The typical lifespan of a German Shepherd is generally between 9 to 13 years. This can vary based on factors such as genetics, diet, exercise, and overall health care. Regular veterinary check-ups, a balanced diet, and maintaining a healthy weight can contribute to a longer and healthier life for your German Shepherd companion.
