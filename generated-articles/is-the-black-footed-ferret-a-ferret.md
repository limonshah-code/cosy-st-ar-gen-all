### Is The Black-footed Ferret A Ferret?

Yes, the Black-footed Ferret (*Mustela nigripes*) is indeed a true ferret. It is a member of the genus *Mustela* within the weasel family (Mustelidae), making it a close relative of other ferrets, weasels, and polecats. Unlike the domestic ferret, it is a wild, native North American species.
