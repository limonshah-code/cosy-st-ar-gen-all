### Origins of the Border Terrier

The Border Terrier breed developed in the Cheviot Hills. This area sits between Scotland and England. Farmers needed a dog that could keep up with horses. They also needed a dog that could go to ground after foxes.

Breeders developed the Border Terrier for specific hunting tasks. These dogs needed courage and stamina. They also required a narrow body to fit into fox dens. Their compact build served a practical purpose. This hunting heritage still shapes the breed's instincts today.
