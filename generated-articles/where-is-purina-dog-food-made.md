### Does Purina use human-grade ingredients?
Purina aims for pet-food-grade ingredients that are safe and nutritious for dogs, not necessarily human-grade. Purina uses ingredients from human food supply chains and processes them in pet food facilities under strict quality controls for pets.

Purina maintains stringent quality control measures for ingredients. They source from approved suppliers who meet strict quality and safety criteria.
