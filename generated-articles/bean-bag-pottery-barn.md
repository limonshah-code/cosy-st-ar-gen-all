## Maintaining Your Pottery Barn Bean Bag: Care Tips

Proper care ensures your Pottery Barn bean bag stays beautiful and comfortable. Regular maintenance is simple. It prevents stains and extends the life of the fabric and filling. I find that a little effort goes a long way.

Most Pottery Barn bean bags come with removable covers. This feature makes cleaning much easier. Check the care label inside the cover for specific instructions. Many covers are machine washable, making cleanup a breeze. Always use a mild detergent and cold water to protect the fabric.

For spills or spot cleaning, act quickly. Blot the area with a clean cloth and a gentle cleaning solution. Avoid rubbing, as this can spread the stain. Regular vacuuming also helps remove dust and crumbs from the fabric. This keeps your bean bag looking fresh.

The inner lining and filling also need attention.
