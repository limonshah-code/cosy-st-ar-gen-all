### Understanding Spain's New Animal Welfare Law

Spain's new Animal Welfare Law, enacted in 2023, marks a significant step. It brings sweeping changes to how pets are owned and cared for. The law focuses on zero animal cruelty and abandonment. It promotes adoption over purchase. I believe this law will greatly improve pet lives in Spain.

The law includes mandatory pet owner courses. These courses teach proper pet care and responsibility. They cover topics like nutrition, behavior, and legal obligations. This ensures owners are well-prepared for their commitment. It helps new owners understand the true needs of their pets.

Breeding and selling pets are now under tighter control. Pet shops can no longer sell dogs or cats directly. This encourages adoption from shelters. It also aims to reduce impulse purchases. The law sets a new standard for animal protection across the country.
