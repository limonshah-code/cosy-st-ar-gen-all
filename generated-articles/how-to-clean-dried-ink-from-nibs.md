## Unlock Smooth Writing: How To Clean Dried Ink From Nibs

Have you ever picked up your favorite pen, only to find the ink won't flow? Dried ink on the nib is a common problem for many writers, artists, and calligraphers. It happens when ink hardens, blocking the delicate channels. Cleaning dried ink from nibs ensures your pens perform their best. This guide offers practical steps and tips. We will cover various cleaning methods, necessary tools, and preventive measures. We aim to help you keep your pens in perfect working order. Let's get your pens writing smoothly again.

### Takeaway

*   **Soak:** Use water or a mild cleaning solution to soften dried ink.
*   **Rinse:** Flush nibs thoroughly to remove loosened ink particles.
*   **Disassemble:** Take apart pen components for deeper cleaning when necessary.
*   **Brush:** Gently scrub stubborn residue with a soft brush.
*   **Maintain:** Clean pens regularly to prevent ink from drying.

### The Quick Answer

To clean dried ink from nibs, first soak the nib in cool water for several hours or overnight. For stubborn ink, use a specialized pen cleaning solution or a mild ammonia-based mixture. Gently scrub the nib with a soft brush or cloth after soaking. Rinse thoroughly with clean water, then dry the nib completely before reassembling or storing the pen.
