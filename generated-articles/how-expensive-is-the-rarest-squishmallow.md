## Protecting Your Investment: Caring for Valuable Squishmallows

Once you own a rare or expensive Squishmallow, caring for it becomes important. Proper care ensures it retains its value. It also keeps your cherished collectible in pristine condition for years. Treating your Squishmallow as an investment means protecting it.

### Proper Storage Techniques

Store your valuable Squishmallows in a clean, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors over time. Keep them away from high humidity areas. Humidity can lead to mold or mildew growth. A closed display cabinet or a plastic storage bin with a lid works well. This protects them from dust, dirt, and pet hair. Ensure the storage area maintains a stable temperature. Extreme temperature changes are not good for plush toys.

### Cleaning Methods for Preservation

Spot cleaning is the recommended method for Squishmallows. Avoid putting them in a washing machine. The machine can damage the stuffing and fabric. Use a damp cloth with mild soap. Gently dab any dirty areas. Do not rub harshly. Allow the area to air dry completely. Never use harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. These can ruin the fabric or colors. For stubborn stains, consult a professional cleaning service that specializes in delicate textiles. Prevention is better than cleaning. Handle them with clean hands.

### Preserving Hang Tags and Tush Tags

The original hang tag significantly adds to a rare Squishmallow's value. These tags often include special edition information. They also confirm authenticity. Protect the hang tag by keeping it attached. You can use a plastic tag protector. This prevents creasing, tearing, or damage. Avoid removing tags unless absolutely necessary. The tush tag, sewn into the Squishmallow, also provides important production details. Ensure it remains legible and intact. A damaged tag means lower value.

### Why Condition Maintains Value

Collectors prioritize condition. A Squishmallow described as "new with tags" (NWT) sells for the highest price. This means it has no flaws, no signs of wear, and both original tags. Even minor flaws like a detached tag, a small stain, or faded colors can reduce value significantly. Think of it like a collectible coin or stamp. Its condition directly correlates with its worth. Your care directly impacts future resale potential.

By following these simple care tips, you protect your valuable Squishmallow collection. You maintain their aesthetic appeal and market value. This ensures they remain treasured possessions.
