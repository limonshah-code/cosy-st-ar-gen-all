## How To Clean Docs At Home: Your Complete Guide

I love my Docs. They are more than just boots; they are a statement. Over time, however, my trusty Dr. Martens can collect dirt, scuffs, and grime. Keeping them fresh and looking great is important. This guide will show you exactly how to clean Docs at home. I will share easy steps to maintain their iconic look. We will cover everything from basic cleaning to stain removal and proper conditioning for different materials. You will learn how to make your boots last longer.

### Takeaway:

*   Gather the right tools: brushes, cloths, and cleaners.
*   Start by removing laces and loose dirt.
*   Clean leather gently with mild soap and water.
*   Address scuffs and stains with targeted methods.
*   Condition leather to keep it soft and prevent cracks.
*   Air dry boots completely, away from direct heat.
*   Store Docs properly to maintain their shape.

### Concise Answer:

To clean Docs at home, start by removing laces and loose dirt. Use a damp cloth with mild soap for leather, or specific cleaners for other materials. Gently scrub, wipe clean, then condition leather. Always air dry away from heat and store properly to maintain their appearance and lifespan.
