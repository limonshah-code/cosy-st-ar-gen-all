### Assembling Your Slime Valentines

Assembling your **free slime Valentines Day card printable** is a simple craft project. It does not require special skills. Children can help with many of the steps. This makes it a fun family activity.

#### What You Need:

*   Printed slime Valentines cards
*   Small containers of slime (store-bought or homemade)
*   Scissors or a paper cutter
*   Strong glue dots or double-sided tape
*   A hole punch (optional)
*   Ribbon or string (optional)

#### Step-by-Step Assembly:

1.  **Cut Out Cards:** Carefully cut each Valentine card. Use the guidelines on the printable. A paper cutter provides neat edges. If using scissors, take your time.
2.  **Prepare Slime:** Ensure your slime is in small, sealed containers. These containers prevent leaks. They also make the slime easy to attach.
3.  **Attach Slime:** Place a glue dot or piece of strong double-sided tape on the back of the slime container. Press it firmly onto the designated spot on the card. Ensure it is secure.
4.  **Punch Hole (Optional):** If you wish to attach the slime with ribbon, punch two small holes on the card. Punch them above and below the area where the slime container will sit. Thread a ribbon through the holes. Tie it around the slime container. This creates a secure and decorative attachment.
5.  **Personalize:** Have your child write their name on the "From" line. They can also add a special message. This makes each card unique.
