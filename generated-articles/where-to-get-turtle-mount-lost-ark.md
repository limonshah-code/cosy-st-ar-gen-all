Yes, there are three colors: Green, Blue, and Yellow. The Green Turtle is earned through a questline and island souls. The Blue Turtle comes from Max Rapport with Nineveh. The Yellow Turtle comes from Max Rapport with Mari.

### Q4: What are Island Souls and why are they important for the Turtle Mount?
Island Souls are collectible items found on various islands in Lost Ark. The "Island of Yearning Soul" and "Tooki Island Soul" are crucial for obtaining the Green Turtle Mount. They are rare drops from specific mobs or events on those islands.

No, you cannot. The Turtle Mounts are account-bound. Once you earn them, they are permanently tied to your roster. You cannot trade them, sell them on the Auction House, or purchase them from other players.
