## Dog Ate Corn Cob? Urgent Steps to Take Now

Few things cause more panic than seeing your beloved dog chew on something it shouldn't. Imagine coming home after a backyard barbecue to find a discarded corn cob, or worse, your dog looking guilty with pieces around them. This is a common, yet incredibly dangerous, situation many dog owners unfortunately face. If your dog eats a corn cob, immediate action is absolutely crucial to prevent severe health issues. I understand your worry; my own dog once grabbed something he shouldn't have, and the fear is real. This article helps you understand the significant risks involved with corn cob ingestion. We will identify alarming signs that suggest a problem and outline the exact steps you must take. We also cover how veterinarians diagnose and treat this emergency, and offer practical tips to keep your dog safe from future incidents. My goal is to equip you with the knowledge to protect your pet's well-being.

### Takeaway

*   **Contact your vet immediately.** Do not wait for symptoms to appear.
*   **Do not induce vomiting** without veterinary guidance.
*   **Watch for signs** like vomiting, lethargy, appetite loss, or abdominal pain.
*   **Prevention is key;** dispose of corn cobs securely.
