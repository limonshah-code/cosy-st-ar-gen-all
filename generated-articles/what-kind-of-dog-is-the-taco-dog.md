Chihuahuas are very small dogs. They usually weigh between 2 and 6 pounds. Their height ranges from 6 to 9 inches at the shoulder. They have large, erect ears and large, round eyes. Their small size makes them ideal for apartment living. They do not require a lot of space. Despite their small stature, they are surprisingly sturdy. They can be active and playful. ### Care and Health Needs for Chihuahuas

Chihuahuas have specific health considerations due to their size. They can be prone to certain conditions. These include dental problems, patellar luxation, and heart issues. Regular veterinary check-ups are essential. A balanced diet designed for small breeds helps maintain their weight. Overfeeding can lead to obesity. This creates health risks.

Their small mouths mean they can have crowded teeth. This makes dental hygiene important. Brushing their teeth regularly helps prevent issues. Chihuahuas also need protection from cold weather.
