## Size and Space Adaptation: Fitting Every Corner

Fitting a closet system into your available space is crucial. Ikea Pax and Aurdal come in different dimensions. Understanding these sizes helps you select the best fit for your room. We will compare how each system adapts to various spaces.

### Pax Dimensions

The Pax system is known for its wide range of sizes. It offers multiple widths, depths, and heights. You can find Pax frames in widths like 50 cm, 75 cm, and 100 cm. Depths typically include 35 cm and 58 cm. Heights often come in 201 cm and 236 cm. This variety allows for significant flexibility in planning.

Pax can create a freestanding wardrobe. It can also fill an entire wall to give a built-in appearance. The taller frames maximize vertical storage. The different depths let you choose between more compact or more spacious storage. This makes Pax suitable for both small bedrooms and larger dressing rooms.

When planning, measure your space carefully. Consider ceiling height and wall length. Remember to account for door swings or sliding door clearance. Pax's modularity means you can combine different frame sizes. This helps you achieve a custom fit even in awkward corners. It provides solutions for many unique room layouts.

### Aurdal Dimensions

Aurdal generally offers a more limited selection of standard sizes. Its frames tend to be shallower than Pax's. Common widths might be similar, but depths are often narrower, such as 35 cm. Heights usually match common room dimensions. This streamlined range simplifies the decision-making process.

Aurdal's shallower depth makes it ideal for smaller rooms. It also works well in narrow hallways or tight spaces. It does not protrude as much into the room. This can make a small area feel more open. If you have limited floor space, Aurdal offers an efficient solution.

The fewer size options mean less flexibility for very specific, non-standard spaces. However, it excels in common room layouts. Aurdal aims for simple integration. It provides functional storage without overwhelming a room. This system is a great choice for apartments or compact bedrooms.

Both systems need accurate measurements for successful installation. Pax offers greater versatility for unique spaces. Aurdal provides straightforward options for standard or smaller rooms. Choose the system that best matches your room's dimensions.

## Installation and Assembly: DIY Friendliness

Assembling Ikea furniture is a common task for many homeowners. Both Pax and Aurdal require assembly. However, their complexity differs significantly. We will examine the DIY-friendliness of each system.

### Pax Assembly Experience

Assembling a Pax wardrobe can be a more involved process. The system has many parts, especially when you include various Komplement interior organizers. Frame assembly requires careful attention to detail. Attaching backs, shelves, and rails can take time.

You will need basic tools like a screwdriver and a hammer. An electric drill can speed up the process. Always follow the instructions step-by-step. Planning your internal layout beforehand helps avoid redoing work. Despite the effort, many find the result of a custom Pax wardrobe rewarding.

Aurdal is designed for easier and quicker setup. It has fewer components and a more straightforward construction. The assembly instructions are typically simpler to follow. This makes Aurdal a better option if you prefer a less time-consuming DIY project.

You can often assemble Aurdal units alone. The frames are lighter and less complex. Connecting shelves and rails is generally more intuitive. Aurdal's focus on basic functionality translates to fewer steps. This makes the installation process less daunting for beginners.

Even with its simplicity, reading the instructions remains important. Ensure all screws are tightened properly for stability. The ease of assembly is a major selling point for Aurdal. It appeals to those who want functional storage without a long installation commitment.

## FAQ Section

### Can Pax and Aurdal systems be combined?

No, Pax and Aurdal systems generally cannot be combined directly. They have different frame construction, depths, and internal fitting systems. Their modular components are not interchangeable. It is best to choose one system and build your entire closet solution using its specific parts for seamless integration and stability.

### Is Ikea Pax worth the investment?

Yes, Ikea Pax is often considered worth the investment for its extensive customization. It allows you to create a truly tailored storage solution. Its robust construction and wide range of interior organizers provide excellent long-term value. If you need specific storage for a diverse wardrobe, Pax delivers.

### Is Aurdal suitable for a walk-in closet?

Aurdal can be suitable for a walk-in closet, especially if you prioritize simplicity and budget. Its clean lines and streamlined components create an open feel. However, Aurdal offers fewer deep storage options and less customization than Pax. For extensive walk-ins, you might desire Pax's greater flexibility.

### What are the main advantages of Pax over Aurdal?

The main advantages of Pax over Aurdal include far greater customization options, a wider range of sizes, and more robust construction. Pax's Komplement system offers diverse interior organizers. It can create truly personalized storage solutions. Pax also provides more aesthetic choices for doors and finishes.

### Does Aurdal offer sliding doors like Pax?

Currently, Aurdal typically features hinged doors or open frames. It does not offer the same wide array of sliding door options as Pax. Sliding doors are a signature feature of the Pax system. If sliding doors are a must-have for your space, Pax is the system you should consider.

### How long does it take to assemble each system?

Aurdal generally takes less time to assemble due to its simpler design and fewer components. A single Aurdal unit might take 1-2 hours. Pax, being more complex and customizable, often requires 3-5 hours or more for a medium-sized unit. Larger Pax wardrobes or those with sliding doors can take even longer, often requiring two people.
