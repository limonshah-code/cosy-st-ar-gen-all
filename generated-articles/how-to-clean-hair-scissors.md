## How To Clean Hair Scissors: A Comprehensive Guide

Do you want your hair scissors to last a long time? Regular cleaning is very important. Dirty scissors can pull hair, cause uneven cuts, and even spread germs. Many people do not realize how much a clean pair of shears helps their haircuts. Keeping your tools clean makes sure they perform at their best.

This article shows you **how to clean hair scissors** correctly. We will cover why cleaning matters and what items you need. You will learn easy steps for daily cleaning and deep disinfection. We also discuss how to keep your scissors sharp and rust-free. My goal is to help you maintain your valuable cutting tools.

### Takeaway

*   Clean hair scissors after every use to remove hair and debris.
*   Disinfect shears regularly to prevent germ spread.
*   Use specific tools like soft cloths, rubbing alcohol, and scissor oil.
*   Store scissors in a dry, safe place to avoid damage and rust.

Hair scissors require regular cleaning to stay sharp and hygienic. To clean them, wipe off hair, wash with mild soap and water, disinfect with alcohol, and then dry and oil them. This process removes debris, kills germs, and protects the blades from rust, ensuring smooth and precise cuts.

## Why Cleaning Your Hair Scissors Matters

Cleaning your hair scissors is not just about keeping them shiny. It directly impacts your cutting results and tool lifespan. Dirty scissors can quickly lose their edge. Hair, product buildup, and oil collect on the blades. This buildup causes friction, making the scissors harder to open and close. It also dulls the cutting edge faster.

Unclean scissors can also become a health concern. They can transfer bacteria and fungi from one person to another. This is especially true in professional settings. Even for personal use, you risk introducing germs to your scalp or skin if your tools are not sanitized. Regular cleaning prevents these problems. It ensures sharp blades and safe use every time you cut hair. This practice saves you money by making your scissors last longer.

Moreover, neglecting your scissors leads to rust. Moisture and leftover chemicals speed up corrosion. Rust ruins the blades and can make them unusable. Proper cleaning and drying prevent rust formation. You protect your investment when you care for your tools. A simple cleaning routine makes a big difference. It helps your scissors stay sharp and perform well for many years.

## Step-by-Step Daily Cleaning Process

Cleaning your hair scissors after every use is a good habit. This quick cleaning prevents hair buildup and keeps them hygienic. It takes only a few minutes but extends the life of your shears greatly. I always do this to keep my tools in top shape.

Here are the steps for daily cleaning:

1.  **Wipe Away Hair:** First, open your scissors fully. Use a soft, dry cloth or a paper towel to wipe off all loose hair from both blades. Pay attention to the pivot area. Hair often gets stuck there.
2.  **Remove Stubborn Debris:** If hair is stuck, use a small brush or an old toothbrush. Gently brush away any remaining hair or product residue. Make sure to get into the crevices.
3.  **Clean with Mild Soap (Optional):** If there is visible dirt or heavy product buildup, you can wash your scissors. Mix a small amount of mild dish soap with warm water. Dampen a cloth with this soapy water. Wipe down the blades and handles. Do not submerge the entire scissor. This prevents water from getting into the pivot screw.
4.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Use a clean cloth dampened with plain warm water. Wipe off all soap residue. Any soap left behind can cause problems later.
5.  **Dry Immediately and Completely:** This step is crucial. Use a clean, dry microfiber cloth. Dry every part of your scissors right away. Pay extra attention to the pivot screw and edges. Even a tiny bit of moisture can cause rust over time. You should not air-dry them.

This quick routine keeps your scissors ready for the next use. It also makes deep cleaning less frequent. Consistent daily care is key to maintaining sharp and functional hair scissors. ## Deep Cleaning and Disinfecting Hair Scissors

While daily cleaning removes hair, deep cleaning sterilizes your scissors. Disinfection kills bacteria, viruses, and fungi. This is very important for hygiene, especially if you share scissors or use them in a professional setting. You should deep clean your hair scissors at least once a week or after every few uses.

Follow these steps for a thorough clean:

1.  **Disassemble (If Possible):** Some scissors have a removable pivot screw. If yours does, carefully loosen the screw and separate the blades. This allows for better cleaning of all surfaces. If not, just open them wide.
2.  **Clean with Soap and Water:** Wash the separated blades and handles with warm water and mild soap. Use a small brush to scrub off any stubborn grime. Rinse them very well under running water to remove all soap.
3.  **Disinfect with Alcohol:** After washing and drying, grab your rubbing alcohol. Dampen a clean cloth or cotton swab with 70% or higher isopropyl alcohol. Wipe down all surfaces of the blades and handles thoroughly. Allow the alcohol to air dry completely. Alcohol evaporates quickly, leaving no residue. It effectively sanitizes the metal.
4.  **Consider Barbicide (for Professionals):** If you are a professional, consider using a barber-grade disinfectant like Barbicide. Follow the product instructions precisely for dilution and soak time. This offers a higher level of sterilization.
5.  **Dry Completely:** After disinfection, ensure every part is bone dry. Use a soft, clean towel. Even tiny water droplets can cause rust.
6.  **Reassemble and Oil:** If you disassembled your scissors, put them back together. Apply a drop of scissor oil to the pivot screw area. Open and close the scissors a few times to spread the oil. This lubricates the joint and protects against rust.

Deep cleaning ensures your hair scissors are safe and ready for precise cuts. It removes unseen threats and keeps your tools in excellent condition.

## Maintaining Your Shears: Oiling and Storage

Beyond cleaning, proper oiling and storage are critical for your hair scissors. These steps protect the blades and keep the pivot mechanism smooth. Without this care, even clean scissors can fail quickly. I have seen many good shears ruined by poor maintenance.

### Oiling for Smooth Operation

Oiling is vital for your scissors' performance. The pivot screw is the moving part that benefits most from lubrication.

*   **Frequency:** Oil your scissors after every deep cleaning, or at least once a week if you use them often. If you notice them feeling stiff, it's time to oil them.
*   **Type of Oil:** Use specific scissor oil or a light machine oil. Do not use household oils like cooking oil, as they can gum up the mechanism.
*   **Application:**
    1.  Hold the scissors with the blades open at a 90-degree angle.
    2.  Place one tiny drop of oil on the pivot screw where the two blades meet.
    3.  Open and close the scissors several times. This spreads the oil evenly.
    4.  Wipe off any excess oil from the blades with a soft, clean cloth. Too much oil can attract dust and hair.

Oiling reduces friction, making the blades move smoothly. It also helps prevent rust on the metal parts. This simple step keeps your scissors feeling new.

### Proper Storage to Prevent Damage

How you store your hair scissors impacts their longevity and sharpness. Incorrect storage can lead to dull blades, nicks, or even rust.

*   **Dry Environment:** Always store your scissors in a dry place. Humidity is the enemy of metal tools.
*   **Protective Case:** Keep your scissors in a dedicated leather pouch, plastic case, or stand. This prevents accidental drops and contact with other metal tools. Other tools can ding or dull the edges.
*   **Separate from Other Tools:** Do not throw your hair scissors into a drawer with combs, clips, or other heavy items. They need their own space.
*   **Blade Protectors:** Some scissors come with blade guards. Use them if you have them. They offer extra protection for the sharp edges.

Proper storage keeps your hair scissors safe from physical damage and environmental factors. This care ensures they are always ready for your next haircut. Remember, a sharp tool is a safe tool, and proper care maintains that sharpness.

## Common Cleaning Mistakes to Avoid

Even with good intentions, you can make mistakes when cleaning hair scissors. These errors can damage your tools or shorten their life. Knowing what to avoid is as important as knowing what to do. I learned some of these the hard way.

Here are common mistakes to steer clear of:

*   **Using Harsh Chemicals:** Never use bleach, oven cleaner, or strong household cleaners. These chemicals can corrode the metal, strip coatings, and dull the blades. Stick to mild soap and rubbing alcohol.
*   **Not Drying Completely:** This is a big one. Any moisture left on the scissors, especially around the pivot or blade edges, will cause rust. Always dry them immediately and thoroughly with a clean, soft cloth.
*   **Submerging Scissors in Water:** Avoid soaking your entire scissors. Water can get trapped in the pivot mechanism. This leads to rust from the inside out. It is better to wipe them clean with a damp cloth.
*   **Using Abrasive Materials:** Do not use rough sponges, steel wool, or abrasive cleansers. These will scratch the blades and ruin the finish. They can also dull the sharp edges. Stick to soft cloths and brushes.
*   **Ignoring the Pivot Screw:** The pivot screw area is where hair and gunk often collect. Neglecting this spot leads to stiffness and poor performance. Always clean and oil this area well.
*   **Sharing Without Disinfecting:** Using scissors on multiple people without proper disinfection can spread germs. This is a health risk. Always disinfect between clients or uses.
*   **Using the Wrong Oil:** Household oils or thick greases can attract dust and hair, making your scissors sticky. Use only light scissor oil or specific lubricating oil.

Avoiding these mistakes helps you keep your hair scissors in excellent condition. Proper care ensures they remain sharp, smooth, and hygienic for a long time.

Knowing when to call in an expert saves your scissors from permanent damage. I always recommend this step for serious issues.

Here are situations where you should seek professional help:

*   **Dull Blades:** If your scissors are pulling hair, bending it, or not cutting cleanly, they are dull. Home sharpening kits rarely achieve the precise edge needed for hair cutting. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning Hair Scissors

### How often should I clean my hair scissors?
You should wipe your hair scissors clean after every single use to remove hair and debris. A deeper clean with disinfection should happen at least once a week. For professional barbers or stylists, disinfection is necessary after every client to prevent the spread of germs. Consistent cleaning extends the life and performance of your shears.

### Can I put my hair scissors in a dishwasher or sterilizer?
No, you should never put hair scissors in a dishwasher. Dishwashers use harsh detergents, high heat, and strong water pressure. These can dull the blades, warp the metal, and cause rust. Professional sterilizers like UV light cabinets or barbicide solutions are different. They are designed for specific tool sterilization. Household dishwashers are not suitable.

### What should I do if my scissors start to rust?
If you see light surface rust, you can try to remove it. Use a soft cloth dampened with rubbing alcohol or a rust-removing oil like WD-40. Gently rub the affected area. Wipe off all residue. If the rust is deep or covers a large area, it is best to take your scissors to a professional sharpener. They can assess the damage and sometimes restore them.

### Is it safe to use household oil instead of scissor oil?
It is not ideal to use household oils like cooking oil or general lubricating oil. These oils can be too thick, attracting dust and hair, which gums up the pivot. They may also contain ingredients that can damage the metal over time. Always use a light, specialized scissor oil or machine oil designed for precision tools. This ensures proper lubrication without negative side effects.

### How do I store my clean hair scissors properly?
Store your clean and dry hair scissors in a protective case or pouch. This prevents the blades from touching other tools or hard surfaces. Direct contact can cause nicks or dull the edges. Keep the case in a dry environment to avoid moisture buildup, which leads to rust. Proper storage protects your investment.

### Can I sharpen my hair scissors at home?
While home sharpening kits exist, they are generally not recommended for hair cutting scissors. Hair scissors require a very precise edge. Amateur sharpening can often damage the blade angle, making the scissors perform worse. It is always best to send your hair scissors to a professional sharpener. They have the specialized equipment and skills to restore the edge correctly.
