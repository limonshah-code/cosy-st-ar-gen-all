## Do Dog Whiskers Grow Back? Understanding Their Regrowth

Have you ever looked at your dog's adorable face and noticed those long, stiff hairs around their muzzle? These are not just ordinary hairs; they are your dog's whiskers, also known as vibrissae. These sensory tools are crucial for your dog's daily life. Many dog owners often wonder what happens if these important hairs get cut or damaged. Specifically, the question arises: will a dog's whiskers grow back?
