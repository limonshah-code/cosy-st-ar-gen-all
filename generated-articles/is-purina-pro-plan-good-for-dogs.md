### Gradual Transition

When switching your dog to a new food, including Purina Pro Plan, a gradual transition is essential. Sudden changes can upset your dog's digestive system.

*   **Slow Introduction:** Start by mixing a small amount of the new Pro Plan food with your dog's old food. Over 7-10 days, gradually increase the proportion of the new food while decreasing the old food.
    *   **Day 1-2:** 75% old food, 25% Pro Plan
    *   **Day 3-4:** 50% old food, 50% Pro Plan
    *   **Day 5-6:** 25% old food, 75% Pro Plan
    *   **Day 7-10:** 100% Pro Plan
*   **Monitor for Changes:** Watch your dog for any signs of digestive upset. Look for changes in stool consistency, vomiting, or appetite loss. If issues arise, slow down the transition further.
*   **Benefits of Gradual Change:** A slow transition allows your dog's digestive system to adapt. It helps prevent stomach upset. This makes the switch a positive experience for your dog.

A proper transition ensures your dog smoothly adjusts to the new diet. It also confirms that Purina Pro Plan is good for your dog, as they can adapt without issues.
