### Rabies Vaccination and Antibody Titer Test

Rabies vaccination is a cornerstone requirement for dog entry into the USA. Every dog must have a current rabies vaccine. The vaccine must be administered by a licensed veterinarian. For dogs coming from high-risk rabies countries, like India, an additional step is often required: a rabies antibody titer test. This test measures the level of rabies antibodies in the dog's blood. It confirms the vaccine was effective. The test, called a Fluorescent Antibody Virus Neutralization (FAVN) test, must be performed by an approved laboratory. The blood sample must be taken at least 30 days after vaccination.

The FAVN test result must be above a certain level (0.5 IU/ml). This shows adequate protection against rabies. The test result is valid for a specific period, usually one year.
