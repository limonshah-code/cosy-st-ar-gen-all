## Engaging the Lesser Dog in Battle

Once you are walking through Snowdin Forest, a screen transition will signal a battle. This is your chance to **encounter a Lesser Dog**. The battle screen appears, and Lesser Dog takes its place as your opponent. It is a small, white dog wearing a helmet and carrying a tiny spear. Do not let its adorable appearance fool you into thinking it is not a "battle."

Lesser Dog's attacks are straightforward and not particularly difficult to dodge. It typically throws small, white dog barks at you that fly across the screen. These are easy to avoid by moving your soul (the small heart icon) in the battle box. Sometimes it might use its spear to attack. Simply observe the patterns and move your soul out of the way. Dodging is key to surviving any battle in Undertale.

My strategy always involves focusing on the "Act" menu immediately. You will see several options there. You can "Check" the monster to get a brief description and some stats. This tells you that Lesser Dog has high defense and low attack, but that is less important if you are sparing it. The most crucial action for Lesser Dog is "Pet." This is what you must choose.

Do not select "Fight" unless you intend to follow a Genocide route. Choosing "Fight" goes against the spirit of a true Lesser Dog encounter. Your goal is interaction, not destruction. The battle with Lesser Dog is more like a puzzle or a dialogue than a traditional combat sequence. Embrace this difference, and you will unlock the true charm of this character.

## The Art of Petting: Sparing Lesser Dog

This is where the real fun begins when you **encounter a Lesser Dog**. Once you are in battle, select the "Act" option. From there, you will see "Pet." Go ahead and choose it. What happens next is one of Undertale's most iconic and humorous moments. Lesser Dog's neck begins to stretch. It extends a little with each pet.

You must continue petting Lesser Dog. It is not a one-time action. Each time you select "Pet," its neck grows longer, humorously reaching outside the battle box. The game provides funny text descriptions along with the animation. These descriptions comment on its ever-growing neck and its increasing happiness. This interaction is core to its character.

I remember laughing out loud the first time I saw its neck disappear off-screen. It is truly a unique visual gag. You generally need to pet Lesser Dog about five to six times to achieve the desired outcome. After enough petting, Lesser Dog will become extremely happy and pacified. The game will then tell you that Lesser Dog is "A dog who has seen the world." At this point, you can spare it.

Once Lesser Dog is sufficiently petted, the "Spare" option in the "Mercy" menu will turn yellow. This indicates that you can successfully spare the monster without further interaction. Simply select "Spare," and the battle will end peacefully. This method is the definitive way to complete your **encounter with a Lesser Dog** on a pacifist run. ## FAQ Section

### Q: Is Lesser Dog a hard boss to beat in Undertale?
A: No, Lesser Dog is not a hard boss at all. It is one of the easiest monsters to deal with in Undertale. Its attacks are simple to dodge. The game encourages you to spare it rather than fight it. It's more of a humorous interaction than a true combat challenge.

### Q: How many times do I need to pet Lesser Dog to spare it?
A: You generally need to pet Lesser Dog about five to six times to fully spare it. Each pet action makes its neck grow longer. After a sufficient number of pets, the "Spare" option will become available in the Mercy menu, allowing you to end the battle peacefully.

### Q: What happens if I choose to kill Lesser Dog instead of sparing it?
A: If you choose to kill Lesser Dog, it will count towards your kill total. This action pushes you further down the Genocide route. It goes against the pacifist path of the game. Killing any monster impacts the game's narrative and endings significantly.

### Q: Is Lesser Dog related to Greater Dog in Undertale?
A: Lesser Dog and Greater Dog are both dog-themed monsters found in Snowdin, and they are both part of the Royal Guard. However, they are distinct encounters with different mechanics. Lesser Dog is smaller and requires petting, while Greater Dog is larger and involves different "Act" options to spare.

### Q: Can I encounter Lesser Dog multiple times in Undertale?
A: Yes, you can encounter Lesser Dog multiple times. It appears as a random encounter in various parts of Snowdin Forest. If you pass through the area again, you might find it again. This is true for many of the non-boss monsters in the game.

### Q: Does petting Lesser Dog give me any special rewards or items?
A: Petting Lesser Dog does not give you any special items or experience points. Its reward is purely in the humorous interaction and the satisfaction of sparing a monster. It is part of the game's charm and helps you complete a pacifist run, which leads to a specific ending.
