## The Shiloh Shepherd: A Majestic Breed Overview

The Shiloh Shepherd is another remarkable giant breed. It originated in the 1970s. Tina Barber started developing the breed. She wanted to preserve the original German Shepherd temperament and size. She sought to eliminate common health issues found in the modern German Shepherd. The Shiloh Shepherd aims for good health and a stable temperament.

Shiloh Shepherds are known for their calm and intelligent nature. They are often less high-strung than some German Shepherd lines. This makes them excellent family pets. They are patient and protective. They get along well with children and other animals if socialized properly. I appreciate their calm presence in a home.

This breed often has a softer, gentler expression than a typical German Shepherd. They come in various coat types, including smooth and plush. Their coats also come in many colors. This includes sable, black, and silver. Their overall appearance is one of grace and power. They have a strong but elegant build.

Male Shiloh Shepherds typically stand 28 to 30 inches tall. Their weight ranges from 100 to 140 pounds. Females are usually 26 to 28 inches tall. They weigh between 80 and 120 pounds. These dimensions put them squarely in the large dog category. They are impressive in size.

Shiloh Shepherds are known for their deep loyalty. They thrive on human companionship. They do not do well when left alone for long periods. They need to be part of the family. Their calm disposition makes them adaptable to various living situations. They need enough space and exercise.

## FAQ Section

### Are King Shepherds good family dogs?

Yes, King Shepherds are excellent family dogs. They are known for their calm, gentle, and protective nature. They bond deeply with their families. They are generally patient and tolerant with children. Proper socialization from a young age ensures they are well-behaved around everyone. Their loyalty makes them wonderful companions.

### Do Shiloh Shepherds shed a lot?

Yes, Shiloh Shepherds do shed a lot. They have a dense double coat. This coat sheds moderately year-round. They experience heavy shedding during seasonal changes, typically twice a year. Regular brushing, at least a few times a week, helps manage the shedding. Daily brushing is needed during peak shedding seasons.

### What are the exercise needs of King Shepherds?

King Shepherds have moderate to high exercise needs. They require daily physical activity to stay healthy and happy. This includes long walks, playtime in a securely fenced yard, or even hiking. About 60-90 minutes of exercise per day is a good guideline. Mental stimulation, like training or puzzle toys, is also important for them.

### Are Shiloh Shepherds easy to train?

Shiloh Shepherds are intelligent and eager to please. This makes them relatively easy to train. They respond best to positive reinforcement methods. Their sensitive nature means harsh training methods are not suitable. Consistency and patience are key. Early obedience training and socialization are vital for them.

### How much do King Shepherds and Shiloh Shepherds cost?

The cost of a King Shepherd or Shiloh Shepherd puppy can vary widely. It generally ranges from $1,500 to $3,500 or more. This depends on the breeder's reputation, lineage, and location. Beyond the initial purchase, consider ongoing costs. These include food, veterinary care, grooming, and training.

### Which breed is better for a first-time owner?

Both King Shepherds and Shiloh Shepherds are large, intelligent dogs. They require consistent training and socialization. A first-time owner comfortable with commitment might do well with either. Shiloh Shepherds are often described as more sensitive and less high-strung, which might make them slightly easier for a dedicated novice.
