## Understanding Minor League Baseball Structure

Minor League Baseball is a complex system. It helps develop players for Major League Baseball (MLB). MLB teams have a "farm system" of minor league affiliates. These affiliates are like training grounds for young players. The system has different levels. Each level presents a new challenge for players.

The levels range from Rookie League to Triple-A. Rookie League is the lowest level. Players often start here right after being drafted. They learn basic professional baseball skills. After Rookie League, players move up. The next steps include Low-A, High-A, and Double-A. Each promotion means harder competition. It also means better opponents. This steady progression helps players grow their skills.

Triple-A is the highest level in the minor league system. It is one step below Major League Baseball. Players at this level are often very close to being ready for the MLB. Some players even move between Triple-A and MLB frequently. This happens when teams need a player for a short time. They might call up a Triple-A player. If you are wondering, "what level are the El Paso Chihuahuas," the answer is Triple-A. They are at the top of this development ladder.
