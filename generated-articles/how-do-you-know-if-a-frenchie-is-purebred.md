## Spotting a Purebred Frenchie: Key Indicators

Do you own a French Bulldog? Perhaps you are thinking about getting one. You might ask: **"How do you know if a Frenchie is purebred?"** It is a common question. Many people wonder about their dog's background. Understanding if your Frenchie is purebred helps you know more about its health, behavior, and appearance. Purebred French Bulldogs have specific characteristics. This guide will help you learn to identify them. We will look at papers, physical traits, behavior, and DNA testing.

### Takeaway

*   **Check documentation:** Pedigree papers and kennel club registrations prove purebred status.
*   **Observe physical traits:** Purebred French Bulldogs match specific breed standards
