## Virtual Walls and Keep Out Zones

Sometimes you need to prevent your iRobot from entering certain areas. This is where virtual walls and keep-out zones become very useful. These features help you get your iRobot to clean a certain room, and *only* that room, by blocking off others. They act as invisible barriers for your robot. I use these often to protect pet food bowls or keep the robot away from cables.

Virtual Walls are physical devices. They emit an infrared beam. This beam creates an invisible line that your Roomba will not cross. You place these devices on the floor. They are battery-powered. You can set them to create a straight line barrier. Or, some models offer a halo mode. This creates a circular exclusion zone around the device. These are great for temporarily blocking doorways or protecting specific items. They work well for defining smaller, immediate boundaries.

Keep Out Zones are virtual barriers you draw on your home's map within the iRobot Home app. This feature is available on iRobot models with Imprint™ Smart Mapping. You open the map in the app. Then, you can draw rectangular zones on any part of the map. Your iRobot will avoid these zones during cleaning jobs. This is perfect for areas like a child's play area, a pet's litter box, or a section with fragile items. These zones are saved on the map. They apply to all future cleaning tasks unless you remove them.

Both virtual walls and keep-out zones give you control. They ensure your robot cleans only where you want it to. This helps protect your belongings. It also prevents your iRobot from getting stuck. Using these tools effectively means more efficient and worry-free cleaning. They are an essential part of targeted robot vacuum use. ## Troubleshooting Common Room Cleaning Issues

Even with the best setup, your iRobot might occasionally have issues cleaning a certain room. Knowing how to troubleshoot common problems saves you time and frustration. I have run into a few issues myself, and usually, they have simple solutions. We want our iRobots to clean a room in under 90 minutes, not get stuck for hours. One common issue is the robot missing parts of a room or skipping it entirely. First, check your map in the iRobot Home app. Is the room correctly identified and labeled? Are there any unassigned areas? Sometimes the map needs a refresh or a minor edit. If you have defined keep-out zones, make sure they do not accidentally block off parts of the room you want cleaned. Also, ensure no virtual wall devices are active near the room's entrance.

If your iRobot gets stuck frequently in a specific room, check for obstacles. Look for loose cables, fringes of rugs, or small items it might pick up. Clear these items from the floor. Also, check the robot's brushes and wheels. Hair or debris can tangle them. This affects its movement and ability to navigate. Regular maintenance, like cleaning the brushes, keeps your robot running smoothly. Compare this to how Dyson vacuums handle debris. Another issue might be the robot not returning to its base after cleaning a specific room. This often happens if the base is moved or if the robot cannot find its way back. Ensure the base is in an open area with good Wi-Fi signal. Avoid placing it behind furniture. If the problem persists, try a soft reset of your iRobot. This can resolve temporary software glitches. Sometimes, deleting the map and letting the robot create a new one can also solve persistent navigation problems. Remember to always keep the robot's software updated.

## FAQ Section

### Can my iRobot clean multiple specific rooms in one job?

Yes, you can select multiple specific rooms for your iRobot to clean in a single job. Using the iRobot Home app, after you tap "Clean" or "New Job," you can tap on each room you want the robot to visit. Your iRobot will then create a cleaning path to address all selected rooms sequentially before returning to its Home Base.

### What if my iRobot doesn't create a good map?

If your iRobot's map is not accurate, first ensure the robot has completed several full cleaning runs. Clear the floors of obstacles during these runs. If the map is still poor, delete the existing map in the iRobot Home app and allow the robot to re-map your home from scratch. Ensure good lighting conditions during the mapping process.

### Do all iRobot models support room-specific cleaning?

No, not all iRobot models support room-specific cleaning. This feature, known as Imprint™ Smart Mapping, is available on newer, higher-end Roomba models like the i7, s9, j7, and most Braava jet m6 models. Older or entry-level models typically navigate randomly and do not have mapping capabilities for targeted room selection.

### How do I tell my iRobot to clean only under the dining table?

You can tell your iRobot to clean only under the dining table by creating a "Clean Zone" in the iRobot Home app. First, ensure your dining room is mapped. Then, within the app, navigate to your map, select the option to create a new Clean Zone, and draw a box around the dining table area. Name it, and then you can select this zone for cleaning.

### Why is my iRobot getting stuck in a certain room?

Your iRobot might get stuck in a certain room due to several reasons. Look for physical obstacles like loose cables, clothes, or thick rugs. Check if the robot's brushes or wheels are tangled with hair or debris. Ensure the room has adequate lighting for navigation. Also, check for any accidental virtual wall or keep-out zone settings that might be confusing the robot.
