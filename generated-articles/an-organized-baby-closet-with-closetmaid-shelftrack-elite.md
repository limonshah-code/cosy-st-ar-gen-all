### Step-by-Step Installation of Closetmaid Shelftrack Elite

Installing the Closetmaid Shelftrack Elite system is a straightforward DIY project. You do not need specialized tools. First, gather your tools: a drill, level, measuring tape, pencil, and stud finder. Ensure your closet walls are clean. You might want to consider how to [declutter your closet](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-declutter-your-closet) before you begin installation. This prepares the space for the new system.

Start by marking the horizontal Shelftrack rail's position. Use your level to draw a straight line across the back wall. This line dictates the top of your system. Locate wall studs using your stud finder. Mark these stud locations on your line. You must mount the Shelftrack rail into at least two studs for stability. Drill pilot holes and secure the rail with the provided screws. This rail carries the main weight.

Once the main rail is secure, hang the vertical standards. These simply hook onto the horizontal track. Space them according to your plan. Typically, they are 24 inches apart for shelves and rods. Secure the bottom of each standard to the wall if specified by the instructions. Now you can attach your shelves, rods, and baskets. They simply clip into the slots on the vertical standards. Adjust them to your desired heights. Double-check everything for stability. Now you have a custom, sturdy baby closet system ready for use.
