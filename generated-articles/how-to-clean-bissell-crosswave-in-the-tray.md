## Keep Your Bissell Crosswave Tray Spotless and Fresh

Having a clean home is a wonderful feeling. You probably rely on your Bissell Crosswave to make your floors shine. This amazing machine washes and vacuums at the same time, saving you effort. However, sometimes we forget one crucial step: cleaning the machine itself, especially the tray. If you do not clean the Bissell Crosswave in the tray, dirt, grime, and odors can build up. This buildup harms your machine's performance and spreads unpleasant smells.

This guide will show you how to clean your Bissell Crosswave's tray thoroughly. We cover everything from daily rinses to deep cleaning methods. You will learn about the right tools, proper drying techniques, and simple tips for prevention. Following these steps ensures your Crosswave stays fresh and ready for action. Let's make sure your cleaning tool is as clean as your floors!

### Takeaway

Keeping your Bissell Crosswave tray clean is simple. It prevents odors and maintains machine function.
*   Rinse the tray after every use.
*   Deep clean the tray weekly or bi-weekly.
*   Use warm water and mild soap for manual cleaning
