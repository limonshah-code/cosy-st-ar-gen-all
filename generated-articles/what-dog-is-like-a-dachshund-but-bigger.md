## Seeking a Dachshund-Like Dog But Bigger? Find Your Match!

Many people adore the Dachshund. Their long bodies, short legs, and spirited personalities are truly unique. But what if you love these traits yet want a larger companion? You are not alone in this search. Many dog lovers look for a dog that is like a Dachshund but bigger, offering more presence without losing that charming low-slung appeal.

Finding a dog with similar physical traits or a comparable personality can feel tricky. This article will help you. We will explore several dog breeds that share key characteristics with the beloved Dachshund, but come in a larger package. We will look at their appearance, temperament, and care needs. Get ready to discover your next furry family member.

### Takeaway

*   **Basset Hounds:** The most common answer for a larger, short-legged, long-bodied dog.
*   **Corgis:** Offer a sturdy, low-to-the-ground build, similar to Dachshunds, but are heavier.
*   **Swedish Vallhunds:** A less common choice, they share the long body and short legs with an active herding nature.
*   **Mixes:** Dachshund crosses can result in a larger dog that still holds some desired traits.

### What dog is like a Dachshund but bigger?

The Basset Hound is often considered the dog most like a Dachshund but bigger. It shares the characteristic long body and short legs, paired with a similar hunting instinct. Other breeds such as the Welsh Corgi and Swedish Vallhund also offer a larger, low-slung appearance, though their body shapes and temperaments vary.

### Understanding the Dachshund's Unique Charm

Dachshunds are quite distinct among dog breeds. They have a very recognizable silhouette. Their long, muscular bodies sit on short, stubby legs. This build helps them hunt badgers in their burrows, which is what they were bred for.

Dachshunds are also known for their big personalities. They are often brave, intelligent, and quite stubborn. They form strong bonds with their families. Many people love these traits but wish the dog was a bit larger. They might want a more substantial dog for play or just a bigger presence in the home. It is important to know what you like about Dachshunds to find a good match. This helps in finding a dog that is similar but has more size.

### The Basset Hound: A Classic "Dachshund But Bigger" Contender

When people ask for a dog like a Dachshund but bigger, the Basset Hound is usually the first breed that comes to mind. Basset Hounds share the Dachshund's very long body and very short legs. They have loose skin, long droopy ears, and a soulful expression. A Basset Hound typically weighs 40-65 pounds, much larger than a 16-32 pound standard Dachshund.

Basset Hounds are scent hounds, like Dachshunds. This means they love to follow their noses. They are generally calm and good-natured at home. However, once they catch a scent, they can become very focused. They are friendly with people and other animals. Their deep bark is also quite famous. If you want a bigger version of the Dachshund's build, the Basset Hound is an excellent choice.

### Welsh Corgis: Sturdy and Low to the Ground

The Welsh Corgi is another breed that offers a similar low-to-the-ground stance, though their body shape differs slightly from a Dachshund. There are two types: the Pembroke Welsh Corgi and the Cardigan Welsh Corgi. Both have long bodies and short legs, but they are sturdier and more powerfully built than a Dachshund. Pembrokes weigh 25-30 pounds, while Cardigans can reach 25-38 pounds.

Corgis were bred for herding cattle. They are intelligent, active, and playful dogs. They have a lot of energy and need regular exercise. Their personalities are often described as big dogs in small bodies. While they do not have the exaggerated length of a Dachshund, their low stature makes them feel similar in many ways. They are a great option if you like the sturdy, short-legged look and want a dog bigger than a Dachshund.

### Swedish Vallhund: The Herding "Dachshund-Like" Dog

The Swedish Vallhund is a lesser-known breed that perfectly fits the description of a dog like a Dachshund but bigger. These dogs have a distinctive long body and short legs, similar to a Dachshund. However, they are a Nordic breed, known for their herding abilities. They weigh around 20-35 pounds, making them slightly larger than a standard Dachshund, but with a more robust build.

Vallhunds are intelligent and energetic dogs. They require mental stimulation and physical activity. Their herding instincts mean they love to chase and "herd" things. They are alert and make good watchdogs. Their tail can vary from bobtail to long, curled over the back. If you are looking for a unique breed with the Dachshund's body shape but a different background and slightly larger size, the Swedish Vallhund is worth exploring.

### Dachshund Mixes: A Larger Variation

Sometimes, the answer to finding a dog like a Dachshund but bigger is a Dachshund mix. When a Dachshund is crossed with a larger breed, the resulting puppies can inherit the Dachshund's distinctive body traits while gaining more size. The temperament of a German Shepherd Dachshund mix can be a blend of both parent breeds, potentially being loyal and intelligent with a spirited side. ### Key Considerations When Choosing Your Companion

Choosing any dog requires careful thought. When you are looking for a dog like a Dachshund but bigger, consider several factors. Size is important, but so are temperament, exercise needs, and potential health issues. A larger dog often means more food and space. ### FAQ Section

#### Are Basset Hounds good family dogs?
Yes, Basset Hounds are generally very good family dogs. They are known for being patient, gentle, and tolerant with children. Their calm nature makes them suitable for homes that can provide them with moderate exercise. They are also friendly with strangers and other pets, making them a popular choice for families.

#### Do Corgis bark a lot?
Corgis can be quite vocal. They were originally bred to herd, and barking was part of their job to move cattle. They often bark to alert their owners of new sounds or people. Training from a young age can help manage excessive barking. Their barks are usually sharp and loud.

#### What are the common health issues for short-legged dogs?
Short-legged dogs, like Dachshunds, Basset Hounds, and Corgis, often face spine problems. This is due to their long backs and short legs. Intervertebral Disc Disease (IVDD) is common. They may also be prone to obesity, which can worsen back issues. Joint problems like hip and elbow dysplasia can also occur in larger, short-legged breeds.

#### Is a Swedish Vallhund easy to train?
Swedish Vallhunds are intelligent and eager to please, making them generally easy to train. They respond well to positive reinforcement methods. However, their herding instincts mean they can be stubborn or try to "herd" family members. Early socialization and consistent training are important for a well-behaved Vallhund.

#### How much exercise does a Basset Hound need?
Basset Hounds need moderate exercise. Despite their calm demeanor, they are scent hounds and enjoy exploring. Daily walks are important to keep them fit and happy. They love to sniff around, so a secure yard is helpful. Over-exercising them in their youth should be avoided due to their joint structure.
