## Deep Clean Your Exterior Stone Surfaces

Your home's exterior stone adds character and beauty. It can be a stunning feature, enhancing curb appeal. However, outdoor elements like dirt, grime, mold, and mildew can diminish its appearance. Knowing **how to clean exterior stone** effectively protects your investment and keeps your property looking its best.

I often see people struggling with dirty stone walls or patios. They want to restore the original beauty of their stone. This article will guide you through the process. We will cover different stone types, the right tools, and safe cleaning methods. I will help you tackle common stains. You will learn how to maintain your stone for lasting beauty.

### Takeaway

*   **Identify Your Stone:** Always know your specific exterior stone type to choose the correct cleaning method and products.
*   **Start Gently:** Begin with the mildest cleaning solution and lowest pressure.
*   **Test in a Hidden Spot:** Before full application, test any cleaning product on an inconspicuous area.
*   **Protect Surroundings:** Shield plants, landscaping, and adjacent surfaces from cleaning solutions.
*   **Rinse Thoroughly:** Ensure all cleaning residues are completely washed away from the stone.
*   **Consider Sealing:** Apply a sealant after cleaning to protect porous stone and ease future maintenance.

To clean exterior stone, first identify its type. Then, use gentle methods like brushing with water and mild soap. For tough stains, choose specific stone-safe cleaners. Always test products on a small, hidden area. Rinse thoroughly to prevent damage. This keeps your outdoor stone looking its best.
