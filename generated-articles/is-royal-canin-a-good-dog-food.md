Some dogs have unique or severe dietary sensitivities. Standard Royal Canin formulas, even the specialized ones, might not address these rare issues. They can help identify specific allergens.

Many pet parents prefer dog foods with a focus on "whole" or "natural" ingredients. These brands often highlight single-source proteins, organic components, and human-grade elements. Finally, some owners simply disagree with Royal Canin's overall nutritional philosophy. If you hold strong beliefs about pet nutrition that diverge from Royal Canin's science-backed, ingredient-flexible approach, it might not be the right fit for you. ### Can I switch my dog to Royal Canin suddenly?
No, it's best to transition your dog to Royal Canin gradually over 7-10 days. Start by mixing a small amount of Royal Canin with their old food. Slowly increase the proportion of Royal Canin each day while decreasing the old food. This helps prevent digestive upset like diarrhea or vomiting.

### Does Royal Canin cause any digestive issues?
Royal Canin designs its formulas for high digestibility, which typically minimizes digestive issues. However, any new food can cause temporary upset during transition. Some dogs might be sensitive to specific ingredients.
