## Smart Storage Solutions for a Compact Entry

Maximizing space is key when you have a small entry. Our "before and after" shows how much difference smart storage makes. We decided against bulky furniture that would eat up valuable floor space. Instead, we focused on custom, built-in solutions.

We installed a narrow, floating bench. This bench provides a spot to sit down while putting on shoes. More importantly, it features hidden storage underneath. This is perfect for tucking away boots or extra gloves. It keeps the floor clear and maintains a clean look. This type of multi-functional furniture is a game-changer for small areas.

Above the bench, we added sturdy hooks for coats and bags. Hooks are much less intrusive than a full coat rack. They offer quick access to outer wear. We chose hooks that aligned with our overall design aesthetic. This kept the look cohesive. Below the hooks, we installed a small floating shelf. This shelf holds a decorative tray for keys and mail. It keeps essential items contained and organized.

These DIY storage solutions are ideal for small spaces. They ensure everything has its place. This helps us maintain an organized and clutter-free entry. ## Illusion of Space: Design Tricks for Small Entries

When dealing with a small entry, design choices are powerful tools. They can create the illusion of more space. We strategically used light colors. Our walls received a fresh coat of bright white paint. This immediately made the area feel larger and more open. Light colors reflect natural light, pushing the walls outward visually.

Mirrors are another essential element in small space design. We hung a large, decorative mirror on one wall. It serves a functional purpose for last-minute checks. It also reflects light from the adjacent living room. This reflection tricks the eye into perceiving a bigger area. The mirror added depth and visual interest without adding bulk.

We adopted a minimalist approach to decor. Too many items in a small entry can feel overwhelming and cluttered. We limited accessories to a few key pieces. A small plant, a scented candle, and a piece of art were enough. Each item served a purpose or added beauty without taking up much room. This keeps the space feeling airy.

Strategic lighting also played a role. We chose a flush-mount ceiling light that provided ample illumination. This light source is compact but bright. We also considered adding a small sconce if space allowed. Good lighting eliminates dark corners, which can make a small entry feel cramped. These design tricks combine to make our small entry feel far more generous than its actual footprint.

## FAQ Section

### How can I make my small entry look bigger?

To make a small entry look bigger, use light paint colors on the walls. Install a large mirror to reflect light and create depth. Choose compact, wall-mounted storage solutions instead of bulky furniture. Keep decor minimal to avoid visual clutter. Good lighting also helps brighten and expand the space.

### What storage solutions work best in tiny entryways?

For tiny entryways, consider floating shelves, wall-mounted hooks, and benches with hidden storage. Custom built-ins designed to fit specific dimensions are also excellent. Slim shoe cabinets or baskets under a bench keep items organized. Vertical storage options maximize limited floor space effectively.

### Should I use dark or light colors in a small entry?

Light colors are generally best for small entries. Light hues like white, cream, or pale gray reflect light, making the space feel more open and airy. Dark colors absorb light and can make a small area feel even smaller and more confined. Using light colors creates an illusion of greater size.

### What are cost-effective DIY ideas for an entryway?

Cost-effective DIY entryway ideas include painting walls a lighter color. You can also build simple floating shelves from reclaimed wood. Adding a decorative mirror from a thrift store or using peel-and-stick wallpaper for an accent wall offers an affordable upgrade. Update existing hardware for a fresh look.

### How long does a small entry DIY makeover take?

A small entry DIY makeover typically takes a few days to a week, depending on the scope. Painting and installing simple shelves might be a weekend project. More involved tasks like adding wainscoting or building custom furniture could extend the timeline. Proper planning helps manage the project duration.
