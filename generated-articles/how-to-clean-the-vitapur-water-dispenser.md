## Keep Your Vitapur Water Dispenser Sparkling Clean

Have you ever wondered about the freshness of the water flowing from your Vitapur water dispenser? It delivers clean water every day. Over time, however, these appliances can gather dust, grime, and even mineral buildup inside. A clean dispenser is crucial for your health and the longevity of the unit.

Maintaining your Vitapur water dispenser ensures you enjoy pure, refreshing water without worry. Neglecting regular cleaning can lead to unwanted tastes or odors. It also prevents the dispenser from working its best. This article provides a clear guide on how to clean the Vitapur water dispenser. We will cover everything from gathering supplies to deep cleaning internal parts and proper reassembly. You will learn the best methods for sanitizing your unit. We will also share tips for routine maintenance. Get ready to transform your water dispenser into a pristine source of hydration.

### Takeaway

*   Disconnect and drain your Vitapur dispenser completely before cleaning.
*   Use a vinegar or bleach solution to sanitize the water reservoir and lines.
*   Clean external surfaces and the drip tray regularly to prevent grime.
*   Rinse all components thoroughly to remove cleaning agents.
*   Perform routine maintenance to keep your dispenser operating well.

### How do you clean a Vitapur water dispenser?

To clean a Vitapur water dispenser, disconnect it, drain all water, and remove the bottle. Prepare a sanitizing solution using food-grade vinegar or diluted bleach. Pour this into the reservoir, scrub internal parts, and let it sit. Drain the solution, then rinse the dispenser multiple times with fresh water. Finally, clean the external surfaces and drip tray.

Keeping your Vitapur water dispenser clean is essential for many reasons. It is more than just about appearances. A clean dispenser directly impacts the quality of your drinking water. It also affects the lifespan of your appliance.

Over time, minerals from water can build up inside the dispenser's lines and reservoir. This mineral buildup, often called scale, can restrict water flow. It can also create an environment where unwanted particles grow. These particles can affect the taste and smell of your water. They can also pose health concerns if consumed regularly. I always advise people to clean their water sources.

Regular cleaning prevents these issues from starting. It keeps your water tasting fresh and clean. Think of it like brushing your teeth; you do it to keep them healthy. Cleaning your water dispenser keeps your water supply healthy. It helps avoid costly repairs too. A dispenser free of buildup works more efficiently. This means it uses less energy and lasts longer. Taking a little time for maintenance saves you trouble later. It ensures you always have access to safe, clean drinking water.

## Essential Supplies for Vitapur Water Dispenser Cleaning

Food-grade white vinegar is a popular and safe choice. It works well to dissolve mineral deposits and sanitize. Alternatively, a very diluted bleach solution can be used for deep sanitization. If you choose bleach, use only a small amount and rinse very carefully. You will also need clean, soft cloths or sponges. These help scrub surfaces without causing scratches. A small bottle brush or a pipe cleaner can reach tight spots. For tough mineral deposits, a soft-bristled brush might be useful. Finally, you will need a source of fresh, clean water for rinsing. This ensures no cleaning solution remains in the dispenser. I always use new cloths for this job.

## Step-by-Step Guide: Draining and Disconnecting Your Dispenser

Starting the cleaning process correctly is key. The first vital steps involve properly draining and disconnecting your Vitapur water dispenser. This ensures safety and prevents any electrical hazards. It also prepares the unit for a thorough internal clean.

Always begin by unplugging the dispenser from the wall outlet. Electricity and water do not mix. This step protects you from electric shock. Next, remove the water bottle from the top of the dispenser. If it is a bottom-load model, open the cabinet and slide the bottle out. Place the empty bottle aside. Now, you need to drain all the remaining water from the internal reservoirs. Start by dispensing water from both the hot and cold spigots until no more water comes out. You can use a pitcher or a bucket for this. This empties most of the water.

After emptying the spigots, locate the drain plug at the back or bottom of the dispenser. Place your large bucket or basin underneath this plug. Carefully unscrew the drain plug. Let all the remaining water flow out into the bucket. This water might contain mineral residue or small particles. Allow it to drain completely. Once all water is out, screw the drain plug back tightly. Now your dispenser is empty and safe to move for cleaning. This careful process protects you and the appliance.

*   Unplug the power cord from the wall outlet.
*   Remove the water bottle from the dispenser.
*   Dispense water from hot and cold spigots until empty.
*   Place a bucket under the drain plug at the back or bottom.
*   Unscrew the drain plug to fully drain the internal reservoir.
*   Screw the drain plug back securely once all water is out.

This systematic approach makes sure your Vitapur water dispenser is ready for deep cleaning. It prevents potential messes and ensures your safety throughout the cleaning process.

## Deep Cleaning the Water Reservoir and Spigots

After draining and disconnecting your Vitapur dispenser, it is time for the deep cleaning. The internal reservoir and spigots are critical areas. They need thorough cleaning to ensure your water is truly fresh. This part removes buildup that affects water quality.

### Cleaning the Internal Reservoir

The internal reservoir is where water sits before dispensing. This area needs careful attention. First, prepare your cleaning solution. For white vinegar, fill the reservoir with a mixture of one part vinegar to three parts hot water. If using bleach, add one teaspoon of bleach to a gallon of water. Never mix bleach and vinegar. This creates dangerous fumes. Pour your chosen solution into the reservoir. Let it sit for at least 30 minutes to an hour. For heavy buildup, you can let it sit longer, perhaps even a few hours.

While the solution sits, use a clean bottle brush or sponge to scrub the inside surfaces of the reservoir. Make sure to reach all sides and the bottom. This dislodges any mineral deposits or film. For tough calcium buildup, the vinegar solution is particularly effective. You can find specific tips on [how to clean calcium buildup on refrigerator water dispenser](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-calcium-buildup-on-refrigerator-water-dispenser). After scrubbing, drain the solution through the spigots and the drain plug at the back. Be sure to empty it completely.

### Cleaning the Spigots and Dispensing Area

The spigots are the final point of contact for your water. They can collect grime and dust. Dip a small brush or a cotton swab in your cleaning solution. Carefully scrub around the spigot nozzles. Pay attention to any crevices where dirt might hide. You can also press the spigot levers to let a small amount of the cleaning solution flow through. This cleans the internal mechanism of the spigots.

After cleaning with your solution, it is vital to rinse thoroughly. Refill the reservoir with fresh, clean water. Dispense several cups from both hot and cold spigots. Drain the remaining water from the back plug. Repeat this rinsing process at least two to three times. This ensures all traces of the cleaning solution are gone. I always rinse until I can no longer smell any cleaning agent. Proper rinsing is crucial for safe drinking water. For other dispenser models, methods might vary slightly, like those described in [how do you clean a Primo water dispenser](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-do-you-clean-a-primo-water-dispenser). This careful approach guarantees a clean and safe drinking experience from your Vitapur.

## Cleaning the External Surfaces and Drip Tray

Once the internal parts of your Vitapur water dispenser are pristine, shift your attention to the exterior. The external surfaces and drip tray are visible areas that gather dust, spills, and grime. Keeping these areas clean improves the dispenser's appearance. It also maintains a hygienic environment around your water source.

Start with the drip tray. This component catches spilled water and drips from the spigots. It often collects standing water, which can lead to mold or mineral stains. Remove the drip tray and its grate from the dispenser. Empty any accumulated water. Wash the tray with warm, soapy water. Use a sponge or brush to scrub away any stains or residue. For tougher stains or mold, a solution of equal parts white vinegar and water works well. Rinse the drip tray thoroughly with clean water. Let it air dry completely before returning it. Detailed instructions can be helpful, similar to [how to clean Primo water dispenser drip tray](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-primo-water-dispenser-drip-tray).

Next, clean the main body of the dispenser. Use a soft, damp cloth dipped in mild dish soap and water. Gently wipe down all exterior surfaces. This includes the sides, front panel, and any control buttons. Avoid using abrasive cleaners or scouring pads. These can scratch or damage the dispenser's finish. For stainless steel surfaces, use a cleaner specifically designed for stainless steel. This prevents streaks and maintains its shine. Pay special attention to the area around the spigots. This area often gets splashed. Finish by wiping all surfaces with a clean, dry cloth. This removes any soap residue and leaves the dispenser looking spotless. A clean exterior reflects the fresh water inside.

## Sanitizing with Vinegar or Bleach Solutions

Sanitizing is a key step in cleaning your Vitapur water dispenser. This process eliminates any remaining particles and keeps your water safe. You can choose between food-grade white vinegar or a highly diluted bleach solution. Both are effective, but they require careful handling.

### Using White Vinegar for Sanitization

White vinegar is a natural and safe sanitizing agent. It is food-grade, so it poses no health risks if traces remain. It also helps break down mineral deposits effectively. To sanitize with vinegar, fill the dispenser's reservoir with a solution of one part white vinegar to three parts hot water. I find this ratio works well. Let this solution sit in the reservoir for at least 30 to 60 minutes. For heavy scale or buildup, you can extend this time to a few hours.

While the solution sits, operate the hot and cold spigots briefly. This allows the vinegar solution to flow through the internal lines and spigots. Use a clean bottle brush to scrub the interior walls of the reservoir. This helps dislodge any stubborn film or deposits. After the soaking time, drain the entire vinegar solution. You can drain it through the spigots and the drain plug at the back of the unit. Refill the reservoir with fresh, clean water. Then, flush the system by dispensing water from both spigots. Drain the rinse water. Repeat this rinsing process two to three times. This ensures no vinegar taste remains.

### Using Bleach for Deep Sanitization

Bleach offers strong sanitization. However, it requires extreme caution due to its potency. Use bleach only if you are comfortable with its application and thorough rinsing. Prepare a solution of one teaspoon of unscented, regular household bleach per gallon of water. Never use more than this amount. Pour this diluted bleach solution into the dispenser's reservoir. Let it sit for no more than 15-20 minutes. Prolonged exposure can harm the dispenser parts.

During the soaking time, operate the spigots briefly to allow the bleach solution into the lines. After 15-20 minutes, drain the entire bleach solution from the dispenser. This includes draining through the spigots and the back drain plug. This is the most critical part: extensive rinsing. Refill the reservoir with fresh, clean water. Dispense water through the spigots, then drain from the back. Repeat this rinsing at least five to seven times. Continue rinsing until you can no longer detect any bleach odor. Any lingering bleach can be harmful. I personally prefer vinegar because it is safer. Choose the method that best suits your comfort level. Both methods achieve a sanitized dispenser.

## Rinsing and Reassembling Your Vitapur Dispenser

The final stages of cleaning your Vitapur water dispenser are crucial. Thorough rinsing removes all cleaning agents. Proper reassembly ensures your dispenser works correctly and safely. Skipping these steps can lead to bad-tasting water or appliance issues.

### Thorough Rinsing Procedures

After sanitizing with vinegar or bleach, rinsing is paramount. This step removes any cleaning solution residue from the internal components. Residue can affect water taste or, in the case of bleach, pose health risks. Start by refilling the dispenser's main reservoir with clean, fresh tap water. Use fresh water directly from your faucet. Do not put a new water bottle on yet.

Once the reservoir is full, dispense several cups of water from both the hot and cold spigots. This pushes the rinse water through the internal lines. Let the water run until the reservoir is nearly empty. Then, locate the drain plug at the back or bottom of the dispenser. Place a bucket underneath and remove the plug. Let all the remaining rinse water drain out completely. Screw the drain plug back securely. Repeat this entire rinsing cycle two to three times for vinegar solutions. For bleach solutions, you need to rinse five to seven times. Continue until you cannot smell or taste any cleaning agent. This meticulous rinsing ensures your water will be pure.

### Reassembling Your Dispenser

Once you are certain all cleaning solution is gone, it is time to reassemble. Ensure the drain plug at the back is firmly in place. This prevents leaks. If you removed the drip tray and its grate, reinsert them into their proper slots on the front of the dispenser. Make sure they click securely into place. Next, carefully place a new, full water bottle onto the dispenser. For top-load models, lift the bottle and align it with the piercing mechanism. For bottom-load models, slide it into the cabinet and connect the pump hose.

Allow a few minutes for the water to fill the internal reservoirs. You will hear gurgling sounds as the air escapes. Once filled, plug the dispenser back into the electrical outlet. Turn on the hot and cold functions if your model has them. Wait for the water to cool and heat to their respective temperatures. Before drinking, dispense a small amount of water from both spigots. This purges any air from the lines. Your Vitapur water dispenser is now clean, sanitized, and ready to provide fresh water. Remember, proper maintenance extends the life of your appliance.

## Regular Maintenance Tips for a Pristine Dispenser

Cleaning your Vitapur water dispenser thoroughly is a great start. However, consistent routine maintenance keeps it pristine between deep cleans. Simple habits can prevent buildup and ensure continuous fresh water. These tips will save you time in the long run.

Firstly, make it a habit to wipe down the exterior surfaces daily or every few days. Use a clean, damp cloth. This removes dust, fingerprints, and small splashes before they become stubborn stains. Pay special attention to the spigots. They are often touched and can accumulate grime. This quick wipe-down keeps your dispenser looking tidy and hygienic.

Secondly, clean the drip tray weekly. The drip tray catches spills and can quickly become a breeding ground for mildew. Remove it, empty any water, and wash it with warm, soapy water. Rinse thoroughly and let it dry. This prevents unpleasant odors and stains. You might also consider checking [how to clean a water filter](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-water-filter) if your Vitapur model includes one, as filter maintenance impacts water quality significantly.

Thirdly, always replace water bottles promptly when empty. Do not let an empty bottle sit on the dispenser for long periods. This exposes the internal reservoir to more air and dust. When replacing a bottle, quickly wipe the neck of the new bottle. This prevents outside particles from falling into the dispenser. If you have a bottom-load dispenser, ensure the pump intake tube is clean before inserting it into a new bottle. Lastly, mark your calendar for deep cleaning sessions. I recommend a thorough cleaning every 3-6 months. This schedule helps prevent significant mineral buildup and keeps your water quality high. Adhering to these simple practices will keep your Vitapur water dispenser in top condition.

## Troubleshooting Common Vitapur Cleaning Issues

Even with a detailed guide, you might encounter a few common issues when cleaning your Vitapur water dispenser. Knowing how to troubleshoot these problems can save you frustration. Most cleaning challenges have simple solutions.

### Dealing with Stubborn Mineral Buildup (Scale)

Stubborn mineral buildup, or scale, is a frequent problem, especially in areas with hard water. If a standard vinegar solution does not fully remove it, increase the vinegar's concentration. Try a 1:1 ratio of white vinegar to hot water. Let this stronger solution soak for several hours, or even overnight, if the buildup is severe. Use a non-abrasive scrubber or a dedicated bottle brush to gently scrub the affected areas. Do not use metal scrapers, as they can damage the reservoir's surface. After soaking and scrubbing, rinse exceptionally well, repeating the rinsing steps multiple times. For specific guidance on mineral removal, consult resources like [how to clean calcium buildup on refrigerator water dispenser](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-calcium-buildup-on-refrigerator-water-dispenser).

### Resolving Lingering Odors After Cleaning

A lingering odor after cleaning usually means the rinsing was not thorough enough. If you used bleach, this is a serious concern. Immediately repeat the rinsing process multiple times. Fill the reservoir with fresh water, dispense, and drain. For bleach, do this at least five to seven times until no smell remains. If the odor is from vinegar, it is less harmful but still unpleasant. Repeat rinsing two to three more times. You can also add a small amount of baking soda (one tablespoon per gallon of water) to a rinse cycle to help neutralize odors, then follow with more plain water rinses.

### Preventing Mold or Mildew Growth

Mold or mildew can appear in areas with constant moisture, like the drip tray. If you find mold during cleaning, sanitize the area with your chosen solution (vinegar or diluted bleach). For the drip tray, a scrub brush and a vinegar solution usually work. To prevent future growth, ensure the drip tray is emptied and dried frequently. Also, make sure the dispenser is in a well-ventilated area. After deep cleaning, allow all parts, especially the drip tray, to air dry completely before reassembly. This reduces moisture that supports mold growth. For other bottom-load models, understanding general maintenance, such as [how to clean Glacier Bay water dispenser bottom load](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-glacier-bay-water-dispenser-bottom-load), can offer additional insights into preventing mildew in internal, hard-to-reach areas. Addressing these common issues helps you keep your Vitapur dispenser in top condition.

## FAQs About Cleaning Your Vitapur Water Dispenser

### How often should I clean my Vitapur water dispenser?
You should deep clean your Vitapur water dispenser every 3 to 6 months. This prevents mineral buildup and keeps the water fresh. For the drip tray and exterior, wipe them down weekly to remove dust and spills. Regular cleaning ensures safe and great-tasting water.

### Can I use dish soap to clean the inside of my Vitapur water dispenser?
No, avoid using dish soap inside the water reservoir or lines. Dish soap can leave a residue that is hard to rinse completely. This residue can affect the taste of your water. Stick to food-grade white vinegar or a very diluted bleach solution for internal cleaning.

### What should I do if my water dispenser has a strange taste after cleaning?
A strange taste after cleaning often means cleaning solution residue remains. If you used vinegar, rinse the system several more times with fresh water until the taste is gone. If you used bleach, this is more serious. Rinse repeatedly, filling and draining the dispenser many times, until there is no bleach smell or taste.

### Is it safe to use bleach to sanitize my water dispenser?
Yes, it is safe if used correctly and sparingly. Use only one teaspoon of unscented, regular household bleach per gallon of water. Let it sit for no more than 15-20 minutes. Crucially, rinse the dispenser thoroughly at least five to seven times with fresh water to remove all bleach traces.

### How can I prevent mineral buildup in my Vitapur dispenser?
Regular cleaning every 3-6 months with a vinegar solution helps prevent mineral buildup. Vinegar is excellent for dissolving scale. In areas with very hard water, you might need to clean more frequently. Using filtered water can also reduce mineral deposits over time.

### Can I clean the hot water tank in my Vitapur dispenser?
Most Vitapur dispensers have sealed hot water tanks that are not easily accessible for cleaning by the user. The cleaning solution you pour into the main reservoir will circulate through some hot water lines. Always ensure the hot water function is off and the water is cool before starting any cleaning process.
