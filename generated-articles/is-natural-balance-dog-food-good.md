## Understanding Natural Balance Dog Food Quality

### A Look at Brand Philosophy and History

Natural Balance Pet Foods began in 1989. Dick Van Patten, an actor and animal welfare advocate, founded it. His vision was to create healthy, natural dog food. He wanted food made with premium ingredients. The brand quickly became known for its commitment to quality.

They emphasized transparency in their ingredient sourcing. The company also focused on strict safety testing. This approach built trust among pet owners. Natural Balance aimed to produce food that was both nutritious and safe.

### Core Product Lines and Offerings

Natural Balance is famous for its diverse product lines. Their Limited Ingredient Diets (LID) are especially popular. These diets use a single animal protein source. They also include a limited number of carbohydrates. This helps dogs with food sensitivities.

Other product lines include grain-free formulas and traditional kibbles. They offer options for weight management and healthy digestion. Pet owners can choose from many flavors. These often feature unique proteins like duck, salmon, or bison. This range makes it easier to cater to picky eaters or dogs with specific tastes.

### Limited Ingredient Diets (LID) Explained

Natural Balance is a pioneer in Limited Ingredient Diets. These formulas contain fewer ingredients. They are designed for dogs with food allergies or sensitivities. Common allergens like corn, wheat, soy, dairy, and eggs are often excluded.

An LID diet simplifies ingredient lists. This makes it easier for owners to pinpoint problematic ingredients. For example, a duck and potato LID formula contains only duck as the protein. Potatoes are the main carbohydrate. This reduces the chance of an allergic reaction. Many vets recommend LID diets for dogs experiencing digestive issues or skin problems. This approach focuses on essential nutrition.
