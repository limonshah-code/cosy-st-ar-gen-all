## Understanding the Bell Turtle in Elden Ring

The first time you see a Bell Turtle, it is hard to forget. These immense, multi-legged structures move slowly across the landscape. They carry a large, bell-shaped building on their back. Many players call them "Bell Turtles" because of their slow pace and the giant bell sound. These are not creatures in the traditional sense. They are ancient constructs called Walking Mausoleums. Each Mausoleum protects something valuable.

Their primary purpose is to allow you to duplicate boss remembrances. After defeating a major boss in Elden Ring, you receive a Remembrance. This special item lets you choose one of two unique boss weapons, spells, or incantations. Sometimes, you want both options, or you made a choice you regret. This is where the Bell Turtles become incredibly useful. They provide a way to get a second copy of a Remembrance. This means you can obtain both unique items from a single boss. For example, if you defeated Loretta, you could get both her Ash of War and her spell. I find this mechanic incredibly helpful for build variety.

These structures are often near significant landmarks or boss arenas. Their presence indicates a powerful foe once lived or died nearby. The lore suggests they may be tombs for demigods, or even parts of them. They are a silent reminder of the vast, complex history of the Lands Between. Knowing what to do with them greatly enhances your gameplay. You should always look for these giants as you explore. They offer unique chances to grow stronger.

## Locating Bell Turtles Across the Lands Between

Bell Turtles are rare but powerful features in Elden Ring. You will not find them in every region. They appear in specific, often open-world, areas. Each one offers a chance to duplicate a Remembrance. Knowing their locations saves you time. I always make a note of where they are on my map. There are a total of seven Walking Mausoleums in the game. Six of them have bells, and one does not. The ones without bells require a different approach to access.

Here are the general locations where you can find these Bell Turtles:

*   **Weeping Peninsula:** This is often the first Bell Turtle players encounter. It moves near the Castle Morne Lift Site of Grace. It is a good place to practice stopping one.
*   **Liurnia of the Lakes:** This region has three Bell Turtles.
    *   One is south of the Mausoleum Compound. It walks near the cliffside.
    *   Another patrols the west side of the lake, north of the Road to the Manor Site of Grace.
    *   The third is found within the consecrated snowfield. It is in the freezing mist.
*   **Mountaintops of the Giants:** One Bell Turtle walks the frozen plains here. It is near the Freezing Lake. This one can be tricky to stop due to the environment.
*   **Forbidden Lands:** There is one Bell Turtle here, close to the Grand Lift of Rold. It stands guard over a path.
*   **Deeproot Depths:** This region has a unique Walking Mausoleum. It does *not* have bells. This means you must access it differently.

When you spot a Bell Turtle, approach it cautiously. Some areas around them might have enemies. Always clear the path before you try to stop them. Knowing their exact spot helps you plan your approach. I always check my map for their movement patterns. This helps me get ready for the next step.

## Stopping a Bell Turtle: The Key to Access

Stopping a Bell Turtle is the first real challenge. These colossal structures walk endlessly unless you intervene. Their weak points are specific and require precise attacks. You need to target the glowing white skull-like growths on their legs. I learned this the hard way during my first attempt. I tried hitting the legs anywhere, but only the skulls worked. There are different methods based on the Bell Turtle type.

For most Bell Turtles, you will see a collection of white, brittle skull barnacles on their feet. These glow faintly. Your goal is to destroy all of them. Use any weapon for this task. Melee attacks are common, but ranged options like bows or spells also work well. Each successful hit will chip away at the skull. Once you break all skulls on one foot, it will shatter. Continue this process for all legs.

Here are the common steps:

1.  **Identify the Skulls:** As the Bell Turtle walks, look closely at its legs. You will see white, glowing skull barnacles clinging to them.
2.  **Target and Attack:** Get close enough to hit these skulls. They are typically near the ground.
3.  **Break All Skulls:** You must destroy every single skull barnacle. They shatter when broken. Once all are gone, the Bell Turtle will pause its movement. It will slowly kneel to the ground. This creates the opening you need.

Some Bell Turtles in areas like the Mountaintops of the Giants might have skulls higher up. For these, consider using a ranged weapon or a Spirit Ash that can attack from a distance. Sometimes, the terrain itself provides a height advantage. For instance, you might jump from a nearby rock formation. Always pay attention to the environment. Understanding how to manage these massive beings is a core part of your Elden Ring experience. When I find a turtle in my yard, I know what to do with it, but these Elden Ring turtles are a whole different beast!

### Stopping Specific Bell Turtle Types

Not all Bell Turtles stop the same way. Some are more straightforward, while others require a clever approach.

#### Ground-Based Skull Barnacles

Most Bell Turtles have skull barnacles that are easy to reach. They are low to the ground on the feet. Just run up and hit them. You can use any melee weapon. I usually prefer a quick weapon for this. This method is the most common. It is found in areas like the Weeping Peninsula and Liurnia. Always ensure no enemies are near before you begin. You do not want distractions while focused on the feet.

#### Jump-On Bell Turtles

A few Bell Turtles do not have skulls on their feet. Instead, their skulls are on their upper body or back. To reach these, you must find a high point nearby. Look for cliffs, large rocks, or collapsed structures. You need to jump onto the Mausoleum's back. This can be tricky. You must time your jump as the Bell Turtle passes by. Once on its back, you will see the skulls. Attack them like any other. This type is found in Liurnia, specifically the one near the Mausoleum Compound. Another similar one is in the Consecrated Snowfield. If you wonder how to tell how old a turtle is, these ancient constructs definitely give off an old vibe!

#### No-Skull Bell Turtle

There is one unique Walking Mausoleum in the Deeproot Depths. This one has no visible skulls. It also lacks bells. To stop this Mausoleum, you must attack the ghostly build-up around its feet. There are spectral figures clinging to its base. These are the weak points. Hit them until they dissipate. This will make the Mausoleum kneel. It is a different approach, but the goal is the same. Remember, each Bell Turtle has its own weakness.

## Entering the Walking Mausoleum: Inside the Bell Turtle

Once you successfully stop a Bell Turtle, its massive frame will slowly lower to the ground. It looks like it is taking a nap. This moment reveals the entrance. A large door on one side of the Bell Turtle will open. This is your gateway into its interior. I always feel a sense of accomplishment when the door finally opens. It is a sign of a puzzle solved.

Here is what to expect when you enter:

1.  **Wait for it to Kneel:** Do not rush. The Bell Turtle needs time to fully lower itself. The ground will shake slightly as it settles.
2.  **Locate the Entrance:** A large, archway-shaped door will appear on one of its sides. This door will glow faintly, indicating it is now accessible.
3.  **Go Inside:** Walk through the open door. You will find yourself in a surprisingly spacious interior. It often feels like a grand, empty hall. There are no enemies inside, so you can explore safely.

Inside the Walking Mausoleum, the atmosphere is quiet and solemn. You will find a single altar or a glowing pool of light. This is the place where the duplication process happens. The silence is profound. It emphasizes the importance of the ritual you are about to perform. It is a sacred space for the dead. ## Duplicating Remembrances: The Bell Turtle's True Power

The real reason to seek out Bell Turtles is to duplicate remembrances. This process allows you to get a second copy of a boss's unique item. It gives you more flexibility with your character build. I find this especially useful for New Game+ runs. I can get both weapons or spells from a boss. This helps me try out different strategies.

Here is how the duplication process works:

1.  **Interact with the Altar:** Inside the Walking Mausoleum, approach the central altar or glowing pool. An interaction prompt will appear.
2.  **Select a Remembrance:** A menu will pop up showing all the Remembrances you currently possess. You can only duplicate Remembrances from bosses you have *already defeated* in your current playthrough.
3.  **Confirm Duplication:** Choose the Remembrance you wish to duplicate. The game will ask for confirmation. Once you confirm, the duplicate Remembrance appears in your inventory.

There are a few important rules to remember for duplication:

*   **One per Mausoleum:** Each Walking Mausoleum can only duplicate *one* Remembrance per playthrough. Once used, that particular Bell Turtle becomes inactive. It cannot duplicate another Remembrance until your next journey through the Lands Between (New Game+).
*   **Specific Remembrances:** You can only duplicate Remembrances you have personally acquired. You cannot duplicate a Remembrance if you have already used the original one to get an item. Make sure you still hold the Remembrance in your inventory.
*   **Mausoleums without bells:** The Bell Turtle in Deeproot Depths is special. It can duplicate any Remembrance, including those of Demigods. The other Mausoleums (with bells) can only duplicate non-Demigod Remembrances. This is a subtle but important distinction. Always check which Mausoleum you are in.

This process is straightforward. It is a game-changer for many players. Having the ability to double your Remembrance power lets you explore more build options. It means you do not have to choose between two great items. You can have both! This greatly impacts how you approach boss rewards.

## Troubleshooting Common Bell Turtle Issues

Sometimes, things do not go as planned with the Bell Turtles. You might encounter issues when trying to stop them or enter their interior. I have run into a few snags myself. It is helpful to know common problems and how to fix them. Understanding these solutions saves time and frustration.

Here are some common issues and their fixes:

*   **Issue: The Bell Turtle won't stop.**
    *   **Cause:** You likely missed some skull barnacles. They are sometimes hard to see. Or you are hitting parts of the legs that are not the skulls.
    *   **Solution:** Circle the Bell Turtle and look very closely at all its feet. Make sure every single glowing skull is destroyed. Sometimes, one might be obscured by terrain or its movement. For the jumping Mausoleums, ensure you are hitting the skulls on its back. Make sure your hits register. Use a stronger weapon if your current one is too weak to shatter them quickly.
*   **Issue: I can't find the entrance after it stops.**
    *   **Cause:** The entrance door might be on a different side than you expect. Or you might not have waited long enough for it to fully kneel.
    *   **Solution:** Once the Bell Turtle stops, walk around its base. Look for the large, glowing archway. It usually appears on the side that faced away from you when it was moving. Give it a moment to complete its kneeling animation. The door will become visible and interactive.
*   **Issue: I already used this Bell Turtle, but it's still walking.**
    *   **Cause:** You used this specific Bell Turtle in your current playthrough.
    *   **Solution:** A Bell Turtle can only duplicate one Remembrance per playthrough. Once used, it will return to its walking state. You cannot use it again until you start a New Game+. You need to find a different Bell Turtle if you have more Remembrances to duplicate.
*   **Issue: The duplication menu does not show the Remembrance I want.**
    *   **Cause:** You may have already used that specific Remembrance. Or you are in the wrong type of Walking Mausoleum.
    *   **Solution:** Ensure you still have the original Remembrance in your inventory. If you already consumed it for an item, you cannot duplicate it. Also, remember that Bell Mausoleums (with bells) cannot duplicate Demigod Remembrances. The one in Deeproot Depths (without bells) is the only one that can duplicate any Remembrance. Check which type of Mausoleum you are in. If you want to know what kind of turtle I have, I need to look at its features. Similarly, knowing which Bell Turtle type you are at helps.

Solving these problems ensures you get the most from these unique structures. Stay patient and observe your surroundings.

If a Bell Turtle will not stop, you likely missed some skull barnacles. You must destroy all the glowing white skull-like growths on its feet or back. Circle the Mausoleum carefully and ensure every single skull is shattered. Use a ranged weapon if the skulls are high up or hard to reach.

### Why is a Bell Turtle inactive after I use it?
A Bell Turtle becomes inactive because it can only duplicate one Remembrance per playthrough. Once you use it, its purpose is fulfilled for that game cycle. To use it again, you must start a New Game+. If you need to duplicate another Remembrance, you must find a different, unused Bell Turtle.
