## FAQ Section

### Do Deer Head Chihuahuas grow larger than Apple Head Chihuahuas?
Deer Head Chihuahuas often appear larger due to their more elongated bodies and longer legs. They can indeed weigh slightly more or be a bit taller at the shoulder than Apple Heads. However, both types generally fall within the standard Chihuahua weight range, typically between 2 to 6 pounds, with some reaching up to 10 pounds. Their size difference is more about proportion than overall mass.

### Are Deer Head Chihuahuas healthier than Apple Head Chihuahuas?
Many believe Deer Head Chihuahuas are generally healthier due to fewer exaggerated features. Their less domed head may reduce the risk of hydrocephalus. Their longer snouts may lead to fewer breathing issues. However, both types can face common small dog health problems like patellar luxation. Overall health depends on genetics, diet, and care.

### Can a Deer Head Chihuahua be a purebred?
Yes, a Deer Head Chihuahua is a purebred Chihuahua. The "Deer Head" description refers to a specific set of physical traits, not a separate breed. They share the same genetic lineage as Apple Head Chihuahuas. The only difference is that their physical appearance does not meet the specific conformation standards set by some kennel clubs, like the AKC, for show purposes.

### What is the lifespan of a Deer Head Chihuahua?
Deer Head Chihuahuas typically have a long lifespan, similar to other Chihuahuas. They can live between 12 to 20 years, with an average of 15-18 years. Their longevity depends on factors like genetics, diet, exercise, and regular veterinary care. A healthy lifestyle contributes significantly to their overall well-being and extended life.

### Do Deer Head Chihuahuas have a soft spot on their head?
No, Deer Head Chihuahuas do not typically have a persistent soft spot on their head, known as a molera. While Chihuahua puppies of both types are born with one, it usually closes completely by adulthood in Deer Head Chihuahuas. Apple Head Chihuahuas are more prone to retaining a molera throughout their lives due to their distinct skull structure.
