## Recognizing a Labrador Retriever: Key Physical Features

If you want to identify a Labrador Retriever, several physical traits stand out. Understanding these features can help you recognize this wonderful breed. Their size is a key indicator. Labs are medium to large-sized dogs. Males typically weigh 65-80 pounds and stand 22.5-24.5 inches tall. Females are slightly smaller, usually 55-70 pounds and 21.5-23.5 inches tall.

Their head shape is distinctive. Labs have a broad skull and a strong, muscular neck. Their eyes are medium-sized and usually brown or hazel, expressing kindness and intelligence. Their ears are pendulous, meaning they hang close to the head. This gives them a gentle expression. Their muzzle is moderately long and tapers slightly.

The Labrador's coat is another important feature. They have a short, dense double coat. The outer coat is somewhat coarse, while the undercoat is soft and water-resistant. This coat protects them from cold water and harsh weather. As mentioned, colors are black, yellow, and chocolate. Their tail is often described as an "otter tail." It is thick at the base and tapers towards the tip, covered with dense fur. This tail acts like a rudder when they swim.

Their overall build is sturdy and athletic. They have strong, well-boned legs and compact feet. Their webbed paws are a unique trait, excellent for swimming. Recognizing these features helps you appreciate the breed's functional design. This design makes them perfect for roles like water rescue, just like Zuma.
