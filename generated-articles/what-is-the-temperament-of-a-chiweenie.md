### Takeaway

*   Chiweenies are loyal and affectionate companions.
*   They can be stubborn, requiring consistent training.
*   Early socialization is vital for good behavior.
*   They may bark at strangers or new sounds.
*   Regular exercise keeps them happy and healthy.

The **Chiweenie temperament** is a spirited mix of Chihuahua and Dachshund traits. They are generally loyal, affectionate, and playful companions. They often show intelligence but can also be stubborn.
