The unique coat of a Panda German Shepherd comes from a specific gene. This gene is called the piebald gene. It is a dominant gene that causes areas of white unpigmented fur. This gene affects how pigment cells develop in the skin. When a dog inherits this gene, it stops pigment from forming in certain areas. This results in the white patches you see.

This gene is present in many dog breeds. In German Shepherds, it is very rare. The first known Panda German Shepherd was born in 2000. Her name was Lewcinka. She was a purebred German Shepherd. Yes, Panda German Shepherds are 100% purebred German Shepherds.
