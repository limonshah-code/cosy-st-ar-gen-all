The capital city of the state of Chihuahua is Chihuahua City. It is located in the central part of the state. Chihuahua City serves as the political, economic, and cultural heart of the region. It is a major urban center with a rich history and modern amenities.

### What are some major geographic features of Chihuahua?

Chihuahua boasts two main geographic features. The Chihuahuan Desert covers much of its eastern and central parts. This arid region is North America's largest desert. The Sierra Madre Occidental mountain range dominates the western portion. It features deep canyons and forests.
