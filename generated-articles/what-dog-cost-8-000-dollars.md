### Tibetan Mastiff

The Tibetan Mastiff is a majestic and ancient breed. It originates from the Himalayan mountains. These dogs are known for their enormous size and thick coats. They served as guardians for livestock and homes.

Their rarity and impressive appearance contribute to their high price. A well-bred Tibetan Mastiff puppy can easily start at $8,000. Some top-tier puppies from champion lines sell for significantly more.

### Samoyed

Samoyeds are beautiful dogs with a friendly disposition. They have a striking white coat and a famous "Sammy smile." They come from Siberia, where they pulled sleds and herded reindeer.

Their unique appearance and charming personality make them popular. Quality Samoyed puppies from reputable breeders often cost $8,000 or more. This price reflects their pure lineage and excellent health.

### Akita

The Akita is a powerful and dignified breed from Japan. They are known for their loyalty and courage. Akitas were originally used for hunting and guarding.

Their strong presence and devotion make them desirable pets. High-quality Akita puppies, especially those with champion lineage, can fetch prices around $8,000. Some even cost more depending on the breeder and specific traits.

### Azawakh

The Azawakh is a sleek and elegant sighthound. It comes from West Africa. These dogs are known for their slim build and graceful movement. They were traditionally used for hunting and guarding.

Azawakhs are rare outside their native region. This rarity significantly drives up their price. You can expect an Azawakh puppy to cost $8,000 or more from a reputable breeder. Their unique history and distinct appearance contribute to this value.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes a dog so expensive?
A dog's high price comes from several factors. Rarity of the breed, champion bloodlines, and extensive health testing are key. Breeder reputation, the care provided to puppies, and ethical breeding practices also contribute. These elements ensure a healthy and well-socialized puppy.

### Are expensive dogs healthier than cheaper ones?
Not always. While reputable breeders of expensive dogs perform health screenings, no dog is guaranteed perfect health. Mixed breeds can sometimes be healthier due to greater genetic diversity. All dogs need regular vet care and a good diet.

### Can I find a high-priced breed for less money?
Yes, sometimes. Adoption from breed-specific rescues is one way. These dogs may be older or have specific needs. You can get a purebred dog for a much lower adoption fee. Researching less prominent breeders might also yield lower prices, but caution is necessary to avoid puppy mills.

### What are the ongoing costs for an expensive dog?
Ongoing costs are substantial. Expect to pay for premium food, regular vet check-ups, and specialized grooming. Training classes, pet insurance, and quality supplies also add up. These expenses are significant throughout the dog's life.

### Do all purebred dogs cost $8,000?
No, most purebred dogs do not cost $8,000. This price point is typical for rare breeds, those with exceptional pedigrees, or very specific characteristics. Many purebred dogs from reputable breeders cost between $1,500 and $4,000.

### Is an expensive dog worth the money?
The "worth" of an expensive dog is subjective. If you desire a specific breed with unique traits and can commit to all its needs, it may be worth it for you. The bond and joy a dog brings are priceless. This is true regardless of the initial cost.
