### Who said, "Man's best friend"?
The phrase "man's best friend" is widely attributed to George Graham Vest. He used it in an 1870 courtroom speech during a trial about a dog. His eloquent defense of a dog's loyalty became famous. This phrase has since become synonymous with the special bond between humans and dogs.
