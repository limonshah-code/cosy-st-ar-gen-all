The short-tailed weasel, or stoat, is smaller than the long-tailed weasel. It measures about 7 to 13 inches long. Its tail is shorter in proportion to its body. The least weasel is the smallest carnivore in North America. It is the smallest of Ohio's weasels. It measures only 6 to 8 inches long. Its tiny size makes it difficult to spot. This weasel has a short tail with no black tip. Its summer coat is brown on top and white underneath. In winter, most least weasels in Ohio turn entirely white.
