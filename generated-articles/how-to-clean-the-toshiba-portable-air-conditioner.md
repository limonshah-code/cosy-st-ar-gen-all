## Maintain Your Toshiba Portable AC with Regular Cleaning

Keeping your living space cool and comfortable often relies on appliances like your Toshiba portable air conditioner. These units work hard to battle the heat, but they collect dust and debris over time. A dirty AC can struggle to cool your room, use more energy, and even affect your indoor air quality
