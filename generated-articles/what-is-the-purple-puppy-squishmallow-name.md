## Caring for Your Purple Squishmallow Friend

Caring for your purple Squishmallow ensures it stays soft and squishy for a long time. Proper care extends its life and keeps it looking its best. Squishmallows are easy to maintain, but a few simple steps are helpful.

First, gentle cleaning is key. Squishmallows are surface-washable. You can spot clean small stains with a damp cloth and mild soap. Gently rub the area until the stain lifts. Then, blot with a clean, dry cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. These can damage the fabric and color.

For a deeper clean, you can machine wash your Squishmallow. Place it inside a pillowcase or laundry bag. Use cold water and a gentle cycle. Use a small amount of mild, dye-free detergent. This protects the colors, especially for purple Squishmallows. Avoid washing with other items that might have zippers or buttons. These could snag the plush.

After washing, air drying is best. Do not put your Squishmallow in the dryer on high heat. High heat can melt the polyester fibers and ruin the squishy texture. Instead, air dry it in a well-ventilated area. You can gently fluff it as it dries. This helps maintain its shape and softness. Some people use a low-heat, no-tumble setting on a dryer, but air drying is safer.

Proper storage also helps. Keep your Squishmallow away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade its vibrant purple color over time. Store it in a clean, dry place. Avoid placing it in dusty or damp areas. This helps prevent mildew or odors. Regularly rotating your Squishmallows in storage also helps them retain their shape. My own Squishmallows always get a gentle wash and then air dry on a rack. They come out looking brand new every time.
