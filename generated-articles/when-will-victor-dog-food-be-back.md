## Safe Alternatives to Victor Dog Food During the Wait

### Gradual Food Transition

When introducing new dog food, do it slowly. Mix a small amount of the new food with your dog's current food. Gradually increase the new food portion over 7-10 days. Reduce the old food amount at the same rate. This method allows your dog's digestive system to adjust. It minimizes upset stomach, diarrhea, or vomiting.

For example, start with 25% new food and 75% old food for a few days. Then move to 50/50 for a few more days. Finish with 75% new and 25% old. Finally, you can feed 100% new food. This slow approach makes the change much easier for your dog. Remember, consistency helps.
