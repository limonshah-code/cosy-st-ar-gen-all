Sheraton Hotels & Resorts is owned by Marriott International. Marriott acquired Sheraton as part of its purchase of Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide in 2016. Sheraton is not owned by Hilton Worldwide. Hilton is a separate, competing hotel company with its own distinct portfolio of brands.

## The Journey of Sheraton: From Independent Hotels to Global Brand

Sheraton Hotels has a long and interesting history. It started as a small hotel chain. The first Sheraton hotel opened in Springfield, Massachusetts, in 1937. Ernest Henderson and Robert Moore founded the company. They named it after a weather vane on one of their early properties. Over the decades, Sheraton grew steadily. It became a well-known name in the hotel industry. Its properties became famous for offering quality stays.

By the mid-20th century, Sheraton expanded significantly. It became one of the first hotel chains with international properties. The brand was known for its full-service hotels. These hotels often featured large ballrooms and conference facilities. Sheraton catered to both business travelers and leisure guests. Its distinct design and service standards set it apart. This historical growth paved the way for its future.

In 1998, a major change happened for Sheraton. Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide acquired the brand. This acquisition brought Sheraton into a larger family of hotel brands. Starwood had a diverse portfolio. It included brands like Westin, Le Méridien, and W Hotels. Sheraton became a cornerstone of Starwood's premium offerings. Its integration into Starwood helped modernize its image. It also expanded its global reach further.

Starwood invested in renovating many Sheraton properties. They worked to update the brand for contemporary travelers. Sheraton remained a vital part of the Starwood Preferred Guest (SPG) loyalty program. This program was very popular among frequent travelers. It offered valuable points and elite benefits. The Starwood acquisition was a pivotal moment for Sheraton. It set the stage for its next big transition. ## Marriott International's Extensive Hotel Portfolio

Marriott International stands as the largest hotel company in the world. It operates and franchises a vast collection of hotels and resorts. The company has a diverse range of brands. These brands cater to almost every type of traveler. Marriott's portfolio includes luxury, premium, select, and extended-stay properties. This broad selection allows them to capture a wide market share. They offer options from budget-friendly stays to ultra-luxury experiences.

The acquisition of Starwood Hotels & Resorts in 2016 was a monumental event. This deal created the hospitality giant we know today. Marriott added Starwood's 11 distinct brands to its existing 19 brands. This merger brought Sheraton into the Marriott family. It also included other popular brands like Westin, W Hotels, and St. Regis. The integration was a complex but successful process. It solidified Marriott's position as a global leader.

Sheraton holds a significant place within Marriott's premium brand category. It stands alongside other well-known names like Marriott Hotels and Delta Hotels. Sheraton aims to provide full-service experiences. These experiences include comfortable rooms, dining options, and meeting spaces. Marriott has focused on revitalizing the Sheraton brand. They have invested in modernizing many properties. The goal is to enhance the guest experience.

Marriott's powerful loyalty program is Marriott Bonvoy. This program merged the Marriott Rewards, Starwood Preferred Guest (SPG), and The Ritz-Carlton Rewards programs. Marriott Bonvoy offers members a wide array of benefits. These benefits include earning and redeeming points at thousands of hotels worldwide. Members also get elite status, exclusive offers, and personalized experiences. The integration of Sheraton into Bonvoy means guests can earn and redeem points at Sheraton properties. This provides a seamless experience for loyal Marriott customers. ## Hilton Worldwide: A Distinct and Powerful Competitor in Hospitality

Hilton Worldwide is another global leader in the hospitality industry. It operates independently from Marriott International. Hilton has a rich history dating back to 1919. Conrad Hilton founded the company. It has grown into a vast network of hotels and resorts. Hilton's portfolio is diverse. It spans luxury, full-service, focused-service, and extended-stay segments. This breadth allows Hilton to compete across various market tiers.

Hilton's brand family is impressive. It includes iconic names like Waldorf Astoria, Conrad Hotels & Resorts, and Hilton Hotels & Resorts. Other popular brands include DoubleTree by Hilton, Embassy Suites, Hampton by Hilton, and Homewood Suites by Hilton. Each brand targets a specific type of traveler. For example, Waldorf Astoria focuses on luxury. Hampton Inn offers consistent, comfortable stays. This distinct brand strategy helps Hilton maintain its strong market presence.

Hilton Honors is Hilton's award-winning loyalty program. It provides members with numerous benefits. These benefits include earning and redeeming points for stays. Members also enjoy free Wi-Fi, digital check-in, and late checkout. Elite status tiers offer additional perks. These perks include complimentary breakfast and room upgrades. Hilton Honors aims to reward loyal guests. It strives to enhance their travel experiences.

It is important to understand that Sheraton and Hilton are separate entities. They are direct competitors in the global hotel market. While both offer a wide range of hotel experiences, they operate under different corporate umbrellas. Travelers choose between them based on brand preference. They also consider location, price, and loyalty program benefits. Hilton continues to expand its global footprint. It introduces new brands and innovates its services.

## Why Hotel Mergers Happen: Benefits for Brands and Travelers

Hotel mergers and acquisitions are common in the hospitality industry. Large companies buy smaller chains for several strategic reasons. These deals help companies expand their market share quickly. They gain new properties and customer segments. This growth allows them to compete more effectively on a global scale. It creates a stronger, more resilient business.

One key reason for mergers is increased global reach. Acquiring another brand instantly adds hundreds or thousands of properties to a portfolio. This means the acquiring company can offer more destinations to its customers. It strengthens its presence in key markets. This expanded footprint benefits both the company and its guests. It makes travel more convenient and offers more choices.

Loyalty programs also play a huge role. When two hotel companies merge, their loyalty programs often combine. This creates a much larger program with more earning and redemption opportunities. For example, the Marriott-Starwood merger created Marriott Bonvoy. This combined program offered millions of members more hotels to choose from. It increased the value of their accumulated points. This is a significant draw for customers.

Mergers also create economies of scale. The combined company can negotiate better deals with suppliers. They can streamline operations and reduce overhead costs. This efficiency can lead to better profits. It can also allow for more investment in property renovations and guest services. While integrations can be challenging, the long-term benefits are often substantial. These benefits extend to travelers who gain more consistent service and greater loyalty program value.

## Choosing Your Stay: Comparing Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors

When you plan a trip, choosing the right hotel loyalty program matters. Marriott Bonvoy and Hilton Honors are two of the largest. They both offer great benefits. However, they have distinct features. Understanding these differences helps you pick the best one for your travel habits. Both programs reward frequent stays.

Marriott Bonvoy has an enormous global footprint. It boasts over 30 brands and thousands of properties. This vast selection means you can find a Marriott hotel almost anywhere you travel. Bonvoy points can be earned and redeemed across all these brands. The program offers several elite status tiers. These tiers provide perks like room upgrades, late checkouts, and bonus points. Bonvoy also has valuable transfer partners. You can convert points to airline miles with many carriers. This flexibility appeals to a wide range of travelers.

Hilton Honors offers a compelling loyalty program. It covers over 18 brands and a global presence too. Honors is known for its strong elite status benefits, especially at the Gold and Diamond tiers. These often include complimentary breakfast for two and executive lounge access. Hilton also makes it easy to earn points. They offer various promotions and co-branded credit cards. Honors points can be redeemed for free nights, experiences, and more. Hilton often has good point redemption values.

When comparing them, consider your travel patterns. If you value extensive global coverage and a vast luxury portfolio, Bonvoy might be better. If you prioritize consistent perks like free breakfast, Hilton Honors could be a stronger choice. Both programs have their strengths. They both offer ways to make your travel more rewarding. Many travelers belong to both programs. They choose which to use based on their destination and specific travel needs.
