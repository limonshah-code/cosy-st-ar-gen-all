## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is the Cane Corso a mix of two specific modern breeds?
No, the Cane Corso is not a mix of two specific modern breeds. Its lineage traces directly back to the ancient Roman Molossian dogs, particularly the *Canis Pugnax*. ### What was the primary purpose of the Cane Corso's ancestral development?
The Cane Corso's ancestors, the Roman Molossian dogs, were initially bred for war. After the Roman Empire, their purpose shifted to versatile working roles in rural Italy. ### Did the Cane Corso almost disappear from existence?

The Cane Corso is generally more athletic, agile, and active compared to other Mastiff types like the Neapolitan Mastiff or English Mastiff.
