A topical numbing cream is applied before the Clear + Brilliant treatment to minimize discomfort. Each Clear + Brilliant treatment session is relatively quick. After the initial numbing cream application, which takes about 30 minutes, the actual laser treatment takes approximately 15 to 20 minutes. This makes it easy to fit into a busy schedule, often referred to as a "lunchtime treatment."

#### Q3: What is the downtime after a Clear + Brilliant treatment?
Clear + Brilliant is known for its minimal downtime. The results from Clear + Brilliant treatments can be long-lasting, often for several months, especially with proper skincare and sun protection. Clear + Brilliant can help improve the appearance of mild acne scars, particularly those affecting skin texture and tone. It works by stimulating collagen and promoting cell turnover. #### Q6: Are Clear + Brilliant results permanent?
Clear + Brilliant results are not permanent because the aging process and environmental damage continue.
