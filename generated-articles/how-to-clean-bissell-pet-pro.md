## Easy Guide to Clean Your Bissell Pet Pro

Having pets brings so much joy to our lives. It also means dealing with pet hair, muddy paw prints, and occasional accidents on your carpets. Your Bissell Pet Pro deep cleaner is a true hero for keeping your home fresh and clean. But how do you keep your hero in top shape? Learning **how to clean Bissell Pet Pro** is vital for its long-term performance and efficiency.

A well-maintained machine works better and lasts longer. It ensures your carpets get the deep clean they need every time. This comprehensive guide will walk you through each step of cleaning your Bissell Pet Pro, from tanks to brushes. We will cover everything you need to know. Get ready to give your trusty cleaner the care it deserves.

### Takeaway

*   Empty and rinse dirty water tank after every use.
*   Clean brush rolls thoroughly to remove pet hair and debris.
*   Rinse filters regularly to maintain suction power.
*   Flush clean water tank to prevent solution buildup.
*   Allow all parts to air dry completely before reassembly and storage.

To clean your Bissell Pet Pro, start by emptying and rinsing both water tanks. Then, remove and clean the brush rolls and filter to clear pet hair and debris. Finally, wipe down the machine's exterior and allow all components to air dry completely before storing. This process ensures optimal performance and hygiene.

### Why Cleaning Your Bissell Pet Pro Is Essential for Pet Owners

Your Bissell Pet Pro works hard to remove dirt and stains. It pulls up pet hair, dander, and odors from your carpets. Over time, these collected residues can build up inside the machine. This buildup impacts its performance. A dirty cleaner cannot clean effectively.

Regular cleaning helps your Pet Pro operate at its best. It prevents clogs in hoses and spray nozzles. It also stops bad odors from forming inside the machine. Imagine cleaning your carpet with a machine that smells bad; the odor might transfer. This defeats the purpose of cleaning.

Furthermore, proper maintenance extends the life of your appliance. Pet hair and debris can strain the motor and brush mechanisms. Keeping these parts clean reduces wear and tear. You are protecting your investment when you keep your machine clean. It is a simple step that yields big results for your home and your cleaner.

Before you begin cleaning your Bissell Pet Pro, it helps to have all your tools ready. This makes the process smooth and quick. You will not need anything fancy, just a few common household items. Preparedness saves time and effort. It ensures you complete the task efficiently.

Having these items on hand means you can tackle any part of your machine. It ensures a thorough clean from start to finish. Ready your supplies, and let's get started on bringing your Pet Pro back to pristine condition. We want your machine to perform its best for every cleaning job.

### Step-by-Step: Cleaning the Dirty Water Tank and Filters

The dirty water tank is where all the extracted grime and pet mess ends up. It needs immediate attention after every cleaning session. Neglecting this step allows residue to dry and harden. This makes future cleaning much harder. I always empty mine right away.

First, remove the dirty water tank from your Bissell Pet Pro. Take it to a sink or utility tub. Pour out all the dirty water and debris. You will often see a mix of muddy water, pet hair, and lint.

Next, rinse the tank thoroughly with warm water. Shake it vigorously to dislodge any remaining gunk. You can use a small amount of mild dish soap for stubborn residue. Scrub the inside surfaces with a brush if needed. Pay attention to corners and edges where dirt can hide.

Your Bissell Pet Pro also has filters that require cleaning. These filters capture fine particles and protect the motor. Locate the filters, usually near the dirty water tank or motor housing. Carefully remove them according to your model's instructions.

Rinse the filters under warm running water. Gently rub them to remove trapped dirt and pet dander. Make sure no soapy residue remains if you used soap. Allow the filters to air dry completely before putting them back. A damp filter can harbor mold or damage the motor. For more detailed instructions on filter care, you can refer to guides like [how to clean Bissell vacuum filters](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-bissell-vacuum-filters). This step ensures proper airflow and consistent suction for your deep cleaner.

### Deep Cleaning Your Bissell Pet Pro Brush Rolls and Nozzle

The brush rolls are the workhorses of your Bissell Pet Pro. They agitate carpet fibers to dislodge dirt and pet hair. Because of this, they accumulate a lot of debris. This section needs regular deep cleaning to maintain effectiveness. I find that this is often the dirtiest part.

Start by turning off and unplugging your Bissell Pet Pro. Lay the machine on its back to access the brush roll area. Most Pet Pro models have a removable cover over the brush rolls. Release the latches or screws to open this cover. Lift out the brush rolls carefully.

You will likely find pet hair, carpet fibers, and other debris tangled around the bristles. Use scissors or a hair removal tool to cut through these tangles. Pull off all the hair and discard it. Be gentle to avoid damaging the bristles. A clean brush roll ensures effective scrubbing.

Rinse the brush rolls under warm water. Use a small brush to scrub away any caked-on dirt or residue. Make sure water flows freely through all parts of the brush. You want every bristle to be clean. For specialized brush cleaning tips, especially if your model is similar to others with specific brush designs, check out resources like [how to clean Bissell CrossWave Pet Pro brush](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-bissell-crosswave-pet-pro-brush).

After cleaning the brush rolls, turn your attention to the nozzle. The nozzle is the clear or opaque cover that suctions up the dirty water. Remove any visible hair or large debris from the nozzle opening. Use a small brush to clean inside the suction path if possible. A clogged nozzle reduces suction power. Rinse the nozzle with warm water. Ensure all channels are clear. Allow the brush rolls and nozzle to air dry completely before reattaching them. This prevents mildew and maintains suction efficiency.

### Maintaining the Clean Water Tank and Formula System

The clean water tank holds your fresh water and cleaning formula. While it may seem less prone to dirt, it still needs attention. Residue from cleaning solutions can build up. This buildup can affect the machine's performance. It can also lead to blockages over time.

After each use, empty any remaining clean water and cleaning solution from the tank. Do not store solution in the tank for extended periods. This prevents chemical degradation and potential clogs. Old solution can also become less effective. Always start with fresh solution for the best results.

Rinse the clean water tank thoroughly with warm water. Swirl the water around to dislodge any leftover solution residue. You can add a small amount of clear water and run it through the machine for a few seconds. This helps flush the internal formula lines. This simple flush prevents stickiness and clogs.

Pay attention to the tank cap and any small valves. These can also accumulate residue. Wipe them clean with a damp cloth. Make sure the cap seals properly when you put it back. A good seal prevents leaks during operation. A clean formula system means your carpets get the right amount of solution. This ensures a consistent, effective clean every time you use your Bissell Pet Pro.

### Cleaning the Hose, Attachments, and Exterior of Your Bissell Pet Pro

Many Bissell Pet Pro models come with a hose and various attachments for spot cleaning. These tools are invaluable for stairs, upholstery, and hard-to-reach areas. However, they also collect dirt and pet hair. It is easy to overlook these parts, but they need proper cleaning too.

First, detach the hose and any accessories from your main unit. Inspect the hose for any blockages. Pet hair or dried-up solution can get stuck inside. You might need to run warm water through the hose to clear it. A long, flexible brush can help dislodge stubborn clogs. Ensure the hose is clear before reattaching it.

Next, clean each attachment. These might include crevice tools, upholstery tools, or stain tools. Rinse them under warm water. Use a small brush to scrub away any grime. Pay close attention to suction openings and spray nozzles on the attachments. Dried solution can block these crucial parts. Clear nozzles ensure even spray and suction.

Finally, give the exterior of your Bissell Pet Pro a good wipe-down. Use a damp microfiber cloth to remove any dust, dirt, or splatters. This not only makes your machine look better but also prevents dirt from being redeposited on your clean carpets. I like to keep the outside as shiny as the inside. Allow all attachments and the hose to air dry completely. Store them neatly so they are ready for the next job. This complete cleaning keeps your whole system working efficiently.

### Reassembly, Drying, and Smart Storage for Your Bissell Pet Pro

After all the cleaning is done, proper reassembly and drying are the final, crucial steps. Rushing this part can undo all your hard work. It can lead to mold, mildew, or even damage to your machine. I always take my time with drying. Patience here prevents future problems.

Ensure every single component is completely dry before reassembling. This includes the dirty water tank, clean water tank, filters, brush rolls, nozzle, hose, and attachments. You can air dry them on a clean towel or drying rack. Allow several hours, or even overnight, for thorough drying. Air circulation helps immensely.

Once everything is dry, carefully reassemble your Bissell Pet Pro. Start by placing the brush rolls back into their housing. Secure the brush roll cover. Reinsert the dry filters into their proper slots. Attach the clean water tank and then the dirty water tank. Make sure all parts click securely into place. A loose part can affect performance or cause leaks.

Store your clean and dry Bissell Pet Pro in a cool, dry place. Avoid areas with extreme temperatures or high humidity. This helps maintain the integrity of its components. Keeping it in a protected spot also prevents dust from accumulating on your newly cleaned machine. Proper storage ensures your Pet Pro is ready to tackle the next pet mess with full power. ### Frequently Asked Questions About Cleaning Your Bissell Pet Pro

**How often should I clean my Bissell Pet Pro?**
You should empty and rinse the dirty water tank after every use. Deep clean the brush rolls, filters, and other components every 3-4 uses. If you have multiple pets or use it frequently, consider cleaning these parts more often. Regular cleaning prevents buildup and keeps your machine performing well.

**Can I use harsh chemicals or bleach to clean my Bissell Pet Pro?**
No, I do not recommend using harsh chemicals or bleach. These can damage plastic components, rubber seals, and internal mechanisms. Stick to warm water and mild dish soap for cleaning. For stubborn odors, a diluted vinegar solution can be used, but rinse thoroughly afterward.

**What happens if I do not clean my Bissell Pet Pro regularly?**
Not cleaning your Bissell Pet Pro can lead to several problems. You might notice reduced suction, clogged spray nozzles, and unpleasant odors. Pet hair and debris can also strain the motor, potentially shortening the machine's lifespan. Dirty internal parts make your machine less effective at cleaning carpets.

**How do I remove tangled pet hair from the brush roll effectively?**
To remove tangled pet hair, first, unplug the machine and remove the brush rolls. Use a pair of scissors or a specialized hair removal tool to carefully cut through the tangled hair. Then, gently pull the cut hair away from the bristles. Rinse the brush roll thoroughly afterward.

**Can I put Bissell Pet Pro parts in the dishwasher?**
Most Bissell Pet Pro parts are not dishwasher safe. High heat from a dishwasher can warp plastic components and damage seals. Always hand-wash parts with warm water and mild soap. Check your specific model's user manual for exact care instructions. Air drying is always the best method.

**How long should I let the parts dry before reassembling?**
Allow all cleaned parts to air dry completely for several hours, or even overnight. This is crucial to prevent mold and mildew growth inside the machine. Make sure there is no moisture left on any component before you put your Bissell Pet Pro back together.
