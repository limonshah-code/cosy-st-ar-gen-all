## Clean Your Miele Coffee Machine: A Complete Guide

Imagine starting your day with a perfect cup of coffee from your Miele machine. That rich aroma and smooth taste make mornings brighter. A clean machine is key to this daily ritual. Without regular cleaning, coffee quality drops. Your machine's lifespan also shortens significantly.

This guide helps you keep your Miele coffee machine in top condition. We will cover all cleaning aspects. You will learn about daily rinsing, weekly maintenance, descaling, and deep cleaning for various parts. Follow our simple steps for a consistently excellent coffee experience and a durable machine.

### Takeaway

Keeping your Miele coffee machine clean is easy. Follow these steps for great coffee and a longer machine life:

*   **Daily:** Rinse the water tank, empty the drip tray, and flush the milk system.
*   **Weekly:** Clean the brew unit, coffee grounds container, and milk components.
*   **Monthly/As Prompted:** Perform descaling using Miele tablets.
*   **Regularly:** Use Miele cleaning tablets for coffee residue.
