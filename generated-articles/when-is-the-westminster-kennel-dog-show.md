### FAQ Section

#### Is the Westminster Dog Show always in February?
No, the Westminster Dog Show is no longer always in February. For over a century, the show took place in February. In recent years, it moved its dates. It now typically occurs in May. #### How often does the Westminster Dog Show happen?
The Westminster Dog Show happens once a year. It is an annual event. Dog enthusiasts and competitors look forward to it every spring. This consistent schedule makes it a long-standing tradition. Each year brings new hopefuls and champions.

#### Can I attend the Westminster Dog Show in person?
Yes, you can attend the Westminster Dog Show in person. Tickets become available a few months before the event. You can buy them through the official Westminster Kennel Club website. #### What part of the Westminster Dog Show is Best in Show?
The Best in Show competition is the final part of the Westminster Dog Show. It usually happens on the last night of the event. Seven group winners compete for this top honor. Each winner represents one of the seven dog groups. The judge picks one dog as the Best in Show.

#### Is the Westminster Dog Show televised?
Yes, the Westminster Dog Show is televised. You can watch key parts of the show on television channels like FOX or FS1. Many events are also available via streaming services. Peacock often provides extensive live coverage. Check official schedules close to the show dates for specific broadcast times.

#### How long has the Westminster Dog Show been around?
The Westminster Dog Show is one of the oldest sporting events in the United States. It started in 1877. This makes it older than the Kentucky Derby and the modern Olympic Games. It has taken place every year since its beginning, except for a few instances.
