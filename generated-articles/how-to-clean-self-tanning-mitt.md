## Mastering How To Clean Your Self Tanning Mitt for Flawless Tans

Achieving a beautiful, streak-free self-tan often feels like an art form. You carefully exfoliate, apply moisturizer, and then smoothly glide the self-tanner onto your skin using your trusty mitt. This mitt is your best friend in the tanning process, ensuring an even application and protecting your hands from unwanted stains. But what happens to your mitt after it has done its job? It collects residual product, dead skin cells, and sometimes even bacteria.

Keeping your self tanning mitt clean is not just about hygiene; it directly impacts the quality of your next tan. A dirty mitt can lead to patchy results, streaks, and an uneven finish. Imagine putting effort into your tanning routine, only for a grimy mitt to ruin the outcome. Regular cleaning extends the mitt's life, saves you money, and ensures every application looks professional. This guide will teach you simple yet effective ways to clean your self tanning mitt, covering everything from immediate rinses to deep cleaning methods. We will make sure your mitt stays in top condition, ready for your next radiant glow.

### Takeaway

*   **Rinse Immediately:** Rinse your mitt with warm water and mild soap right after each use. This prevents tan from drying and staining.
*   **Hand Wash:** For a deeper clean, hand wash with gentle soap, gently scrubbing to remove all residue.
*   **Machine Wash:** Use a delicate cycle with cold water and mild detergent for a thorough wash. Place the mitt in a mesh laundry bag.
*   **Air Dry:** Always air dry your mitt away from direct heat or sunlight. This preserves its shape and material.
*   **Store Properly:** Store your dry mitt in a clean, dry place to prevent mildew and contamination.

To clean a self tanning mitt, immediately rinse it under warm water after each use. Apply a small amount of mild soap or body wash, gently rubbing the mitt's surface to remove tan residue. Rinse thoroughly until the water runs clear. Squeeze out excess water and air dry completely. This keeps the mitt hygienic and ready for your next application.

## Gathering Your Cleaning Arsenal: What You Need

Before you start cleaning your self tanning mitt, it is good to gather all your supplies. This makes the process smooth and efficient. You do not need many items, but having the right ones on hand helps achieve the best results. The main goal is to remove all the self-tanner residue without damaging the mitt's material.

First, you will need a mild cleansing agent. This could be a gentle hand soap, a body wash, or even a mild shampoo. Avoid harsh detergents or bleach, as these can break down the mitt's fibers and reduce its effectiveness. Next, you will need warm running water. A sink faucet is perfect for this. Sometimes, stubborn stains need a bit of extra effort. For these, an old toothbrush or a soft-bristled brush can be very helpful. These tools allow you to gently scrub away dried tan without harming the mitt. Finally, a clean towel and a place to air dry the mitt are essential. A drying rack or a clothesline works well to ensure proper air circulation. Having these simple items ready ensures you can tackle any tanning mitt mess with confidence.

## The Gentle Touch: Hand Washing Your Tanning Mitt

Hand washing is often the best and quickest way to clean your self tanning mitt, especially right after you finish applying your tan. This method is gentle on the mitt's fabric and effective at removing fresh residue. It is also perfect for those who prefer to avoid using a washing machine or only need a quick refresh. I find that immediate rinsing makes a huge difference in preventing stains.

### Immediate Rinse After Each Use

Right after applying your self-tanner, do not let the mitt sit with product on it. Take it to the sink immediately. Turn on warm water and rinse the mitt thoroughly. Gently rub the mitt against itself under the running water. You will see the self-tanner color washing away. Keep rinsing until the water coming off the mitt runs clear. This initial rinse prevents the tan from drying and setting into the fibers. It is a simple step, but it saves a lot of effort later on. This quick action is key to maintaining your mitt's cleanliness and extending its lifespan.

### Deep Cleaning Steps

For a more thorough clean, or if some residue remains after the initial rinse, follow these steps. First, wet the mitt completely with warm water. Apply a small amount of mild soap, body wash, or even baby shampoo directly onto the mitt. Gently work the soap into the fabric, using your fingers to rub and massage the mitt. Pay extra attention to any areas with visible tan residue. You can use a soft-bristled brush, like an old toothbrush, to gently scrub stubborn spots. Avoid harsh scrubbing, as this can damage the mitt. Rinse the mitt under warm running water again, squeezing gently to remove all the soap. Repeat this soap and rinse process if necessary until the water runs completely clear and no tan residue is visible. This careful hand washing ensures your mitt is fresh and clean. ## For a Deeper Clean: Machine Washing Your Tanning Mitt

Sometimes, your self tanning mitt needs a more vigorous clean than hand washing can provide. If you have used your mitt many times, or if it has very stubborn, dried-on tan stains, machine washing can be a good option. Not all mitts are machine washable, so always check the care label first. However, most standard self-tanning mitts made from microfiber or velvet can handle a gentle machine cycle. This method offers a deep clean with less manual effort.

### Preparing for Machine Wash

Before throwing your mitt into the washing machine, take a few preparatory steps. First, shake off any loose debris or dried tan flakes from the mitt. If there are any particularly large or hardened patches of tan, try to gently scrape them off with your fingernail or a soft brush. For mitts that are very saturated with product, a quick rinse under warm water beforehand can help. Next, and this is important, place your tanning mitt inside a mesh laundry bag. This protects the mitt from snagging or getting damaged by other items in the wash. It also prevents the mitt from getting lost or stuck in the machine's mechanisms. Washing it in a bag also keeps any tan residue from staining other clothes.

### Choosing the Right Cycle

When it comes to machine washing your self tanning mitt, selecting the correct wash cycle is crucial. You want to clean it effectively without causing any damage. Always choose a delicate or gentle cycle on your washing machine. This setting uses less agitation and a slower spin, which is much kinder to the mitt's delicate fibers. Use cold water instead of hot water. Hot water can set stains and potentially damage the mitt's material or adhesive. As for detergent, use a mild liquid laundry detergent. Avoid using harsh chemical detergents, bleach, or fabric softeners. Fabric softeners can leave a residue on the mitt that might affect future tan applications. Once the cycle finishes, remove the mitt promptly from the washing machine to avoid mildew or creasing. This ensures your mitt emerges clean and ready for proper drying. ## Drying Your Self Tanning Mitt Correctly: The Final Step

Proper drying of your self tanning mitt is just as important as cleaning it. If you do not dry your mitt correctly, it can lead to several problems. A damp mitt is a perfect environment for mildew and bacteria to grow, which makes it smelly and unhygienic. Incorrect drying can also warp the mitt's shape or damage its material, making it less effective for future applications. The goal is to get it completely dry without using harsh heat.

After washing, gently squeeze out any excess water from the mitt. Do not wring it aggressively, as this can stretch or damage the fabric. The best method for drying your self tanning mitt is air drying. Lay the mitt flat on a clean towel or hang it from a clothesline or drying rack. Make sure there is good air circulation around it. Avoid placing it in direct sunlight, as UV rays can degrade the mitt's material over time. Also, never put your self tanning mitt in a tumble dryer. The high heat can shrink the mitt, melt the fibers, or damage the internal foam layer. High heat can also cause any residual tan to bake onto the fabric, creating permanent stains. Allow the mitt to dry completely, which might take several hours depending on humidity. Once dry, your mitt will be fresh, clean, and ready for your next perfect tan application.

## Tackling Stubborn Stains and Buildup on Your Mitt

Even with regular cleaning, sometimes your self tanning mitt can develop stubborn stains or product buildup. These deep-set marks can be frustrating, making your mitt look unsightly and potentially impacting your tan. But do not worry; there are ways to tackle these persistent issues. The key is patience and the right approach. I have found that a little extra attention can bring a mitt back to life.

For particularly tough spots, start with a pre-treatment. Apply a small amount of mild liquid detergent or a specialized stain remover for delicate fabrics directly onto the stained area. Let it sit for about 10-15 minutes. This gives the cleaning agent time to penetrate and loosen the dried-on tan. After pre-treatment, gently rub the stained area with your fingers or a soft brush, like an old toothbrush. You can also try soaking the mitt. Fill a basin with warm water and a capful of mild detergent. Submerge the mitt and let it soak for 30 minutes to an hour. This helps to soften and dissolve stubborn residue. After soaking, proceed with hand washing as described earlier, ensuring you rinse thoroughly. Repeat the process if needed. If the stain persists, it might be time to consider replacing the mitt, as some tan dyes can permanently color the fabric over time. However, these methods often work wonders, restoring your mitt to a cleaner state.

## FAQ Section

### Can you put a tanning mitt in the washing machine?
Yes, most self tanning mitts are machine washable. You should place the mitt in a mesh laundry bag to protect it. Use a delicate or gentle cycle with cold water and a mild detergent. Avoid hot water and harsh cleaning agents. Always check the care label on your specific mitt first.

### How often should I clean my self tanning mitt?
You should clean your self tanning mitt immediately after every single use. This is the best way to prevent tan residue from drying and staining the fabric. A quick rinse with mild soap is usually sufficient for immediate cleaning. A deeper clean is needed periodically.

### What kind of soap should I use?
You should use a mild soap for cleaning your tanning mitt. Gentle hand soap, body wash, or even baby shampoo works well. Avoid harsh chemical detergents, bleach, or fabric softeners. These products can damage the mitt's fibers and affect future tan applications.

### How do you get fake tan out of a mitt?
To remove fake tan from a mitt, rinse it immediately with warm water and mild soap. Gently rub the mitt to loosen the tan. For stubborn stains, pre-treat with a mild detergent and let it soak for 10-15 minutes. Then hand wash or machine wash on a delicate cycle.

### Can I use fabric softener on my tanning mitt?
No, you should avoid using fabric softener on your tanning mitt. Fabric softeners can leave a residue on the mitt's surface. This residue can interfere with the even application of self-tanner during your next use. It might also reduce the mitt's absorbency.

### How do I dry my tanning mitt quickly?
The best way to dry your tanning mitt is by air drying it completely. Gently squeeze out excess water, then lay it flat on a clean towel or hang it on a drying rack. Avoid direct sunlight or using a tumble dryer, as heat can damage the mitt's material. Drying naturally helps keep its shape.
