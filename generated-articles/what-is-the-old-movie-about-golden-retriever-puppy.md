Absolutely! *Marley & Me* begins with the main characters adopting Marley as a tiny, mischievous Golden Retriever puppy. The film then follows Marley's life journey. It shows his growth from puppyhood to old age. This offers a comprehensive look at life with a Golden Retriever.

### Q3: What makes Golden Retrievers good movie dogs?
Golden Retrievers possess several traits ideal for movies. They are intelligent and highly trainable. They have a gentle and patient temperament. Their friendly demeanor makes them easy to work with on set. Their appealing appearance also contributes to their popularity.
