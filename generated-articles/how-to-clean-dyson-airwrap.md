## Unlock Peak Performance: How To Clean Your Dyson Airwrap

Hello there! My Dyson Airwrap is my styling secret, and I know it is for many others too. This amazing tool creates beautiful hair styles. However, like any precision device, it needs regular care. Learning how to clean Dyson Airwrap properly keeps it working perfectly. This guide helps you maintain your styler. We will cover cleaning the filter, attachments, and the main body. Keeping your Airwrap clean ensures its longevity and peak performance.

### Takeaway

*   Regularly clean the Dyson Airwrap filter for optimal airflow.
*   Detangle and wipe down attachments after each use.
*   Use a damp cloth for the main body and dry it completely.
*   Follow Dyson's specific instructions for filter cleaning tools.
*   Store your Airwrap and attachments in a clean, dry place.

To clean your Dyson Airwrap, first ensure it is unplugged and cool. Then, remove and clean the filter cage with the provided brush. Next, detach and wipe down all styling attachments. Finally, gently clean the main body with a soft, damp cloth.

### Understanding Why Cleaning Your Dyson Airwrap Is Essential

Regular cleaning of your Dyson Airwrap does more than just make it look good. It directly impacts the tool's performance. My Airwrap relies on strong, consistent airflow. Hair products, dust, and lint can block this airflow. This blockage makes the Airwrap less effective. It also causes the motor to work harder. Harder work means more wear and tear.

Dirty filters reduce suction and styling power. Attachments clogged with hair or product buildup do not work well. They can snag hair or distribute heat unevenly. A clean Airwrap provides better styling results. It helps maintain the tool's lifespan. You protect your investment by keeping it tidy. I always make sure to clean mine.

Ignoring cleaning can lead to long-term damage. The motor can overheat. The device might shut off unexpectedly. You might even notice a burning smell. These are signs your Airwrap needs attention. Proper care prevents these issues. It ensures every styling session is smooth and effective. I recommend cleaning it often.

### Your Dyson Airwrap Filter: The Heart of Airflow

The filter on your Dyson Airwrap is critical. It protects the motor from dust and debris. A clean filter ensures strong, consistent airflow. This airflow is essential for the Coanda effect to work. If the filter is dirty, the Airwrap cannot style hair effectively. You will notice weaker airflow and less powerful curling.

Dyson provides a special filter cleaning brush with your Airwrap. This brush is designed to remove trapped particles. You should clean the filter regularly. I typically clean mine every few weeks. This frequency depends on how often I use my Airwrap. It also depends on the amount of hair product I use.

Cleaning the filter is a simple process. First, unplug your Airwrap. Let it cool down completely. Then, slide down the pre-filter cage. This cage covers the main filter. Use the Dyson cleaning brush to gently clean the mesh filter. Rotate the brush while cleaning. This helps remove all debris. You can find more detailed instructions on [how to clean the filter on Dyson Airwrap](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-filter-on-dyson-airwrap).

#### H3: Step-by-Step Filter Cleaning Process

Cleaning the Airwrap filter requires a few steps. It ensures thorough removal of buildup.

1.  **Unplug and Cool Down:** Always disconnect your Airwrap from power. Let it sit for a few minutes. This prevents any accidental activation. It also allows the motor to cool.
2.  **Remove the Pre-Filter Cage:** Hold the main body of the Airwrap. Locate the silver band at the bottom. Gently slide this band down. The pre-filter cage will detach. It exposes the main filter.
3.  **Use the Cleaning Brush:** Take the small, cylindrical brush Dyson provides. Insert the brush into the exposed filter mesh. Twist and rotate the brush. You will see dust and hair come off. Continue until the filter looks clean. For specific tips, check out [how to clean my Dyson Airwrap filter](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-my-dyson-airwrap-filter).
4.  **Wipe Down the Cage:** Use a soft, dry cloth. Wipe down the detached pre-filter cage. Remove any lint or product residue. Make sure it is completely dry.
5.  **Reassemble:** Slide the pre-filter cage back onto the Airwrap. Push it up until it clicks into place. Ensure it is securely fastened.

This process keeps your Airwrap running efficiently. I have noticed a big difference in airflow after cleaning. For information on how often to do this, consider reading about [how often to clean the Dyson Airwrap filter](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-often-to-clean-the-dyson-airwrap-filter).

### Cleaning Your Dyson Airwrap Attachments

The various attachments are what make the Dyson Airwrap so versatile. From curling barrels to smoothing brushes, each one plays a vital role. However, these attachments collect hair and product buildup. This buildup affects their performance. Dirty brush bristles can pull hair. Clogged barrels might not curl effectively. Regular cleaning ensures they deliver perfect results every time.

I make it a point to clean my attachments after every few uses. This prevents excessive buildup. Disconnect the attachment from the main body. For brush attachments, gently pull out any trapped hair. A comb or a soft brush helps with this. You can also tap the attachment against a hard surface. This dislodges loose hair.

For all attachments, use a slightly damp cloth. Wipe down the surfaces. Pay extra attention to the air vents on the curling barrels. Product residue can block these small openings. A cotton swab can help reach tight spots. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaners. These can damage the finish. Let all attachments air dry completely before reattaching them. You can find more specific advice on [how to clean Dyson Airwrap attachments](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-dyson-airwrap-attachments).

#### H3: Specific Cleaning for Different Attachments

Each Airwrap attachment has unique features. Therefore, each needs specific attention.

*   **Curling Barrels:** Hair products and natural oils can coat these. After detaching, wipe them with a damp cloth. Make sure the small air holes are clear. A soft toothbrush can help dislodge stubborn residue from these tiny openings. Dry them completely before storage.
*   **Smoothing Brushes (Firm & Soft):** These collect the most hair. First, remove as much hair as possible by hand. A fine-tooth comb or the tip of a toothpick helps. Then, gently wipe the bristles and plastic base with a damp cloth. Be careful not to get the cloth too wet. Air dry with the bristles facing down.
*   **Round Volumizing Brush:** Similar to smoothing brushes, this collects hair. Remove hair strands from the bristles. Wipe the bristles and handle. Ensure no water enters the internal components.
*   **Coanda Smoothing Dryer:** This attachment often accumulates lint and dust. Wipe the surface with a damp cloth. Ensure the vents are free of obstruction. Keep it dry before storing or reuse.

By giving each attachment specific care, you ensure optimal performance. My styling results are always better with clean tools.

### Cleaning the Main Body of Your Dyson Airwrap

The main body of your Dyson Airwrap houses the motor and controls. While it does not get as much direct hair or product buildup as the attachments, it still needs cleaning. Dust, fingerprints, and residual hairspray can accumulate. Keeping the main body clean ensures good hygiene. It also maintains its sleek appearance. This part requires a gentle touch.

Always make sure the Airwrap is unplugged before you begin. Ensure it is completely cool. Never immerse the main body in water. Water can damage the internal electronics. A soft, slightly damp cloth is your best friend here. I use a microfibre cloth for this task. It picks up dust and grime effectively.

Gently wipe down the entire surface of the main body. Pay attention to the handle and the areas around the buttons. These areas are touched most often. They can collect oils and dirt from your hands. If there are any stubborn marks, a small amount of mild soap on the cloth can help. Always follow up with a clean, damp cloth to remove any soap residue. Finally, dry the main body thoroughly with a soft, dry cloth. This prevents water spots and ensures no moisture enters the device.

Preventative measures are key to long-term Airwrap health. You can reduce the frequency of deep cleaning by adopting simple habits. I always try to brush my hair thoroughly before using my Airwrap. This removes loose hair and reduces tangles. Less hair in the styler means less to clean later.

Using hair products wisely also helps. Apply sprays or mousses before drying, but avoid excessive amounts. Heavy or sticky products can leave residue. This residue builds up on attachments and inside the device. If you use a lot of product, consider cleaning your attachments more often. Wipe them down after each use. A quick wipe can prevent sticky buildup.

Storing your Airwrap properly is another important step. Dyson Airwraps come with a storage case. Use it! This case protects the device from dust and accidental damage. Store your Airwrap and its attachments in a clean, dry environment. Avoid humid places like bathrooms. Humidity can affect electronics. Proper storage keeps your Airwrap ready for its next use.

Regular, light cleaning is better than infrequent, heavy cleaning. I do a quick wipe-down of my attachments after most uses. I clean the filter every few weeks. This consistent routine keeps my Airwrap in top condition. It ensures I get perfect styling results every time. Good habits extend the life of your device.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### How often should I clean my Dyson Airwrap?
You should clean the filter every few weeks, or after about 20-30 uses. Clean attachments after every few uses or when you notice product buildup. A quick wipe of the main body can be done weekly. Regular, light cleaning is better than waiting for heavy buildup.

#### Can I wash Dyson Airwrap attachments with water?
You can wipe attachments with a damp cloth, but avoid submerging them in water. Water can get into electronic components or cause rust. Always dry them thoroughly before reattaching or storing. Never use harsh detergents.

#### What happens if I don't clean my Dyson Airwrap filter?
If you do not clean the filter, airflow will decrease. This weakens the Coanda effect, making styling less effective. The motor will work harder, which can lead to overheating and potential damage. This reduces the device's lifespan.

#### Can I use a vacuum cleaner to clean the Airwrap filter?
Dyson provides a specific cleaning brush for the Airwrap filter. While a vacuum might seem effective, it can be too powerful. It might damage the delicate filter mesh. Stick to the Dyson-provided brush for safe and effective cleaning.

#### How do I know if my Airwrap filter needs cleaning?
You will notice reduced airflow, weaker styling power, or the device might stop working intermittently. The white light on your Airwrap might flash. This indicates a clogged filter. Check the filter visually; you will likely see dust or lint.

#### Is it okay to use my Airwrap in the shower or near water?
No, never use your Dyson Airwrap in the shower or near standing water. It is an electrical appliance. Water exposure can cause electric shock or serious damage to the device. Always keep it away from wet environments.
