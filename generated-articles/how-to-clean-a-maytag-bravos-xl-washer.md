## A Fresh Start: Cleaning Your Maytag Bravos XL Washer

A clean washing machine makes your clothes smell fresh. We often forget our washer needs cleaning too. Your Maytag Bravos XL washer works hard, so it can build up dirt, detergent residue, and even mildew. This build-up affects how well your clothes get clean. It can also leave unpleasant odors.

I remember when my clothes started smelling musty even after a wash. I realized my washer was the problem. That's why I am sharing these important steps for cleaning your Maytag Bravos XL washer. This guide will walk you through everything. You will learn how to clean the drum, dispensers, and filters. We will also cover how to prevent future issues. Let us make sure your Maytag Bravos XL always runs efficiently.

### Takeaway:

*   Regularly clean your Maytag Bravos XL washer to prevent odors and residue.
*   Use a washing machine cleaner or vinegar and baking soda for the drum.
*   Clean dispenser drawers, gaskets, and filters manually.
*   Run a 'Clean Washer' cycle monthly for maintenance.
*   Leave the lid open between washes to promote airflow and prevent mildew.

To clean a Maytag Bravos XL washer, run a specific "Clean Washer" cycle with a commercial cleaner or white vinegar. Manually clean the detergent dispensers, the lid gasket, and the wash plate. Do not forget to regularly check and clean the washer's internal filter. This ensures fresh laundry and prevents odors.

### Essential Tools and Supplies for Washer Cleaning

Keeping your Maytag Bravos XL washer spotless requires a few basic items. You likely have most of these cleaning tools in your home already. Gathering everything before you start makes the process easier. I always make sure I have my supplies ready to avoid interruptions. This preparation helps me complete the task efficiently.

First, you will need a washing machine cleaner. Brands like Affresh or Tide Washing Machine Cleaner work very well. Alternatively, white distilled vinegar and baking soda are excellent natural options. These items effectively break down grime and eliminate odors. I often use vinegar and baking soda for a natural approach.

Next, gather some soft cloths or microfiber towels. These are useful for wiping down surfaces. A small brush, like an old toothbrush, helps clean tight spots. You might need a spray bottle to mix solutions. Lastly, gloves are a good idea to protect your hands from cleaners. Having these items on hand ensures a thorough cleaning for your Maytag Bravos XL washer.

### Cleaning the Maytag Bravos XL Washer Drum

The drum is where all the action happens. It needs regular cleaning to remove soap scum, mineral deposits, and mold. A dirty drum transfers grime to your clean laundry. My washing machine drum always feels so much cleaner after a good scrub. You can clean the Maytag Bravos XL washer drum using a self-clean cycle or with manual effort.

#### Running a Self-Clean Cycle

Your Maytag Bravos XL washer likely has a "Clean Washer" cycle. This cycle uses extra water and hot temperatures to clean the drum. It is a very convenient feature for regular maintenance. I run this cycle once a month.

Start by adding your chosen cleaner. If using a commercial washer cleaner, follow its package directions. If using natural cleaners, pour two cups of white distilled vinegar directly into the drum. Close the lid and select the "Clean Washer" cycle. Let the cycle complete fully. The machine will do all the hard work for you. This simple step keeps your drum in great shape.

#### Manual Drum Cleaning

Sometimes, the self-clean cycle is not enough for heavy build-up. You might need to clean the drum manually. This gives you direct control over stubborn spots. I often combine manual cleaning with the self-clean cycle.

First, wipe down the inside of the drum with a damp cloth. Look for any visible grime or residue. For tougher spots, make a paste of baking soda and a little water. Apply this paste to the stained areas. Let it sit for a few minutes to break down the grime. Then, scrub with a soft brush or cloth. You can also run an empty hot water cycle after this manual cleaning. This helps rinse away any remaining residue. Consider this more intensive deep cleaning if you encounter specific issues, like finding sand in your washer. ### Tackling the Dispenser Drawers and Gaskets

The detergent and fabric softener dispensers often get overlooked. They can accumulate sticky residue. This residue fosters mold and mildew growth. My dispenser drawers used to get very slimy before I started cleaning them regularly. The lid gasket is another area that traps moisture and grime.

To clean the dispenser drawers, carefully pull them out. Most Maytag Bravos XL models allow the drawers to be removed. If yours do not, clean them in place as best you can. Wash the removable drawers with warm, soapy water. Use a small brush or an old toothbrush to scrub away all residue. Rinse them thoroughly and let them air dry before putting them back. This simple step stops detergent from clogging.

The lid gasket, also called the seal, needs attention too. Use a cloth dampened with a vinegar solution to wipe all around the gasket. Pay close attention to any folds or crevices. Mold loves to hide in these areas. You might also find lint or even pet hair trapped there. If you frequently find pet hair in your laundry, you might want to look into [how to get dog hair out of washer](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-get-dog-hair-out-of-washer) for more specific tips. After cleaning, dry the gasket thoroughly to prevent new mildew growth.

### Maintaining Your Maytag Bravos XL Washer Filter

Your Maytag Bravos XL washer has a filter. This filter traps lint, coins, and other small items. A clogged filter can affect your washer's performance. It can also cause drainage issues or leave residue on clothes. I learned the hard way how important filter cleaning is.

Locating the filter usually involves looking at the bottom front of the machine. Refer to your Maytag Bravos XL user manual if you cannot find it. There is often a small access panel. Place a towel underneath the filter area. This catches any water that might leak out. Slowly unscrew or unclip the filter cap. Be ready for some water to come out.

Once you remove the filter, clean it thoroughly. Remove all lint, debris, and foreign objects. Rinse the filter under running water. Use a small brush to scrub off any stubborn residue. Inspect the filter housing for any gunk. Wipe it clean with a cloth. Then, screw the filter back into place securely. Cleaning this filter regularly is key. It ensures your machine runs well. ### Eliminating Stubborn Odors and Mildew

Unpleasant odors are a common complaint with washing machines. These smells often come from mildew and bacteria growth. The Maytag Bravos XL, like any washer, can develop these issues. I remember thinking my washer would never smell good again. But with the right steps, you can get rid of those musty smells.

White vinegar is an excellent natural deodorizer and disinfectant. Run an empty hot water cycle with two cups of white vinegar poured directly into the drum. This helps sanitize the machine and breaks down odor-causing bacteria. For an extra boost, add half a cup of baking soda during the rinse cycle. Baking soda absorbs odors. It also helps to freshen the machine. This combination is very effective.

After cleaning, always leave your washer lid open between washes. This allows air to circulate inside the drum. Air circulation prevents moisture from getting trapped. Trapped moisture is what leads to mildew growth and bad smells. You can also wipe down the drum and gasket after each use. These habits keep your washer smelling fresh. A consistently deep-cleaned washing machine prevents many issues. ### Preventing Future Build-up and Stains

Prevention is always better than cure. Taking proactive steps keeps your Maytag Bravos XL washer clean. It also extends its lifespan. I have found that a few simple habits make a big difference. These steps save you time and effort in the long run.

First, use the correct amount of detergent. Using too much detergent creates excessive suds. These suds leave residue in your machine and on your clothes. This residue is a breeding ground for mold. Always check the detergent instructions. High-efficiency (HE) washers like the Maytag Bravos XL require special HE detergent. They also need less of it.

Second, avoid leaving wet clothes in the washer for long periods. Remove laundry promptly after the cycle finishes. This prevents mildew from forming on clothes and inside the drum. Finally, continue your monthly cleaning routine. Run the "Clean Washer" cycle with vinegar or a commercial cleaner regularly. Wipe down the dispenser and lid gasket after each use. These small efforts ensure your Maytag Bravos XL stays sparkling.

### FAQ Section

#### How often should I clean my Maytag Bravos XL washer?
You should clean your Maytag Bravos XL washer once a month. This regular cleaning prevents detergent build-up and mildew. It also keeps your clothes smelling fresh. If you use your washer very frequently, consider cleaning it every two to three weeks.

#### Can I use bleach to clean my Maytag Bravos XL washer?
Yes, you can use bleach. Bleach is a powerful disinfectant. Pour one cup of bleach into the dispenser or directly into the drum. Then, run a "Clean Washer" cycle or a hot water cycle. Always ensure the washer is empty when using bleach.

#### What causes the Maytag Bravos XL washer to smell bad?
Bad smells often come from mildew and bacteria. These grow in the damp, dark areas of the washer drum and gasket. Residue from detergent and fabric softener also contributes to odors. Using too much detergent or leaving the lid closed traps moisture, making smells worse.

#### How do I clean the detergent dispenser in my Maytag Bravos XL?
Remove the dispenser drawer if possible. Wash it thoroughly with warm, soapy water and a brush. For non-removable dispensers, wipe them down with a cloth soaked in vinegar. Pay attention to crevices where residue collects. Rinse and dry completely.

#### Is a commercial washer cleaner better than vinegar for cleaning?
Both commercial cleaners and vinegar are effective. Commercial cleaners are formulated to tackle specific washer issues. Vinegar offers a natural, cost-effective alternative. It works well for general cleaning and odor removal. Choose based on your preference and the level of cleaning needed.

#### Why is my Maytag Bravos XL leaving residue on my clothes?
Residue on clothes often points to a dirty washer. It can be due to excessive detergent use or a clogged filter. Hard water minerals or undissolved detergent can also cling to fabrics. Regular cleaning of the drum, dispensers, and filter can solve this problem.
