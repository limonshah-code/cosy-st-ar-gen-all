### Is the Westminster Dog Show held annually?

Yes, the Westminster Dog Show is an annual event. It has been held every year since 1877, making it the second-longest continuously held sporting event in the United States, after the Kentucky Derby. Its consistent annual staging highlights its enduring popularity and status as a major tradition in the world of purebred dogs. The show consistently brings top canines to New York City.
