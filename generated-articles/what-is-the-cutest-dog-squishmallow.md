## Caring for Your Cutest Dog Squishmallow

Once you find your cutest dog Squishmallow, proper care ensures its longevity. You want your beloved plush to stay soft and huggable for years. A well-cared-for Squishmallow remains a comforting presence. Simple steps keep it looking its best.

**Regular cleaning is important**. Spot cleaning is usually sufficient for small marks. Use a damp cloth with mild soap. Gently dab the stained area. Avoid harsh chemicals that could damage the fabric. For a deeper clean, Squishmallows are machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with cold water. Place the Squishmallow inside a pillowcase for protection. This prevents damage during the wash.

**Drying correctly is crucial**. Air drying is the best method. Place your Squishmallow on a clean, dry towel. Allow it to air dry completely. Avoid using high heat in a dryer. High heat can melt the fibers and ruin the squishy texture. If you must use a dryer, choose a low-heat, tumble-dry setting. Add some clean, dry towels to help absorb moisture. This also helps maintain the plushness.

**Storage affects their condition**. Keep your Squishmallow in a clean, dry place. Avoid direct sunlight, which can fade colors over time. Storing them in airtight containers protects them from dust. This also guards against pests if you plan to store them long-term. Displaying them on a shelf is also a good option. Just remember to dust them occasionally.

**Gentle handling prevents wear and tear**. While Squishmallows are durable, excessive pulling can damage seams. Treat your plush friend with care. This ensures their embroidered features and soft fabric remain intact. Following these simple care instructions will keep your cutest dog Squishmallow in pristine condition. It will remain a source of joy for many years.
