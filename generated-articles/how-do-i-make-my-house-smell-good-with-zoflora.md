## Everyday Zoflora Applications for Freshness

Zoflora is incredibly versatile. You can use it in many parts of your home for daily freshness. Always remember to dilute it according to the instructions on the bottle. A common dilution is 1 part Zoflora to 40 parts water, but check your specific product. This ensures safety and effectiveness for regular use.

### Freshening Floors and Surfaces

For sparkling floors, add a capful or two of diluted Zoflora to your mop bucket. Mop your hard floors as usual. The disinfectant action cleans, and the lovely scent fills the room. I love stepping onto a freshly mopped floor that smells amazing. It instantly brightens my mood.

Wipe down surfaces with a diluted Zoflora solution. This works well for kitchen counters, bathroom sinks, and even door handles. Just spray the solution onto a cloth and wipe. It disinfects and leaves a subtle, clean smell. Remember to test on a small area first for delicate surfaces. This helps prevent any damage.
