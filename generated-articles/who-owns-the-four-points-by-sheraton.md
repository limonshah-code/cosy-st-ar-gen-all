## The Franchise Model: How Individual Hotels are Owned

Most Four Points by Sheraton hotels operate under a franchise model. This means that while Marriott International owns the brand, individual hotels are often owned by separate entities. These entities can be individuals, investment groups, or real estate companies. They enter into a franchise agreement with Marriott. This agreement gives them the right to use the Four Points by Sheraton name and its operational system.

A franchise agreement is a legal contract. It outlines the responsibilities of both the brand owner (Marriott) and the hotel owner. The hotel owner agrees to follow Marriott's brand standards. This includes everything from room design to customer service protocols. In return, the owner gains access to Marriott's powerful reservation system, marketing efforts, and loyalty program. They also benefit from the brand's established reputation.

This model is popular in the hospitality industry. It allows rapid expansion for hotel brands. Marriott does not need to invest capital to build or buy every hotel. Instead, they license their brand. This creates a vast network of hotels under their umbrella. It also provides business opportunities for local investors and operators. This structure is common for many large hotel chains.

## Operational Control Versus Property Ownership

### Management Models for Hotels

Hotels generally operate under a few management models. The most common are:
*   **Franchise Model:** The brand (Marriott) licenses its name to an independent property owner. The owner manages the hotel according to brand standards. This is the primary model for Four Points by Sheraton.
*   **Managed Model:** The brand (Marriott) manages the hotel on behalf of the property owner. Marriott is responsible for daily operations, staffing, and marketing. The property owner pays a fee to Marriott for this service.
*   **Owned Model:** The brand (Marriott) directly owns and operates the hotel property. This is less common for large hotel chains, especially for mid-tier brands. Marriott generally owns very few of its thousands of properties.

For Four Points by Sheraton, the franchise model is dominant. This allows for scalability and capital efficiency for Marriott. It also empowers local entrepreneurs to invest in a globally recognized brand. Each model has its own financial and operational implications for both the brand and the property owner. Understanding these models helps clarify who does what in the hotel business.

### FAQ Section

**1. Is Four Points by Sheraton owned by Hilton?**
No, Four Points by Sheraton is not owned by Hilton. Four Points by Sheraton is a brand under Marriott International. Marriott acquired the brand as part of its purchase of Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide in 2016. This merger significantly expanded Marriott's global brand portfolio.

**2. Does Marriott own all Four Points by Sheraton hotels?**
Marriott International owns the Four Points by Sheraton brand itself, including its trademarks and operating standards. However, Marriott does not typically own the individual hotel buildings. Most Four Points by Sheraton hotels are owned by independent third parties who operate them under a franchise agreement with Marriott.

**3. What kind of hotel is Four Points by Sheraton?**
Four Points by Sheraton is a select-service hotel brand within the Marriott International portfolio. It targets both business and leisure travelers looking for comfortable accommodations and essential amenities. These hotels usually offer standard guest rooms, a restaurant or bar, and meeting facilities. They provide reliable quality at a good value.

**4. Can I earn Marriott Bonvoy points at Four Points by Sheraton?**
Yes, you can earn and redeem Marriott Bonvoy points when you stay at any Four Points by Sheraton hotel. Since Four Points is part of Marriott International, it participates fully in the Marriott Bonvoy loyalty program. This allows guests to collect points across the vast Marriott network and use them for various travel benefits.

**5. What is the difference between Sheraton and Four Points by Sheraton?**
Sheraton is a full-service brand, typically offering a wider range of amenities like multiple restaurants, larger event spaces, and more extensive concierge services. Four Points by Sheraton is a select-service brand. It focuses on essential amenities and offers a more streamlined experience, often at a lower price point than full-service Sheratons. Both brands are now part of Marriott International.

**6. Who founded Four Points by Sheraton?**
Four Points by Sheraton was originally founded by ITT Sheraton in 1995. It was created as a mid-market brand to replace some of their older Sheraton Inns. Later, Starwood Hotels & Resorts acquired ITT Sheraton, and then Marriott International acquired Starwood, bringing Four Points into the Marriott family.
