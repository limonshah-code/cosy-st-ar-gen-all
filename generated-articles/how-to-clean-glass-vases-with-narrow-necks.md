Have you ever looked at your beautiful glass vase, only to see a cloudy film or water spots hiding its true sparkle? Cleaning glass vases with narrow necks can feel like a daunting task. ### Takeaway

*   Use household items like rice, vinegar, or salt for effective cleaning.
*   Select the right tools, such as flexible brushes or magnetic cleaners.
*   Address stubborn hard water stains with acidic solutions.
*   Dry vases properly to prevent new spots and streaks.
*   Regular cleaning prevents buildup and keeps vases looking their best.

### How do you clean glass vases with narrow necks?

You can clean glass vases with narrow necks by using a combination of abrasive materials like rice or salt with cleaning agents such as vinegar or dish soap. Pour these into the vase, swirl vigorously, then rinse thoroughly. For stubborn stains, specialized brushes or magnetic cleaners help reach tight spots.
