## The Premier Choice: Seaweed for Dreamlight Valley Turtles

When you ask, "What to feed turtles in Dreamlight Valley?" the top answer is always Seaweed. This unassuming item is the ultimate favorite food for all turtle variations in the game. Providing them with Seaweed ensures you get the maximum friendship gain and the best possible rewards. I always make sure to have some Seaweed on hand.

Seaweed is a common item found throughout Dreamlight Valley. You do not need to aim for a specific fishing spot or a rare bubbling circle. Simply cast your fishing line into any open water, away from the colored ripples. There is a high chance you will pull up Seaweed. This makes it an incredibly accessible resource for daily turtle feedings. I often gather a stack of Seaweed while doing other fishing activities.

Feeding a turtle its favorite food strengthens your bond quickly. Each time you give a turtle Seaweed, you fill up a portion of its friendship bar. Once that bar is full, the turtle becomes a permanent companion option. This special bond means they can accompany you on your adventures. Seaweed is truly the golden ticket for turtle companionship.

## Alternative Sustenance: Feeding Fish to Turtles

While Seaweed is the top choice, turtles in Dreamlight Valley are not entirely picky. They will also accept any type of fish you offer. This is a great fallback option if you are short on Seaweed or just have an abundance of fish from your fishing trips. Fish count as a "liked" food for turtles.

Any fish caught in the Valley works. This includes common fish like Cod or Tilapia, and even rarer ones like Anglerfish or Fugu. The type of fish does not matter for the turtle's acceptance. However, feeding them a liked food like fish will give you a lesser reward compared to their favorite food, Seaweed. The friendship gain will also be smaller.

I sometimes use excess fish for feeding if my inventory is full or I need to clear space. It is a practical way to manage resources while still building some rapport with the turtles. Remember, consistency in feeding, even with liked foods, still contributes to your overall friendship level. So, do not hesitate to offer a fish if Seaweed is out of reach.

## Maximizing Friendship with Your Valley Turtles

Building friendship with the turtles in Dreamlight Valley requires a bit of strategy. The key is consistent feeding. You can feed each individual turtle only once per day. After you feed a turtle, it will not accept food again until the next day resets. I always make a mental note of the turtles I have fed.

There are different color variations of turtles found across the biomes. Each color is a separate critter for friendship tracking. For example, the green turtle in one area has a different friendship bar than the blue turtle in another. To fully befriend all turtles, you need to feed each unique color variation until its friendship bar is maxed out. This means more Seaweed or fish.

To achieve maximum friendship, I prioritize feeding Seaweed. It fills the friendship bar fastest. Once a turtle's friendship is maxed, it will occasionally drop Dream Shards, Star Coins, or other valuable items when you feed it. At this point, the option to make it your companion becomes available. This ongoing benefit makes the daily feeding routine worthwhile.

## Where to Forage for Turtle Treats

Knowing what to feed turtles is important, but knowing where to get it is even more so. Both Seaweed and fish are acquired through fishing. This activity is a core part of resource gathering in Dreamlight Valley. I spend a lot of time fishing, which naturally provides me with turtle food.

To find Seaweed, you simply need a fishing rod. Stand near any body of water, whether it is the ocean, a pond, or a river. Cast your line into an area where there are no bubbling circles. You will frequently reel in Seaweed. This method is reliable and easy, making Seaweed a sustainable food source for your turtle friends. You can gather large quantities quickly.

For fish, the process is similar but with a twist. To catch specific fish, you need to aim for the bubbling circles in the water. White circles usually indicate common fish, blue circles mean uncommon fish, and gold circles signify rare fish. Any fish you catch can be given to turtles. So, even if you are fishing for quest items or cooking ingredients, you are simultaneously stocking up on turtle food. This makes foraging for turtle treats efficient.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where do I find Seaweed in Dreamlight Valley?
You can find Seaweed by fishing in any body of water within Dreamlight Valley. Cast your fishing line into open water, away from any bubbling circles. There is a high chance you will pull up Seaweed. This makes it a very common and easy item to gather for feeding turtles.

### What rewards do I get from feeding turtles in Dreamlight Valley?
Feeding turtles, especially their favorite food (Seaweed), provides valuable rewards. These can include Star Coins, various crafting materials, or Dream Shards. Once you fully befriend a turtle by feeding it consistently, you also unlock it as a companion, allowing it to follow you around the Valley.

### Can I feed turtles any type of fish in Dreamlight Valley?
Yes, turtles in Dreamlight Valley will accept any type of fish you catch. While fish are not their favorite food like Seaweed, they count as a "liked" food. Feeding them fish will still increase their friendship level and provide some rewards, though generally less valuable than favorite food drops.

### How often can I feed turtles in Dreamlight Valley?
You can feed each individual turtle once per day in Dreamlight Valley. After feeding a turtle, it will not accept another food item until the next daily reset occurs. Remember that different colored turtles are considered separate critters, so you can feed each unique color variation daily.

### Do different turtle colors like different foods?
No, all turtle color variations in Dreamlight Valley share the same food preferences. Their favorite food is always Seaweed, and they will accept any type of fish as a liked food. The different colors simply represent unique critter individuals you can befriend separately to unlock them as companions.
