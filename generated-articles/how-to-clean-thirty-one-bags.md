## How To Clean Thirty One Bags

Have you ever looked at your favorite Thirty-One bag and wished it looked as new as the day you bought it? I know I have! These bags are amazing; they help us carry everything from groceries to gym clothes. They stand up to daily life, but they can get dirty. Keeping them clean is important for their look and their life.

Learning how to clean Thirty-One bags properly ensures they last longer. It also keeps them hygienic. This guide will show you simple and effective methods. We will cover everything from quick spot treatments to deeper cleans. You can keep your beloved bags fresh and ready for any adventure. Let us get started on making your Thirty-One bags shine again!

### Takeaway

*   **Identify Material:** Always check your bag's fabric first for the best cleaning method.
*   **Spot Clean Regularly:** Address small spills immediately to prevent stains from setting.
*   **Gentle Hand Wash:** For deeper cleaning, hand washing with mild soap is usually safest.
*   **Avoid Machine Washing (Unless Specified):** Most Thirty-One bags are not machine-friendly.
*   **Air Dry Thoroughly:** Always air dry bags completely to prevent mold and maintain shape.
*   **Store Properly:** Keep bags in a cool, dry place when not in use to maintain freshness.

To clean Thirty-One bags, first, identify the fabric type. Spot clean small messes with mild soap and water. For a deeper clean, hand wash the bag gently. Always air dry completely. Avoid machine washing unless the care tag explicitly allows it.

### Understanding Your Thirty-One Bag's Material

Before you start any cleaning, it is smart to know what your Thirty-One bag is made from. Different materials need different care. Most Thirty-One bags use durable fabrics like polyester, canvas, or quilted cotton. Some bags have special coatings. Others might feature faux leather accents.

Knowing the material helps you choose the right cleaning products and methods. Using the wrong cleaner can damage the fabric. It might fade colors or ruin water-resistant properties. Check the tag inside your bag for specific care instructions. If there is no tag, you can often find material details on the Thirty-One website. This first step makes sure your cleaning efforts are successful and safe for your bag.

Polyester is a common material. It is known for being sturdy and resisting water. Canvas is another popular choice. It is strong but can absorb more liquid. Quilted cotton bags are soft but might need more gentle handling. Faux leather sections require special wiping. They do not do well with harsh scrubbing. Always think about the material before you apply anything. Your bag will thank you for it.

### Essential Supplies for Cleaning Your Bags

Having the right tools makes cleaning much easier and more effective. You do not need many fancy items. Simple household supplies often work best. Gathering everything beforehand saves time. It also ensures you have what you need when a mess happens.

Here is a list of what I keep ready for my Thirty-One bags:

*   **Mild Soap or Detergent:** Choose a gentle, dye-free soap. Baby shampoo or a mild dish soap works well. Harsh chemicals can strip colors. They can also damage fabric fibers.
*   **Soft Cloths or Sponges:** Microfiber cloths are good. They are soft and absorbent. Sponges also work for gentle scrubbing. Avoid abrasive scrubbers. They can scratch or wear down fabric.
*   **Soft Brush (Optional):** A soft-bristled brush, like an old toothbrush, helps with stubborn spots. Use it gently. It can lift dirt from textured fabrics.
*   **Clean Water:** You will need a basin or sink filled with cool or lukewarm water. A spray bottle with water is also useful for rinsing small areas.
*   **Paper Towels:** These help blot up excess moisture. They also help absorb stains.
*   **Old Towels:** Lay these down to protect surfaces. They also absorb drips while you clean.
*   **Rubbing Alcohol (for specific stains):** Only use this on very tough stains like ink, and test it first.
*   **Stain Remover (fabric-safe):** Look for a cleaner designed for fabric. Make sure it is safe for the specific material of your bag.

Having these items on hand means you are prepared for any cleaning task. It helps keep your Thirty-One bags in top condition. This readiness supports better bag longevity.

### General Spot Cleaning Techniques for Thirty-One Bags

Spot cleaning is your first line of defense against dirt and spills. It is perfect for small marks or splashes. This method keeps your bag looking fresh between deeper washes. Quick action makes a big difference. Addressing a stain right away prevents it from setting into the fabric.

Here is how I spot clean my Thirty-One bags:

1.  **Remove Excess:** First, use a clean cloth or paper towel to blot any liquid spills. Scrape off any solid bits carefully with a dull knife or spoon. Do not rub the stain. Rubbing pushes the mess deeper into the fibers.
2.  **Prepare Cleaning Solution:** Mix a small amount of mild soap with cool water. A few drops of dish soap in a cup of water is enough. You can also use a gentle fabric cleaner.
3.  **Test in an Inconspicuous Area:** Always test your cleaning solution first. Find a hidden part of the bag, like the inside of a pocket. Apply a tiny amount. Check for color changes or damage after a few minutes. This step prevents visible problems.
4.  **Blot the Stain:** Dip a clean, soft cloth into your soap solution. Wring out most of the water. You want the cloth damp, not soaking wet. Gently blot the stained area. Work from the outside of the stain inwards. This stops the stain from spreading.
5.  **Rinse and Repeat:** Dampen another clean cloth with plain water. Blot the area to rinse away the soap residue. Repeat blotting with clean, damp cloths until no soap remains. Soap left behind can attract more dirt.
6.  **Air Dry:** Let the treated area air dry completely. Do not use heat. Heat can set stains. You can pat the spot with a dry towel to speed drying.

Spot cleaning is a simple way to maintain your bags. It helps keep them looking good for a long time. This method is effective for daily upkeep.

### Deep Cleaning Your Thirty-One Totes and Bags

Sometimes, a bag needs more than just a quick spot clean. When your Thirty-One tote has seen many adventures, it might need a full wash. Deep cleaning helps remove accumulated dirt, grime, and odors. This process is often best done by hand to protect the bag's structure and fabric.

Here is a step-by-step guide for hand-washing your Thirty-One bag:

1.  **Empty and Prepare:** First, remove everything from your bag. Turn it upside down and shake out crumbs or debris. Open all pockets and zippers. You can use a small vacuum attachment to clean the inside thoroughly. Remove any removable inserts or bases if possible.
2.  **Pre-treat Stains:** If there are specific tough stains, pre-treat them. Apply a small amount of mild stain remover directly to the spot. Let it sit for a few minutes as per product instructions. Be sure the product is safe for your bag's fabric.
3.  **Prepare Wash Basin:** Fill a clean sink or large basin with cool or lukewarm water. Add a small amount of mild, dye-free soap or gentle detergent. Swirl the water to create suds. Remember, less soap is often better.
4.  **Submerge and Gently Wash:** Place your Thirty-One bag into the soapy water. Allow it to soak for about 10-15 minutes. This helps loosen dirt. Gently agitate the bag in the water. Use a soft cloth or sponge to gently scrub any dirty areas. Pay attention to handles and bottoms.
5.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Drain the soapy water. Refill the basin with clean, cool water. Swish the bag around to rinse out the soap. You may need to drain and refill the water several times. Keep rinsing until no suds appear and the water runs clear. Soap residue can leave marks or attract dirt.
6.  **Press Out Excess Water:** Do not wring or twist the bag. This can damage its shape. Instead, gently press the water out of the fabric. Lay the bag on a clean, dry towel. Roll the bag up in the towel and press firmly. This absorbs much of the moisture.
7.  **Air Dry Completely:** Hang your bag upside down or lay it flat on a clean, dry surface. Make sure air can circulate around it. You can stuff the bag with clean, dry towels to help it keep its shape. Let it air dry completely. This can take 24-48 hours. Drying it thoroughly prevents mildew and odors. Never use a machine dryer. High heat can shrink or damage the bag.

This gentle hand-washing method helps your bags look fresh and extend their life. It is a good practice for general bag care. Cleaning similar items like [how to clean fabric grow bags](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-fabric-grow-bags) follows many of these same gentle principles.

### Handling Specific Stains on Thirty-One Bags

Stains are an inevitable part of using bags daily. Different types of stains need different approaches. Knowing how to tackle common issues saves your bag from lasting damage. Here are some strategies for specific tough stains you might find on your Thirty-One bag.

#### Ink Stains

Ink can be tricky. Act fast if you notice an ink mark.

1.  **Blot, Don't Rub:** Use a clean paper towel to blot any wet ink. Do not spread it.
2.  **Alcohol Application:** Dip a cotton swab in rubbing alcohol. Gently dab the ink stain. You will see the ink transfer to the swab. Change swabs often.
3.  **Rinse and Wash:** Once the ink is gone, rinse the spot with a damp cloth. Then, follow up with a mild soap and water spot clean. Let it air dry.

#### Grease or Oil Stains

These stains can be stubborn. They need a substance to break down the oil.

1.  **Absorb First:** Sprinkle cornstarch or baking soda on the fresh grease stain. Let it sit for at least 30 minutes, or even overnight. This powder will absorb the oil.
2.  **Brush Off Powder:** Gently brush off the powder.
3.  **Dish Soap Treatment:** Apply a small amount of liquid dish soap directly to the stain. Rub it in gently with your finger or a soft brush. Dish soap is good at cutting grease.
4.  **Rinse and Repeat:** Rinse the area with a damp cloth. Repeat if needed. Air dry the bag.

#### Mud or Dirt Stains

Mud is easy to deal with if you let it dry.

1.  **Let it Dry:** Do not try to clean wet mud. Let it dry completely.
2.  **Scrape and Brush:** Once dry, gently scrape off the hardened mud. Use a stiff brush to remove remaining bits.
3.  **Spot Clean:** Then, use your mild soap and water solution to spot clean any lingering marks.
4.  **Air Dry:** Ensure the bag fully air dries.

#### Food Stains (Coffee, Juice, etc.)

These are often water-soluble.

1.  **Blot Immediately:** Blot up as much as you can with a clean cloth.
2.  **Mild Soap Solution:** Apply a mild soap and water solution. Gently blot the stain.
3.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse with clean water. Make sure all soap is gone.
4.  **Optional Vinegar Rinse:** For fruit juice or coffee, a mix of half water, half white vinegar can help. Apply, blot, then rinse with plain water.

Always test any new cleaning method on a hidden area first. This prevents accidental damage. With the right approach, you can keep your Thirty-One bags free of tough stains.

### Machine Washing Thirty-One Bags: When Is It Safe?

Many people wonder if they can simply toss their Thirty-One bags into the washing machine. The answer is often "no," but there are exceptions. Most Thirty-One bags are designed for durability and structure. Machine washing can compromise these features. It can cause bags to lose their shape, fade, or damage special coatings. However, some specific Thirty-One products might be machine washable.

**Always check the care tag first.** This is the most important step. If the tag says "spot clean only" or "hand wash," then stick to those instructions. Ignoring the care tag can void any product warranty. It can also lead to a ruined bag.

**When it might be safe:**
Some very simple, unlined fabric pouches or bags without special hardware might be machine washable. For example, if you have a basic canvas tote from Thirty-One that is just plain fabric, it might handle a gentle machine wash. However, this is rare for their structured bags. If the bag is similar to [canvas bags that you can machine wash](https://cosyhomecreation.com/can-you-machine-wash-canvas-bags), then you might consider it. However, Thirty-One often uses polyester blends and structured inserts. These materials are not always suitable for machine washing.

**If you must machine wash (use extreme caution):**

1.  **Delicate Cycle:** Use the gentlest cycle on your washing machine. A "delicate" or "hand wash" setting is best.
2.  **Cold Water:** Always use cold water. Hot water can cause colors to bleed or fabric to shrink.
3.  **Mild Detergent:** Use a small amount of mild, dye-free liquid detergent. Avoid harsh powders.
4.  **Protective Bag:** Place your Thirty-One bag inside a large mesh laundry bag or a pillowcase. This protects zippers and other hardware. It also prevents the bag from snagging.
5.  **Remove Inserts:** Take out any rigid inserts or base supports before washing. These will deform in the machine.
6.  **Air Dry Only:** After washing, remove the bag immediately. Do not put it in the dryer. Air dry it completely, as described in the deep cleaning section. Stuff it to help maintain shape.

My recommendation is to **avoid machine washing** unless the care tag explicitly states it is safe. Hand washing or spot cleaning is almost always the safer option. It protects your investment and keeps your bags looking good for longer. Your Thirty-One bags will stay looking great with this careful approach.

### Cleaning Specific Thirty-One Bag Features

Thirty-One bags often come with useful features like mesh pockets, insulated linings, and sturdy zippers. Each of these components might need specific care. Cleaning these parts properly ensures your whole bag stays clean and functional. Ignoring these details can lead to unseen dirt or damage.

#### Mesh Pockets

Mesh pockets are great for storing water bottles or quick-grab items. However, they can trap dirt and lint.

*   **Shake Out:** First, turn the bag upside down and shake out any loose debris.
*   **Wipe Down:** Use a damp cloth with mild soap to wipe the mesh. Gently scrub if needed.
*   **Rinse and Dry:** Rinse with a clean, damp cloth. Allow the mesh to air dry fully. Make sure no water pools in the mesh holes.

#### Insulated Linings (e.g., for Coolers)

Many Thirty-One bags have insulated linings, especially their cooler bags or lunch totes. These need special attention to stay hygienic.

*   **Empty and Wipe:** After use, empty the lining completely. Wipe it down immediately with a damp cloth.
*   **Sanitize (Optional):** For food spills, use a solution of mild soap and water. You can also use a mixture of white vinegar and water (50/50). Spray it inside and wipe clean. Vinegar helps sanitize and remove odors.
*   **Air Dry Open:** Leave the bag open to air dry completely. This is crucial to prevent mold and mildew. Do not close it until it is perfectly dry inside.

#### Zippers and Hardware

Zippers, D-rings, and other metal hardware can get dirty or sticky.

*   **Wipe with Damp Cloth:** Use a damp cloth to wipe down zippers and metal hardware. This removes dust and grime.
*   **Brush for Crevices:** For stubborn dirt in zipper teeth, use a soft toothbrush. Dip it in mild soapy water. Gently brush the teeth.
*   **Lubricate (Optional):** If a zipper feels stiff, a tiny bit of zipper lubricant or even a graphite pencil lead can help it glide. Apply sparingly.

#### Webbing Handles and Straps

These parts get a lot of hand contact and can pick up dirt and oils.

*   **Scrub Gently:** Use a soft brush or cloth with mild soapy water. Scrub the handles and straps.
*   **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse well with a clean, damp cloth.
*   **Air Dry:** Allow them to air dry completely.

Paying attention to these specific features helps maintain your entire Thirty-One bag. It keeps it looking good and working well for all your needs.

### Drying and Storing Your Clean Thirty-One Bags

Proper drying and storage are just as important as the cleaning itself. If you do not dry your bag correctly, you risk mildew growth. This can cause unpleasant odors and damage the fabric. Incorrect storage can also lead to creases or loss of shape. I always take extra care at these final steps.

#### Drying Your Thirty-One Bags

1.  **No Machine Dryer:** Never put your Thirty-One bag in a machine dryer. High heat can cause the fabric to shrink. It can also melt plastic components or ruin special coatings. The bag's structure might warp.
2.  **Air Dry Only:** Always air dry your bag. This is the safest method.
3.  **Choose a Good Spot:** Find a clean, well-ventilated area. A clothesline outdoors on a sunny, breezy day is ideal. Indoors, hang it in a room with good air circulation. Keep it away from direct heat sources like radiators.
4.  **Maintain Shape:** To help the bag keep its shape, you can stuff it. Use clean, dry towels or crumpled newspaper. This prevents the fabric from collapsing as it dries. Open all zippers and pockets. This allows air to reach every part.
5.  **Flip and Rotate:** Periodically flip the bag or rotate it. This ensures all sides dry evenly. Depending on humidity, a bag can take 24-48 hours to dry completely. Feel all seams and hidden areas to ensure they are dry. Any dampness can lead to mold.

#### Storing Your Thirty-One Bags

Once your bag is perfectly dry, proper storage helps maintain its condition.

1.  **Clean and Empty:** Ensure the bag is completely clean and empty before storing it.
2.  **Stuff for Shape (Optional):** For structured bags, you can lightly stuff them with acid-free tissue paper or old clean towels. This prevents them from collapsing or creasing.
3.  **Breathable Storage:** Store bags in a breathable bag. A cotton dust bag or an old pillowcase works well. Avoid plastic bags. Plastic can trap moisture and encourage mildew.
4.  **Cool, Dry Place:** Keep your bags in a cool, dry place. A closet shelf or a drawer is good. Avoid direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors over time. Avoid damp basements or attics. These areas can foster mold growth.
5.  **Hang Carefully:** If hanging, use a sturdy hanger. Make sure the bag's weight does not strain the handles.

By following these drying and storage tips, you extend the life and beauty of your Thirty-One bags. They will be fresh and ready for your next outing.

### Maintaining Your Thirty-One Bags Between Washes

Regular maintenance helps keep your Thirty-One bags looking good longer. It also reduces the need for frequent deep cleaning. Simple habits can make a big difference. I practice these tips to ensure my bags stay fresh and ready for use.

Here are some easy ways to maintain your bags:

*   **Empty Regularly:** After each use, take everything out of your bag. Shake out any crumbs, dust, or small debris. This prevents dirt from accumulating inside. It also stops forgotten items from causing issues later.
*   **Wipe Down Exterior:** Give the exterior a quick wipe. Use a dry or slightly damp cloth. This removes surface dust and minor smudges. It takes only a minute but keeps the bag looking neat.
*   **Spot Clean Immediately:** Do not let spills sit. If you spill coffee or get a smudge, spot clean it right away. Use the techniques we discussed earlier. Quick action prevents stains from setting deeply into the fabric.
*   **Protect from Elements:** Try to avoid placing your bag directly on dirty floors or wet surfaces. If it starts raining, protect your bag from heavy downpours. Water-resistant sprays can offer some extra protection for fabric bags. Always test such sprays in a hidden area first.
*   **Rotate Bags:** If you own several Thirty-One bags, rotate their use. This gives each bag a break. It reduces wear and tear on any single bag. This practice helps all your bags last longer.
*   **Use Inserts or Pouches:** To protect the interior, use smaller pouches or inserts. They organize items like makeup, pens, or snacks. This contains potential spills or leaks. You can then clean the smaller pouch easily.
*   **Deodorize Naturally:** If your bag starts to smell stale, use natural deodorizers. Place an open box of baking soda inside overnight. You can also use charcoal air purifying bags. These absorb odors without adding chemicals.
*   **Check Hardware:** Periodically check zippers and other hardware. Make sure they move freely. Wipe away any grit or grime from zipper teeth. This keeps them working smoothly.

These small, consistent efforts pay off. They keep your Thirty-One bags in excellent condition. They save you time and effort on major cleaning tasks. Plus, a well-maintained bag always looks better.

### FAQs About Cleaning Thirty-One Bags

Here are some common questions people ask about how to clean Thirty-One bags.

**Q1: Can I put my Thirty-One bag in the washing machine?**
Most Thirty-One bags are not machine washable. Machine washing can cause bags to lose their shape, fade, or damage special coatings and hardware. Always check the care tag. If the tag says "spot clean only" or "hand wash," avoid the washing machine to protect your bag.

**Q2: How do I remove a pen mark from my Thirty-One bag?**
For pen marks, act quickly. Use a cotton swab dipped in rubbing alcohol. Gently dab the ink stain, allowing the ink to transfer to the swab. Replace the swab often. Follow up with a mild soap and water spot clean. Always test alcohol on a hidden area first.

**Q3: What should I use to clean the inside of my insulated Thirty-One bag?**
To clean the inside of insulated bags, wipe it down with a damp cloth and mild soap. For food spills, a solution of white vinegar and water (50/50) helps sanitize and remove odors. Ensure you leave the bag open to air dry completely to prevent mold or mildew.

**Q4: How do I prevent my Thirty-One bag from getting dirty quickly?**
Prevent your bag from getting dirty quickly by emptying it after each use and shaking out debris. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth regularly. Use smaller pouches or inserts inside to contain potential spills. Avoid placing your bag on dirty surfaces whenever possible.

**Q5: Is it safe to use bleach on my Thirty-One bags for tough stains?**
No, it is generally not safe to use bleach on Thirty-One bags. Bleach can cause irreversible fading, weaken fabric fibers, and damage special coatings. For tough stains, stick to pre-treatment with fabric-safe stain removers or specific methods for stain types like ink or grease.

**Q6: My bag smells a bit stale, how can I freshen it up?**
To freshen up a stale-smelling bag, ensure it is completely dry inside. Place an open box of baking soda or a few charcoal air purifying bags inside overnight. These natural absorbents help neutralize odors. Allow the bag to air out in a well-ventilated area for a few hours.
