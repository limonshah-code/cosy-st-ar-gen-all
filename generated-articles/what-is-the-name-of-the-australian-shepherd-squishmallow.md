For a deeper clean, some people machine wash their Squishmallows. Use a mesh laundry bag to protect Reggie during the wash cycle. Wash on a delicate setting with cold water and mild detergent. Remove him immediately after the cycle ends. Squeeze out excess water gently. Then, air dry completely. Again, avoid machine drying.

Proper storage also helps keep Reggie pristine. Store him in a dry, clean place. Avoid direct sunlight, as it can fade the colors. Keep him away from sharp objects that could tear the fabric. If you have many Squishmallows, consider storing them in soft bins or clear bags. This keeps dust off them.

### Best Practices for Squishmallow Care

*   **Spot Clean First:** For small stains, use a damp cloth and mild soap. This prevents over-washing.
*   **Hand Wash for Best Results:** Gently wash by hand in a sink with cold water and mild soap. Rinse well.
*   **Use a Laundry Bag for Machine Wash:** If machine washing, use a large mesh bag on a delicate, cold cycle. This protects the plush.
*   **Air Dry ONLY:** Never put a Squishmallow in a dryer. High heat damages the fibers and texture. Lay flat on a clean towel or hang to dry.
*   **Store Properly:** Keep your Reggie in a clean, dry area away from direct sunlight and sharp items.
