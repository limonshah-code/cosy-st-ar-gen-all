## Preparing Your Foot Long Hot Dogs: Cooking Tips

Once you have your foot long hot dogs, proper preparation ensures they taste their best. Foot longs take a bit longer to cook than standard hot dogs because of their size. Different cooking methods bring out different flavors and textures. Choosing the right method depends on your preference and equipment.

Always read the package instructions first. These guidelines are specific to the hot dog brand. Pay attention to recommended cooking times and temperatures. Overcooking hot dogs can make them dry. Undercooking is unsafe.

### Grilling and Broiling

Grilling is a favorite method for hot dogs. The smoky flavor and char marks add a lot to the taste. Preheat your grill to medium heat. Place the hot dogs over direct heat, turning them frequently. They usually take about 8-12 minutes to cook through. Foot longs might need a minute or two longer.

Broiling in the oven also gives a nice char. Place hot dogs on a baking sheet under a preheated broiler. Turn them often to ensure even cooking. This method is great when you cannot grill outside. ### Boiling and Frying

Boiling hot dogs is a simple and fast method. Bring a pot of water to a boil, then reduce the heat to a simmer. Add the hot dogs and let them cook for 5-7 minutes. Foot longs might need 8-10 minutes. This method keeps them juicy. For more details on boiling, see [how long does it take for hot dogs to boil](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-long-does-it-take-for-hot-dogs-to-boil).

Pan-frying adds a nice crispy exterior. Heat a little oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the hot dogs and cook, turning them until they are browned and heated through. This can take 7-10 minutes. ## Frequently Asked Questions About Foot Long Hot Dogs

### Are all foot long hot dogs the same size?
No, while they are all generally longer than standard hot dogs, their exact length can vary. Some might be exactly 12 inches (one foot), while others might be slightly shorter or longer depending on the brand and how they are manufactured. Always check the packaging for specific dimensions.

### Can I freeze foot long hot dogs?
Yes, you can freeze foot long hot dogs. Most hot dogs come pre-packaged and are suitable for freezing. If you open a package, you can re-wrap the hot dogs tightly in plastic wrap and then foil before freezing. This helps prevent freezer burn and keeps them fresh for longer periods.

### What are popular brands of foot long hot dogs?
Several well-known brands offer foot long hot dogs, though availability varies by region. Some popular brands include Nathan's Famous, Oscar Mayer, and Ball Park. Store brands from large grocery chains or wholesale clubs like Costco also often provide foot long options.

### Are foot long hot dogs more expensive?
Generally, yes, foot long hot dogs are often slightly more expensive per hot dog than standard-sized hot dogs. This is due to the larger amount of product. However, buying them in bulk at wholesale clubs can often reduce the per-unit cost, making them more economical for larger quantities.

### How do I cook a foot long hot dog properly?
Foot long hot dogs require a bit more cooking time than standard ones. You can grill, boil, pan-fry, or oven-bake them. For grilling, use medium heat and turn often for 8-12 minutes. For boiling, simmer for 8-10 minutes. Always cook until heated through and golden brown.

### Do foot long hot dogs require special buns?
Yes, foot long hot dogs definitely need special buns. Regular hot dog buns are too short. You will need to purchase specific foot long hot dog buns, which are designed to perfectly fit the longer sausages. These are usually found in the bread aisle near the standard hot dog buns.
