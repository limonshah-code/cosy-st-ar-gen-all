## Caring for Your German Shepherd Squishmallow Plush

Taking care of your Reginald Squishmallow is important. Proper care keeps him soft and clean. This ensures he lasts for many years. Squishmallows are easy to maintain. They do not require complex cleaning methods. Following a few simple steps will keep your German Shepherd Squishmallow in great condition. I always recommend checking the tag for specific instructions.

Most Squishmallows are machine washable. Use a gentle cycle with cold water. Place your Squishmallow in a pillowcase or laundry bag. This protects the fabric during washing. Use a mild detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach. These can damage the soft material. After washing, reshape your Squishmallow.

Do not put Squishmallows in the dryer. High heat can damage the fibers. It can also melt the polyester stuffing. Air drying is the best option. You can lay your Squishmallow flat on a clean towel. Allow it to air dry completely. This might take several hours. Make sure it is fully dry before storing or playing with it again. Dampness can lead to mold or mildew.

Spot cleaning is also an option for small spills. Use a damp cloth with mild soap. Gently rub the affected area. Then, use a clean damp cloth to wipe away soap residue. Let the spot air dry. This method is good for quick clean-ups. It prevents the need for a full wash.

Proper storage helps maintain shape. Keep your Squishmallow away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors over time. Store it in a clean, dry place. A bin or shelf works well. Avoid squishing it too tightly with other items. This helps maintain its plush shape. I treat my Squishmallows like valuable companions. Giving them proper care ensures their softness endures.
