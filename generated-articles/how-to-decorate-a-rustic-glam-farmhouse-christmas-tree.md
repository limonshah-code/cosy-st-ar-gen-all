## Decorate Your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree

The holiday season brings joy and a chance to make our homes festive. I love transforming my space for Christmas. This year, the Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree style has captured my heart. It blends cozy charm with sparkling elegance.

This article will show you how to create your own beautiful Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree. We will explore choosing the right tree, layering lights and ribbon, selecting ornaments, and adding special touches. Get ready to design a holiday centerpiece that feels both warm and luxurious. Your tree will be a beacon of holiday cheer.

### Takeaway

*   Start with a quality artificial or real tree as your foundation.
*   Layer warm white lights for an inviting glow.
*   Incorporate wide ribbon or burlap for rustic texture.
*   Mix rustic ornaments (wood, metal) with glam ornaments (glitter, glass).
*   Use picks and fillers to add depth and unique elements.
*   Finish with a distinct tree topper and skirt or collar.
*   Arrange all elements carefully for a balanced, eye-catching design.

To decorate a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree, blend natural, rustic elements like wood and burlap with glamorous touches such as metallics, sparkle, and elegant ornaments. Layer lights, ribbon, and diverse ornaments to create a cohesive, inviting, and dazzling holiday display that celebrates both styles.

## Understanding Rustic Glam Farmhouse Style

The Rustic Glam Farmhouse style combines two distinct design approaches. Rustic elements bring natural textures and a lived-in feel. Think wood, burlap, galvanized metal, and simple, cozy items. These parts evoke a sense of warmth and history.

Glam components add sparkle, shine, and sophistication. Metallics, glitter, crystals, and luxurious fabrics are common. These elements bring a touch of elegance and celebration. When combined, they create a look that is both inviting and refined. Our [rustic glam farmhouse living room](https://cosyhomecreation.com/our-rustic-glam-farmhouse-living-room-our-diy-house) uses similar ideas.

For a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree, we aim for balance. We want the warmth of the farmhouse style. We also desire the sparkle and richness of glam decor. This mix makes a tree that feels unique and personal. It tells a story of cozy elegance.

The color palette for this style often includes neutrals like white, cream, and gray. It also features earthy tones such as brown and deep green. These form the base. Then, we add metallics like gold, silver, or rose gold for the glam touch. Sometimes, a pop of deep red or forest green enhances the festive mood. The key is to select colors that complement each other.

Choosing the right base tree is also important. A traditional green tree works well. A flocked tree, with its snow-dusted look, also fits this style beautifully. It provides a soft, wintry backdrop for both rustic and glam elements. The tree’s size and shape should suit your space. A well-chosen tree sets the stage for your decorating.

## Building Your Tree's Foundation: Lights and Ribbon

Creating a strong foundation for your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree starts with lights. I always begin here. Proper lighting makes your tree glow. It also highlights all your decorations.

I recommend warm white LED lights. These lights provide a cozy, inviting glow. They fit the farmhouse aesthetic perfectly. You want to use plenty of lights. A good rule of thumb is 100 lights per foot of tree height. For example, a 7-foot tree needs about 700 lights. This ensures a bright and even display.

String your lights from the inside out. Start near the trunk and wrap them around branches. Work your way to the tips. Then, move to the next section and repeat. This method adds depth. It also lights the tree from within. This makes the tree sparkle beautifully from all angles.

Next comes the ribbon. Ribbon adds texture and fills empty spaces. It helps tie the rustic and glam elements together. You can use several types of ribbon for a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree.

Consider wide burlap ribbon for the rustic touch. It offers a wonderful natural texture. For the glam element, choose a wired metallic ribbon. Gold, silver, or rose gold options work well. A glittery mesh ribbon also adds sparkle. You can also [make your Christmas tree look amazing on a budget](https://cosyhomecreation.com/5-ways-to-make-your-christmas-tree-look-amazing-on-a-budget) by creatively using ribbon.

There are different ways to apply ribbon.
*   **Vertical Strands:** Cut long pieces of ribbon. Tuck one end deep into the tree near the top. Let it cascade down the tree. Gently curve it as you go. Secure it at various points.
*   **Horizontal Weave:** Weave wider ribbon in and out of branches. This creates a fuller look. It drapes around the tree's circumference.
*   **Looped Bows:** Make small bows from ribbon. Attach them to branches. This adds pops of color and texture throughout.

I often combine these methods for a richer look. Use the rustic ribbon first. Then, layer the glam ribbon over it. This creates depth and interest. Remember to step back often. This helps you see how the ribbon looks from different angles. It also allows you to adjust it as needed.

## Choosing Ornaments: Mixing Rustic Charm with Glam Sparkle

Selecting ornaments is where your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree truly comes to life. This step is about blending two styles. You want rustic charm and glam sparkle. The key is to balance them.

Start with your rustic ornaments.
*   **Natural Materials:** Include ornaments made from wood. Small wooden slices, carved birds, or simple stars work well. Pinecones, either natural or lightly flocked, are great. Burlap or twine ornaments also add a rustic touch.
*   **Farmhouse Icons:** Look for ornaments like miniature galvanized buckets, milk cans, or small signs with farmhouse sayings. Distressed metal shapes, like barns or animals, also fit.

Next, add your glam ornaments.
*   **Shine and Sparkle:** Choose metallic ornaments in gold, silver, bronze, or rose gold. These can be shiny balls, mercury glass, or glitter-dusted shapes. Crystals or faceted ornaments catch the light beautifully.
*   **Elegant Textures:** Velvet ornaments, especially in deep jewel tones or neutral creams, add a luxurious feel. Pearlescent finishes also bring a subtle elegance.

I like to use a variety of sizes and shapes. This creates visual interest. Place larger ornaments deeper into the tree. They fill space and add depth. Hang smaller, more delicate ornaments on the outer branches. This makes them stand out.

Don't forget specialty ornaments. These can be family heirlooms or unique finds. They add personal meaning to your tree. Consider a few personalized ornaments too. They make the tree feel extra special. For more ideas on how to make unique holiday crafts, see how we decorated [a rustic glam kid-friendly Christmas tree](https://cosyhomecreation.com/a-rustic-glam-kid-friendly-christmas-tree-unique-holiday-crafts).

Think about grouping some ornaments. Cluster three small ornaments together on a branch. Or, hang a large statement ornament by itself. This creates focal points. Ensure your ornaments are evenly distributed. You want sparkle and rustic elements throughout the tree. Avoid having all the glam on one side and all the rustic on another. This balance is crucial for the Rustic Glam Farmhouse style.

## Adding Depth with Picks, Sprays, and Fillers

After placing lights, ribbon, and main ornaments, your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree still needs more. Picks, sprays, and fillers add dimension. They fill in gaps. They also enhance the overall texture and style of your tree. I find these elements truly elevate a design.

Picks are individual stems with decorative elements. Sprays are larger bundles. Fillers are smaller items that tuck into branches. These items can be either rustic or glam. They help integrate the two styles further.

For rustic picks and sprays, look for:
*   **Natural Elements:** Frosted pinecone picks, berry sprigs, or faux evergreen branches. Birch branches or cotton stems add a soft, natural texture. Burlap or plaid fabric picks also fit the farmhouse theme.
*   **Earthy Tones:** Choose picks in browns, creams, deep greens, and subtle reds. These colors blend well with the rustic look.

For glam picks and sprays, consider:
*   **Sparkling Accents:** Glitter-dusted branches, metallic leaf sprays, or crystal-beaded picks. Feather boas or shimmery floral picks add elegance. These catch the light and add drama.
*   **Luxurious Materials:** Velvet leaves, satin flowers, or faux pearls on stems. These items bring a sophisticated touch.

I always distribute picks and sprays evenly around the tree.
*   **Start from the Top:** Place some picks near the top. Let them cascade down slightly.
*   **Work Inward:** Insert picks deeper into the tree's branches. This creates fullness and depth.
*   **Outer Branches:** Add some picks to the outer branches. This extends the decoration. It also helps fill any sparse spots.

You can also create small clusters of picks. For instance, combine a frosted berry pick with a glittery leaf spray. This makes a mini arrangement. It draws the eye. When you [use Christmas tree picks](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-use-christmas-tree-picks) effectively, they make a significant difference.

Remember to vary the placement and direction of your picks. Some can stand upright. Others can angle down or across. This organic placement makes the tree look more natural and abundant. These fillers complete the look. They make your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree appear lush and professionally decorated.

## The Perfect Tree Topper and Base Skirt

The tree topper is the crowning jewel of your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree. It defines the style and draws the eye upward. Choosing the right one is essential for a cohesive look.

For a rustic glam theme, you have several options:
*   **Traditional Star or Angel:** A classic choice. For rustic glam, pick one with metallic accents. A mercury glass star or a distressed metal angel works well. Look for glitter details or a weathered finish.
*   **Burlap Bow:** A large, multi-loop burlap bow offers a strong rustic statement. You can combine it with a smaller metallic bow or sparkling picks for glam.
*   **Cluster of Picks and Sprays:** Instead of a single topper, create a custom one. Gather several rustic and glam picks. These can be frosted berries, glittery branches, or metallic leaves. Tie them together. Secure them firmly at the top of the tree. This option allows for unique customization.

I always make sure the topper is proportionate to the tree. A small topper on a tall tree looks lost. A huge topper on a short tree looks heavy. It should sit comfortably without bending the top branch. Securing it well ensures it stays put throughout the season.

The tree skirt or collar is equally important for the base of your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree. It covers the stand. It also adds another layer of decor. This element helps to ground the tree.

Consider these options for your rustic glam look:
*   **Faux Fur Tree Skirt:** A soft, luxurious faux fur skirt brings glam and coziness. White, cream, or light gray fur works well. It adds warmth and texture.
*   **Burlap or Plaid Skirt:** A classic burlap skirt offers pure rustic charm. A red and black buffalo plaid skirt also fits the farmhouse aesthetic. You can embellish it with simple lace or metallic ribbon.
*   **Galvanized Metal Tree Collar:** A popular farmhouse choice. It creates a clean, industrial look at the base. It also gives a modern rustic feel. This option is durable and easy to clean.
*   **Woven Basket Collar:** A large woven basket can serve as a tree collar. It adds natural texture and a true farmhouse feel. This option is very charming.

Choose a skirt or collar that complements the rest of your decor. The color and material should tie into your tree's theme. A white faux fur skirt contrasts beautifully with a green tree. It also pairs well with gold or silver glam accents. A galvanized collar adds a sturdy, rustic element. Both options create a finished look for your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree.

## Bringing it All Together: A Step-by-Step Decorating Guide

Decorating your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree is a layered process. Following a step-by-step approach helps ensure a balanced and beautiful result. I have found this method helps me create a cohesive look every time.

Here's my guide to creating your masterpiece:

1.  **Set Up Your Tree:**
    *   Assemble your artificial tree or secure your real tree in its stand.
    *   Fluff out all the branches thoroughly. Spend time shaping each branch. This creates a full base for your decorations. This step is crucial for overall density.
    *   Make sure the tree is stable and upright.

2.  **Add Your Lights:**
    *   String your warm white LED lights. Start at the top. Work your way down and around.
    *   Push some lights deep into the tree. Also, wrap some lights around the outer tips of branches. This creates depth. It makes the tree glow from inside and out.
    *   Ensure even distribution. Plug them in as you go to check the spread.

3.  **Place Your Ribbon:**
    *   Integrate your rustic (burlap, plaid) and glam (metallic, glitter) ribbons.
    *   Cut ribbons to various lengths. Tuck one end into the tree. Let it cascade or weave it through branches.
    *   Create vertical lines, horizontal waves, or even small bows. Distribute them evenly around the tree.

4.  **Hang Main Ornaments:**
    *   Start with your larger, filler ornaments. Place these deeper into the tree. They fill gaps and add a base layer.
    *   Then, hang your rustic ornaments (wood, galvanized metal) and glam ornaments (sparkling balls, mercury glass). Distribute them evenly.
    *   Vary their placement. Avoid clumping similar ornaments together.

5.  **Insert Picks, Sprays, and Fillers:**
    *   Tuck in your chosen picks and sprays. These add texture and dimension.
    *   Use frosted pinecones, berry sprigs, glitter branches, or velvet leaves.
    *   Push them into any open spaces. Ensure they complement the ornaments.
    *   This step adds fullness and a designer touch. For more detailed instructions, you can follow a [step-by-step guide on how to decorate a Christmas tree](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-decorate-a-christmas-tree-step-by-step).

6.  **Add Specialty Ornaments and Personal Touches:**
    *   Now, place your most treasured or delicate ornaments. These should be visible.
    *   Include any personalized items or family heirlooms. These make your tree unique.
    *   Step back and assess the tree. Adjust any ornaments or picks as needed.

7.  **Attach the Tree Topper:**
    *   Carefully secure your chosen tree topper. Ensure it stands straight.
    *   It should be proportionate to the tree's height.

8.  **Complete the Base:**
    *   Finally, place your tree skirt or collar around the base.
    *   Arrange it neatly. This finishes the look and hides the tree stand.

Following these steps helps you build a beautiful Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree. You will have a tree that is both welcoming and stunning.

## Preserving Your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree

Once your beautiful Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree is decorated, you want it to look great all season. Proper care ensures its longevity. It also keeps it sparkling and fresh. I always take a few steps to maintain my tree.

**For Artificial Trees:**
*   **Dust Regularly:** Over time, dust can settle on ornaments and branches. Use a feather duster or a soft, dry cloth. Gently wipe down surfaces. This keeps the sparkle vibrant.
*   **Check Lights:** If a section of lights goes out, check for loose bulbs. Replace any burnt-out ones promptly. This maintains a consistent glow.
*   **Avoid Over-Touching:** Too much handling can shift ornaments or flatten branches. Adjust decorations only when necessary.
*   **Keep Pets Away:** Pets can be curious about a new, shiny tree. Secure your tree. Consider barriers if needed. This prevents damage to ornaments or the tree itself. We have tips on [how to block a dog from a Christmas tree](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-block-a-dog-from-a-christmas-tree).

**For Real Trees:**
*   **Water Daily:** Real trees need plenty of water. Check the stand's water level every day. Refill it as needed. A well-hydrated tree retains its needles better.
*   **Keep Away from Heat:** Place your tree away from heat sources. Fireplaces, radiators, and heating vents dry out a tree quickly. This makes it a fire hazard.
*   **Monitor Needle Drop:** Excessive needle drop indicates the tree is drying out. Remove it if it becomes too dry.
*   **Clean the Stand:** You can learn [how to clean your Christmas tree stand](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-Christmas-tree-stand) to prevent sap buildup for next year.

**General Tips for Both Tree Types:**
*   **Protect Ornaments:** If you have special or delicate ornaments, place them where they are less likely to be bumped. Use clear fishing line for extra security if needed.
*   **Use a Timer for Lights:** A light timer is a simple solution. It turns lights on and off automatically. This saves energy. It also extends bulb life.
*   **Seasonal Refresh:** If your tree starts looking a bit tired, gently fluff a few branches. Adjust any ornaments that have shifted. Sometimes, a small tweak makes a big difference.

By following these simple steps, your Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree will remain a beautiful focal point. It will bring joy throughout the entire holiday season. This care ensures your efforts last.

## FAQs About Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree Decor

### What colors work best for a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree?
For a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree, use a base of neutrals like white, cream, and gray. Add earthy tones such as deep green and brown. Then, introduce metallics like gold, silver, or rose gold for the glam touch. A pop of deep red or forest green also enhances the festive feel.

### How do I combine rustic and glam elements without making the tree look cluttered?
Balance is key. Start with rustic elements like burlap ribbon or wooden ornaments. Then, layer in glam items such as glitter balls or metallic picks. Distribute both styles evenly across the tree. Use a consistent color palette to unify the look. Avoid too many different textures or colors.

### What kind of lights should I use for this theme?
Warm white LED lights are perfect for a Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree. They provide a cozy and inviting glow. This complements both the rustic charm and the glam sparkle. Aim for about 100 lights per foot of tree height for optimal brightness and depth.

### Can I DIY some of the decorations for my Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree?
Absolutely! DIY decorations fit the farmhouse aesthetic well. You can make wooden slice ornaments, burlap bows, or pinecone accents. Crafting simple signs or personalized tags also adds a unique touch. These handmade items enhance the rustic charm.

### What are good options for a tree topper and base for this style?
For a topper, consider a distressed metal star, a large burlap bow, or a custom cluster of rustic and glam picks. For the base, a white faux fur tree skirt adds glam. A classic burlap skirt or a galvanized metal tree collar provides a rustic touch. These elements complete the tree's look.

### How do I store my Rustic Glam Farmhouse Christmas Tree decorations?
Store ornaments in divided containers. Use tissue paper for delicate items. Keep ribbons rolled to prevent creases. Store picks and sprays flat. This helps preserve their shape. Label boxes clearly for easy setup next year. This ensures your decorations last many seasons.
