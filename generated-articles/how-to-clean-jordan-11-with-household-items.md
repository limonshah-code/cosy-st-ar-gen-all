## How To Clean Jordan 11 With Household Items

Do your prized Jordan 11s look a little less than perfect? It happens to the best of us. These iconic sneakers gather dirt and grime over time. You might think you need special cleaners for these shoes. However, you can make your Jordan 11s shine again using simple items from your home. This guide will show you how to clean Jordan 11 with household items, step by step. I will cover everything from the shiny patent leather to the clear icy soles. This way, you save money and keep your kicks looking new.

### Takeaway:

*   **Gather simple supplies:** Dish soap, baking soda, old toothbrushes, and cloths are key.
*   **Understand materials:** Different parts of the Jordan 11 need specific cleaning methods.
*   **Pre-clean first:** Always remove loose dirt before wetting the shoe.
*   **Clean methodically:** Tackle patent leather, mesh, and soles separately for best results.
*   **Dry properly:** Air dry shoes away from direct heat to prevent damage.

To clean Jordan 11s using household items, you need common products like mild dish soap, baking soda, an old toothbrush, and a few soft cloths. These items effectively remove dirt, scuffs, and stains from patent leather, mesh, and rubber soles. This method helps restore the shoe's appearance without harsh chemicals.

### Gather Your Arsenal: Essential Household Cleaning Items for Jordan 11s

You do not need many special tools to clean your Jordan 11s. Many effective cleaning agents sit right in your kitchen or bathroom. I always start by gathering my supplies before I begin. This makes the cleaning process smooth and efficient. It also ensures I have everything on hand when I need it.

First, you need **mild dish soap**. This works wonders for general dirt and grime. I often choose a gentle, clear soap to avoid any potential staining. A small amount mixed with water creates a powerful, yet safe, cleaning solution. Dish soap breaks down grease and dirt without harming the shoe materials.

Next, grab some **baking soda**. This versatile powder acts as a mild abrasive and odor neutralizer. I find it excellent for lifting stubborn stains from soles and whitening rubber. You can create a paste with baking soda and water. This paste provides a scrubbing action without causing damage.

An **old toothbrush** is a must-have in your cleaning kit. Its soft bristles reach into tight spaces. It gently scrubs away dirt from mesh, stitching, and textured areas. I also use it to work cleaning solutions into the material. Make sure it is clean before you start.

You will also need a few **microfiber cloths** or soft towels. These are perfect for wiping down surfaces. They absorb moisture and buff the patent leather to a shine. I always have at least two on hand: one for cleaning and one for drying. A [soft cloth is also useful for cleaning delicate surfaces like exhaust tips with household items](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-exhaust-tips-with-household-items), showing its versatility.

Finally, prepare a bowl of **warm water**. This water helps create cleaning solutions. It also rinses away soap residue effectively. I use lukewarm water, as hot water can sometimes be too harsh for certain materials. These simple household items are the foundation for bringing your Jordan 11s back to life.

### Pre-Clean Prep: Getting Your Jordan 11s Ready for a Spa Day

Before you apply any liquid, you must prepare your Jordan 11s. This initial preparation step is very important. It removes loose dirt and prevents it from turning into mud during cleaning. I always take my time with this stage. It sets up the rest of the cleaning process for success.

First, **remove the laces**. Laces collect a lot of dirt and grime themselves. Taking them off allows you to clean the shoe's tongue and eyelets properly. You can wash the laces separately later. This also prevents them from getting tangled or in the way.

Next, use a **dry, soft-bristled brush** to remove loose dirt. An old toothbrush works well here, or a dedicated shoe brush. Gently brush the entire shoe, focusing on the sole and areas where dirt collects. I brush in one direction to lift the dirt away. This step is like dusting your furniture before wiping it down.

You also need to **understand the materials** of your Jordan 11s. These shoes feature patent leather, mesh, and rubber soles. Each material reacts differently to cleaners. For instance, patent leather needs a very soft touch. Mesh can handle a bit more scrubbing, but still requires care. Knowing this helps you choose the right cleaning approach for each section. Understanding shoe materials is also key when you learn [how to clean Timberland boots with household items](https://www.cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-timberland-boots-with-household-items), as different leathers and suedes require specific attention.

Inspect your shoes for any specific stains or scuffs. This helps you plan your attack. Note where the dirt is heaviest or what kind of marks you see. This pre-inspection allows for targeted cleaning. You can focus your efforts where they are most needed. Taking these steps ensures you do not spread dirt around. It also protects your shoes from unnecessary damage.

### Tackling the Patent Leather: Shine and Scuff Removal

The patent leather on Jordan 11s is iconic. It gives the shoe its signature shine. However, this material also shows scuffs and fingerprints easily. Cleaning patent leather requires a gentle touch to maintain its glossy finish. I take special care with this section to keep the shine.

Start by mixing a small amount of **mild dish soap** with warm water. Create a slightly sudsy solution. Do not use too much soap, as it can leave residue. I find a pea-sized amount in a cup of water is usually enough. This creates a gentle cleaning liquid.

Dip a **microfiber cloth** into the soapy water. Wring out most of the liquid. The cloth should be damp, not dripping wet. Excess water can get into the shoe's seams. Gently wipe down the entire patent leather surface. I use small, circular motions to lift dirt and smudges. This motion helps to clean the surface evenly without harsh scrubbing.

For stubborn scuff marks, you have a few options. I often start with **non-gel toothpaste**. Apply a tiny dab of white toothpaste to the scuff. Use a clean, damp cloth or a cotton swab to gently rub the toothpaste into the mark. The mild abrasive in toothpaste helps to buff away superficial scuffs. Rub gently for a minute or two.

Alternatively, for more persistent scuffs, **rubbing alcohol** can work. Dip a cotton swab in rubbing alcohol. Lightly dab the scuff mark. Do not rub too hard or use too much alcohol. Rubbing alcohol can dry out the patent leather if used excessively. I use this method sparingly and only on tough marks. Wipe away any residue immediately with a damp cloth.

Finally, wipe the entire patent leather area with a clean, damp cloth to remove all soap or toothpaste residue. Then, use a dry microfiber cloth to buff the surface. This step restores the patent leather's brilliant shine. Proper care of this material is essential for your Jordan 11s to look their best.

### Reviving the Mesh and Uppers: Gentle Cleaning Techniques

The mesh and fabric uppers of your Jordan 11s also collect dirt. These areas need a different approach than the patent leather. They are more porous and can absorb stains. My goal is always to clean them effectively without damaging the material. This requires gentleness and patience.

Prepare a cleaning solution with **mild dish soap and warm water**. This is the same solution you used for the patent leather. Again, ensure it is not too soapy. You do not want to oversaturate the mesh. I find a light, airy foam works best.

Dip an **old toothbrush** or a soft-bristled brush into the solution. Gently scrub the mesh and fabric areas. Use small, light circular motions. Do not press too hard. Hard scrubbing can fray the mesh or push dirt deeper into the fabric. I focus on areas with visible dirt or discoloration. This technique carefully lifts dirt from the weave of the material.

For tougher stains on the mesh, you can try a slightly stronger solution. A mix of equal parts **white vinegar and water** can help. Dip a clean cloth or a cotton swab into this solution. Gently dab the stained area. Let it sit for a few minutes. Then, gently scrub with your toothbrush. Vinegar is a natural disinfectant and stain remover.

After scrubbing, wipe the mesh and fabric areas with a clean, damp cloth. This removes any soap or cleaning solution residue. Make sure you get all the suds out. Leftover soap can attract more dirt. I wipe until the cloth comes away clean. This ensures no sticky residue remains.

Allow the mesh to air dry completely. Do not try to speed up the drying process with heat. Heat can cause the material to warp or shrink. I usually let them sit overnight. This step is critical for preserving the integrity of the fabric. Taking care of the mesh keeps your Jordan 11s looking fresh. ### Bringing Back the Brightness: Cleaning Jordan 11 Soles and Midsoles

The soles and midsoles of Jordan 11s often show the most wear. They pick up dirt, scuffs, and can even yellow over time. Getting these areas bright again makes a big difference in the shoe's overall look. I have a few tricks up my sleeve for these tough spots. Cleaning your soles and midsoles thoroughly can significantly improve their appearance.

For general sole cleaning, create a paste with **baking soda and water**. Mix about two parts baking soda to one part water until it forms a thick paste. This paste is a gentle abrasive that lifts dirt and brightens rubber. I often use this mixture for most of the sole.

Apply the baking soda paste to the rubber soles and midsoles. Use an **old toothbrush** to scrub the paste into the material. Focus on the grooves and textured areas. The bristles help to work the paste into the dirt. I scrub in small, firm circles. You will see the dirt lifting away as you scrub. This method works well for embedded grime. This process is similar to [how you might clean skateboard bearings with household items](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-skateboard-bearings-with-household-items), where a good scrub and appropriate cleaning agent make a difference on hard surfaces.

For stubborn scuffs and dark marks, a **melamine foam eraser (Magic Eraser)** is incredibly effective. Dampen the eraser slightly. Gently rub it over the scuff marks on the midsole and sole. The abrasive nature of the melamine foam lifts marks without harsh chemicals. I use a light touch to avoid damaging the finish. Be careful not to scrub too hard, especially near painted areas.

If your icy soles have started to yellow, baking soda paste can offer some help. While it will not completely reverse severe yellowing (which needs specialized products), it can brighten them. Apply the paste and let it sit for 10-15 minutes before scrubbing. The natural bleaching action of baking soda helps to lift some of the discoloration. I have seen good results with consistent, gentle application.

After scrubbing, wipe away all the paste and dirt with a clean, damp cloth. Rinse the cloth often to ensure you are not just spreading dirt around. Then, dry the soles thoroughly with a clean, dry cloth. This process leaves your soles looking significantly cleaner and brighter.

### Lace and Insole Refresh: Don't Forget the Details

Often, we focus so much on the shoe's exterior that we forget the smaller parts. The laces and insoles of your Jordan 11s also need attention. Clean laces and fresh insoles complete the overall refreshed look. These details contribute a lot to the shoe's presentation. I never skip these steps.

First, let us talk about the **laces**. They get dirty very quickly. After removing them during the pre-cleaning stage, you can wash them. My preferred method is to put them in a small mesh laundry bag. Then, I toss them into the washing machine with a load of similar colored clothes. Use a regular detergent. This is a simple and effective way to clean them.

If you prefer to hand wash, soak the laces in a bowl of warm, soapy water for about 15-20 minutes. Use the same mild dish soap solution. After soaking, gently rub the laces together to remove any remaining dirt. You can use an old toothbrush for stubborn spots. Rinse them thoroughly under running water until all soap is gone. Hang them to air dry completely.

Now, for the **insoles**. Insoles absorb sweat and odors. It is important to keep them clean. Remove the insoles from your shoes. You can wipe them down with a damp cloth lightly dipped in your mild dish soap solution. Do not saturate them, as they can take a long time to dry. I focus on wiping the top surface.

For odor control, **baking soda** is your best friend. After wiping, sprinkle a thin layer of baking soda directly onto the insoles. Let it sit for several hours, or even overnight. Baking soda absorbs odors naturally. After sitting, simply shake off or vacuum the baking soda. This leaves the insoles feeling and smelling fresher.

Allow both the laces and insoles to air dry fully before putting them back into the shoes. Placing damp components back into a shoe can lead to mold or mildew. I always ensure everything is bone dry. This attention to detail makes a big difference in the overall cleanliness and freshness of your Jordan 11s.

### Drying and Finishing Touches: Proper Care for Longevity

You have put in all the hard work to clean your Jordan 11s. The final steps are just as important as the cleaning itself. Proper drying and finishing protect your shoes and keep them looking great for longer. Incorrect drying can cause damage or alter the shoe's shape. I always make sure to finish strong.

The most important rule for drying is **air drying**. Never put your Jordan 11s in a dryer. The heat from a dryer can cause the patent leather to crack. It can also warp the rubber soles and shrink the mesh. I always place my shoes in a well-ventilated area, away from direct sunlight or heat sources. A fan can help circulate air and speed up the process.

To help maintain the shoe's shape during drying, **stuff them with paper towels or shoe trees**. Crumpled paper towels absorb internal moisture. They also help the shoe keep its original form as it dries. I change the paper towels every few hours if they become damp. This prevents the inside from staying wet too long. Newspaper works too, but be careful of ink transfer onto light-colored linings.

Once your Jordan 11s are completely dry, give them a **final inspection**. Check for any missed spots or lingering residue. If you find any, a quick wipe with a clean, damp cloth should fix it. Then, re-lace your shoes with your freshly cleaned laces. This step brings the whole look together.

For an added layer of protection, consider a **water and stain repellent spray** designed for sneakers. While this is not a household item, it is a worthwhile investment. It creates an invisible barrier. This barrier helps repel liquids and makes future cleaning easier. I apply this spray after every deep clean.

Finally, store your Jordan 11s properly. Keep them in their original box or a shoe container. This protects them from dust and sunlight. Storing them correctly helps preserve their clean state and extends their lifespan. Following these finishing touches ensures your Jordan 11s remain pristine.

### FAQ Section

**Q1: Can I use a washing machine to clean my Jordan 11s?**
A1: No, I do not recommend putting your Jordan 11s in a washing machine. The harsh cycles and hot water can damage the delicate patent leather, mesh, and glue. It can cause cracking, shrinking, or separation of materials. Hand cleaning with mild household items is safer and gives you more control. This method protects the integrity and appearance of your iconic sneakers.

**Q2: How do I remove yellowing from the icy soles with household items?**
A2: For mild yellowing, a paste of baking soda and a little water can help. Apply the paste, scrub gently with an old toothbrush, and let it sit for 10-15 minutes before wiping clean. While household items can brighten, they cannot fully reverse severe yellowing. Specialized de-yellowing products are usually needed for serious cases.

**Q3: What if I do not have a Magic Eraser?**
A3: If you do not have a Magic Eraser, you can use baking soda paste or non-gel white toothpaste for scuffs on the soles and midsoles. Apply a small amount and gently scrub with an old toothbrush or a damp cloth. These items offer a mild abrasive action that helps lift surface marks. Always test on a small, inconspicuous area first.

**Q4: Can I use bleach on the white parts of my Jordan 11s?**
A4: I strongly advise against using bleach on your Jordan 11s. Bleach can cause irreversible damage, including discoloration, weakening of materials, and even yellowing the soles further. Stick to mild household cleaners like dish soap, baking soda, and white vinegar for safe and effective cleaning. These items are much gentler on the shoe's materials.

**Q5: How often should I clean my Jordan 11s?**
A5: The frequency of cleaning depends on how often you wear them and where you wear them. For light wear, a quick wipe-down after each use helps. For regular wear, a deeper clean every few weeks or once a month is a good idea. Spot clean stains immediately to prevent them from setting. Regular maintenance keeps your shoes looking fresh.

**Q6: Are there any household items to avoid when cleaning Jordan 11s?**
A6: Yes, avoid harsh chemicals like bleach, acetone (nail polish remover), and strong detergents. These can strip the finish from patent leather, degrade adhesives, and damage fabric. Also, stay away from abrasive scrub brushes that can scratch the patent leather or fray the mesh. Stick to mild soaps and soft brushes for the best results.
