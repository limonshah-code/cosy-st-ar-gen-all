### What kind of paper should I use for printing?
We recommend using white cardstock (65-80 lb) for best results. Cardstock gives the tags a professional, sturdy feel. However, regular printer paper works fine too, especially if you plan to mount the tags on another backing. Choose what you have or prefer.
