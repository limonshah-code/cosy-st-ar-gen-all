## Specialized Diets: Prescription and Health Management

Hills Science Diet offers a distinct line of prescription diets. These foods are known as Hills Prescription Diet. They are formulated to manage specific health conditions. Veterinarians typically recommend these diets. You cannot buy them without a veterinary authorization. This ensures dogs receive the correct medical nutrition.

These diets help dogs with various medical issues. They can support kidney health, digestive problems, or allergies. Other formulas target urinary health, joint mobility, or weight loss. Each prescription diet contains precise levels of nutrients. These nutrients work to alleviate symptoms and support recovery. This targeted approach makes them a vital tool in veterinary medicine.

### Common Prescription Diet Categories

*   **Digestive Care (i/d):** These diets help dogs with gastrointestinal issues. They are highly digestible and often contain prebiotics.
*   **Urinary Care (c/d, s/d):** These formulas help manage urinary stone formation. They adjust mineral levels to support bladder health.
*   **Weight Management (r/d, w/d, Metabolic):** These diets are lower in calories and higher in fiber. They help dogs achieve and maintain a healthy weight.
*   **Kidney Care (k/d):** These diets reduce the workload on kidneys. They have controlled protein and phosphorus levels.
*   **Dermatology/Allergy Care (d/d, z/d):** These formulas use novel or hydrolyzed proteins. They help manage food sensitivities and skin conditions.
