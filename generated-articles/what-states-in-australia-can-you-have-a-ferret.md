Have you ever dreamed of bringing a playful, curious ferret into your home? These small, energetic creatures make wonderful companions for many. However, if you live in Australia, owning a ferret is not as simple as visiting your local pet store. The question, "What states in Australia can you have a ferret?" comes with a complex answer. Australia has some of the world's strictest animal welfare and biosecurity laws. These laws aim to protect our unique native wildlife and ecosystems. We will explore which Australian states permit ferret ownership, the strict conditions that apply, and why certain states have outright bans.
