## DIY Eco-Friendly Cleaning Recipes for Your Home

While commercial eco-friendly products are fantastic, sometimes the most sustainable option is to make your own. DIY cleaning solutions are often cheaper. They also give you complete control over the ingredients. Plus, they eliminate packaging waste entirely. I love the simplicity and effectiveness of these homemade cleaners.

Here are some simple, effective DIY recipes:

### All-Purpose Cleaner

*   **Ingredients:** 1 part white vinegar, 1 part water, a few drops of essential oil (lemon or tea tree).
*   **Method:** Mix everything in a spray bottle. Shake well before each use.
*   **Use:** This cleaner works on most surfaces. It is great for countertops, sinks, and glass. Vinegar is a natural disinfectant and degreaser. The essential oil adds a fresh scent.

### Glass Cleaner

*   **Ingredients:** 2 cups water, 1/2 cup white vinegar, 1/4 cup rubbing alcohol, 2 drops essential oil (optional).
*   **Method:** Combine ingredients in a spray bottle.
*   **Use:** Spray on windows and mirrors. Wipe with a microfibre cloth for streak-free results. The alcohol helps with quick drying.

### Toilet Bowl Cleaner

*   **Ingredients:** 1/2 cup baking soda, 1/4 cup white vinegar, 10 drops tea tree essential oil.
*   **Method:** Sprinkle baking soda into the toilet bowl. Add vinegar. Let it fizz for 15-30 minutes. Add essential oil. Scrub with a toilet brush. Flush.
*   **Use:** Baking soda scours and deodorizes. Vinegar disinfects and removes limescale. Tea tree oil adds extra antibacterial power.

### Grout Cleaner

*   **Ingredients:** Baking soda, hydrogen peroxide.
*   **Method:** Mix baking soda with enough hydrogen peroxide to form a paste. Apply to grout lines. Let it sit for 10-15 minutes. Scrub with a brush. Rinse well.
*   **Use:** This combination effectively brightens and cleans grout.

### Drain Cleaner

*   **Ingredients:** 1/2 cup baking soda, 1/2 cup white vinegar, boiling water.
*   **Method:** Pour baking soda down the drain. Follow with vinegar. Cover the drain and let it sit for 30 minutes. Finish with a kettle of boiling water.
*   **Use:** This can clear minor clogs and freshen drains. It is much safer than harsh chemical drain cleaners.

Using these simple ingredients can replace many store-bought products. They are non-toxic, biodegradable, and incredibly versatile. Baking soda and vinegar are staples in my cleaning cupboard. They prove that you do not need fancy chemicals for a clean home.
