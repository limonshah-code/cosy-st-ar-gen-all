## Clean Your Kenmore Top Load Washer Filter for Peak Performance

Has your Kenmore top load washer been leaving clothes less than perfectly clean? Perhaps you notice a strange smell or find lint stuck to your freshly washed items. These are common signs that your washer's filter needs attention. A dirty filter reduces efficiency and can even damage your machine.

I will guide you through the straightforward process of how to clean the filter on the Kenmore top load washer. This task is simpler than you might think. Regular filter cleaning is essential for maintaining your washer's performance. It also helps extend its lifespan and ensures your laundry comes out truly clean. Let's get started on improving your washer's effectiveness today.

### Takeaway

*   Locate your Kenmore top load washer's filter, often near the pump or dispenser.
*   Prepare for water spillage by placing towels and a shallow pan.
*   Carefully remove the filter and clear all accumulated debris.
*   Rinse the filter thoroughly under running water to remove soap scum and lint.
*   Reinstall the clean filter securely, ensuring a tight seal.
*   Perform routine filter checks to prevent clogs and maintain washer efficiency.

### Clear Answer to Your Main Query

To clean the filter on your Kenmore top load washer, locate the drain pump filter, usually behind an access panel or near the bottom front. Drain any residual water, carefully unscrew or unclip the filter, remove lint and debris, rinse it thoroughly, and then reinstall it securely.

## Understanding Your Kenmore Top Load Washer Filter

Every Kenmore top load washer has a filter system designed to catch lint, debris, and small items. This filter prevents these particles from clogging the drain pump. Without this filter, socks, coins, and pet hair could damage your machine's internal components. The filter plays a vital role in keeping your washer running smoothly.

You might wonder where this filter is located. Most Kenmore top load models have a drain pump filter. It is often found at the bottom front of the washer. Sometimes, it is behind a small access panel. Other models might have a lint filter integrated into the agitator or at the top rim of the wash tub. Check your washer's user manual for the exact location. Identifying the correct filter is the first step towards proper cleaning.

Ignoring this filter can lead to many problems. Your clothes may not get clean. They might come out with lint or a foul smell. The machine itself might struggle to drain water. This can cause error codes or even machine failure. Keeping the filter clean helps your washer work its best. It keeps your laundry fresh. This simple maintenance task protects your investment.

### Different Types of Filters in Kenmore Top Load Washers

Kenmore top-load washers use different filter types. Knowing your specific model helps with proper cleaning.

*   **Drain Pump Filter:** This is the most common filter found in newer Kenmore top-load washers. It sits near the drain pump, usually at the bottom front. It collects larger debris before water drains.
*   **Lint Filter (Self-Cleaning):** Many modern top-load washers have self-cleaning lint filters. These are often integrated into the agitator or the wash basket itself. They break down lint during the wash cycle. You do not manually clean these.
*   **Lint Trap (Manual):** Older Kenmore top-load models might have a manual lint trap. These are sometimes located along the top rim of the wash tub. They are mesh screens that you remove and clean by hand.

Always consult your washer's user manual. This tells you which filter your specific model uses. It also shows you its exact location. Understanding your filter type ensures you clean it correctly and efficiently.

## Locating and Accessing the Filter on Your Kenmore Top Load Washer

Finding the filter on your Kenmore top load washer is a key step. The location varies by model. However, there are common places to check. Most modern Kenmore top loaders feature a drain pump filter. This is the main filter you need to clean.

Start by looking at the bottom front of your washing machine. Many Kenmore models have a small access panel here. This panel might be a hinged door or a removable kick plate. You might need a flathead screwdriver to gently pry it open. Once opened, you will likely see a round cap or a hose with a cap. This is usually where the drain pump filter resides.

For some older Kenmore top load washers, the lint filter might be different. It could be a mesh screen built into the agitator. The agitator is the central post in your washer. Other older models might have a removable mesh screen along the top rim of the wash basket. If you cannot find a panel at the bottom, check these areas. If your washer's manual specifies a "self-cleaning" lint filter, you might not have a manual filter to clean.

Consulting your user manual is the best method. It shows precise diagrams for your specific model. If you do not have the manual, you can often find it online. Search for your Kenmore washer's model number. This ensures you target the correct filter. Knowing where to look saves time and prevents unnecessary disassembly. Proper access is crucial for an effective cleaning job.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning the Drain Pump Filter

Cleaning your Kenmore top load washer's drain pump filter is straightforward. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a thorough clean. This process removes built-up lint and debris. It helps your washer drain properly.

1.  **Prepare the Area:** Lay towels on the floor around the front of your washer. Place a shallow pan or baking tray directly under the filter access point. This will catch any water that drains out. Remember to [unplug your washing machine and turn off the water supply](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-deep-clean-washer-machine) before you start. Safety comes first when working with appliances.

2.  **Drain Residual Water:** Most drain pump filters have a small drain hose next to them. Uncap this hose and allow any remaining water to flow into your shallow pan. This prevents a big mess when you remove the main filter. Once the water stops, re-cap the small hose.

3.  **Remove the Filter:** Carefully unscrew or unclip the main drain pump filter. Turn it counter-clockwise. You may feel some resistance. Expect more water to drain out as you remove it. Be prepared with your towels and pan. Once unscrewed, pull the filter straight out.

4.  **Clean the Filter:** You will likely find a significant amount of lint, hair, and small objects on the filter. Use your fingers to pull off large pieces of debris. Use an old toothbrush or a soft brush to scrub away remaining grime. Rinse the filter thoroughly under running water. Use warm water and a little mild soap or white vinegar. Make sure all channels are clear. This step is critical for proper drainage. If you find pet hair, consider methods for [how to get dog hair out of washer](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-get-dog-hair-out-of-washer) in future washes to reduce filter buildup.

5.  **Clean the Filter Housing:** Look inside the filter housing in the washer. Use a damp cloth to wipe away any gunk or slime you see. Ensure nothing is blocking the opening where the filter sits. A clean housing helps the filter work better.

6.  **Reinstall the Filter:** Once the filter is clean and dry, carefully screw it back into its housing. Turn it clockwise until it is finger-tight. Do not overtighten it. Ensure it sits snugly to prevent leaks. Re-secure the access panel.

7.  **Restore Power and Water:** Plug your washer back in. Turn the water supply back on. Run a quick rinse cycle without any clothes. This checks for leaks. It also flushes out any remaining cleaning solution. If no leaks appear, you did a great job! Regularly [cleaning your water filter](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-water-filter) helps maintain overall water quality for your appliances.

This detailed process helps you maintain your Kenmore top load washer. It improves cleaning efficiency. It also prevents costly repairs.
