## Common Health Concerns for French Bulldogs as They Age

French Bulldogs are prone to specific health issues. These concerns often become more apparent with age. Being aware of them helps owners provide timely care. Early detection and management are key for an aging French Bulldog. Winston, at 7, could start showing signs of these conditions.

**Brachycephalic Obstructive Airway Syndrome (BOAS):** This is a common issue for short-nosed breeds. It affects their breathing. Symptoms include snoring, snorting, and difficulty breathing. These can worsen with age or weight gain. Surgery can help in severe cases. Owners must monitor their dog's breathing closely. I always listen for any changes in my dog's breathing patterns.

**Spinal Issues:** French Bulldogs are susceptible to intervertebral disc disease (IVDD). Their unique spine structure makes them vulnerable. Symptoms include pain, weakness, or paralysis. Avoiding jumping and rough play helps prevent injury. Weight management also reduces strain on the spine. Regular check-ups can identify early signs.

**Joint Problems:** Arthritis and patellar luxation (slipping kneecap) are common. Older French Bulldogs may show stiffness or lameness. Supplements like glucosamine can help. Anti-inflammatory medications may be prescribed. Providing soft bedding and ramps can ease movement. This directly impacts their comfort.

**Dental Disease:** Older dogs are more prone to dental problems. Plaque and tartar buildup lead to gum disease. This causes pain and can affect overall health. Regular dental cleanings by a vet are crucial. Daily brushing at home also helps. Good dental hygiene supports long-term health.

**Eye Conditions:** Conditions like cherry eye or cataracts can occur. Cataracts particularly affect older dogs. They can impair vision. Regular eye exams can detect these issues. Some conditions are treatable with medication or surgery. My vet always checks my dog's eyes thoroughly during visits.

Owners should watch for any changes in Winston's health. Regular vet visits are vital for screening. They can identify these concerns early. This proactive approach helps maintain his quality of life.
