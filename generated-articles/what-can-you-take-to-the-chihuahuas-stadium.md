### Beverage and Food Guidelines: What You Can Bring to the Ballgame

Stadiums have specific rules about outside food and beverages. The Chihuahuas Stadium is no different. Generally, you cannot bring outside food or drinks into the stadium. This rule helps the stadium manage its concessions. It also helps control what enters the venue.

This helps fans stay hydrated, especially during warm weather games. Empty, reusable water bottles are also often allowed. You can fill them at water fountains inside the stadium. This is an eco-friendly option.

Special considerations apply for infants. You can bring baby food, formula, or bottles for babies. These items are exempt from the outside food policy. They are essential for young children. Security staff may inspect these items at the gate. This is a standard procedure for everyone's safety.

Do not try to bring alcoholic beverages. The stadium sells alcohol, and outside alcohol is strictly forbidden. This rule is in place for safety and licensing reasons. Trying to sneak in alcohol can lead to removal from the stadium. Always follow these guidelines to have a pleasant visit.
