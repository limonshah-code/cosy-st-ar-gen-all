## Navigating the Market: Buying and Selling LPS Today

The return of Littlest Pet Shop has changed the market dynamics. Both new and vintage LPS figures are in demand. Understanding this market helps collectors and sellers. For new LPS, major retailers are the primary source. Stores like Walmart, Target, and Amazon carry the new line. Basic Fun! also sells directly through their website. Prices for new figures are set by the manufacturer. They are generally affordable. This makes it easy for new collectors to start. Blind boxes are popular. They add an element of surprise. Collecting full sets from blind boxes can be challenging. It might require multiple purchases.

The vintage LPS market is different. These figures are no longer mass-produced. Their availability depends on secondary markets. Websites like eBay, Mercari, and Etsy are common places to buy and sell. Social media groups also facilitate trades and sales. Prices for vintage LPS vary wildly. Factors include the pet's rarity, its condition, and current demand. Some individual figures can fetch hundreds of dollars. Full playsets or large lots also sell well.

When buying vintage LPS, checking authenticity is important. There are many fakes or custom figures. Learn about common signs of reproduction. Comparing pictures with known authentic figures helps. Look at the neck peg, magnet hole, and body shape. Experienced collectors often share guides online. Ask for clear photos from different angles. This helps avoid disappointment. Condition matters a lot. A figure with scratches, paint chips, or missing parts will sell for less.

Selling LPS, both new and vintage, requires good presentation. Clean figures thoroughly before listing them. Take clear, well-lit photos. Write detailed descriptions. Include any flaws or damage. Accurate descriptions build trust with buyers. Pricing is crucial. Research similar listings. Price your items competitively. For vintage LPS, remember to factor in rarity. What was once common might now be harder to find.

The market for LPS figures is robust. The comeback has stimulated interest in both old and new. This means more opportunities for buying, selling, and trading.
