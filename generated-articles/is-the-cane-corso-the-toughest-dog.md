The Cane Corso's toughness comes from a combination of factors. These include their large, muscular build, impressive bite force, and historical working background. They also possess significant mental fortitude, courage, and a deep loyalty to their families, making them formidable protectors.

Yes, a Cane Corso can protect a family effectively. Their strong protective instincts, imposing presence, and willingness to defend their loved ones make them excellent guardians. Cane Corsos are generally not recommended for first-time dog owners. They require an experienced owner who understands large, powerful working breeds.
