## Chihuahua Temperament and Indoor Living

Chihuahuas are known for their strong personalities. Their temperament greatly influences their suitability for indoor living. These dogs often bond deeply with one person. ## Health Considerations for Indoor Chihuahuas

Chihuahuas are generally healthy dogs. However, they have specific health considerations. Being an indoor dog means you can monitor them closely. Patellar luxation is a common orthopedic issue. This affects their kneecaps. Hydrocephalus can also occur, especially in puppies. Heart conditions like patent ductus arteriosus are seen. Regular veterinary check-ups are very important.

Their small size makes them prone to injury. Be careful when stepping or sitting. Avoid dropping them. Keep them warm in colder climates. Their thin coats offer little protection. Dental health is also a major concern for Chihuahuas. They often develop dental disease early. Regular teeth cleaning and veterinary dental care are vital. These proactive steps ensure your indoor Chihuahua stays healthy. A healthy dog is a happy companion.

### Common Health Issues

*   **Patellar Luxation:** This is a condition where the kneecap slips out of place. It can cause lameness. Regular vet checks help detect it early.
*   **Hypoglycemia:** Low blood sugar can affect Chihuahua puppies. Ensure they eat regular, small meals. Watch for signs of weakness.
*   **Dental Disease:** Chihuahuas are prone to plaque and tartar buildup. This leads to gum disease. Daily brushing and professional cleanings are essential.
*   **Tracheal Collapse:** Their small windpipes can sometimes collapse. This causes a honking cough. Avoid pulling on their collar. Use a harness instead.
*   **Open Fontanel:** Some Chihuahuas are born with a soft spot on their skull. This requires careful handling. Protect their head from bumps.
