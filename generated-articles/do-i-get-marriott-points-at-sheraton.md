### Understanding the Marriott Bonvoy Program and Sheraton Hotels

The Marriott Bonvoy program is one of the world's largest hotel loyalty programs. It covers an expansive collection of brands. When Marriott acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts, Sheraton became a part of the Marriott family. This merger significantly expanded the options for Marriott Bonvoy members. It made Sheraton a valuable destination for earning and redeeming points.

Before the merger, Sheraton operated under the Starwood Preferred Guest (SPG) program. SPG was highly regarded by many travelers. The integration of SPG into Marriott Bonvoy was a large undertaking. It aimed to create a single, unified loyalty experience. Today, all Sheraton properties fully participate in the Bonvoy program. This means you can earn points, use elite status benefits, and redeem points at these hotels. It simplifies travel planning for loyal members like me. I find it very convenient to have all my hotel rewards in one place.

The Marriott Bonvoy program offers many ways to earn points. Stays at Sheraton hotels are a primary method. The points you earn can be used across the entire Marriott Bonvoy portfolio. This includes luxury brands like The Ritz-Carlton and St. Regis, as well as extended-stay options like Residence Inn. This flexibility is a major benefit for travelers. Knowing that my Sheraton stays help me earn points for future luxury trips is a great feeling. It really makes every stay more rewarding.
