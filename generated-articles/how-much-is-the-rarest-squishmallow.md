Many rare Squishmallows originate from exclusive partnerships with specific retailers. Stores like Walgreens, Target, or even smaller boutique shops sometimes offer unique Squishmallow designs. ## Identifying a Truly Rare Squishmallow

Many factors contribute to a Squishmallow's rarity and value beyond just its appearance. Knowing how to properly inspect a Squishmallow can help you determine its authenticity and potential worth. This process involves examining specific details, particularly the tags and unique production characteristics. I have learned to pay close attention to these small but important signs.

### Checking the Hang Tag and Tush Tag

The hang tag is the paper tag attached to the Squishmallow, usually by a plastic loop. It often includes the Squishmallow's name, biography, and collection information. The tush tag is sewn into the Squishmallow itself and lists manufacturing details, safety warnings, and often a production date or code. Both tags are crucial. A rare Squishmallow with pristine, intact tags is significantly more valuable than one without. I always ensure these tags are present and readable.

### Understanding Production Variations

Sometimes, slight variations in design or material occur during production runs. These variations might include different fabric textures, color shades, or even small accessory changes. These "variants" can sometimes be rarer than the standard version. Researching specific Squishmallow lines and their known variations can help you identify these unique pieces. The subtle differences can make a big impact on rarity.

### Size and Unique Traits

The size of a Squishmallow can also play a role in its rarity. Certain characters might be more common in smaller sizes but extremely rare in larger formats, or vice-versa. Additionally, unique traits like a special holographic tag, a metallic finish, or a character with a unique accessory might indicate a special edition. These distinguishing features often signal a higher level of rarity.
