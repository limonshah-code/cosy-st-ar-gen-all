**Training and Socialization:** German Shepherds are highly trainable. They excel in obedience and protection work. However, they need consistent training from puppyhood. Early socialization is also vital. This ensures they grow into well-adjusted adults. They should meet many people, places, and other animals. Without proper guidance, their protective instincts can become overprotective.

**Exercise Needs:** This breed is energetic. They require a lot of physical activity every day. Long walks, runs, and active play sessions are essential. **Grooming:** German Shepherds have a thick double coat. They shed a lot, especially seasonally.
