## Clean Mold From Your Pebble Shower Floor Safely

Pebble shower floors add a touch of natural beauty to any bathroom. Their unique texture feels wonderful underfoot. However, their uneven surface and many grout lines create perfect hiding spots for mold. If you see black, green, or pinkish stains, mold has found a home.

Cleaning mold from pebble shower floors requires careful steps. Harsh chemicals can damage the natural stones or the grout. I want to help you restore your shower's beauty without causing harm. This guide will walk you through safe, effective methods. We will cover everything from preparation to prevention. Let's make your pebble shower sparkle again.

### Takeaway

*   **Safety First:** Always wear gloves, eye protection, and ensure good ventilation.
*   **Gentle Solutions:** Use natural cleaners like vinegar or hydrogen peroxide first.
*   **Thorough Scrubbing:** A stiff brush is essential for reaching grout lines and pebble crevices.
*   **Rinse Well:** Flush away all cleaning solutions and mold spores.
*   **Prevent Recurrence:** Improve ventilation, dry the shower after use, and apply a sealant regularly.
