## Professional Baseball Leagues in Mexico

Mexico has a well-established professional baseball structure. Two main leagues dominate the scene. These are the Liga Mexicana de Béisbol (LMB) and the Liga Mexicana del Pacífico (LMP). Each league has its own season and unique history. Both leagues are highly competitive.

### Liga Mexicana de Béisbol (LMB)

The Liga Mexicana de Béisbol, or Mexican Baseball League, is the premier summer professional baseball league in Mexico. It is similar to Major League Baseball in the United States. The LMB has a long history, dating back to 1925. Teams play a full schedule from April to August. Then, they enter a playoff series.

The Dorados de Chihuahua are part of this prestigious league. They compete against teams from major Mexican cities. Playing in the LMB means the Dorados face high-level competition. It showcases top talent.

### Liga Mexicana del Pacífico (LMP)

The Liga Mexicana del Pacífico, or Mexican Pacific League, is Mexico's top winter baseball league. It runs from October to January. The LMP is very popular, especially in the northwestern part of Mexico. Many players from the LMB and even MLB play in the LMP during the off-season. This makes the LMP very high-quality baseball.

While Chihuahua's main professional team, the Dorados, plays in the LMB, the LMP is still important to Mexican baseball culture. It provides another avenue for fans to enjoy baseball. It also allows players to develop their skills. Both leagues contribute to Mexico's strong baseball tradition.
