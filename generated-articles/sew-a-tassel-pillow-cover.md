## Sew A Tassel Pillow Cover: Your DIY Style Guide

It allows you to create custom pieces that perfectly match your style. This guide will help you craft beautiful, stylish pillows for your home. We will cover everything from choosing materials to the final stitches.

Sewing a tassel pillow cover adds texture and charm to any room. It is a fantastic way to update your decor without spending a lot of money. You can select fabrics and colors that truly reflect your taste. Prepare to transform your home with these chic, handmade accents.

### Takeaway

*   **Gather Materials:** Collect your chosen fabric, tassels, sewing supplies, and an insert.
*   **Measure and Cut:** Precisely cut fabric pieces according to your desired pillow size.
*   **Sew the Cover:** Construct the main pillow cover using your preferred closure style.
*   **Attach Tassels:** Securely sew or glue tassels to the pillow cover's edges or corners.
*   **Finish and Enjoy:** Insert your pillow form and admire your new, custom decor piece.

### How do I sew a tassel pillow cover?

To sew a tassel pillow cover, you need fabric, tassels, a sewing machine, and basic tools. First, cut your fabric to size. Then, sew the main pillow cover, leaving one side open for the insert. Next, attach your tassels to the edges. Finally, close the last seam, and you have a new pillow cover.

#### Choosing the Perfect Fabric

The fabric choice sets the tone for your entire pillow. Consider how the pillow will be used. Durable fabrics like cotton duck or canvas work well for high-traffic areas. Softer fabrics like linen or velvet add a touch of luxury. Choose a fabric that complements your room's existing colors and patterns.

Think about the fabric's weight and drape. A thicker fabric holds its shape better for a structured pillow. Lighter fabrics might need a lining for stability. Always pre-wash your fabric to prevent shrinking later. This step ensures your pillow cover maintains its size and shape.

### Preparing Your Fabric and Tassels

Before you start sewing, proper preparation saves time and ensures a professional finish. This involves careful measuring and cutting of your fabric. It also includes getting your tassels ready for attachment. Taking these steps seriously makes the rest of the project much easier.

Do not rush this stage. Accuracy here will lead to a better end result. You want your pillow cover to fit your insert perfectly.

#### Accurate Measurements for Success

Start by measuring your pillow insert. Most standard inserts are square, like 18x18 inches or 20x20 inches. For a snug fit, cut your fabric slightly smaller than the insert size. For example, for an 18-inch insert, cut your fabric pieces to 17.5 inches. This helps the pillow look full and neat.

If you plan an envelope closure, you will need three pieces of fabric. Two will be for the back, overlapping in the center. The front piece will be a single square. For a zipper closure, you need two main fabric pieces. Always double-check your measurements before cutting.

#### Pre-Washing: A Smart First Step

Pre-washing your fabric is a crucial step that many skip. Fabric can shrink when washed for the first time. If you sew your cover and then wash it, it might no longer fit your insert. Wash your fabric the same way you plan to wash the finished pillow cover.

This step also removes any sizing or chemicals from the fabric. It makes the fabric softer and easier to work with. Once washed and dried, iron your fabric smooth. This provides a clean, flat surface for precise cutting and sewing.

#### Readying Your Tassels for Attachment

If you are using store-bought tassels, inspect them. Ensure they are all the same length and in good condition. If they have loops, these loops are where you will sew them to the fabric. For handmade tassels, trim any uneven ends.

Decide on the placement of your tassels. Will they be on all four corners? Or along all four edges? Mark their positions lightly on your fabric. This helps with even spacing later. Prepare all your tassels before you start sewing the main cover.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Constructing Your Pillow Cover

Now comes the exciting part: assembling your pillow cover. There are several ways to make a basic pillow cover. We will focus on an easy envelope closure and a zipper closure. Both methods are simple enough for beginners. Choose the one you feel most comfortable with.

Proper seam allowances are important for a neat finish. Use a standard 1/2-inch or 5/8-inch seam allowance. Stitching slowly and evenly helps create strong seams.

#### Sewing an Easy Envelope Closure

An envelope closure is great for beginners. It does not require a zipper. You will need one front piece of fabric and two back pieces. The back pieces overlap to create an opening. Cut your front fabric piece to your desired finished size, e.g., 17.5x17.5 inches.

For the back pieces, cut two rectangles. Each should be the same width as your front piece. Their combined length should be about 5-6 inches longer than the front piece's length. For an 18-inch pillow, each back piece might be 17.5 inches wide by 12-13 inches long. Hem one long edge of each back piece. Place the front piece right side up. Lay the two back pieces on top, right side down, overlapping them in the center. Pin all around and sew the four sides. Turn right side out. #### Installing a Simple Zipper Closure

A zipper closure offers a clean, modern look. It also makes inserting and removing the pillow form very easy. You will need two fabric pieces for the front and back. Cut them the same size, e.g., 17.5x17.5 inches. Choose a zipper that is slightly shorter than one side of your pillow.

Place the two fabric pieces right sides together. Sew one edge, leaving a gap in the center where the zipper will go. Press the seam open. Pin the zipper face down over the open seam allowance. Stitch the zipper in place using a zipper foot. Then, open the seam stitches over the zipper. Finish sewing the remaining three sides of the pillow cover. ### Adding the Tassels for a Designer Touch

Once your pillow cover is sewn, it is time to add the tassels. This step truly brings your design to life. The placement and method of attachment are important for a professional look. Take your time to get the spacing right.

Tassels transform a simple pillow into a decorative accent. They add texture, movement, and a playful touch. Think about where you want them to draw the eye.

#### Strategic Tassel Placement

Most people attach tassels to the four corners of the pillow. This is a classic and elegant look. You can also place them along all four edges for a fuller effect. Mark your desired placement points with a fabric marker or pin. Ensure they are evenly spaced.

Consider the size of your tassels. Larger tassels might look better spaced further apart. Smaller tassels can be closer together. Experiment with different layouts before you commit to sewing them on. This helps visualize the final design.

#### Secure Attachment Methods

The most secure way to attach tassels is by sewing them. If your tassels have loops, stitch through the loop directly onto the pillow fabric. Use a strong thread and make several small, firm stitches. You want them to withstand everyday use.

For tassels without loops, you might need to create a small fabric tab. Sew the tassel to the tab first, then sew the tab to the pillow. You can also use a strong fabric adhesive for temporary placement before sewing. Make sure all tassels are firmly attached. You do not want them coming loose.

### Finishing Touches and Care Tips

With your tassels attached, your sew a tassel pillow cover project is almost complete. The final steps make sure your pillow looks its best. Knowing how to care for your new creation helps it last longer. A well-stuffed and well-maintained pillow brings joy for years.

These finishing touches are important. They ensure your hard work results in a beautiful, durable item. Enjoy the last steps of your sewing journey.

#### Stuffing for a Fuller Look

Choose a pillow insert that matches the size of your cover. For a very full pillow, you can use an insert that is one inch larger than your cover. For example, use an 18-inch insert for a 17-inch cover. This prevents sagging and gives a plush look. Gently push the insert into the cover, working it into the corners.

Smooth out any wrinkles in the fabric. Make sure the insert sits evenly inside the cover. A properly stuffed pillow looks professionally made. It also feels more comfortable and inviting.

#### Keeping Your Tassel Pillow Looking New

Care for your tassel pillow cover based on the fabric and tassel materials. Always check fabric care labels if available. Most cotton or linen pillow covers can be machine washed on a gentle cycle. Use cold water to prevent shrinking or color fading.

If your tassels are delicate, hand-washing might be better. Or, remove the tassels before washing the cover. Air dry your pillow cover to protect both the fabric and the tassels. Iron on a low setting if needed, avoiding direct heat on the tassels. Proper care ensures your DIY creation remains beautiful.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Can I sew a tassel pillow cover without a sewing machine?

Yes, you can sew a tassel pillow cover without a sewing machine. You will need a needle, thread, and sharp scissors. Hand sewing takes more time but allows for careful, precise stitches. A strong backstitch is best for durable seams. You can also use fabric glue for some parts, but sewn seams are stronger for pillows.

#### What fabric is best for tassel pillows?

Durable fabrics like cotton twill, canvas, or linen blends are excellent choices for tassel pillows. They hold their shape well and stand up to daily use. For a softer feel, consider velvet or brushed cotton. Always choose a fabric that complements the style and colors of your existing decor. Pre-wash your fabric to prevent shrinking later.

#### How do I choose the right size tassels?

Choose tassel size based on your pillow's dimensions and desired look. For an 18-inch or 20-inch throw pillow, tassels that are 2-4 inches long often look best. Smaller pillows might suit shorter tassels. Larger tassels make a bolder statement. Hold them up to your fabric to visualize the effect before buying or making them.

#### How do I clean a tassel pillow cover?

To clean a tassel pillow cover, check the fabric and tassel materials first. Many cotton or linen covers are machine washable on a gentle, cold cycle. For delicate tassels or fabrics like silk, hand-washing or spot cleaning is safer. Always air dry your pillow cover to protect both the fabric and tassels. Avoid harsh chemicals.

#### Can I use different types of tassels?

Absolutely! Using different types of tassels adds texture and interest to your pillow. You can mix colors, sizes, or materials. For example, combine chunky wool tassels with thinner, silk ones. Or use tassels with a mix of beads. This creates a unique, custom look that truly reflects your personal style.
