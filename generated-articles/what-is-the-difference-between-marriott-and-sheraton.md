## Understanding the Relationship: Marriott's Hotel Umbrella

Many people think Marriott and Sheraton are competing companies. This is not the case. Marriott International is a massive hotel company. It owns many different hotel brands. Sheraton is one of the most recognizable brands within Marriott's extensive portfolio.

Marriott acquired Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide in 2016. This acquisition brought Sheraton into the Marriott family. Before this, Sheraton was part of Starwood. Now, it operates under the Marriott Bonvoy loyalty program. This integration greatly expanded Marriott's global reach. It also diversified its offerings. Marriott wanted to provide choices for every type of traveler. Sheraton filled an important niche within this strategy.

### A Brief History of Sheraton's Journey

Sheraton has a rich and long history. The first Sheraton hotel opened in Springfield, Massachusetts, in 1937. It quickly grew into an international chain. Sheraton became known for its strong presence in city centers. It was also popular for its large convention hotels. The brand had a reputation for full-service hospitality. This includes restaurants, bars, and ample meeting facilities.

Over the decades, Sheraton expanded worldwide. It was one of the first Western hotel brands to establish a presence in China. This global expansion cemented its position. Sheraton became a household name for travelers around the world. Its acquisition by Marriott was a significant moment in its history. It brought Sheraton under the wing of an even larger hospitality leader. ## Loyalty Programs: The Marriott Bonvoy Advantage

One of the biggest benefits of Sheraton being part of Marriott is the loyalty program. Both Marriott and Sheraton hotels participate in Marriott Bonvoy. This means you earn points when you stay at either. You can also redeem points at any of the Marriott Bonvoy brands. This unified program is a huge advantage for travelers.

Marriott Bonvoy has millions of members worldwide. It offers different elite status tiers. These tiers provide various benefits. You might get room upgrades, late check-out, or free breakfast. Achieving status with Marriott Bonvoy works the same, regardless of the Marriott brand you choose. This means your stays at a Sheraton hotel count towards your status. They help you unlock better perks.

For example, I recently stayed at a Sheraton. I earned Bonvoy points. These points then helped me qualify for higher status. Later, I used those points for a stay at a different Marriott brand. This seamless integration is a major draw. It removes any complexity. Guests do not need to manage multiple loyalty accounts.

Marriott Bonvoy points have good value. You can use them for free nights. You can also redeem them for experiences. The program aims to reward loyal guests. It encourages them to stay within the Marriott family of brands. Sheraton plays a crucial role in this system. It offers many locations for members to earn and redeem points.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are Sheraton hotels part of Marriott?

Yes, Sheraton hotels are part of Marriott International. Marriott acquired the brand in 2016 when it purchased Starwood Hotels & Resorts Worldwide. This means Sheraton operates under the Marriott umbrella.

### What is Marriott Bonvoy?

Marriott Bonvoy is Marriott International's loyalty program. Guests earn points for stays at Sheraton and other Marriott brands. They can redeem points for free nights and other rewards. The program also offers elite status with various benefits.

### Is Sheraton a luxury brand?

Sheraton is generally considered an upscale or upper-upscale brand. It offers a full-service experience with many amenities. However, it is not typically in the same luxury tier as brands like The Ritz-Carlton or St. Regis within the Marriott portfolio.

### How does the guest experience compare between Marriott and Sheraton?

The guest experience at Sheraton is consistent and full-service. It focuses on classic comfort and robust amenities like Club Lounges. Marriott, as a whole, offers a wider range of experiences. These range from budget-friendly to ultra-luxury, depending on the specific brand chosen.

### Are Sheraton hotels expensive?

The cost of Sheraton hotels varies by location, season, and specific property. They are generally priced in the upper-upscale category. They offer good value for their full range of services and amenities. You can find competitive rates, especially during off-peak times.

### Can I earn Bonvoy points at Sheraton?

Absolutely. Since Sheraton is a Marriott brand, you earn Marriott Bonvoy points for eligible stays. You can also use your Bonvoy points to book nights at any Sheraton property worldwide. This makes it a great option for Bonvoy members.
