### Recognizing Underweight or Overweight Signs

Knowing the signs of an unhealthy weight is important. If your Field Golden Retriever is underweight, their ribs, spine, and hip bones may be very prominent. Their waist might be excessively tucked in. They might lack muscle mass. This could indicate insufficient food intake. It could also signal an underlying health issue. Consult your vet if you suspect your dog is too thin.

Signs of being overweight include difficulty feeling ribs. Their waist might be absent. You might see fat deposits over their back and tail base. Overweight dogs often move slowly. They may have less stamina. Obesity puts extra strain on joints. It increases the risk of heart disease and diabetes. Early intervention is key for both underweight and overweight conditions.
