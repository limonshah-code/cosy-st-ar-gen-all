## Uncovering the Value of the Rarest Squishmallows

Squishmallows are soft, cuddly plush toys that captured many hearts. They come in hundreds of characters, sizes, and colors. These plush friends range from simple animals to unique fantasy creatures. Their popularity grew quickly, making them a global phenomenon.

Many people enjoy collecting Squishmallows. It is fun to find new ones and add them to a growing collection. However, some Squishmallows are harder to find than others. These rare ones often fetch high prices on the secondary market. This makes many collectors wonder: how much is the rarest Squishmallow worth? Understanding their value helps collectors make smart choices. This article explores the factors that make a Squishmallow rare and valuable. We will also discuss some of the most expensive ones known. Finally, we will guide you on how to value your own collection.

### Takeaway

*   **Rarity Factors:** Limited editions, exclusive releases, and unique tags drive Squishmallow value.
*   **Top Values:** The rarest Squishmallows can sell for hundreds to thousands of dollars.
*   **Verification:** Always check for authentic tags and buy from trusted sellers to avoid fakes.
*   **Market Research:** Use sold listings on reputable sites to determine current market worth.
*   **Condition Matters:** New with tags (NWT) Squishmallows hold the highest value.

### How much is the rarest Squishmallow worth?

The rarest Squishmallows can be worth a significant amount. Their value ranges from a few hundred dollars to several thousand dollars. Specific characters, their size, and their release details determine the exact price. For example, some highly sought-after Squishmallows like Jack the Black Cat (500 Limited Edition) have sold for over \$4,000.
