## Create a Rustic Boho Glam Christmas Tree

Are you ready to create a truly magical holiday display this year? Many people seek a Christmas tree style that feels both cozy and luxurious. This year, leave behind traditional decorating norms. Embrace a blend of natural elements, free-spirited bohemian charm, and sophisticated sparkle.

I love to explore different themes for my Christmas trees. The rustic boho glam Christmas tree trend offers something truly special. It combines the warmth of the countryside with an artistic, elegant touch. This approach allows for creative expression and a beautiful, personalized holiday centerpiece. Get ready to discover how to design a stunning tree. We will cover everything from the foundation to the final shimmering touches.

### Takeaway

*   Choose a suitable tree base, considering real, artificial, flocked, or green options.
*   Select a color palette that blends earthy neutrals with metallic accents.
*   Layer various types of lights for a deep, inviting glow.
*   Mix natural, bohemian, and glamorous ornaments for rich texture and sparkle.

*   Finish with a unique tree topper and a complementary skirt or collar.

### How do I create a rustic boho glam Christmas tree?

You create a rustic boho glam Christmas tree by blending natural textures, bohemian elements, and luxurious accents. Start with a foundational color scheme of earthy neutrals and metallic pops. Then layer lights, unique ornaments, and textural fillers. This method creates a warm, free-spirited, and elegant holiday centerpiece.

## The Foundation: Choosing Your Rustic Boho Glam Christmas Tree

The first step in designing your beautiful rustic boho glam Christmas tree is selecting the right tree. The tree itself acts as your canvas. Its style and size impact the overall look. You have excellent options, whether you prefer real or artificial.

Thinking about the tree’s appearance helps guide your choices. A beautiful tree ensures your decorations shine. I always consider the space where the tree will stand. This helps me choose the perfect foundation for my holiday masterpiece.

### Real vs. Artificial Christmas Trees

Choosing between a real and an artificial tree depends on your preferences. Real trees offer a wonderful, fresh pine scent. They create a traditional holiday experience. Real trees also connect you to nature.

Artificial trees provide convenience and longevity. They come in many shapes and sizes. You can reuse them year after year. Artificial trees also allow for more flexibility in design. I find both options work well for a rustic boho glam aesthetic.

### Flocked vs. Natural Green

The finish of your tree is important. A natural green tree provides a classic backdrop. Its deep color makes metallic and natural ornaments stand out. This option offers a vibrant, organic feel.

A flocked tree has a beautiful snow-dusted appearance. The white flocking adds instant rustic charm and glamour. It makes colors pop and enhances a cozy, winter wonderland vibe. I have used both, and flocked trees especially elevate the glam factor. ## Lighting Up Your Rustic Boho Glam Tree

Proper lighting is essential for any Christmas tree. For a rustic boho glam tree, lighting does more than just illuminate. It creates warmth, depth, and a magical ambiance. Thoughtful light placement makes your tree truly glow.

I always start with lights before any ornaments. This ensures an even distribution. It also allows me to adjust for the perfect luminous effect. Layering different types of lights enhances the overall beauty.

### Warm White or Fairy Lights?

Warm white lights are a classic choice. They emit a soft, inviting glow. This light color enhances the cozy, rustic feel. They make natural textures look beautiful. Warm white lights create a traditional holiday warmth.

Fairy lights offer a delicate, whimsical sparkle. These tiny lights can be strung loosely or wrapped tightly. They add a magical, ethereal quality. Fairy lights are perfect for the bohemian part of the look. You can use them to highlight specific ornaments. I often combine both for a layered effect.

### Layering Lights for Depth

Layering lights adds significant depth to your tree. Start with larger, standard warm white lights placed deeper within the branches. This technique illuminates the tree from the inside out. It creates a rich, full glow. This base layer gives your tree a substantial sparkle.

Next, add a second layer of smaller, brighter lights closer to the branch tips. These could be fairy lights or mini LED lights. This second layer adds sparkle and defines the tree's shape. It catches the eye and makes your ornaments shimmer. This dual lighting approach gives your rustic boho glam tree a professional, radiant finish.

## Ornaments: Mixing Rustic Charm with Boho Chic and Glamour

Ornaments are where the rustic, boho, and glam elements truly come together. This is the most exciting part of decorating. You blend different styles to create a cohesive yet diverse collection. Each ornament contributes to the tree's unique story.

I love to search for pieces that fit these three categories. Mixing them purposefully creates a balanced design. This approach ensures your tree feels both eclectic and elegant. It makes your tree a true reflection of personal style.

### Natural and Handmade Ornaments

Begin with rustic and natural elements. Think wood slice ornaments, pinecones, and dried orange slices. These items connect your tree to nature. They add a grounded, earthy feel. Handmade ornaments bring unique charm.

Consider ornaments made from burlap, twine, or woven raffia. These materials introduce texture and a touch of farmhouse appeal. They embody the rustic spirit. I also love using small, faux antlers or bird's nests for an unexpected natural touch.

### Bohemian Touches

Next, layer in bohemian accents. Macrame ornaments are perfect for this. Their intricate knots and fringe add a free-spirited vibe. Consider ornaments with tassels or intricate beading. These elements provide a whimsical, handcrafted feel.

Look for ornaments featuring feathers or dreamcatcher designs. These reflect a bohemian aesthetic. They introduce softness and movement to the tree. I also like using global-inspired patterns or mosaic-style ornaments. They add an artistic, worldly touch. ## Tree Fillers and Ribbons: Adding Depth and Flow

Tree fillers and ribbons are crucial for a full and cohesive rustic boho glam tree. They fill in gaps and add layers of texture. These elements also help tie the entire design together. They create a lush, abundant look.

I never underestimate the power of these additions. They transform a sparse tree into a truly magnificent display. Using them effectively makes your tree feel professionally decorated. It brings a sense of fluidity and elegance.

### Picks and Sprays

Picks and sprays are excellent for adding depth and texture. Look for rustic options like frosted berry branches or natural pinecone picks. These bring more natural elements into the tree. They create an organic, earthy feel.

Consider metallic or glitter-dusted picks for a glam touch. Gold branches or sequined floral sprays add sparkle. They catch the light and provide elegance. ### Ribbons and Garlands

Ribbons and garlands provide movement and softness. Choose ribbons in velvet, burlap, or sheer metallic fabrics. Velvet adds a luxurious, glam touch. Burlap maintains the rustic aesthetic. Drape them vertically or weave them horizontally through the branches.

Consider beaded garlands or wooden bead strands for a bohemian feel. These add delicate texture and shimmer. They create a beautiful flow around your tree. Using different widths and textures of ribbons and garlands adds rich visual appeal.

## The Tree Topper and Skirt: Finishing Touches

The tree topper and skirt complete your rustic boho glam Christmas tree. These elements provide the finishing polish. They frame your beautiful creation from top to bottom. Selecting the right ones ties your entire theme together.

### Unique Tree Topper Ideas

A rustic boho glam tree calls for a unique tree topper. Avoid traditional stars or angels unless they have a modern twist. Consider a large, handcrafted macrame star or a gold geometric star. These options blend bohemian artistry with glam sophistication.

Another idea is a cluster of metallic leaves or feathers. This creates an organic yet elegant focal point. You could also use a beautiful, oversized bow made from velvet or a metallic ribbon. This adds a luxurious, soft touch. The topper should draw the eye upwards beautifully.

### Skirt or Collar Selection

The tree skirt or collar grounds your tree. A faux fur tree skirt adds incredible softness and glam. It creates a luxurious base for your tree. Burlap or woven fabric skirts maintain the rustic and bohemian vibe. These materials feel natural and unpretentious.

A galvanized metal tree collar offers a modern rustic touch. It provides a clean, polished look. Consider a woven basket tree collar for a strong bohemian statement. ## FAQ Section

### Q1: What defines rustic boho glam style for Christmas trees?
Rustic boho glam style blends three distinct elements. Rustic brings natural, earthy textures like wood and burlap. Boho adds free-spirited, handcrafted items such as macrame and feathers. Glam introduces luxurious sparkle with metallics, velvet, and glitter. This unique combination creates a Christmas tree that is cozy, artistic, and elegant all at once.

### Q2: What colors work best for a rustic boho glam Christmas tree?
The best colors are earthy neutrals mixed with metallic accents. Think creams, whites, beiges, and warm browns for the rustic base. Then, add pops of gold, silver, copper, or rose gold for the glam. You can also incorporate muted jewel tones like deep emerald or sapphire for an extra touch of sophistication. This palette ensures a balanced and visually rich tree.

### Q3: How can I make my tree look "glam" without being overly flashy?
To add glam without being overly flashy, focus on subtle sparkle and luxurious textures. Use mercury glass ornaments for a vintage shimmer rather than bright glitter. Incorporate velvet ribbons or faux fur accents for softness and richness. Choose metallic ornaments in brushed finishes instead of high-shine. Layer warm white lights for an inviting glow, rather than bright, cool tones.

### Q4: Are DIY ornaments suitable for this style?
Absolutely, DIY ornaments are perfect for the rustic boho glam style. They embody the handcrafted, unique spirit of bohemian decor. You can create wood slice ornaments, macrame pieces, or feather decorations. These handmade touches add personality and charm. They also contribute to the rustic and bohemian aspects of the design.

### Q5: How do I incorporate natural elements effectively?
Incorporate natural elements by using items like pinecones, dried orange slices, or branches. Wood bead garlands and raffia accents also work well. Distribute these elements evenly throughout the tree. Mix them with your glam and boho ornaments. This ensures a balanced look where nature feels integrated, not just placed on top.

### Q6: What tree topper should I choose for a rustic boho glam look?
Choose a tree topper that bridges the three styles. A large macrame star with metallic threading is a great option. A geometric gold star or a cluster of oversized metallic leaves also works. A luxurious bow made from velvet or a metallic textured ribbon can also be a beautiful choice. Select something unique that ties into your overall theme.
