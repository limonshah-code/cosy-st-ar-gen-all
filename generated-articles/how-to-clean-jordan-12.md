## How to Clean Your Jordan 12s: A Step-by-Step Guide

Your Jordan 12 sneakers are more than just shoes; they are a piece of history. Michael Jordan wore them during his iconic "Flu Game," making them a cherished item for many. Keeping these classic sneakers looking their best requires proper care. Dirt, scuffs, and general wear can quickly diminish their appeal.

I understand the desire to keep your prized footwear spotless. This guide will show you exactly how to clean Jordan 12s, ensuring they retain their original look. We will cover everything from understanding the materials to deep cleaning stubborn stains. You will learn the best tools, solutions, and techniques to use. By the end, your Jordan 12s will look fantastic, ready for your next outing or display.

### Takeaway

To clean your Jordan 12s effectively, follow these key steps:

*   **Gather Tools**: Collect soft brushes, cloths, and mild cleaning solutions.
*   **Dry Brush First**: Always remove loose dirt before applying liquids.
*   **Target Materials**: Use specific cleaning methods for leather, suede, and rubber.
*   **Clean Systematically**: Work from the upper down to the soles.
*   **Air Dry Carefully**: Allow shoes to dry naturally away from direct heat.
*   **Protect Regularly**: Apply a protector spray after cleaning to maintain freshness.

To clean Jordan 12s, remove loose dirt with a dry brush. Then, use a mild cleaning solution with a soft brush or cloth on the uppers, focusing on leather and carefully treating suede or nubuck sections. Scrub midsoles and outsoles with a stiffer brush. Rinse with a clean cloth and air dry completely.

## Understanding Jordan 12 Materials Before You Clean

Before you start scrubbing, it is important to know what your Jordan 12s are made of. Different materials require different cleaning approaches. The Jordan 12 typically features a mix of premium leather, often a rich, full-grain type, and sometimes nubuck or suede overlays. Its distinctive design includes a durable rubber outsole and midsole. Understanding these components prevents damage during cleaning.

Knowing your materials helps you choose the correct brushes and solutions. Using the wrong product on suede can ruin it. Harsh chemicals can dry out or crack leather. I always check the specific release of my Jordan 12s to confirm their exact materials. This small step protects your investment and makes cleaning more effective.

### Premium Leather Care

Most Jordan 12 models feature large sections of premium leather on the upper. This leather is usually soft but durable. It can withstand gentle scrubbing. However, it also needs conditioning to stay supple and prevent cracking.

When cleaning leather, use soft brushes or microfiber cloths. These tools will not scratch the surface. A mild, pH-neutral cleaning solution works best. Harsh detergents can strip the leather's natural oils. I always test any new solution on a small, hidden area first.

### Suede and Nubuck Sensitivity

Some Jordan 12 colorways, like the "Reverse Flu Game," use nubuck or suede overlays. These materials are delicate and require special care. They are easily damaged by too much moisture or abrasive brushes. Water can leave stains or alter the texture.

For suede or nubuck, use a dry suede brush to remove dirt. If wet cleaning is necessary, use a specialized suede cleaner and apply it sparingly. Blot, do not rub, to avoid matting the fibers. Always brush the nap in one direction after drying to restore its appearance. Knowing how to care for these specific materials is key to preserving your sneakers. If you own a specialized pair like the Reverse Flu Game, careful material identification is even more important for cleaning. ### Durable Rubber Soles

The Jordan 12's midsole and outsole are made from sturdy rubber. This part of the shoe endures the most contact with the ground. It can accumulate tough dirt, scuffs, and even show yellowing over time. Fortunately, rubber is the most resilient material on your Jordan 12s.

You can use stiffer brushes on the rubber soles without fear of damage. Stronger cleaning solutions are also acceptable for this area. Focus on removing embedded dirt from the traction patterns. Addressing yellowing on the soles may require specific products. We will discuss this more in a later section.

## Essential Cleaning Tools and Solutions for Jordan 12s

Having the right tools makes a significant difference in how effectively you clean your Jordan 12s. You do not need a vast collection of expensive products. A few basic items will get the job done. Gathering everything beforehand also streamlines the cleaning process. This preparation saves time and reduces frustration.

I always lay out my tools on a clean surface before I begin. This way, I have everything within reach. Proper tools ensure you clean without causing any damage to your valuable sneakers. They help you achieve a thorough and professional-looking clean.

### Gentle Brushes and Cloths

You will need at least two types of brushes. A soft-bristle brush is essential for the uppers, especially on leather or any delicate fabric. A medium-stiff brush works well for the midsoles and outsoles. These brushes help dislodge dirt without scratching the surface.

Microfiber cloths are also vital. They are soft, highly absorbent, and lint-free. Use one for applying solution and another for wiping away grime. I prefer several cloths so I can keep a clean one handy for the final wipe-down.

### Choosing a Cleaning Solution

A mild cleaning solution is perfect for most Jordan 12s. Many sneaker cleaning kits offer excellent options specifically formulated for various shoe materials. You can also make a simple DIY solution at home. A mix of warm water and a small amount of mild dish soap works well. Avoid harsh detergents or bleach, especially on colored materials.

I often use a ratio of about one part soap to five parts water. This creates a gentle but effective lather. For deeper cleaning or specific stains, consider specialized sneaker cleaners. These often contain ingredients designed to break down tough dirt. If you're looking for solutions using common household items, you might find some crossover ideas that work well for your J12s. ### Protecting Your Workspace

Cleaning shoes can be messy. Protect your work surface by laying down old newspapers or towels. This prevents cleaning solutions and dirty water from staining your floors or tables. A small bowl or bucket for your cleaning solution and another for clean water will also be helpful.

I keep a separate bowl for rinsing my brushes. This helps prevent transferring dirt back onto the shoes. Good ventilation is also a plus, especially if you are using commercial cleaning products. A well-prepared workspace makes the entire cleaning experience much more pleasant.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Jordan 12 Uppers

Cleaning the upper part of your Jordan 12s requires a gentle touch and careful attention to detail. This is the most visible part of the shoe, and where materials like leather, suede, or nubuck reside. A systematic approach ensures you clean every corner without damaging the delicate surfaces. I always take my time with this step, as it impacts the overall look of the shoe significantly.

Start by removing the laces. This allows you to clean under them and prevents the laces from getting tangled. You can clean the laces separately, which we will discuss later. Now, your shoes are ready for their initial cleaning.

### Initial Dry Brush Removal

The first step is always to remove loose dirt. Use a soft-bristle brush, or even an old toothbrush, to gently brush away any dry mud, dust, or debris. Work your way around the entire upper, paying attention to seams and crevices. This prevents grinding dirt into the material when you introduce water.

I make sure to brush in one direction, especially if there is any suede or nubuck present. This helps lift the dirt without pressing it deeper into the fibers. A thorough dry brush makes the wet cleaning process much easier and safer.

### Cleaning Leather Sections

Once the loose dirt is gone, it is time for wet cleaning. Dip your soft-bristle brush into your mild cleaning solution. Tap off any excess liquid to avoid oversaturating the leather. Gently scrub the leather in small, circular motions. You will see dirt lifting as you go.

After scrubbing a section, wipe it clean with a damp microfiber cloth. Make sure the cloth is clean and wrung out. You want to remove all soap residue. Repeat this process for all leather areas until they look clean. Remember to be gentle to protect the leather's finish.

### Special Care for Suede/Nubuck Overlays

Cleaning suede or nubuck requires extra caution. As mentioned, these materials do not like excessive moisture. If you have a specific suede cleaner, follow its instructions. Otherwise, lightly dampen a clean microfiber cloth with your mild solution. Blot the stained areas, rather than rubbing.

For stubborn spots on suede or nubuck, use a rubber eraser designed for these materials. Gently rub the spot to lift the dirt. Always finish by brushing the suede or nubuck with a dry suede brush to restore its nap. This brings back the soft, consistent texture. ## Restoring Jordan 12 Midsoles and Outsoles

The midsoles and outsoles of your Jordan 12s take a beating. They are designed to be durable, but they also collect the most grime, scuffs, and sometimes show yellowing. Cleaning these parts effectively can dramatically improve the overall look of your sneakers. I find that a clean sole makes a huge difference in how fresh a shoe appears.

You can be a bit more aggressive with the soles than with the delicate uppers. Use a stiffer brush here to really get into the nooks and crannies of the traction pattern. This part of the cleaning process can be quite satisfying as you see the grime disappear.

### Tackling Midsole Scuffs

The midsole, often white or a light color, is prone to scuffs and dirt lines. Dip a medium-stiff brush into your cleaning solution. Apply firm pressure and scrub the midsole in all directions. For tough scuffs, a magic eraser can work wonders. Dampen it slightly and gently rub the scuff marks. Be careful not to use magic erasers on painted areas or materials other than rubber, as they can be abrasive.

After scrubbing, wipe the midsole thoroughly with a clean, damp cloth. Inspect it closely for any remaining dirt. Repeat the process if necessary. A clean midsole really highlights the shoe's design.

### Deep Cleaning the Outsole Tread

The outsole is where all the traction lives, and where dirt loves to hide. Use your stiffest brush and plenty of cleaning solution. Really dig into the grooves and patterns of the outsole. You might need to rinse your brush frequently as it picks up a lot of dirt.

I often use an old toothbrush for very tight spots in the tread. This allows me to reach all the grime effectively. Rinse the outsole thoroughly with water or wipe it down with a very damp cloth. Make sure no cleaning solution is left behind. A clean outsole provides better grip and looks much better. Many of the techniques for cleaning soles on other models, like the Jordan 11, apply directly here. ### Addressing Yellowing Soles

Over time, especially with clear or translucent rubber, soles can yellow. This is a natural oxidation process. Standard cleaning removes dirt, but not yellowing. To reverse yellowing, you will need a specialized product, often called a "sole whitener" or "de-oxidizer." These products usually contain peroxides.

Apply the sole whitener according to the product's instructions, often with a brush. Cover the treated areas with plastic wrap to activate the solution under UV light (sunlight). Leave it for several hours, checking periodically. Rinse thoroughly afterwards. This process can significantly restore the sole's original color. ## Cleaning Laces, Liners, and Odor Removal

Once the main body and soles of your Jordan 12s are clean, it is time to address the smaller details. Laces can get incredibly dirty, and the interior of the shoe can harbor odors. Taking these extra steps ensures your sneakers are fresh from top to bottom. I find these steps complete the cleaning process, making the shoes feel truly renewed.

Neglecting these areas means the shoes are not truly clean. A clean interior adds to the comfort and hygiene of your footwear. Plus, sparkling white laces instantly make any sneaker look newer.

### Washing Your Laces

Laces are simple to clean. Remove them from the shoes first. You can hand wash them in a bowl of warm, soapy water. Rub them together gently to remove dirt. For stubborn stains, a small amount of laundry detergent can be used.

Alternatively, you can place the laces in a mesh laundry bag and wash them in a washing machine on a delicate cycle. Use cold water and a mild detergent. Air dry the laces completely before reinserting them into your Jordan 12s. This prevents any mildew or stretching.

### Refreshing the Interior Liner

The interior lining of your Jordan 12s can accumulate sweat and dirt. To clean it, mix a very dilute solution of mild soap and water. Use a clean cloth, lightly dampened with this solution, to gently wipe down the interior. Avoid saturating the material.

For a deeper clean, specialized shoe interior cleaners or disinfectant sprays can be effective. Always follow the product's instructions. Allow the interior to air dry completely before wearing the shoes again. This prevents moisture buildup and odor.

### Eliminating Unwanted Odors

Odors in shoes are common, especially after heavy wear. Baking soda is a fantastic natural odor absorber. Sprinkle a generous amount inside each shoe and let it sit overnight, or for 24 hours. The baking soda will absorb the moisture and unpleasant smells.

Afterwards, simply shake out the baking soda. You can also use cedar shoe trees. Cedar wood naturally absorbs moisture and imparts a fresh, woody scent. Commercial shoe deodorizers are also an option. I find a combination of baking soda and good air circulation works best.

## Drying and Post-Cleaning Care for Your Jordan 12s

Proper drying is as crucial as the cleaning itself. Incorrect drying can lead to damage, warping, or unpleasant odors. After all your hard work, you want your Jordan 12s to dry safely and effectively. Post-cleaning care also extends the life and appearance of your sneakers. I always emphasize this stage, as it locks in the clean look.

Once your shoes are sparkling clean, the focus shifts to preserving that freshness. This involves conditioning, protection, and smart storage. These steps contribute significantly to the long-term health of your Jordan 12s.

### Air Drying Techniques

Never use direct heat sources like hair dryers, radiators, or direct sunlight to dry your Jordan 12s. High heat can shrink materials, crack leather, or melt adhesives. Instead, allow your shoes to air dry naturally at room temperature. Find a spot with good air circulation.

To help them dry faster and maintain their shape, stuff the shoes with crumpled newspaper or paper towels. These materials absorb moisture from the inside. Change the paper every few hours if it becomes very damp. This also helps hold the shoe's shape as it dries.

### Conditioning Leather

After cleaning, the leather on your Jordan 12s might feel a bit dry. Applying a leather conditioner is a great way to restore its moisture and flexibility. This helps prevent cracking and keeps the leather looking rich. Use a conditioner specifically designed for footwear leather.

Apply a small amount of conditioner to a clean microfiber cloth. Gently rub it into the leather in circular motions. Allow it to soak in for a few minutes, then buff off any excess with a clean, dry cloth. This step keeps your leather healthy and supple.

### Protecting Your Clean Sneakers

Once your Jordan 12s are dry and conditioned, apply a protective spray. A good quality water and stain repellent spray creates a barrier against future dirt and liquids. This makes subsequent cleanings much easier. Make sure the spray is suitable for all materials on your shoe, especially if you have suede or nubuck.

Hold the spray bottle about 6-8 inches away and apply an even coat. Let it dry completely, often for 24 hours, before wearing your shoes. Reapply the protector every few weeks or months, depending on how often you wear your sneakers. Consistent protection helps maintain that fresh-out-of-the-box look. ## Advanced Tips for Stubborn Stains and Deep Cleaning

Sometimes, standard cleaning methods are not enough for those really tough stains or ingrained dirt. Your Jordan 12s might have encountered oil, ink, or deep scuffs that need a more specialized approach. I have certainly faced my share of stubborn sneaker stains. Do not despair; there are often ways to tackle these challenges without damaging your shoes.

Deep cleaning also refers to restoring the shoe's overall condition, not just removing surface dirt. This might involve more intensive treatments for specific materials or areas. Always remember to proceed with caution and test any stronger product on an inconspicuous area first.

### Handling Tough Stains

*   **Oil and Grease Stains**: For fresh oil stains on leather, sprinkle cornstarch or talcum powder directly on the stain. Let it sit for several hours or overnight to absorb the oil. Brush off the powder and then clean the area with your mild solution. Repeat if necessary. For older stains, a specialized leather degreaser might be needed.
*   **Ink Stains**: Ink is tricky. On leather, try rubbing alcohol or a specialized leather ink remover. Apply a tiny amount to a cotton swab and gently blot the stain, rather than rubbing. On suede/nubuck, professional cleaning is often the safest bet. Alcohol can be very damaging to these materials.
*   **Mud and Dirt Stains (Dried)**: Let mud dry completely. Then, use a stiff dry brush to break off as much as possible. Afterward, proceed with your wet cleaning steps. Trying to clean wet mud often smears it deeper into the material.

### Preventing Future Damage

Prevention is often the best medicine for keeping your Jordan 12s pristine. Regular maintenance is key. Clean your shoes promptly after heavy wear, especially if they get muddy or wet. This prevents dirt from setting in.

Always use a protector spray, as mentioned earlier. Store your sneakers in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Shoe trees help maintain their shape and absorb moisture. Rotating your sneakers, so you do not wear the same pair every day, also gives them time to air out and recover. These habits significantly extend the life and cleanliness of your shoes. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I put Jordan 12s in the washing machine?

I do not recommend putting your Jordan 12s in the washing machine. The harsh agitation and prolonged water exposure can damage the materials. It can also cause adhesives to weaken and colors to bleed. Hand washing ensures a gentler and safer clean for your valuable sneakers.

### How often should I clean my Jordan 12s?

The frequency of cleaning depends on how often you wear your Jordan 12s. I suggest a quick wipe-down after every wear to remove surface dirt. Perform a deeper clean every few weeks or months, especially if they are heavily soiled. Regular maintenance keeps them looking fresh without needing intense cleaning sessions often.

### What can I use instead of a sneaker cleaner?

You can use a simple DIY solution instead of a specialized sneaker cleaner. Mix a small amount of mild dish soap with warm water. This solution is gentle enough for most materials on your Jordan 12s. Always test it on a small, hidden area first to ensure it does not cause any discoloration or damage.

### How do I prevent my Jordan 12 soles from yellowing?

Preventing yellowing is tricky, but possible. Store your Jordan 12s in a cool, dark place, away from direct sunlight or harsh artificial light. UV rays speed up the oxidation process that causes yellowing. You can also use specialized sole protectors or anti-oxidation bags to slow down the process.

### What about scuffs on the leather?

For minor scuffs on leather, a clean, damp cloth can sometimes buff them out. If the scuff is deeper, try a tiny bit of leather conditioner or a specialized leather cleaner. Apply it gently with a microfiber cloth. For stubborn scuffs, shoe polish in a matching color can help disguise them.
