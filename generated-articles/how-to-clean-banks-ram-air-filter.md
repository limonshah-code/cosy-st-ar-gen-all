## Keep Your Engine Running Smoothly: How To Clean Banks Ram Air Filter

Keeping your vehicle's engine running at its best is important. A key part of this is ensuring clean air flows into the engine. If you have a Banks Ram Air Filter, you own a performance upgrade. This filter helps your engine breathe better, boosting power and efficiency.

However, like all filters, your Banks Ram Air Filter collects dirt and debris over time. A dirty filter restricts airflow, which hurts engine performance and fuel economy. Learning how to clean Banks Ram Air Filter properly saves you money. It also extends the life of your filter. This guide provides a full walkthrough. We cover everything from gathering supplies to re-installation.

### Takeaway

Regularly cleaning your Banks Ram Air Filter ensures peak engine performance. Follow these steps for a complete filter service:

*   **Gather the Right Supplies:** Banks brand cleaning kit is best.
*   **Remove the Filter Carefully:** Disconnect components before taking out the filter.
*   **Apply Cleaning Solution:** Let it soak to loosen dirt effectively.
*   **Rinse Thoroughly:** Use low-pressure water from the inside out.
*   **Allow to Air Dry Completely:** Do not use heat, as this can damage the filter.
*   **Re-oil (if applicable):** Apply oil evenly for optimal filtration.
*   **Reinstall the Filter:** Secure all connections properly.

Cleaning your Banks Ram Air Filter involves carefully removing it, applying a specialized cleaning solution, rinsing it with low-pressure water, allowing it to air dry completely, and then re-oiling it evenly before reinstallation. This process restores airflow, maintains engine performance, and extends the filter's lifespan.

## When to Clean Your Banks Ram Air Filter

Knowing when to clean your Banks Ram Air Filter is important for vehicle health. The frequency depends on several factors. Driving conditions are a big part of this. If you drive often on dusty roads, you need to clean it more often.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Removing Your Banks Ram Air Filter

Removing your Banks Ram Air Filter correctly is the first step in the cleaning process. This ensures you do not damage any components. The exact steps might vary slightly by vehicle model and Banks system. Always refer to your vehicle's owner manual or the Banks installation guide for specific details.

Here are the general steps:

1.  **Park Your Vehicle Safely:** Turn off the engine and let it cool down completely. Open the hood.
2.  **Locate the Airbox:** The Banks Ram Air Filter is inside the airbox, usually near the front of the engine bay. It connects to the engine's intake.
3.  **Disconnect Sensors and Hoses:** You might need to disconnect the Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor wiring harness. There may also be vacuum lines or other small hoses connected to the airbox. Handle these carefully. You do not want to break them.
4.  **Loosen Clamps and Fasteners:** The airbox typically uses clamps or bolts to secure its lid or entire assembly. Use a screwdriver or socket wrench to loosen these.
5.  **Remove the Airbox Lid (if applicable):** Some Banks systems have a lid that lifts off after loosening clamps. This reveals the filter inside.
6.  **Carefully Remove the Filter:** Once the airbox is open or accessible, gently lift the air filter out. Take care not to bump it or dislodge excessive dirt into the engine intake. This step is critical to avoid contaminating your engine.

Place the dirty filter on a protected surface. Keep all removed hardware, like clamps and bolts, in a safe place. This ensures you have everything for reinstallation. Remember, patience makes this process easy. ## Thorough Cleaning Process for Your Banks Ram Air Filter

Once you remove the filter, the real cleaning begins. This process restores your filter's ability to trap dirt effectively. Always use the Banks cleaning solution for best results.

Follow these steps for a thorough clean:

1.  **Pre-Clean (Optional):** If the filter has large debris, gently tap it against a hard surface to dislodge loose dirt. Use a soft-bristle brush to remove surface grime. Do not use compressed air. This can damage the filter material.
2.  **Apply Cleaning Solution:** Spray the Banks filter cleaner generously onto all pleats of the filter. Make sure to cover both sides. The cleaner penetrates the dirt and oil.
3.  **Allow to Soak:** Let the cleaning solution soak for about 10-15 minutes. This gives it time to break down the embedded dirt and old filter oil. Do not let it dry on the filter.
4.  **Rinse the Filter:** Using a low-pressure water source, rinse the filter from the **clean side out**. This means you rinse from the inside of the cone to the outside. This pushes the dirt out of the filter, not deeper into it. Continue rinsing until the water runs clear.
5.  **Inspect for Cleanliness:** Hold the filter up to a light source. If you still see dark spots or trapped dirt, repeat the cleaning and rinsing process. Ensure all visible dirt is gone.
6.  **Remove Excess Water:** Gently shake the filter to remove excess water. Do not wring or twist the filter. This can damage the pleats.

This cleaning phase is important. It prepares the filter for the drying and re-oiling steps. A clean filter is a healthy filter for your engine. ## Proper Drying and Re-oiling Your Banks Ram Air Filter

After cleaning, drying and re-oiling are critical steps. Improper drying or incorrect re-oiling can harm your filter and engine. Take your time with these stages.

### Air Drying the Filter

1.  **Place on a Clean Surface:** Set the wet filter on a clean, dry towel or a wire rack. Position it to allow air to circulate freely around all sides.
2.  **Allow for Ample Drying Time:** Let the filter air dry completely. This can take several hours, sometimes up to 24 hours. The exact time depends on humidity and temperature.
3.  **Do Not Rush Drying:** Never use compressed air, heat guns, or direct sunlight to speed up drying. High heat can shrink or distort the cotton gauze. Compressed air can tear the filter material. A damaged filter will not filter effectively.

The filter must be bone dry before you apply oil. Moisture trapped in the filter can interfere with the oil's adherence. It can also lead to issues with your MAF sensor.

### Re-oiling Your Banks Ram Air Filter

This step applies only to oiled Banks Ram Air Filters. If you have a Banks dry filter, you can skip this section.

1.  **Use Banks Filter Oil:** Apply the specific Banks filter oil. This oil is designed to trap fine dirt particles.
2.  **Apply Evenly:** Squeeze a bead of oil along the top of each pleat. Work your way around the filter.
3.  **Allow Oil to Wick:** Let the oil sit for about 20 minutes. The oil will wick and spread naturally across the pleats. It will turn the cotton gauze a uniform color (often red or blue).
4.  **Check for Missed Spots:** Inspect the filter for any light spots. These are areas where the oil did not spread. Apply a small amount of oil to these spots.
5.  **Avoid Over-oiling:** Over-oiling is a common mistake. Too much oil can restrict airflow. It can also contaminate your Mass Air Flow (MAF) sensor. This leads to engine performance issues and check engine lights. A light, even coat is sufficient.

Proper re-oiling ensures your filter functions as designed. It catches particles without hindering airflow. ## Reinstalling and Post-Cleaning Checks

After your Banks Ram Air Filter is clean, dry, and properly re-oiled, it is time to reinstall it. Correct reinstallation is as important as the cleaning itself. It ensures the filter seals properly and your engine functions without issues.

Here are the steps for reinstallation:

1.  **Insert the Filter:** Carefully place the cleaned and oiled filter back into the airbox. Make sure it seats correctly in its housing. The filter should fit snugly.
2.  **Replace Airbox Lid and Fasteners:** If your system has an airbox lid, place it back on. Tighten all clamps, bolts, or clips that hold the airbox together. Ensure a tight seal to prevent unfiltered air from entering the engine.
3.  **Reconnect Sensors and Hoses:** Reattach the MAF sensor wiring harness and any vacuum lines or hoses you disconnected. Make sure all connections are secure. A loose MAF sensor can cause rough idling or engine error codes.
4.  **Double-Check All Connections:** Before closing the hood, inspect all connections. Verify that the air filter, airbox, and all related components are securely in place.
5.  **Start the Engine:** Close the hood and start your vehicle. Listen for any unusual sounds. Check for any warning lights on your dashboard.
6.  **Test Drive (Optional but Recommended):** Take your vehicle for a short drive. Pay attention to engine performance and throttle response. You should notice improved airflow and perhaps a slight boost in performance.

Reinstalling your Banks Ram Air Filter correctly ensures your cleaning efforts pay off. It keeps your engine protected and running efficiently. This routine maintenance is key to long-term vehicle health.

## FAQ Section

### How often should I clean my Banks Ram Air filter?
You should visually inspect your Banks Ram Air filter every 10,000 to 15,000 miles. A full cleaning is usually needed at least once a year. If you drive in dusty environments, like off-road, you will need to clean it more often. Regular checks help determine the exact cleaning frequency.

### Can I use regular soap to clean my Banks filter?
No, you should not use regular soap or harsh detergents. Banks Ram Air filters require a specific cleaning solution. Using household cleaners can degrade the cotton gauze material and strip away the special properties of the filter. Always use the Banks brand cleaning kit for best results.

### What type of oil do I use for a Banks oiled filter?
Always use Banks brand filter oil for re-oiling your Banks Ram Air filter. This oil is specially formulated for their cotton gauze filters. It ensures proper filtration without hindering airflow or damaging the filter. Do not substitute with other oils.

### What happens if I do not clean my Banks filter?
If you do not clean your Banks Ram Air filter, it will become clogged with dirt and debris. This restricts airflow to your engine. Consequences include reduced engine performance, lower fuel economy, and potentially increased engine wear. A dirty filter also loses its ability to filter effectively.

### Is it better to clean or replace my Banks filter?
Banks Ram Air filters are designed to be cleaned and reused, not replaced frequently. Cleaning saves money compared to buying new disposable filters. Proper cleaning maintains the filter's high-performance characteristics. Replace the filter only if it is physically damaged or has tears in the material.

### How long does the Banks filter cleaning process take?
The active cleaning and re-oiling process takes about 30-60 minutes. However, the most time-consuming part is the drying. The filter must air dry completely, which can take several hours, sometimes up to 24 hours. Plan for this drying time before you start.
