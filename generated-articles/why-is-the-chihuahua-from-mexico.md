### Q1: What specific region of Mexico do Chihuahuas come from?
Chihuahuas primarily gained their modern recognition in the state of Chihuahua, Mexico. This large northern state is where American travelers "rediscovered" the breed in the mid-19th century. Ancient ancestors of the Chihuahua, the Techichi dogs, were found across Mesoamerica, including parts of central and southern Mexico, but the modern breed solidified its identity and name from the state of Chihuahua.

### Q2: Were Chihuahuas always small?
Yes, the ancestral dogs believed to be related to Chihuahuas, the Techichi, were also small canines. Archaeological evidence and ancient artwork depict small dogs. This suggests that the small stature is a long-standing characteristic of this lineage in Mexico. This small size made them unique and valued even by ancient civilizations.

### Q3: Is the Techichi dog still alive today?
No, the Techichi dog is not alive today as a distinct breed. It is an extinct ancient dog breed. Modern Chihuahuas are considered direct descendants of the Techichi. The Techichi's genetic lineage continues through the Chihuahua, making the Chihuahua its closest living relative. The ancient Techichi forms the foundational stock for the modern breed.

### Q4: How did Chihuahuas get their name?
The Chihuahua dog breed got its name directly from the state of Chihuahua in Mexico. During the mid-1800s, these small dogs were extensively sold and found in this state. American traders and travelers popularized the dogs and referred to them by their place of origin, which eventually became the official breed name.

### Q5: When did Chihuahuas become popular outside Mexico?
Chihuahuas started gaining popularity outside Mexico in the late 19th century. American travelers brought them back from border towns in Mexico. The American Kennel Club officially recognized the breed in 1904. This recognition helped solidify their popularity in the United States and paved the way for international fame in the early to mid-20th century.

### Q6: Do Chihuahuas have any special meaning in Mexican culture?
Yes, Chihuahuas hold significant cultural meaning in Mexico.
