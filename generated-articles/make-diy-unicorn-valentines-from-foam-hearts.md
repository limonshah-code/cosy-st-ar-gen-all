## Step-by-Step: Crafting Your Unicorn Horn and Ears

Now that you have your supplies, let's create the iconic unicorn horn and ears. These elements define your unicorn's character. First, take a sheet of white, gold, or silver felt. Use a pencil to draw small, elongated triangles for the horns. These should be narrow at the top and wider at the base. You can also draw a slightly curved shape to give the horn more character. Cut out these felt horns carefully.

Next, for the ears, choose a pastel-colored felt, like pink, light blue, or lavender. Draw two small oval shapes with a pointed top for each ear. Think of a cat's ear shape but slightly wider. Cut these out. Then, cut a smaller, contrasting color oval for the inner ear. Pink felt on a white ear looks very cute. Glue the smaller inner ear piece onto the larger ear piece. This adds depth and detail to the ears.

With your horns and ears cut, it's time to attach them to the foam heart. Position the horn at the top center of the foam heart. Apply a small dot of strong craft glue to the base of the horn. Press it firmly onto the foam heart. Hold it in place for a few seconds until it adheres. Then, attach the ears. Place one ear on each side of the horn, slightly below it. Make sure they are evenly spaced.

Apply glue to the bottom edge of each ear and press them onto the foam heart. You can fold the base of the ear slightly to give it a 3D effect. Let the glue dry completely before moving on. This ensures all pieces are secure. A perfectly crafted horn and ears make your unicorn truly magical. These simple steps create the foundational magic for your **foam heart unicorn valentines**. ## Adding Magical Unicorn Hair and Floral Details

After the horn and ears are secure, it's time to give your unicorn a beautiful mane. The hair adds a burst of color and personality. You have many options for hair. Yarn works wonderfully. Cut several strands of colorful yarn, about 4-6 inches long. Choose bright, rainbow colors or soft pastels. Gather the strands together.

Apply a line of glue along the top edge of the foam heart, just behind the horn and between the ears. Press the gathered yarn strands onto this glue line. Fan them out slightly to create a flowing mane effect. You can layer different colors for a vibrant look. Alternatively, use strips of colorful felt or construction paper. Cut these into thin strips and glue them in the same manner. This creates a different texture for the hair.

Floral details enhance the whimsical charm of your unicorn. Small artificial flowers or felt flower cutouts are perfect for this. Glue one or two small flowers near the base of the horn, nestled within the hair. These can be tiny rosebuds or simple daisy shapes. Choose colors that complement your unicorn's mane. You can also add small green leaves cut from felt to make the flowers pop.

Sequins or small rhinestones can be glued onto the flowers for extra sparkle. This step truly transforms your unicorn into a majestic creature. Place them strategically for the best effect. Let all the glue dry completely before handling your unicorn valentine. The flowing hair and delicate flowers make your **DIY unicorn valentines from foam hearts** truly unique. ## Bringing Your Unicorn to Life: Facial Features and Final Touches

The face is where your unicorn truly gains its personality. This step makes your foam heart creation come alive. Begin with the eyes. If using googly eyes, apply a small dot of glue where you want each eye to be. Press the googly eyes firmly in place. Ensure they are straight and evenly spaced. Googly eyes give your unicorn a playful, animated look.

If you are drawing the eyes, use a fine-tip black marker. Draw two small, closed eyelids with a few eyelashes for a sweet, sleepy look. Or, draw simple oval eyes with a dot for the pupil for an alert expression. Experiment with different eye shapes to give each unicorn a unique character. Below the eyes, use a pink marker to draw two small, inverted "U" shapes for rosy cheeks. This adds a touch of cuteness.

Next, draw the mouth. A simple, curved line for a smile works best. You can also add a small "V" shape above the smile for a cute nose. These simple lines complete the unicorn's face. After the facial features, consider adding a few final touches. A sprinkle of iridescent glitter on the horn and ears makes them shine. Apply a thin layer of glue to the horn and ears, then gently sprinkle glitter over them. Tap off any excess glitter.

You can write a sweet message on the back of the foam heart. "You are magical!" or "Happy Valentine's Day!" are perfect. Use a permanent marker so the message does not smudge. These small details ensure your **DIY unicorn valentines from foam hearts** are memorable. Each step builds on the last, creating a lovely finished piece. Remember, simple details can make a big impact.

## FAQ Section

### Q1: Are foam hearts easy for young children to use?
Yes, foam hearts are very kid-friendly. They are lightweight and soft, making them easy for small hands to hold and manipulate. Children can apply glue and stick embellishments with minimal difficulty. Adult supervision is helpful for cutting smaller felt pieces, but much of the craft is manageable for kids.

### Q2: What type of glue works best for foam hearts?
Craft glue or white school glue works well for attaching felt, glitter, and other decorations to foam hearts. Look for glues labeled as "fast-drying" or "multi-purpose." Hot glue guns can be used for quicker adhesion, but only with adult supervision due to heat. Always test a small area first.

### Q3: Can I make these unicorn valentines without glitter?
Absolutely! While glitter adds a magical touch, it is not essential. You can use metallic markers for sparkle on the horn and ears. Self-adhesive rhinestones or sequins provide shine without the mess of loose glitter. Fabric paint or puffy paint can also add textured designs and color.

### Q4: How can I make my unicorn valentines unique?
Personalize your unicorns by using different colored foam hearts, varying yarn colors for the mane, and adding diverse embellishments. Try tiny bows, unique button eyes, or small charms. Encourage children to draw different facial expressions. Each choice makes your unicorn one-of-a-kind.

### Q5: What other uses are there for finished foam heart unicorn valentines?
Beyond giving them as valentines, these crafts have many uses. They can be party decorations, hung as ornaments, or used as refrigerator magnets by attaching a small magnet to the back. They also make great puppets if you glue a craft stick to them. They can brighten any space.

### Q6: How long does it take to make one foam heart unicorn valentine?
The time to make each unicorn varies. A single unicorn might take 15-30 minutes, depending on the complexity of the design and the age of the crafter. Younger children might take longer due to slower fine motor skills. Making multiple unicorns in an assembly-line style can speed up the process.
