## Health Considerations and Their Impact on Cost

Apple Head Chihuahuas are generally healthy dogs, but they are prone to certain health issues. Their unique physical characteristics, especially their head shape, contribute to some breed-specific conditions. Understanding these potential health problems is vital. These conditions can significantly impact your overall ownership costs over their lifespan. Being aware helps you plan for potential veterinary expenses.

### Common Health Issues in Apple Head Chihuahuas

Their distinctive apple-shaped head is a defining feature. However, this trait makes them susceptible to certain conditions. One common issue is hydrocephalus. This condition involves an accumulation of fluid in the brain. It can cause neurological problems and may require lifelong medication or surgery. Breeders should screen for this condition.

Another common problem is patellar luxation. This refers to a dislocating kneecap. It can cause lameness and pain. Surgery is often required to correct this issue. These operations can be expensive. Many small breeds are prone to this.

Dental problems are also very common in Chihuahuas. Their small mouths can lead to overcrowding of teeth. This makes them prone to plaque buildup, gum disease, and tooth loss. Regular dental cleanings are often necessary. These cleanings typically cost $200-$600, depending on the severity and anesthesia needs. You can learn more about specific health challenges here: [what are the health problems with apple head chihuahuas](https://cosyhomecreation.com/what-are-the-health-problems-with-apple-head-chihuahuas).

### Chiari-like Malformation (CLM) and Syringomyelia (SM)

These are serious neurological conditions sometimes found in Chihuahuas. CLM is a skull malformation affecting brain development. SM is a fluid-filled cavity in the spinal cord, often a consequence of CLM. These conditions cause pain, neurological deficits, and can require complex medical management. Diagnosis often involves MRI scans, which are costly. Treatment can range from medication to expensive surgery.

### Hypoglycemia (Low Blood Sugar)

Chihuahua puppies, especially tiny ones, are prone to hypoglycemia. This happens when their blood sugar levels drop too low. It can be life-threatening if not managed quickly. Proper feeding schedules and monitoring are essential. While not always a high-cost issue, severe episodes require immediate veterinary attention. This can lead to emergency vet bills.

### Cost Implications of Health Issues

Managing these health conditions can significantly increase your expenses. Diagnostic tests, specialized medications, and surgeries are all costly. For instance, a patellar luxation surgery can cost $1,500-$3,000 per knee. Hydrocephalus treatment can range from hundreds to thousands of dollars annually. Dental cleanings are often needed yearly or every few years.

Regular veterinary check-ups help with early detection. Choosing a reputable breeder who screens their dogs helps reduce the risk. Pet insurance is highly recommended. It can provide financial relief when these unexpected health issues arise. ## Comparing Apple Head and Deer Head Chihuahua Costs

When people look for a Chihuahua, they often encounter two main head shapes: Apple Head and Deer Head. While the Apple Head is the breed standard, Deer Head Chihuahuas are also popular. ### Key Differences Between Head Types

The primary difference lies in their head shape. An Apple Head Chihuahua has a very round, domed skull. They possess a short muzzle and a pronounced stop. Their eyes are typically large and wide-set. This is the classic look recognized by kennel clubs.

A Deer Head Chihuahua has a more sloped forehead. Their head is narrower and less rounded, resembling a young deer. They have a longer muzzle and a less defined stop. Their eyes might be set closer together. They often have longer legs and a more slender body. While not recognized in the show ring, they are perfectly healthy and charming pets. ### Cost Variations Between Head Types

Generally, the cost difference between Apple Head and Deer Head Chihuahuas is minimal or non-existent for pet-quality dogs. Both types are widely available. Their price is more influenced by the factors mentioned earlier: breeder reputation, lineage, location, and individual traits. A well-bred Deer Head Chihuahua from a reputable breeder can cost as much as an Apple Head.

However, if you are looking for a show-quality Chihuahua, the Apple Head type will command a higher price. This is because the Apple Head is the only accepted head shape in the show ring. Both Apple Head and Deer Head Chihuahuas are prone to similar health issues. These include patellar luxation, dental problems, and heart conditions.
