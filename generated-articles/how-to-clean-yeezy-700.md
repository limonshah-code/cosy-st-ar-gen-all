## Clean Yeezy 700: Keep Your Kicks Fresh

### How do I clean my Yeezy 700?

To clean your Yeezy 700, first remove laces and dry brush loose dirt from the upper and sole. Mix a mild shoe cleaner with water. ## Understanding Your Yeezy 700 Materials

Your Yeezy 700 sneakers are a blend of different materials. Each material needs specific care during cleaning. Knowing these components helps you clean safely and effectively. Let's break down the common materials found on most Yeezy 700 models. This knowledge is the first step to proper care.

The upper part of your Yeezy 700 often features a mix of high-quality suede, durable mesh, and sometimes premium leather accents. Suede offers a luxurious feel but absorbs liquids easily, making it prone to stains. Mesh provides breathability and is generally easier to clean than suede. Leather sections are typically smooth and more resilient. The iconic Boost midsole is a soft, foamy material that provides comfort. It can get discolored and collect dirt. The rubber outsole gives traction and is very durable, but it can trap mud and debris. Understanding these parts prevents damage during cleaning.

### Suede Care on Yeezy 700s

Suede is a delicate material on your Yeezy 700. It requires a gentle touch and specialized cleaning. You should never soak suede in water. Water can harden the suede and leave permanent marks. Use a dedicated suede brush for dry dirt.

For wet stains, blot them immediately with a clean cloth. Do not rub the stain deeper into the material. A good suede eraser can lift minor marks. Always brush suede in one direction to maintain its texture. Proper care keeps your Yeezy 700 suede looking its best.

### Cleaning Yeezy 700 Mesh and Leather

Mesh sections on your Yeezy 700 are more forgiving than suede. They are often made from synthetic fabrics that resist water better. You can use a soft brush with a mild cleaning solution on mesh. Work in circular motions to lift dirt. Rinse carefully with a damp cloth to remove soap residue.

Leather accents are usually easy to wipe clean. A damp cloth with a small amount of mild soap works well. Ensure you do not use harsh chemicals that can dry out or crack the leather. Regular wiping prevents dirt buildup. Treating leather occasionally with a conditioner also helps keep it supple.

### The Boost Midsole and Rubber Outsole

The Boost midsole is the chunky white or colored foam section. It gets dirty easily due to its porous nature. Dirt and grime can embed in the texture. This leads to yellowing or discoloration over time. You will need a good brush and a cleaning solution to brighten it up.

The rubber outsole is the bottom part of your shoe. It deals with all the ground contact. Mud, rocks, and general street grime stick to it. This part requires a firm scrub. You can be more aggressive here than on the delicate upper materials. [How to clean Yeezy 350 sole](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-yeezy-350-sole) offers general tips for similar outsoles. Focusing on these areas will restore your Yeezy 700's clean look.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Clean Yeezy 700 Uppers

Cleaning the upper part of your Yeezy 700 requires a methodical approach. The mix of suede, mesh, and leather means you need to be careful. Haste can cause damage, especially to the suede. Take your time and follow these steps for a thorough clean. This section focuses on the visible upper components.

My goal here is always to be gentle but effective. I prefer to start with dry cleaning. This removes loose dirt before introducing any liquids. It is a crucial first step for all materials.

### 1. Remove Laces and Dry Brush

First, take the laces out of your Yeezy 700s. Cleaning laces separately ensures a complete refresh. Then, grab your soft-bristle brush. Gently brush the entire upper part of the shoe. Use light strokes to remove any loose dirt, dust, or debris. This includes brushing the suede, mesh, and leather sections. [How to clean Yeezy 700 V3](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-yeezy-700-v3) also benefits from this initial dry brush. This prevents dirt from turning into mud when you introduce liquid.

### 2. Prepare Your Cleaning Solution

In a small bowl, mix your mild shoe cleaner with water. Follow the dilution instructions on the cleaner bottle. If using mild soap, add a few drops to a bowl of water. You want a foamy, not overly soapy, solution. The mixture should be gentle enough for delicate materials.

### 3. Spot Cleaning and Gentle Scrubbing

Dip your soft-bristle brush into the solution. Tap off any excess liquid to avoid oversaturating the shoe.

*   **For Mesh Areas:** Gently scrub the mesh in small circular motions. Apply light pressure. The goal is to lift dirt from the fabric weave.
*   **For Leather Panels:** Wipe leather sections with a damp microfiber cloth. If needed, dip the cloth in the cleaning solution and gently wipe.
*   **For Suede Areas:** This is where you must be extra careful. Use minimal liquid on suede. Dip the soft brush into the foam, not the liquid. Lightly brush the suede in one direction. Do not scrub harshly or soak the suede. For tougher spots on suede, use a specialized suede eraser first.

Work on one small section at a time. This allows you to control the amount of moisture. This method prevents water rings or damage to the delicate suede.

### 4. Wipe Clean

After scrubbing a section, immediately wipe it with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. This removes the cleaning solution and lifted dirt. Rinse your cloth frequently to keep it clean. Continue this process until you have cleaned the entire upper. Ensure no soap residue remains on any material. This step is vital for a spotless finish.

## Tackling the Midsole and Outsole: Keeping Your Boost Bright

The Boost midsole and rubber outsole are arguably the parts of your Yeezy 700 that get the dirtiest. They are constantly in contact with the ground. Keeping them clean is essential for the overall look of your sneakers. A bright white Boost midsole makes a huge difference.

I find that these parts often require a bit more elbow grease. The materials here are tougher than the delicate uppers. You can use a slightly firmer brush. Let's get those soles looking fresh again.

### Cleaning the Boost Midsole

The Boost midsole is iconic but also a dirt magnet. Its textured surface traps grime easily. Over time, white Boost can yellow or darken significantly.

1.  **Prepare a Solution:** Use the same mild shoe cleaner solution you prepared earlier. For stubborn yellowing, some people use a very diluted mix of hydrogen peroxide and water, but test this in a small, hidden area first.
2.  **Scrub with a Medium Brush:** Dip your medium-bristle brush into the cleaning solution. Generously scrub the entire Boost midsole. Apply firm but not excessive pressure. Work in circular motions.
3.  **Focus on Crevices:** The Boost material has many small bumps. Use the edge of your brush or a clean toothbrush to get into these crevices. Make sure to remove all embedded dirt.
4.  **Wipe Thoroughly:** After scrubbing, wipe the midsole with a clean, damp microfiber cloth. Repeat this process until all dirt and cleaning solution are gone. You might need to rinse and wring out your cloth multiple times.
5.  **Addressing Yellowing:** If your Boost is heavily yellowed, a specialized midsole cleaner or a very light application of hydrogen peroxide gel (used for retrobrite) can help. Always follow product instructions carefully. ### Deep Cleaning the Rubber Outsole

The rubber outsole is designed for durability and traction. It gathers the most dirt, mud, and grim. This is the toughest part of your Yeezy 700 to clean.

1.  **Remove Loose Debris:** Before wetting, use a firm brush or a stick to dislodge any large pieces of mud or pebbles stuck in the outsole grooves.
2.  **Apply Solution:** Dip your medium-bristle brush or a stiff brush into the cleaning solution. You can be more aggressive with the amount of solution here.
3.  **Scrub Vigorously:** Scrub the entire rubber outsole, focusing on the grooves and traction patterns. Apply significant pressure to remove stubborn dirt. Don't be afraid to scrub hard here.
4.  **Rinse Under Water (Optional):** If the outsole is extremely dirty, you can carefully rinse it under a tap. Ensure no water splashes onto the suede or delicate upper materials.

## Dealing with Laces and Liners: A Complete Refresh

Often overlooked, cleaning your Yeezy 700 laces and insoles makes a huge difference. Dirty laces can make otherwise clean shoes look unkempt. A fresh insole also helps with hygiene and comfort. It gives the entire shoe a full refresh.

I always recommend removing laces and insoles. This allows for a more thorough cleaning of each component. It also prevents dirt from one area transferring to another. Let's make sure every part of your Yeezy 700 is sparkling.

### Cleaning Your Yeezy 700 Laces

Laces soak up dirt, sweat, and grime. They can become discolored over time. Cleaning them separately is the most effective method.

1.  **Remove Laces:** Take out the laces from both shoes.
2.  **Pre-soak (Optional):** For very dirty laces, you can pre-soak them in a bowl of warm water with a small amount of mild detergent. Let them sit for 15-30 minutes.
3.  **Hand Wash or Machine Wash:**
    *   **Hand Wash:** Apply a few drops of mild soap directly to the laces. Rub them together vigorously under warm water. Use a soft brush to scrub any tough spots.
    *   **Machine Wash:** Place the laces inside a mesh laundry bag. Wash them on a delicate cycle with your regular laundry detergent. This protects them from getting tangled or damaged.
4.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Rinse the laces under clean running water until all soap residue is gone.
5.  **Air Dry:** Lay the laces flat on a clean towel or hang them to air dry. Do not put them in a dryer, as this can shrink or damage the material. Once fully dry, you can re-lace your Yeezy 700s.

### Refreshing Your Yeezy 700 Insoles (Liners)

Insoles absorb sweat and can trap odors. Cleaning them regularly improves shoe hygiene.

1.  **Remove Insoles:** Gently pull out the insoles from your Yeezy 700s.
2.  **Shake Off Loose Debris:** Give them a good shake to remove any loose dirt or sand.
3.  **Wash Gently:**
    *   **Surface Cleaning:** Mix a small amount of mild soap with water. Dip a soft brush or a clean cloth into the solution. Gently scrub the surface of the insoles.
    *   **Deep Cleaning (Caution):** Some insoles are machine washable, but check the material first. If unsure, stick to hand washing. Avoid fully saturating insoles made of cardboard or delicate foam, as they can disintegrate.
4.  **Rinse and Wipe:** Wipe the insoles clean with a damp cloth, removing all soap. Do not rinse directly under a tap unless they are fully washable.
5.  **Air Dry Completely:** Lay the insoles flat in a well-ventilated area to air dry. Ensure they are completely dry before putting them back into your Yeezy 700s. Damp insoles can cause odors and mold. For a quick refresh between washes, a sprinkle of baking soda can help absorb odors.

## Advanced Stain Removal for Stubborn Marks on Yeezy 700s

Sometimes, dirt is more than just surface grime. Stubborn stains can appear on your Yeezy 700s, like oil, ink, or dark scuffs. These require more specific treatment than a general clean. Reacting quickly and using the right method is important.

I have dealt with my share of tough stains. The key is to address them as soon as possible. Different types of stains respond to different treatments. Always proceed with caution. [How to clean Yeezy 700 Blue Tint](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-yeezy-700-blue-tint) or other light-colored models may need these advanced techniques more often.

### Dealing with Oil and Grease Stains

Oil and grease stains are tricky, especially on suede and mesh. They can spread and leave a dark mark.

1.  **Act Fast:** The sooner you treat an oil stain, the better your chances of removal.
2.  **Absorb Excess:** Gently blot the stain with a clean paper towel or microfiber cloth. Do not rub, as this can push the oil deeper.
3.  **Apply Absorbent Powder:** Sprinkle a generous amount of cornstarch, talcum powder, or baking soda directly onto the stain. These powders help draw out the oil.
4.  **Let it Sit:** Allow the powder to sit for several hours, or even overnight. It needs time to absorb the oil.
5.  **Brush Off:** Gently brush off the powder with a soft brush.
6.  **Mild Cleaner:** If a mark remains, use a small amount of mild shoe cleaner foam on a cloth. Gently dab and blot the area. Repeat the powder method if necessary.
7.  **Suede Eraser:** For suede, a suede eraser can sometimes lift residual oil after the powder treatment.

### Removing Ink Stains

Ink stains are notoriously difficult to remove from any fabric. Patience and precision are key for your Yeezy 700s.

1.  **Test First:** Always test any ink remover on an inconspicuous area first.
2.  **Alcohol Method:** Dip a cotton swab in rubbing alcohol (isopropyl alcohol). Gently dab the ink stain. Do not rub, as this can spread the ink. Work from the outside of the stain inwards.
3.  **Blot with Clean Cloth:** As the ink lifts, immediately blot it with a clean microfiber cloth. Replace the cotton swab and cloth frequently.
4.  **Repeat:** Continue dabbing and blotting until the stain fades or disappears.
5.  **Mild Cleaner:** Follow up with a light application of mild shoe cleaner to remove any alcohol residue. Air dry.

### Tackling Scuffs and Dark Marks

Scuffs are common, especially on the Boost midsole and rubber outsole.

1.  **For Midsole Scuffs:** A magic eraser (melamine foam sponge) works wonders on Boost midsoles. Dampen the magic eraser and gently rub the scuff marks. Be careful not to use it on suede, as it can damage the nap. This is also effective for general [How to clean Yeezy Boost 700](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-yeezy-boost-700) models, regardless of color.
2.  **For Outsole Scuffs:** Use your medium-bristle brush with cleaning solution. For very stubborn black marks, a small amount of acetone on a cotton swab can be used on the rubber outsole only. Apply with extreme caution and avoid contact with other materials.
3.  **For Suede Scuffs:** Sometimes a suede eraser and a suede brush can buff out light scuffs on suede by restoring the nap.

Remember, persistence is crucial with tough stains. Do not get discouraged if a stain does not come out completely on the first try. Repeat the steps carefully.

## Drying and Protecting Your Yeezy 700s: The Final Touches

After all that careful cleaning, the drying and protection stages are just as important. Improper drying can lead to misshapen shoes or lingering dampness. Protection helps keep your Yeezy 700s cleaner for longer. These steps ensure your hard work lasts.

I always consider drying as part of the cleaning process. Rushing this step can undo all the effort you put in. Patience is key here. Let's make sure your Yeezy 700s dry perfectly and stay protected.

### Proper Drying Techniques

Drying your Yeezy 700s incorrectly can cause damage. Avoid direct heat sources. Heat can shrink materials, warp the Boost midsole, or damage adhesives.

1.  **Stuff with Paper Towels/Shoe Trees:** After wiping your shoes clean, stuff the inside with clean paper towels or old, clean cloths. This absorbs internal moisture and helps maintain the shoe's shape. Shoe trees are an even better option if you have them. Change the paper towels periodically if they become saturated.
2.  **Air Dry Indoors:** Place your Yeezy 700s in a well-ventilated area indoors. Avoid direct sunlight, as this can cause materials to fade or yellow. A room with good air circulation is ideal.
3.  **Allow Ample Time:** Give your shoes at least 24-48 hours to dry completely. Suede and mesh can hold moisture longer. Ensure every part, including inside the shoe, is dry before wearing or storing them. Damp shoes can develop odors and mold. ### Applying Protective Sprays

Once your Yeezy 700s are completely dry, a protective spray is a smart investment. This creates a barrier against water, stains, and dirt. It makes future cleaning much easier.

1.  **Choose a Quality Spray:** Select a water and stain repellent spray specifically designed for sneakers and safe for multiple materials like suede, mesh, and leather. Read reviews and product instructions carefully.
2.  **Apply in a Ventilated Area:** Spray your shoes outdoors or in a well-ventilated room.
3.  **Even Coating:** Hold the spray bottle about 6-8 inches away from the shoe. Apply an even, light coat over all the upper materials. Do not oversaturate the shoe.
4.  **Allow to Dry:** Let the first coat dry completely, usually for 15-30 minutes, or as recommended by the product.
5.  **Second Coat (Optional):** Many sprays recommend a second light coat for maximum protection.
6.  **Cure Time:** Allow the shoes to cure for the recommended time (often 24 hours) before wearing them. This allows the protective barrier to fully set.

### Proper Storage

Storing your Yeezy 700s correctly also contributes to their longevity.

*   **Keep in Original Box or Shoe Bins:** This protects them from dust and light.
*   **Avoid Piling:** Do not pile other shoes on top of your Yeezy 700s, which can crush them.
*   **Use Shoe Trees:** Shoe trees help maintain shape during storage.
*   **Cool, Dry Place:** Store them in a cool, dry environment to prevent material degradation.

## Preventative Care: Keeping Your Yeezy 700s Cleaner Longer

Cleaning your Yeezy 700s is important, but preventing them from getting too dirty in the first place is even better. Proactive measures save you time and effort. Regular maintenance keeps your sneakers looking fresh with minimal deep cleaning.

I find that a little bit of care after each wear goes a long way. This approach preserves the materials and appearance of your valuable sneakers. Let's explore some simple habits you can adopt.

### Regular Light Maintenance

*   **Quick Wipe Down:** After each wear, especially if you have been outdoors, take a minute to wipe down your Yeezy 700s. Use a dry microfiber cloth to remove loose dust and superficial dirt.
*   **Dry Brush:** Keep a soft-bristle brush near your shoe rack. A quick dry brush of the suede and mesh areas can prevent dirt from setting in. This is especially important for [Yeezy 700 cleaning](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-yeezy-700) to keep the various materials pristine.
*   **Inspect for Stains:** Regularly check for small scuffs or stains. Addressing them immediately with a spot cleaner or suede eraser prevents them from becoming permanent. Small marks are much easier to remove than old, set-in ones.

### Avoiding Harsh Conditions

*   **Mind Your Environment:** Try to avoid wearing your Yeezy 700s in harsh weather conditions. Rain, heavy mud, or dusty environments will soil them quickly. Choose appropriate footwear for such situations.
*   **Stay Away from Chemicals:** Do not expose your sneakers to harsh chemicals, oils, or strong dyes. These can cause irreversible stains or damage to the materials.
*   **Be Mindful of Surfaces:** Watch where you step. Avoid walking through puddles or areas with excessive dirt. This simple awareness can dramatically reduce how often you need to deep clean.

### Rotating Your Sneakers

*   **Give Them a Break:** Do not wear the same pair of Yeezy 700s every day. Rotating your sneakers allows them to air out and recover. This also reduces wear and tear on any single pair.
*   **Allow to Breathe:** After wearing, let your shoes air out for a few hours. This helps dissipate moisture and odors.
*   **Use Shoe Trees:** Insert shoe trees when storing your Yeezy 700s. They help maintain the shoe's shape and prevent creasing.

By incorporating these preventative care tips into your routine, you will notice a significant difference. Your Yeezy 700s will stay cleaner, look better, and last longer. This proactive approach is the best way to protect your investment.

## FAQ Section

### How often should I clean my Yeezy 700?

Clean your Yeezy 700s as needed, typically after a few wears if they get dirty. Regular light maintenance like dry brushing after each wear is ideal. Deep cleaning should only happen when significant dirt or stains accumulate. Frequent deep cleaning with liquids can wear down the materials over time. Balance deep cleans with preventative care.

### Can I put my Yeezy 700 in the washing machine?

No, you should not put your Yeezy 700s in a washing machine. The delicate materials like suede and the Boost midsole can be damaged by machine agitation, heat, and excessive water. This can cause shrinkage, color bleeding, or glue degradation. Always hand wash your Yeezy 700s with gentle methods.

To remove yellowing from your Yeezy 700 Boost midsole, use a magic eraser (melamine foam sponge) with a mild shoe cleaner. For stubborn yellowing, a specialized midsole whitener or a very diluted hydrogen peroxide cream (often used for retro-brightening) can be applied carefully. Always test in an inconspicuous area first.
