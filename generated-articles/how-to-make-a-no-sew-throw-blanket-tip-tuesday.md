### Takeaway

Creating a beautiful and warm no-sew throw blanket is easy. You only need two pieces of fleece fabric, scissors, and a ruler.

*   Select soft, complementary fleece fabrics.
*   Cut the fabric to your desired blanket size.
*   Snip fringe strips around the edges.
*   Tie the matching fringe strips together with simple knots.
*   Enjoy a handmade, cozy throw blanket without any sewing.

To make a no-sew throw blanket, you layer two pieces of fleece fabric, cut fringe strips around all edges, and then tie the corresponding top and bottom fringe pieces together using a simple double knot. This method creates a soft, warm, and durable blanket without needing a needle or thread. It is a perfect project for beginners.

## Step-by-Step Guide: How To Make A No Sew Throw Blanket

Making a **no sew throw blanket** is a fun and simple process. Follow these steps carefully. You will have a beautiful, handmade blanket in no time. I enjoy this craft because it shows quick results. Each step builds on the last one.

### 1. Prepare Your Fabric

First, lay one piece of fleece flat on a large, clean surface. Smooth out any wrinkles. Then, place the second piece of fleece directly on top of the first. Align the edges as perfectly as possible. Make sure both pieces are perfectly flat and smooth.

Trim any uneven edges from both layers. Use your sharp fabric scissors. This creates a clean starting point. Some fabric stores cut fabric unevenly. Square off all sides.

### 2. Cut Out the Corners

You need to remove a square from each corner. This prevents the corners from bunching. Measure a 4x4 inch square from each corner. Cut these squares out through both layers of fabric. This step creates a nice, clean edge for your fringe.

### 3. Measure and Cut the Fringe

Now, measure 4 inches in from the edge of the blanket. Draw a line all around the perimeter. This line will be your cutting guide. It ensures all your fringe strips are the same length. I find this step helpful for consistency.

Next, start cutting your fringe. Cut strips from the edge up to your drawn line. Each strip should be about 1 inch wide. Cut through both layers of fabric at the same time. Continue this around the entire blanket. Take your time to make even cuts. Small differences are okay.

### 4. Tie the Knots

This is the most satisfying part! Take one top fringe strip and its matching bottom fringe strip. Tie them together in a double knot. Pull the knot snug but not too tight. A double knot holds securely.

Work your way around the entire blanket. Tie every pair of fringe strips. Be consistent with your knots. This creates a uniform look. You will see your blanket come to life. Once all the knots are tied, give your blanket a gentle shake. It is now ready to use!

## Caring for Your Fleece Throw: Keeping Your Blanket Soft and Cozy

You have put effort into making your **no sew throw blanket**. Now, you want it to last. Proper care ensures your fleece stays soft and cozy. Fleece is generally easy to maintain. A few simple steps keep it looking new.

Always check the fabric care tags if available. Most fleece is machine washable. Use cold water on a gentle cycle. Hot water can damage fleece fibers. It can also cause pilling. Cold water helps maintain the fabric's softness.

Use a mild detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach. Bleach can weaken fabric and affect color. A gentle detergent cleans without stripping the fleece. I find less is more when washing fleece. Too much soap can leave residue.

When drying, tumble dry on low heat or air dry. High heat is the enemy of fleece. It can melt the synthetic fibers. This makes the fleece rough and stiff. Air drying is best if you have time. If using a dryer, check it often. Remove the blanket while it is still slightly damp. This prevents over-drying.

Do not iron your fleece blanket. Heat from an iron will melt the fibers. It can leave permanent marks. If your blanket needs smoothing, a steamer on a low setting might work. However, generally, fleece does not wrinkle easily. You could also learn about [how much it costs to dry clean a blanket](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-much-to-dry-clean-a-blanket) for blankets that require professional care, though fleece usually does not. If you have other types of blankets, knowing [how to clean a Mexican blanket](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-mexican-blanket) can be useful.

Store your blanket in a dry place. Keep it away from direct sunlight. Sunlight can fade colors over time. Folding or rolling your blanket neatly also helps maintain its shape. Follow these tips. Your handmade **no sew throw blanket** will provide comfort for years.

## Frequently Asked Questions About No-Sew Throw Blankets

### How much fabric do I need for a standard no-sew throw blanket?
For a standard throw blanket (approximately 50x60 inches), you will need two pieces of fleece fabric. Each piece should be about 1.5 to 1.75 yards long, depending on the fabric width. Ensure both pieces are the exact same size before you start cutting. Always buy a little extra fabric to account for trimming uneven edges.

### What kind of fleece is best for a no-sew blanket?
Anti-pill fleece is the best choice for a no-sew blanket. It resists forming small, fuzzy balls (pills) on the surface, keeping your blanket looking smooth and new longer. Look for a medium to heavy-weight fleece for extra warmth and coziness. Polar fleece or blizzard fleece also work well.

### Can children make a no-sew throw blanket?
Yes, this is a fantastic project for children! The steps are simple, and it does not involve needles or a sewing machine. Adult supervision is needed for cutting the fabric and fringe, especially with sharp scissors. Tying the knots is a great fine motor skill activity for kids. It builds confidence and creativity.

### How do I make the knots secure so they don't unravel?
To make the knots secure, always tie a double knot. Pull each knot firmly but gently so it is snug against the blanket's edge without stretching the fabric. Avoid pulling too hard, which can distort the fleece. A double knot provides enough friction and tightness to hold the fringe pieces together.

### How long does it take to make a no-sew throw blanket?
The time it takes depends on the blanket size and your speed. A standard throw blanket can typically be completed in 2-4 hours. This includes cutting the fabric, making the fringe, and tying all the knots. It makes for a great weekend afternoon project.

### Can I wash a no-sew fleece blanket in a washing machine?
Yes, you can machine wash your no-sew fleece blanket. Use cold water on a gentle cycle with a mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softener. Tumble dry on low heat or air dry to prevent damage to the fleece fibers and keep the blanket soft. High heat can cause the fleece to become stiff.
