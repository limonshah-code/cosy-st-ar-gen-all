### Passing the Background Check and Safety Measures

Trust is very important for pet owners. Rover understands this. They require all sitters and walkers to pass a background check. This process is a crucial step to become a Rover dog walker. The background check screens for criminal history. It helps ensure the safety of both pets and people.

You will provide your social security number. Rover partners with a third-party service to conduct the check. This process usually takes a few days. Sometimes it takes longer. Do not worry if it is not instant. This safety measure protects you. It also protects pet owners and their beloved animals.

Rover also offers other safety features. They provide 24/7 support. They have the Rover Guarantee. This offers financial protection for eligible bookings. These measures build confidence in the platform. They make Rover a safe place for pet care providers. Understanding these safety aspects helps you feel secure in your work.

### Mastering the Rover Matching Process

No, it is not hard to become a Rover dog walker. The process is straightforward. You create a profile and pass a background check. You list your services and rates. ### What are the requirements to be a Rover dog walker?

You must be at least 18 years old. You need a legal right to work in your country. You should have experience caring for dogs. This can be personal or professional. You also need to pass a background check. A smartphone is necessary for using the Rover app.

### How much can a Rover dog walker earn?

Earnings vary widely based on location and services. You set your own rates on Rover. Experienced walkers in busy areas can earn a good income. Beginners might start with lower rates to attract clients. The more walks you do and the higher your rates, the more you earn.

### Does Rover perform background checks?

Yes, Rover performs background checks on all new sitters and walkers. This is a mandatory step in the application process. They use a third-party service to conduct these checks. This measure helps ensure the safety and trust of the community for all users.

### What services can I offer on Rover?

As a dog walker, you primarily offer dog walks of varying durations. You can also offer drop-in visits. These visits often include a short walk. Other services like dog sitting, house sitting, and dog boarding are also available on Rover. You choose which services to provide.

### How do I get my first clients on Rover?

To get your first clients, ensure your profile is complete and compelling. Use clear photos. Write a detailed description of your experience. Set competitive rates initially.
