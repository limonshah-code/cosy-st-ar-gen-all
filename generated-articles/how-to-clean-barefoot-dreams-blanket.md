## How to Clean a Barefoot Dreams Blanket for Lasting Softness

Your Barefoot Dreams blanket offers unmatched comfort. It feels soft and cozy against your skin. Keeping it clean helps maintain this luxury. You want your blanket to last for many years. Proper cleaning makes sure it stays fluffy and soft.

This guide will show you how to clean your Barefoot Dreams blanket. We will cover materials, steps for washing, and important drying tips. We will also discuss common mistakes to avoid. By the end, you will know exactly how to care for your cherished blanket. Your blanket will stay a favorite item in your home.

### Takeaway

To keep your Barefoot Dreams blanket soft and luxurious:
*   Always use cold water and a gentle cycle.
*   Use a mild, liquid detergent.
*   Never use fabric softener, bleach, or dryer sheets.
*   Air dry flat or tumble dry on the lowest heat setting.
*   Avoid high heat and ironing at all costs.

**To clean a Barefoot Dreams blanket, use cold water and a gentle cycle with a mild liquid detergent. Avoid fabric softener, bleach, and dryer sheets. Tumble dry on the lowest heat setting or, ideally, air dry it flat to preserve its signature softness and texture.**

### Essential Supplies for Cleaning Your Barefoot Dreams Blanket

Before you start, gather your cleaning supplies. Having everything ready makes the process smooth. You do not need many special items. Simple supplies work best for this delicate fabric.

Here is what you will need:
*   **Mild Liquid Laundry Detergent:** Choose a gentle, dye-free, and perfume-free detergent. Harsh detergents strip the fibers of their softness. Avoid anything with bleach or brighteners.
*   **Mesh Laundry Bag (Optional but Recommended):** A large mesh bag protects your blanket during machine washing. It prevents snagging and excessive stretching. This is especially helpful for larger blankets or throws.
*   **Large Basin or Clean Bathtub (for Hand Washing):** If you choose to hand wash, you need a clean space. A tub or large basin works well.
*   **Clean Towels:** Use these to gently press out excess water. They help speed up the drying process.
*   **Drying Rack or Clean Flat Surface:** Air drying is the best method. A sturdy rack or a clean floor space is ideal.

These supplies ensure a safe and effective clean. They help preserve the blanket's texture and softness. Preparing your tools is a simple step. It makes a big difference in the final outcome.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Machine Washing Barefoot Dreams

Machine washing your Barefoot Dreams blanket is possible. You just need to follow specific steps. This method is convenient and effective. It helps keep your blanket fresh. Always check the care label first, but these steps are generally safe.

Here is how to machine wash your blanket:
1.  **Prepare the Blanket:** Shake off any loose debris or pet hair. If your blanket has any visible stains, spot treat them gently. Use a small amount of your mild detergent on the stain. Lightly blot it with a clean cloth.
2.  **Load the Machine:** Place the blanket into a large mesh laundry bag. This protects it from stretching or snagging during the wash cycle. Put the blanket into the washing machine by itself. Do not overload the machine with other items.
3.  **Add Detergent:** Pour a small amount of mild, liquid detergent into the dispenser. Remember, less is more for these delicate fibers. Never use powdered detergents, as they may not dissolve completely. They can leave residue on the fabric.
4.  **Select Settings:** Choose the "delicate" or "hand wash" cycle on your machine. Set the water temperature to "cold." Cold water prevents shrinking and helps maintain the fabric's softness. Hot water can damage the fibers.
5.  **Start Wash:** Begin the wash cycle. Let the machine complete its cycle. Do not let the blanket sit in the washer for long after the cycle finishes. This can cause wrinkles.

Machine washing is safe when done correctly. It helps extend the life of your Barefoot Dreams blanket. For similar delicate items, like certain knit blankets, these gentle washing methods also apply. ### Hand Washing Your Barefoot Dreams Blanket for Extra Care

Hand washing is a great option for Barefoot Dreams blankets. It offers the gentlest clean. This method is ideal for heavily soiled blankets or for extra peace of mind. It takes a little more time but gives you full control.

Follow these steps for hand washing:
1.  **Fill the Basin:** Find a clean sink, large basin, or bathtub. Fill it with cold water. Cold water is key to protecting the delicate fibers. Do not use warm or hot water.
2.  **Add Detergent:** Pour a small amount of mild liquid detergent into the water. Swirl the water gently with your hand. Mix the detergent until it dissolves completely. Avoid creating too many suds.
3.  **Submerge the Blanket:** Carefully place your Barefoot Dreams blanket into the soapy water. Make sure it is fully submerged. Gently press it down to remove any trapped air. Do not agitate or scrub vigorously.
4.  **Soak:** Allow the blanket to soak for about 15-30 minutes. This gives the detergent time to work. It loosens dirt and odors. Soaking prevents you from needing to scrub.
5.  **Rinse Thoroughly:** Drain the soapy water. Refill the basin with clean, cold water. Gently press the blanket to release the soapy water. Repeat this rinsing process several times. Keep rinsing until the water runs clear. No soap residue should remain.
6.  **Remove Excess Water:** Do not wring or twist the blanket. This can stretch and damage the fibers. Instead, gently press the water out of the blanket. You can roll it up in a clean, dry towel. This helps absorb a lot of moisture.

Hand washing is a careful process. It helps maintain the blanket's original shape and softness. It is perfect for those who want to be extra careful. This method ensures your blanket stays luxurious.

### Drying Your Barefoot Dreams Blanket: The Key to Retaining Softness

Drying is the most crucial step for maintaining your Barefoot Dreams blanket's softness. Incorrect drying can ruin its texture. High heat is the enemy of microfiber. It can melt the fibers and make the blanket stiff.

Here are the best ways to dry your blanket:
*   **Air Drying (Recommended):** This is the safest method.
    *   Lay the blanket flat on a clean, dry surface. A drying rack or clean floor works well.
    *   Ensure good air circulation around the blanket.
    *   Flip the blanket periodically to promote even drying.
    *   Air drying can take several hours, or even a full day. Be patient.
    *   Avoid hanging the blanket, especially when wet. The weight of the water can cause it to stretch out of shape.
*   **Tumble Drying (Use with Extreme Caution):** If you must use a dryer, follow these strict rules:
    *   Use the absolute lowest heat setting, preferably "air fluff" or "no heat."
    *   Add a few clean tennis balls to the dryer. These help fluff the blanket and prevent clumping.
    *   Check the blanket frequently. Remove it as soon as it is dry. Even slightly over-drying can damage the fibers.
    *   Never use dryer sheets. They can leave a residue that makes the blanket stiff and less soft.

Patience is vital during the drying process. Rushing it with high heat will damage your blanket permanently. Proper drying ensures your blanket retains its signature plush feel. ### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Cleaning Barefoot Dreams

Avoiding certain cleaning practices is just as important as following the right ones. Some common laundry habits can ruin your Barefoot Dreams blanket. Knowing these pitfalls helps you protect your investment. You want to keep your blanket feeling like new.

Here are the most common mistakes to steer clear of:
*   **Using Hot Water:** Hot water can cause the delicate microfiber to shrink. It can also permanently alter its soft texture. Always stick to cold water for washing.
*   **Using Fabric Softener:** This might seem counterintuitive for a soft blanket. However, fabric softeners leave a residue on microfiber. This residue builds up over time. It makes the blanket stiff and reduces its ability to breathe.
*   **Using Bleach:** Bleach is a harsh chemical. It can damage and discolor the fibers of your blanket. Never use bleach or detergents containing bleach.
*   **Using Dryer Sheets:** Like fabric softeners, dryer sheets leave a waxy coating. This coating clogs the fibers. It reduces the blanket's softness and makes it feel less luxurious.
*   **High Heat Drying:** This is perhaps the biggest mistake. High heat can melt the synthetic fibers. It causes the blanket to become rough, stiff, and matted. Always air dry or use the lowest heat setting.
*   **Ironing:** Never iron your Barefoot Dreams blanket. The direct heat from an iron will melt the fibers. This will permanently ruin the blanket's texture and appearance.

By avoiding these common errors, you ensure your blanket remains soft and beautiful. Proper care extends its life and keeps it a cherished item. These simple rules help maintain its luxurious feel.

### Maintaining Your Barefoot Dreams Blanket Between Washes

Cleaning your Barefoot Dreams blanket properly is important. But what about caring for it between washes? Good maintenance helps keep it fresh. It also extends the time between deep cleans. This saves you effort and protects the fabric.

Here are some tips for daily and weekly care:
*   **Shake it Out Regularly:** Gently shake your blanket outdoors. This removes dust, loose fibers, and pet hair. It helps keep the blanket fluffy and fresh. Do this every few days or weekly.
*   **Spot Clean Small Spills Immediately:** Accidents happen. If you spill something, address it right away. Blot the spill with a clean, damp cloth and a tiny bit of mild detergent. Do not rub, as this can spread the stain. Always blot from the outside in.
*   **Avoid Eating or Drinking in Bed:** This prevents many potential stains. Keeping food and drinks away from the blanket minimizes spills. It keeps the blanket cleaner for longer.
*   **Use a Blanket Cover (Optional):** If you use your Barefoot Dreams blanket daily, consider a removable cover. This adds a layer of protection. You can wash the cover more frequently. This keeps your main blanket cleaner.
*   **Proper Storage:** When not in use, store your blanket in a clean, dry place. Fold it neatly or drape it over furniture. Avoid stuffing it into tight spaces. This helps maintain its shape and fluffiness.
*   **Avoid Direct Sunlight for Long Periods:** While some sun is fine for air circulation, prolonged direct sunlight can cause fading. It can also dry out the fibers over time.

These simple habits make a big difference. They keep your Barefoot Dreams blanket looking and feeling wonderful. Consistent care helps preserve its luxurious qualities. Enjoy your soft blanket for many years with these easy maintenance tips.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use fabric softener on my Barefoot Dreams blanket?**
No, you should never use fabric softener. It leaves a residue that clogs the delicate microfiber fibers. This buildup makes the blanket stiff and reduces its famous softness. Stick to mild liquid detergent only.

**How often should I wash my Barefoot Dreams blanket?**
Wash your blanket as needed, typically every 2-4 weeks with regular use. If it gets dirty quickly, wash it sooner. If it sees light use, you can wash it less often. Frequent shaking and spot cleaning help extend the time between washes.

**Is it okay to put my Barefoot Dreams blanket in the dryer?**
You can, but with extreme caution. Use the "air fluff" or "no heat" setting only. High heat will damage the fibers and make the blanket stiff. Air drying is always the preferred and safest method to maintain its softness.

**What if my Barefoot Dreams blanket feels stiff after washing?**
Stiffness usually means you used high heat, fabric softener, or a harsh detergent. Try re-washing the blanket using cold water and a minimal amount of mild detergent. Then, air dry it completely. Gently fluff it by hand once dry to help restore softness.

**Can I use a stain remover on my Barefoot Dreams blanket?**
Use stain removers very carefully. Opt for a gentle, bleach-free, and dye-free formula. Test it first on an inconspicuous area. Blot the stain gently; do not rub. Rinse the treated area thoroughly with cold water.

**Why does my blanket shed after washing?**
Some initial shedding is normal for new microfiber items. If shedding persists or increases after washing, it might be due to harsh washing cycles or high heat drying. Ensure you use the delicate cycle and air dry to minimize shedding.
