But let's be honest, daily wear can leave them looking a little less than perfect. ### Identify Your Tory Burch Sandal Materials

Knowing what your sandals are made of is the first and most important step. Different materials need different cleaning methods. Using the wrong product can damage your sandals permanently. Tory Burch uses various materials, including smooth leather, patent leather, suede, canvas, raffia, and rubber.

I always check the product description or the tag inside the shoe to be sure. For example, my Miller sandals often combine leather straps with a rubber sole. Some espadrille styles use jute, which needs very careful handling. Taking a moment to identify these materials saves a lot of trouble later on. This careful approach helps you select the correct cleaning tools and solutions.

### Gathering Your Cleaning Tools for Sandals

You need the right tools for a safe and effective cleaning process. Many of these items are likely already in your home. Having everything ready before you start makes the job easier. I collect my supplies on a clean surface to keep things organized.

Here are the essential items I recommend:

*   **Soft-bristled brush or old toothbrush:** Great for removing loose dirt and scrubbing footbeds.
*   **Clean cloths or microfiber towels:** Use these for wiping, drying, and applying cleaners.
*   **Mild soap or cleaner:** A gentle dish soap mixed with water or a specialized shoe cleaner works well.
*   **Small bowl of water:** For mixing solutions and rinsing cloths.
*   **Suede brush and eraser (if applicable):** Essential for cleaning suede surfaces.
*   **Leather conditioner (if applicable):** Keeps leather soft and prevents cracking.
*   **Paper towels or old newspapers:** For stuffing sandals during drying to maintain shape.
*   **Protective spray:** Helps repel water and stains after cleaning.

### Cleaning Leather Tory Burch Sandals Effectively

Many Tory Burch sandals feature beautiful leather straps and footbeds. Leather requires a gentle touch to keep it looking its best. I find that prompt cleaning prevents dirt from setting in. This method works well for smooth leather and pebbled leather styles.

First, use a soft brush to remove any loose dirt or dust from the leather. Next, mix a small amount of mild soap with warm water in a bowl. Dampen a clean cloth with this solution, making sure it is not soaking wet. Gently wipe down the leather straps and any leather parts of the footbed. Wipe in small sections, avoiding harsh rubbing.

After cleaning, use a second clean, damp cloth with plain water to wipe away any soap residue. Then, dry the sandals with a soft, dry cloth. Once the leather is dry, apply a good quality leather conditioner. This step helps restore moisture and keeps the leather supple, preventing it from drying out or cracking. ### How to Clean Suede and Fabric Tory Burch Styles

Suede, canvas, and raffia materials give Tory Burch sandals a unique look. They also need specific care to avoid damage. I always treat these materials with extra caution. Water can stain suede, so dry cleaning methods are often best.

For suede, start by using a dedicated suede brush. Brush gently in one direction to remove dirt and restore the nap of the suede. If you have stubborn spots or scuffs, use a suede eraser to gently rub them away. This process lifts dirt without needing water. If a liquid stain exists, allow it to dry completely before brushing. If your sandals have a suede footbed, this process is also important for freshness. Keeping suede clean requires regular attention, and tackling stains early is key. For more detailed tips on suede footbeds, check out how to clean [sandals with a suede footbed](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-sandals-with-suede-footbed).

For canvas or raffia straps, a mild soap and water solution works well. Dampen a cloth and gently dab at the dirty areas. Avoid saturating the fabric, as this can cause discoloration or damage. Rinse with a clean, damp cloth and blot dry immediately. For espadrille soles, use a dry brush to remove loose dirt. You can gently spot clean any marks with a barely damp cloth, but avoid getting the jute wet.

### Caring for Rubber and Patent Leather Sandals

Tory Burch also offers sandals made from rubber and patent leather. These materials are generally easier to clean but still need proper care. I appreciate how durable these styles often are. They are perfect for casual wear or beach outings.

Rubber sandals, like the popular Miller Cloud slides, are very straightforward to clean. You can simply use warm water and a mild soap. Mix a small amount of dish soap with water. Dip a soft brush or sponge into the solution and gently scrub the entire sandal. This removes dirt, sand, and grime effectively. Rinse the sandals thoroughly under running water to remove all soap residue. Allow them to air dry completely. For specific tips, you might find guidance on [how to clean Gucci rubber sandals](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-gucci-rubber-sandals) helpful, as rubber cleaning methods are similar across brands.

Patent leather has a shiny, smooth finish that can show fingerprints and smudges easily. To clean patent leather, simply wipe it down with a soft, damp cloth. You can add a drop of mild soap to the water for dirtier spots. After wiping, buff the patent leather with a clean, dry microfiber cloth to restore its shine. Do not use abrasive cleaners or brushes, as these can scratch the glossy surface. I always make sure to dry patent leather quickly to avoid water spots.

### Deep Cleaning Footbeds and Removing Foot Marks

The footbeds of sandals often collect the most dirt and grime. Sweat, skin cells, and daily wear leave behind dark foot marks. These marks can make even clean sandals look old. I find that focusing on the footbed brings new life to my sandals. Different footbed materials require specific approaches for effective cleaning.

For leather or cork footbeds, start by gently scrubbing with a soft brush or an old toothbrush. Use a solution of mild soap and warm water. Dip the brush into the solution and lightly scrub the footbed in small circles. This helps lift dirt and residue. Wipe the area with a clean, damp cloth to remove the soap. For stubborn [foot marks on sandals](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-foot-marks-on-sandals), a paste made from baking soda and a little water can work wonders. Apply the paste, let it sit for 10-15 minutes, then gently scrub and wipe clean.

If your sandals have a leather footbed, it is especially important to clean it well. You can find more specific methods for [cleaning a leather footbed of sandals](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-leather-footbed-of-sandals). After cleaning, make sure to dry the footbed completely to prevent mildew. Using a fan or leaving them in a well-ventilated area helps. Addressing these foot marks regularly keeps your sandals looking fresh and extends their wear.

### Tackling Odors and Tough Stains on Sandals

Even with regular cleaning, sandals can sometimes develop odors or stubborn stains. Sweat and moisture contribute to unwanted smells. Specific stains like grass or oil need targeted treatment. I always test any cleaning solution on an inconspicuous area first. This prevents potential damage or discoloration to the sandal material.

For odors, baking soda is your best friend. After cleaning the sandals, sprinkle a generous amount of baking soda over the footbeds. Let it sit overnight or for several hours. Baking soda absorbs odors naturally. Then, simply shake out or vacuum the powder. For persistent smells, you might find more comprehensive advice on [how to clean smelly Ecco sandals](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-smelly-ecco-sandals), which can apply to other brands too.

For tough stains, identify the type of stain if possible. For oil-based stains on leather, a small amount of cornstarch or talcum powder can help absorb the oil. Sprinkle it on, let it sit for a few hours, then brush it off. For colored stains on fabric, a diluted stain remover designed for fabrics can be dabbed on carefully. Always follow the product instructions and rinse thoroughly. For the bottom of your sandals, where dirt and grime accumulate, a stiff brush and soapy water can work wonders. Cleaning [the bottom of sandals](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-bottom-of-sandals) improves hygiene and extends sole life.

### Proper Drying and Protection for Long-Lasting Wear

Drying your Tory Burch sandals correctly is just as important as cleaning them. Incorrect drying can lead to damage, such as shrinking, cracking, or mildew. Protection measures further extend the life and beauty of your footwear. I always make sure my sandals are completely dry before wearing or storing them.

After cleaning, blot any excess water with a clean, dry cloth. Do not place your sandals in direct sunlight or use a hairdryer. High heat can cause materials like leather and rubber to dry out, crack, or warp. Instead, find a well-ventilated area indoors to air dry them. You can stuff the toe area with paper towels or old newspapers. This helps absorb moisture and maintains the sandal's shape as it dries. Just remember to change the paper periodically if it becomes damp.

Once your sandals are completely dry, consider applying a protective spray. There are sprays specifically designed for leather, suede, and fabric that repel water and resist stains. Always follow the product instructions for application. Storing your sandals in a cool, dry place, perhaps in their original dust bags, also helps protect them. This final step ensures your Tory Burch sandals stay beautiful for many seasons.

### FAQ Section

**Q1: Can I put my Tory Burch sandals in the washing machine?**
No, I strongly advise against putting Tory Burch sandals in a washing machine. The harsh agitation and strong detergents can damage materials like leather, suede, and even rubber. This can cause straps to break, soles to detach, or materials to warp. Hand cleaning methods are always safer and more effective for preserving your sandals.

**Q2: How often should I clean my Tory Burch sandals?**
I recommend cleaning your Tory Burch sandals as needed, especially after heavy wear or visible dirt. For regular maintenance, a quick wipe-down after each use helps. Deep cleaning, like addressing footbeds or tough stains, can be done every few weeks or months. Consistent care helps prevent buildup and keeps them fresh.

**Q3: What if my Tory Burch sandals get wet in the rain?**
If your Tory Burch sandals get wet, gently blot away excess water with a clean towel. Do not rub, especially on suede. Stuff them lightly with paper towels to absorb moisture and help maintain shape. Allow them to air dry completely in a well-ventilated area away from direct heat or sunlight.

**Q4: How do I remove scuff marks from patent leather Tory Burch sandals?**
For minor scuff marks on patent leather, you can try gently rubbing them with a little petroleum jelly or a non-gel toothpaste on a soft cloth. Apply a tiny amount, rub gently, then wipe clean with a damp cloth. Buff with a dry microfiber cloth to restore shine. Test in a hidden spot first.

**Q5: Can I use household cleaners like bleach or all-purpose sprays?**
No, I do not recommend using harsh household cleaners like bleach, abrasive cleaners, or all-purpose sprays on your Tory Burch sandals. These products contain chemicals that can discolor, dry out, or permanently damage delicate materials. Always stick to mild soap and water or specialized shoe cleaners for best results.
