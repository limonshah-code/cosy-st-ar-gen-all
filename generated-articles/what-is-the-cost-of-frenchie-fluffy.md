## FAQ Section

### How rare is a Fluffy Frenchie?

Fluffy Frenchies are quite rare. Their long coat comes from a recessive gene (FGF5). Both parent French Bulldogs must carry this specific gene to produce a fluffy puppy. This genetic requirement makes them less common than standard short-haired French Bulldogs. Their rarity is a main factor contributing to their higher price.

### Do Fluffy Frenchies have more health problems?

Fluffy Frenchies do not inherently have more health problems due to their fluffy coat. They are prone to the same health issues as standard French Bulldogs. These include brachycephalic airway syndrome, spinal issues, and allergies. Responsible breeding practices and genetic testing help mitigate these risks.

### Can I get a cheaper Fluffy Frenchie?

You might find cheaper Fluffy Frenchies from backyard breeders or puppy mills. However, these dogs often lack proper health testing and care. This can lead to significant health problems and vet bills later. It is strongly advised to buy from a reputable breeder, even if the initial cost is higher.

### What is a Fluffy Frenchie carrier?

A Fluffy Frenchie carrier is a standard-coated French Bulldog that carries one copy of the recessive FGF5 gene. This gene is responsible for the fluffy coat. A carrier Frenchie will not be fluffy themselves. However, if bred with another carrier, or a fluffy Frenchie, they can produce fluffy puppies. You can learn more about [what a fluffy carrier French Bulldog is](https://cosyhomecreation.com/what-is-a-fluffy-carrier-french-bulldog).

### How much does a Fluffy Frenchie puppy cost on average?

The average cost of a Fluffy Frenchie puppy typically ranges from $8,000 to $30,000. Prices vary significantly based on the breeder's reputation, the puppy's lineage, health clearances, and rare coat colors. Some exceptionally unique puppies can command even higher prices.
