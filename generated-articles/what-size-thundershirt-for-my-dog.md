## Measuring Your Dog for the Perfect Thundershirt Fit

Measuring your dog accurately is the most important step. This ensures you buy the correct Thundershirt size for my dog. You will need a soft tape measure for this task. A fabric tape measure is ideal. Make sure your dog is calm before you start. This makes the process easier for both of you.

First, measure your dog's chest girth. This is the widest part of their chest. Find the area just behind their front legs. Wrap the tape measure around their body at this point. Ensure the tape is snug but not tight. You should be able to slide two fingers underneath it easily. Write down this measurement in inches or centimeters. This number is very important for sizing.

Next, measure your dog's neck circumference. Locate the widest part of their neck. This is typically where their collar sits. Wrap the tape measure around this area. Again, keep the tape snug but not restrictive. You need to ensure comfort. Record this measurement as well. Some Thundershirt charts use neck size.

Finally, consider your dog's weight. While measurements are primary, weight helps confirm the size. Most Thundershirt sizing charts provide weight ranges. Weigh your dog if you are unsure. You can use a pet scale. Or, weigh yourself, then weigh yourself holding your dog. Subtract your weight from the total. These three pieces of information – chest girth, neck circumference, and weight – are crucial. They will guide you to the perfect Thundershirt size. ### How often should I check the Thundershirt's fit?

You should check the Thundershirt's fit regularly, especially if your dog is growing or has experienced weight changes. For adult dogs, checking every few months is a good practice. For puppies, check the fit weekly. Always ensure it remains snug but comfortable.

### Can a Thundershirt be too tight for my dog?

Yes, a Thundershirt can definitely be too tight. If it is too tight, it can restrict your dog's breathing or movement. It can also cause skin irritation. The goal is gentle, constant pressure, not compression. Always ensure you can easily slide two fingers under the straps.

Thundershirts are made from durable fabric. They do not typically stretch out significantly over time with proper use. However, the hook-and-loop fasteners might lose some grip with extensive use. Regular washing and proper care help maintain the vest's integrity and fit.
