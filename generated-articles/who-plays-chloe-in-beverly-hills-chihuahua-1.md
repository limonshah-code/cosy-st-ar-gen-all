## FAQ Section

### Who voices Papi in Beverly Hills Chihuahua 1?
George Lopez provides the voice for Papi in *Beverly Hills Chihuahua 1*. Papi is the energetic and lovestruck male Chihuahua who falls for Chloe. Lopez's distinctive comedic voice adds much humor and warmth to Papi's character. He helps make Papi a very memorable part of the film's cast.

### Was Chloe a real dog in the movie?
Yes, Chloe was portrayed by a real dog, primarily a Chihuahua named Angel, for the live-action filming. Animal trainers worked with Angel and other Chihuahuas to perform the physical actions. The talking mouth movements were then added using computer-generated imagery (CGI) during post-production.

### How many Beverly Hills Chihuahua movies are there?
There are three movies in the *Beverly Hills Chihuahua* series. The first film, *Beverly Hills Chihuahua*, was released in 2008. It was followed by *Beverly Hills Chihuahua 2* in 2011 and *Beverly Hills Chihuahua 3: Viva La Fiesta!* in 2012. Each film continues the adventures of Chloe, Papi, and their family.

### What is Chloe's breed in the movie?
Chloe is a purebred Chihuahua in the movie. She is specifically portrayed as a very small, pampered Chihuahua. Her breed is central to her character's appearance and initial personality, highlighting her small stature in a big world. The film accurately represents the typical size and general appearance of the Chihuahua breed.

### Did Drew Barrymore voice Chloe in all the movies?
Drew Barrymore only voiced Chloe in the first *Beverly Hills Chihuahua* movie. For the sequels, *Beverly Hills Chihuahua 2* and *Beverly Hills Chihuahua 3: Viva La Fiesta!*, the voice role of Chloe was taken over by Odette Annable (formerly Odette Yustman). This change in voice actor is a common occurrence in movie franchises.
