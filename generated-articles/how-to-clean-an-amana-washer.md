## How To Clean An Amana Washer

Nobody wants to wash clothes in a dirty machine. Over time, residue from detergent, fabric softener, dirt, and even mildew can build up inside your Amana washer. This buildup leads to unpleasant odors and can even transfer grime back onto your "clean" laundry. I understand the frustration of finding your freshly washed clothes smelling less than fresh. Learning how to clean an Amana washer correctly ensures your laundry comes out truly clean and extends the life of your appliance. This guide covers everything you need, from supplies to detailed steps, for both top-load and front-load Amana models. You will learn how to maintain your washer and keep it running efficiently.

### Takeaway

To keep your Amana washer clean and odor-free:
*   Run a cleaning cycle with bleach or vinegar monthly.
*   Wipe down the drum, dispenser, and gasket after each use.
*   Clean the drain pump filter every few months.
*   Leave the washer door or lid ajar between cycles to air dry.
*   Use the correct amount of HE detergent for your machine.

To clean an Amana washer effectively, you must regularly address its interior components. Use specialized washer cleaning tablets or a combination of white vinegar and baking soda to flush out residue from the drum. Pay close attention to the dispenser drawer and the door gasket, as these areas often trap grime.

### Why Your Amana Washer Needs Regular Cleaning

Your Amana washer works hard to get your clothes clean. But over time, it can accumulate soap scum, hard water minerals, dirt, and even mildew. This buildup can make your clothes smell less fresh after a wash. It also affects how well your washer performs.

A dirty washer can become a breeding ground for bacteria and mold. These can cause musty odors that cling to your laundry. Regular cleaning prevents these issues. It helps your machine operate at its best efficiency. Cleaning also extends the lifespan of your Amana appliance. A well-maintained washer does a better job, and it lasts longer. I find that a clean machine simply makes laundry day easier.

### Essential Supplies for Amana Washer Cleaning

Before you start, gather the right tools and cleaning agents. Having everything ready makes the cleaning process smooth and effective. You likely have many of these items in your home already. Using the correct supplies protects your washer and ensures a deep clean.

Here is what you will need:
*   **White Vinegar:** This is a natural disinfectant and deodorizer. It breaks down mineral deposits and soap scum.
*   **Baking Soda:** A mild abrasive and deodorizer. It helps scrub away grime and absorb odors.
*   **Bleach (Optional):** Use chlorine bleach for sanitizing and removing tough mildew stains. Ensure your washer model can handle bleach.
*   **Washer Cleaning Tablets:** Brands like Affresh or similar cleaners are designed specifically for washing machines. These are convenient and effective.
*   **Microfiber Cloths or Sponges:** For wiping down surfaces and scrubbing.
*   **Old Toothbrush or Small Brush:** Excellent for reaching tight spaces like dispenser corners or gasket crevices.
*   **Spray Bottle:** Handy for mixing vinegar and water for quick wipes.
*   **Rubber Gloves:** Protect your hands from cleaning solutions.

Always check your Amana washer's user manual for any specific cleaning instructions or warnings. Some parts might require special care. Different materials in your washer may react differently to certain chemicals. I always check my manual first to be safe.

### Step-by-Step: Cleaning the Amana Washer Tub

The washer tub is where all the action happens. It needs a thorough cleaning to remove accumulated grime and odors. The method varies slightly for top-load and front-load Amana models. I will explain both approaches clearly.

#### Top-Load Amana Washer Tub Cleaning

Cleaning a top-load Amana washer tub is straightforward. This process flushes out residue and sanitizes the drum. It should be done once a month, especially if you wash frequently.

1.  **Empty the Washer:** Make sure there are no clothes or items inside the washer drum. Running a cleaning cycle with clothes can damage them.
2.  **Add Cleaning Solution:**
    *   **Option 1 (Vinegar & Baking Soda):** Pour two cups of white vinegar directly into the empty washer tub. Close the lid. Let it sit for about 30 minutes to an hour. Then, open the lid, sprinkle half a cup of baking soda into the tub, and close the lid again.
    *   **Option 2 (Bleach):** Add one cup of liquid chlorine bleach to the detergent dispenser or directly into the tub. Do not mix bleach with vinegar; it creates dangerous fumes.
    *   **Option 3 (Washer Cleaning Tablet):** Place one or two washer cleaning tablets directly into the empty tub. Follow the product instructions.
3.  **Run a Hot Water Cycle:** Select the hottest water setting available on your Amana washer. Choose the largest load size or a "clean washer" cycle if your model has one. Let the cycle run completely. The hot water helps dissolve and rinse away buildup.
4.  **Wipe Down the Tub:** After the cycle finishes, use a clean microfiber cloth. Wipe down the inside of the tub. Pay attention to any areas that still show grime. I sometimes find bits of residue clinging to the sides.
5.  **Rinse Cycle (Optional):** If you used bleach or if you want to be extra thorough, run a short rinse and spin cycle with just water. This ensures all cleaning agents are completely removed.

#### Front-Load Amana Washer Tub Cleaning

Front-load Amana washers are known for energy efficiency, but their sealed design can trap moisture. This makes the door gasket particularly susceptible to mold and mildew. Cleaning the tub and gasket is crucial for these models. For more general advice on maintaining this type of appliance, you can refer to how to clean a front-loading washer.

1.  **Empty and Prepare:** Remove all clothes from the washer drum. Open the detergent dispenser drawer slightly.
2.  **Add Cleaning Solution:**
    *   **Option 1 (Vinegar & Baking Soda):** Pour two cups of white vinegar into the detergent dispenser. You can also add half a cup of baking soda directly into the drum.
    *   **Option 2 (Bleach):** Add one cup of liquid chlorine bleach to the bleach dispenser. Again, never mix bleach and vinegar.
    *   **Option 3 (Washer Cleaning Tablet):** Place one or two washer cleaning tablets directly into the empty drum.
3.  **Run a Self-Clean Cycle:** Most Amana front-load washers have a dedicated "Clean Washer" or "Tub Clean" cycle. Select this option. If not, choose the hottest water setting and the longest wash cycle. Let it run to completion.
4.  **Focus on the Gasket:** This is the rubber seal around the door opening. It's a common spot for mold and mildew.
    *   Once the cycle is done, open the door. Pull back the folds of the rubber gasket.
    *   Spray a mixture of equal parts white vinegar and water onto a cloth.
    *   Wipe down the entire gasket, getting into all the crevices. Use an old toothbrush for stubborn spots.
    *   For heavy mildew, you can apply a bleach solution (1 part bleach to 10 parts water) with a cloth. Let it sit for a few minutes, then wipe thoroughly. Always wear gloves.
5.  **Wipe Down the Drum and Door:** Use a clean, damp cloth to wipe the inside of the drum. Wipe the inside and outside of the washer door, including the glass.
6.  **Air Dry:** Leave the washer door ajar after cleaning, and after every wash cycle. This allows air to circulate and prevents moisture buildup, which stops mold from forming. I always do this; it makes a big difference.

### Cleaning the Dispenser Drawers and Detergent Trays

The dispenser drawers and trays are often overlooked, but they can harbor a lot of gunk. Detergent and fabric softener residues build up here. This leads to blockages and smelly deposits. Cleaning these parts regularly is simple but important.

1.  **Remove the Dispenser Drawer:** Most Amana washers have a removable dispenser drawer. Look for a small tab or button to press, usually at the back of the drawer, to release it. Consult your user manual if you cannot find it.
2.  **Soak the Drawer:** Place the removed dispenser drawer in a sink filled with warm, soapy water. Let it soak for about 15-30 minutes. This helps loosen dried-on detergent and softener.
3.  **Scrub Thoroughly:** Use a small brush, an old toothbrush, or a sponge to scrub all compartments of the drawer. Pay attention to the corners and crevices where residue tends to accumulate. Rinse it well under running water.
4.  **Clean the Dispenser Housing:** While the drawer is soaking, inspect the housing where the drawer sits. Residue can build up inside this area too. Use a damp cloth and your toothbrush to clean any visible grime. For stubborn spots, a spray of vinegar and water can help.
5.  **Rinse and Replace:** Once clean, rinse the dispenser drawer completely. Make sure no soap residue remains. Dry it with a cloth and then slide it back into its slot in the washer.
6.  **Prevent Future Buildup:** After each wash, leave the dispenser drawer slightly ajar to allow it to air dry. This prevents mold and mildew from growing. I also give it a quick wipe once a week.

### Maintaining the Door Gasket and Exterior

The door gasket on a front-load Amana washer is a critical area for maintenance. This rubber seal creates a watertight barrier. However, it easily traps moisture, lint, and residue. This often leads to unpleasant odors and mildew growth. The exterior also needs attention to keep your laundry area looking neat.

1.  **Gasket Cleaning (Front-Load Specific):**
    *   **After Every Wash:** Wipe down the gasket with a clean cloth. This removes moisture and any visible debris like lint or pet hair. This simple step prevents much of the buildup. For issues like pet hair, specific cleaning might be needed, as described in guides like how to get dog hair out of washer.
    *   **Weekly Inspection:** Pull back the folds of the gasket. Look for any trapped items, mold spots, or residue. Use a spray bottle with equal parts white vinegar and water. Spray the gasket thoroughly. Wipe it clean with a microfiber cloth.
    *   **Deep Mildew Treatment:** If you see black mold spots, create a solution of 1 part bleach to 10 parts water. Dip an old cloth in this solution. Carefully wipe down the affected areas of the gasket. Let it sit for 5-10 minutes. Then, wipe it clean with a fresh, damp cloth. Always wear gloves and ensure good ventilation when using bleach. Follow up with a plain water rinse on the gasket.
2.  **Exterior Cleaning:**
    *   **Wipe Down Surfaces:** Use a damp cloth to wipe the top, sides, and front of your Amana washer. This removes dust, spills, and fingerprints.
    *   **Control Panel:** Use a soft, damp cloth to gently wipe the control panel. Avoid spraying water directly onto the panel to prevent damage. Dry it immediately.
    *   **Beneath the Washer:** Periodically pull your washer out if possible. Clean the floor beneath and behind it. Dust and lint can accumulate there.
    *   **Keep Vents Clear:** Ensure any vents on the washer are not blocked by laundry or debris. This helps with proper airflow.

Keeping the gasket clean and dry is the single most important step for front-load washer owners. Leaving the door slightly open between washes is a habit I highly recommend. It allows the gasket to air dry completely.

### Don't Forget the Filter: Cleaning Your Amana Washer's Drain Pump Filter

Many Amana washers, especially front-loaders, have a drain pump filter. This filter traps lint, coins, buttons, and other small items. If you have ever wondered why your clothes come out with sand on them, cleaning this filter could be the solution, similar to how to clean the sand out of the washer. A clogged filter can lead to drainage problems, foul odors, and even error codes. Cleaning it is a vital part of regular washer maintenance.

1.  **Locate the Filter:** The drain pump filter is typically located at the bottom front of the washer. It might be behind a small access panel or a kick plate. Consult your Amana washer's manual for its exact location. I usually find it on the lower right or left.
2.  **Prepare for Water Drainage:** When you open the filter housing, water will likely drain out. Place a shallow pan or tray directly under the filter access door. Keep a few towels handy to catch any spills.
3.  **Open the Access Panel:** Carefully open the small access panel. You might need a screwdriver or a flat tool to pry it open.
4.  **Drain the Remaining Water:** Most Amana washers have a small drain hose next to the filter. Pull this hose out and remove its cap. Allow all the water to drain into your pan. Once the water stops, replace the cap on the hose.
5.  **Remove and Clean the Filter:** Twist the filter counter-clockwise to unscrew and remove it. Be prepared for a small amount of water to still come out.
    *   Remove any lint, hair, coins, or other debris caught in the filter.
    *   Rinse the filter thoroughly under warm running water. You can use an old toothbrush to scrub away any stubborn grime.
    *   Inspect the filter housing and the area behind it for any trapped items.
6.  **Reinstall the Filter and Close Panel:** Once the filter is clean, screw it back in clockwise until it is tight. Make sure it is secure to prevent leaks. Close the access panel.
7.  **Frequency:** Clean this filter every 2-3 months. If you wash many pet blankets or heavily soiled items, you might need to clean it more often. This proactive step prevents many common washer issues.

### Deep Cleaning Your Amana Washer for Stubborn Issues

Sometimes, a regular cleaning cycle isn't enough. Stubborn odors, excessive residue, or persistent mold require a more intensive approach. This is when a deep clean becomes essential to restore your Amana washer to its best condition. For comprehensive guidance on rejuvenating your appliance, you might want to look at how to deep clean a washer machine.

1.  **Addressing Persistent Odors:**
    *   **Vinegar Soak Method:** For very musty smells, run the hottest cycle with two cups of white vinegar in the detergent dispenser. After the washer fills and agitates for a few minutes, pause the cycle. Let the vinegar water sit in the tub for 30 minutes to an hour. This extended soak helps vinegar penetrate and neutralize odors. Then, resume the cycle.
    *   **Baking Soda Follow-Up:** After the vinegar cycle finishes, sprinkle half a cup of baking soda directly into the tub. Run another hot water cycle immediately without any pause. Baking soda helps absorb remaining odors and acts as a gentle abrasive.
    *   **Activated Charcoal (Non-Wash Method):** If odors persist even after cleaning, place an open container of activated charcoal inside the empty washer tub for a day or two. Close the washer door. The charcoal will absorb the bad smells.
2.  **Tackling Hard Water Buildup:**
    *   **Descaling with Citric Acid:** Hard water leaves behind mineral deposits, reducing efficiency and potentially causing spots on clothes. Purchase descaling tablets or pure citric acid powder. Add 1/2 cup of citric acid powder directly to the empty washer drum. Run a hot water cycle. Citric acid is effective at dissolving mineral buildup.
    *   **Vinegar for Maintenance:** Regular vinegar cycles help prevent hard water scale from forming in the first place.
3.  **Removing Stubborn Mold and Mildew:**
    *   **Bleach Shock Treatment (for bleach-safe machines):** If mildew is severe, especially on the gasket or in dispenser areas, a more concentrated bleach application may be needed. Mix 1 part liquid chlorine bleach with 10 parts water in a spray bottle. Spray affected areas, let sit for 10-15 minutes, then scrub with a brush. Rinse thoroughly with clean water. Remember to ensure good ventilation and wear gloves.
    *   **Repeat Cleaning Cycles:** Sometimes, one deep cleaning cycle isn't enough for severe mold or smell. Repeat the bleach or vinegar/baking soda hot water cycles back-to-back.
    *   **Professional Help:** If mold is deeply embedded in inaccessible parts or odors persist despite repeated deep cleaning, consider calling an Amana service technician. There might be an issue with drainage or internal components.

A deep clean can revitalize your Amana washer. It helps it perform like new again. I find that a yearly deep clean, combined with monthly maintenance, keeps my washer in excellent shape.

### Preventative Measures for a Clean Amana Washer

Preventing buildup and odors is easier than cleaning them once they are established. Adopting a few simple habits will keep your Amana washer fresher, cleaner, and more efficient between deep cleans. These small steps make a big difference over time.

1.  **Leave the Door/Lid Ajar:** After every wash cycle, leave the washer door (for front-loaders) or lid (for top-loaders) open for a few hours. This allows the interior of the drum and gasket to air dry completely. Moisture is the primary cause of mold and mildew growth. I always do this, and it really helps.
2.  **Wipe Down the Gasket (Front-Loaders):** Make it a habit to quickly wipe the door gasket dry after each load. This removes trapped moisture, lint, and residue before they become a problem.
3.  **Use the Correct Detergent and Amount:**
    *   **HE Detergent:** Always use High-Efficiency (HE) detergent in HE washers. These detergents produce less suds, which is crucial for HE machines. Regular detergent creates too many suds, leading to residue buildup and potential machine problems.
    *   **Measure Carefully:** Do not use too much detergent. More suds do not mean cleaner clothes. Excess detergent leaves residue in your washer and on your clothes. Follow the manufacturer's recommendations.
4.  **Remove Laundry Promptly:** Do not leave wet clothes sitting in the washer after the cycle finishes. This creates a humid environment perfect for mildew growth and can cause clothes to develop a musty smell.
5.  **Clean Dispenser Regularly:** Give the detergent and fabric softener dispensers a quick wipe or rinse after each use. This prevents sticky residue from hardening.
6.  **Run a Monthly Maintenance Cycle:** Even if your washer does not have a "Clean Washer" cycle, run an empty load once a month using hot water and either a washer cleaner, white vinegar, or bleach. This flushes out hidden grime.
7.  **Check Pockets:** Before washing, always check pockets for coins, tissues, or small items. These can clog the drain pump filter and cause issues.
8.  **Avoid Overloading:** Overloading your Amana washer prevents clothes from getting properly cleaned. It also puts strain on the machine and can leave more residue.

These preventative steps are simple to incorporate into your laundry routine. They ensure your Amana washer remains a fresh and reliable appliance.

### Troubleshooting Common Amana Washer Cleaning Issues

Even with regular cleaning, you might encounter specific issues. Knowing how to troubleshoot these problems can save you time and frustration. Let's look at some common challenges you might face with your Amana washer.

1.  **Persistent Musty Odor:**
    *   **Issue:** Despite cleaning, a musty smell lingers. This is often due to deep-seated mold or moisture.
    *   **Solution:** Perform a deep cleaning cycle with white vinegar, as detailed in the "Deep Cleaning" section. Ensure you focus on the gasket, dispenser, and drain pump filter. Leave the washer door open for at least 24 hours to air out completely. You might need to repeat the deep clean. Check for any standing water in the gasket or filter housing.
2.  **Lint or Residue on Clothes:**
    *   **Issue:** Your "clean" clothes have white flakes, lint, or gritty residue.
    *   **Solution:** This usually indicates too much detergent, a clogged filter, or buildup in the tub.
        *   **Check Detergent Use:** Reduce the amount of detergent you are using, especially if it is not HE detergent.
        *   **Clean Drain Pump Filter:** Refer to the filter cleaning section. A clogged filter can prevent proper draining and lint removal.
        *   **Run a Tub Clean Cycle:** Perform a hot water wash cycle with vinegar to flush out residue from the tub. For specific issues like pet hair, you might find tips in how to get dog hair out of washer. If your clothes contain sand, follow advice on how to clean the sand out of the washer to avoid future residue.
3.  **Water Not Draining Completely:**
    *   **Issue:** Water remains in the tub after a cycle, or the cycle stops mid-wash.
    *   **Solution:** A clogged drain pump filter is the most common cause. Clean the drain pump filter thoroughly. Check the drain hose for kinks or blockages. If the problem persists, the pump itself might be faulty, and you may need professional help.
4.  **Black Spots on Gasket:**
    *   **Issue:** Visible black mold spots on the rubber door seal of a front-loader.
    *   **Solution:** Treat with a bleach solution (1 part bleach to 10 parts water) on a cloth. Let it sit for 10-15 minutes, then scrub and rinse thoroughly. For very stubborn spots, commercial mold removers designed for appliances can be used, but always test in an inconspicuous area first. Ensure you are wearing gloves and have good ventilation.
5.  **Detergent Not Dispensing:**
    *   **Issue:** Detergent or fabric softener remains in the dispenser.
    *   **Solution:** Clean the dispenser drawer and its housing thoroughly. Dried residue often blocks the flow. Ensure the water pressure to your washer is adequate. Sometimes, low water pressure prevents proper flushing of the dispenser.

Addressing these common issues promptly prevents them from worsening. Regular maintenance and knowing these troubleshooting steps will keep your Amana washer running smoothly.

### FAQ Section

**Q1: How often should I clean my Amana washer?**
A1: You should clean your Amana washer at least once a month. If you wash frequently, have a large family, or wash heavily soiled items, consider cleaning it every two to three weeks. Regular cleaning prevents residue buildup and maintains freshness. I aim for once every four weeks.

**Q2: Can I use regular bleach to clean my Amana washer?**
A2: Yes, you can use regular liquid chlorine bleach to clean your Amana washer, especially for sanitizing and removing mold. Add it to the bleach dispenser or directly into the tub. Never mix bleach with vinegar or other cleaners, as it can create harmful fumes.

**Q3: What causes a bad smell in my Amana front-load washer?**
A3: A bad smell in a front-load Amana washer typically comes from mildew and bacteria buildup. This occurs in the rubber door gasket, the dispenser drawer, or the drain pump filter. Moisture trapped in these areas creates an ideal environment for growth.

**Q4: Is it safe to use vinegar and baking soda together in my Amana washer?**
A4: Yes, it is safe and effective to use vinegar and baking soda to clean your Amana washer. Use them in separate steps, not mixed directly. First, run a cycle with vinegar, then follow up with a separate cycle using baking soda. This combination deodorizes and breaks down residue.
