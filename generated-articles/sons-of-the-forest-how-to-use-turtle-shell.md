The Water Collector is a crucial structure for any base. It provides clean drinking water. This prevents dehydration, which is a constant threat. Your turtle shell is the primary component for this item. Without it, you cannot build a reliable water source.

Once you have a turtle shell, sticks, and a tarp, open your survival guide. Navigate to the crafting menu. Select the Water Collector blueprint. You will see the required items highlighted. Place the Water Collector blueprint where you want it. A good spot is near your base.

After placing the blueprint, hold the "E" key to finish building. The turtle shell becomes the base of the collector. The sticks form the frame. The tarp catches the rainwater. This structure automatically collects rainwater. You can then drink from it.

Place Water Collectors strategically. Put them in open areas to catch more rain. You might need several collectors. This ensures you always have enough water. Building multiple collectors reduces your thirst worries. It frees you up for other survival tasks.
