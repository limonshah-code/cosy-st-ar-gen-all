This approach keeps the weave intact. It also preserves the shoe's structure. Think about how you clean other white fabric items. The Boost sole has a distinct bubbly texture. Dirt gets trapped easily in its crevices. Standard brushing might not reach these spots. Stains on Boost foam can be stubborn. Scuffs from walking are common. The material is durable but can yellow over time.

Specialized cleaners exist for Boost soles. A simple household item, like a magic eraser, also works wonders. We will explore both options. Proper cleaning brings back that bright white look. It improves the overall appearance of your NMDs.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning Your NMDs White

Cleaning your white NMDs involves several steps. Following these steps ensures a thorough clean. It also protects your shoes from damage. I always recommend working systematically. This helps you cover all areas.

Each part of the shoe needs attention. We will start with preparation. Then we move to the upper, sole, and laces. Finally, we will discuss drying. This process helps restore your shoes to their bright white condition.

### Preparation for Cleaning

Before you start scrubbing, prepare your shoes.
1.  **Remove laces:** Take out the shoelaces. This allows you to clean the tongue area better. You can wash laces separately.
2.  **Remove excess dirt:** Use a dry, soft brush to remove loose dirt. Brush off mud and dust. This prevents scratching the fabric during wet cleaning.
3.  **Stuff shoes:** Place paper towels or shoe trees inside. This helps the shoe keep its shape. It also absorbs excess moisture later.

These initial steps are crucial. They set the stage for effective cleaning. They also protect the shoes' structure.

### Cleaning the Knit Upper

The knit upper needs a gentle touch.
1.  **Mix solution:** Combine warm water with a small amount of mild soap. Use a bowl for mixing.
2.  **Dampen brush:** Dip a soft-bristle brush into the solution. Avoid soaking the brush.
3.  **Gently scrub:** Lightly scrub the knit upper in small sections. Work in circular motions.
4.  **Wipe clean:** Use a clean, damp microfiber cloth to wipe away soap residue. Repeat as needed.
5.  **Pat dry:** Finish by patting the area with a dry cloth. Do not rub vigorously.

For stubborn stains, use a small amount of stain remover. Apply it directly to the stain. Then, gently scrub. Remember, patience is key with delicate fabrics. This method works well for various white fabrics. Consider similar methods when learning [how to clean a white fur rug](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-a-white-fur-rug).

### Tackling the Boost Sole

The Boost sole often collects the most grime.
1.  **Apply cleaner:** Dampen a magic eraser or apply a specialized Boost sole cleaner.
2.  **Scrub gently:** Rub the magic eraser over scuff marks and dirt. For cleaners, use an old toothbrush.
3.  **Wipe clean:** Use a clean, damp cloth to remove any residue.
4.  **Repeat if necessary:** For tough stains, you might need to repeat the process.

The magic eraser works like a charm on scuffs. It gently abrades the surface dirt away. Specialized cleaners penetrate deeper. They lift embedded grime. Keep your soles looking fresh. You might use similar techniques for [how to keep white wheels clean](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-keep-white-wheels-clean).

### Cleaning the Outsole and Laces

Do not forget these important parts.
*   **Outsole:** Use a stiff brush and soapy water. Scrub the rubber outsole thoroughly. Rinse with a damp cloth. This removes embedded dirt and pebbles.
*   **Laces:** Place laces in a mesh laundry bag. Wash them with a small amount of detergent in a washing machine. Or, hand wash them in soapy water. Rub them together to remove grime. Rinse thoroughly.

Clean laces make a big difference. They brighten the whole shoe. A clean outsole provides better traction. It also prevents tracking dirt indoors.

## Drying Your White NMDs Correctly

Proper drying is as important as proper cleaning. Incorrect drying can cause damage. It can also lead to yellowing or shrinking. Always air dry your NMDs. Never use a machine dryer.

Machine dryers can warp the materials. The heat can melt adhesives. It can also cause the knit upper to shrink. I always tell people to be patient with this step. Good drying ensures your hard work pays off.

### The Air Drying Process

Follow these steps for optimal drying:
1.  **Remove excess water:** Gently press a clean, dry towel against the shoes. This absorbs surface moisture.
2.  **Stuff with paper towels:** Loosely stuff the shoes with white paper towels. These absorb internal moisture. Change them every few hours.
3.  **Place in a well-ventilated area:** Find a spot with good airflow. Avoid direct sunlight. Direct sun can cause yellowing. Avoid placing them near direct heat sources.
4.  **Allow ample time:** Drying can take 24-48 hours. Ensure shoes are completely dry before wearing.

This method preserves shoe shape and material integrity. It prevents mold or mildew growth. It also helps maintain the bright white color.

## Advanced Stain Removal Techniques

Sometimes, tough stains require more than basic cleaning. Grease, ink, or grass stains can be stubborn. Do not give up on your NMDs. Specialized techniques can often save them. I have dealt with many difficult stains. These methods often work.

Always try the gentlest method first. Then, move to stronger solutions if needed. Patience and precision are important here.

### Tackling Specific Stain Types

Different stains respond to different treatments:
*   **Grease/Oil Stains:**
    *   **Blot immediately:** Use a paper towel to absorb excess oil.
    *   **Apply cornstarch/talcum powder:** Sprinkle generously on the stain. Let it sit for several hours. It absorbs the oil.
    *   **Brush off and clean:** Brush away the powder. Then, clean the area with mild soap and water.
*   **Ink Stains:**
    *   **Rubbing alcohol:** Dab a small amount of rubbing alcohol on a cotton swab. Gently blot the ink stain.
    *   **Work from outside in:** This prevents spreading the stain.
    *   **Rinse thoroughly:** Wipe with a damp cloth to remove alcohol residue.
*   **Grass Stains:**
    *   **White vinegar:** Mix equal parts white vinegar and water.
    *   **Apply and scrub:** Dab onto the stain and gently scrub with a soft brush.
    *   **Rinse:** Wipe with a damp cloth.

Always test these solutions on an inconspicuous area first. This prevents potential damage or discoloration. Be very careful with strong chemicals on delicate Primeknit.

### Dealing with Yellowing Boost Soles

White Boost soles can yellow over time. This is common. It happens due to oxidation and UV exposure. You can often restore their brightness.
1.  **Hydrogen Peroxide:** Apply a thin layer of 3% hydrogen peroxide. Use a clean brush or cotton ball.
2.  **Sunlight Exposure:** Place the shoes in direct sunlight. The UV rays activate the peroxide. This bleaching process can take several hours.
3.  **Rinse and repeat:** After treatment, rinse the sole thoroughly. Repeat if necessary.

There are also specialized "sole sauces" or "whitening pens" available. These products contain stronger oxidizers. Always follow their instructions carefully. Be careful not to get these products on the knit upper. It could cause damage. ## FAQs About Cleaning White NMDs

### How often should I clean my white NMDs?

Clean your white NMDs as needed. Wipe them down after each wear for basic maintenance. Perform a deep clean every few weeks or months. This depends on how often you wear them and how dirty they get. Regular care prevents grime buildup.

### Can I put my NMDs in the washing machine?

No, do not put NMDs in the washing machine. The harsh agitation and heat can damage the Primeknit upper. It can also warp the Boost sole. This leads to shrinking or material separation. Always hand wash for the best results.

### What should I do if my NMDs start to smell?

To remove smells, sprinkle baking soda inside the shoes. Let it sit overnight. Baking soda absorbs odors. You can also use shoe deodorizers or cedar shoe trees. Ensure shoes are completely dry before storing them to prevent odor-causing bacteria.

### How can I remove yellowing from the white Primeknit?

Yellowing on Primeknit is tough to reverse. Avoid harsh chemicals like bleach. For mild yellowing, try a solution of mild soap and warm water. Gentle scrubbing might help. For more severe yellowing, consult a professional cleaner. They might have specific restoration methods.

### Are there any products I should avoid using on white NMDs?

Yes, avoid harsh chemicals like bleach, strong detergents, or abrasive cleaners. These can damage the delicate Primeknit. They can also cause permanent yellowing or discoloration. Always use mild, pH-neutral cleaners specifically designed for sneakers or fabrics.

### Can I use household cleaners like Clorox wipes on my NMDs?

It is generally not recommended to use Clorox wipes or similar harsh household cleaners directly on your NMDs. These wipes contain strong chemicals that can cause permanent damage to the delicate knit fabric or lead to discoloration and yellowing over time. Stick to mild soap and water or specialized sneaker cleaners.
