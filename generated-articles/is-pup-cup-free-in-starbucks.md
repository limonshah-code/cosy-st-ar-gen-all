## Is the Pup Cup Safe for All Dogs?

While Pup Cups are a beloved treat, safety for all dogs is a valid concern. The Pup Cup consists of whipped cream. Whipped cream is a dairy product. Many dogs can digest small amounts of dairy. However, many adult dogs are lactose intolerant. Their bodies produce less lactase, the enzyme needed to break down lactose in milk. For these dogs, dairy can cause digestive upset.

Symptoms of lactose intolerance in dogs include:

*   Gas
*   Bloating
*   Diarrhea
*   Vomiting
*   Abdominal pain

If your dog has shown sensitivity to dairy before, avoid the Pup Cup. It is better to be safe than sorry. Even if your dog has no known issues, introduce the treat slowly. Offer a tiny lick first. Watch for any adverse reactions.

For dogs with other health conditions, like pancreatitis or obesity, a Pup Cup might be problematic. Whipped cream is high in fat and sugar. These ingredients can worsen existing health issues. Always consult your veterinarian if you have concerns. Your vet knows your dog's specific health needs best. They can advise if a Pup Cup is appropriate for your particular pet.

### Q6: How big is a Pup Cup?
A Pup Cup is typically a small, espresso-sized paper cup. It holds a small dollop of whipped cream. The size is appropriate for a treat, not a meal, for most dog breeds.
