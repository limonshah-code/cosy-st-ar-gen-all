## Refresh Your Rothy's: How to Clean Inserts

Rothy's shoes offer comfort and style. Their unique construction includes removable inserts, or footbeds. These inserts are crucial for comfort, but they also collect sweat and dirt. Learning how to clean Rothy's inserts keeps your favorite shoes fresh. It also helps extend the life of your footwear.

This guide provides simple steps for cleaning Rothy's inserts. We will cover both machine washing and hand washing methods. You will learn about the right supplies and proper drying techniques. Let's make your Rothy's inserts feel new again.

### Takeaway

Keeping Rothy's inserts clean is simple.
*   Machine wash inserts on a delicate cycle with cold water.
*   Use a mesh laundry bag for protection.
*   Always air dry inserts completely.
*   Hand wash for delicate care or specific stains.
*   Regular cleaning prevents odors and prolongs life.

### Clean your Rothy's inserts using a gentle machine wash or careful hand wash with mild detergent, then always air dry them fully to preserve their shape and material.

## Essential Supplies to Clean Rothy's Footbeds

Gathering the right tools makes cleaning Rothy's inserts easy. You only need a few common household items. These supplies ensure gentle yet effective cleaning. This protects the fabric and shape of your footbeds.

First, you need a mild, eco-friendly detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals or bleach. Strong cleaners can damage the recycled plastic yarn of your inserts. A gentle laundry soap works best. My preferred choice is a liquid detergent without dyes or strong perfumes.

Next, a mesh laundry bag is important for machine washing. This bag protects the inserts during the wash cycle. It prevents them from getting tangled or damaged. A soft brush, like an old toothbrush, can help with spot cleaning. Finally, clean towels are useful for blotting excess water. Having these items ready simplifies the cleaning process.

## Step-by-Step Guide: Machine Washing Rothy's Inserts

Machine washing your Rothy's inserts is efficient and easy. Rothy's designs their products for simple care. This method works well for general cleaning and freshening. Follow these steps for the best results.

### Preparing Your Rothy's Inserts for the Machine

First, remove the inserts from your Rothy's shoes. Shake off any loose dirt or debris. Place each insert into a separate mesh laundry bag. This protects them from wear and tear inside the washing machine. It also keeps them from bumping around too much.

### Loading and Washing Your Inserts

Put the mesh bags with inserts into your washing machine. Add a small amount of mild liquid detergent. Choose a cold water setting. Select the delicate or gentle cycle. This gentle approach prevents damage to the fabric. Avoid using hot water, as it can shrink or warp the inserts. My Rothy's shoes, including the inserts, always go into the machine this way. For a deeper understanding of cleaning Rothy's, including the shoes themselves, you can refer to our guide on [how to clean white Rothy's](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-white-rothys). The principles are much the same for all Rothy's products.

### After the Wash Cycle

Once the cycle finishes, remove the inserts from the mesh bags. Gently squeeze out any excess water. Do not twist or wring them harshly. Place them on a clean towel or drying rack. Air drying is essential to prevent damage and maintain shape. We will cover drying in more detail next. This method keeps your Rothy's inserts clean and ready for more wear.

## Hand Washing Rothy's Inserts for Delicate Care

Hand washing offers a gentle alternative to machine washing. This method is good for spot cleaning or very delicate inserts. It also gives you more control over the cleaning process. Hand washing can be very effective for specific stains or if you prefer not to use a machine.

### Preparing for Hand Washing

Start by filling a basin or sink with cool water. Add a small amount of mild liquid detergent. Swish the water gently to create a little soap. Remove the inserts from your Rothy's shoes. Knock off any loose dirt.

### Washing Your Inserts by Hand

Submerge the inserts in the soapy water. Gently rub them together or use a soft brush. Pay attention to any stained areas. Do not scrub too hard, as this can damage the fabric. Rinse the inserts thoroughly under cool, running water. Ensure all soap residue is gone. For a comprehensive look at hand washing techniques, particularly for other Rothy's components, you can find more guidance on [how to clean Rothy's by hand](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-rothys-by-hand). ### Drying Hand Washed Inserts

Once rinsed, gently press excess water out of the inserts. Do not wring or twist them. Lay them flat on a clean, dry towel. You can roll the towel to absorb more moisture. Then, place the inserts on a drying rack or hang them up. Air drying is the only recommended method. This careful approach helps your inserts last longer and stay in good condition.

## Drying Your Rothy's Inserts Properly for Longevity

Proper drying is as important as the washing process itself. Incorrect drying methods can damage your Rothy's inserts. It can cause shrinking, warping, or even the growth of mold. Always prioritize air drying to maintain the quality of your inserts.

### The Importance of Air Drying

After washing, never put your Rothy's inserts in a machine dryer. High heat from dryers can melt the recycled plastic threads. It can also cause the inserts to lose their shape. This makes them uncomfortable and unusable. Air drying preserves the integrity of the material. It also helps them keep their original form.

### Best Practices for Air Drying

Lay your clean inserts flat on a clean, dry surface. A drying rack works perfectly. Ensure good air circulation around them. You can place them near an open window or a fan. Avoid direct sunlight, as intense UV rays might cause fading. Flip the inserts periodically to ensure even drying.

Make sure the inserts are completely dry before putting them back into your shoes. Damp inserts can lead to mildew and unpleasant odors. This usually takes 12-24 hours, depending on humidity. My personal rule is to wait at least a full day. Patience here prevents future problems.

## Preventing Odor and Dirt in Your Rothy's Inserts

Maintaining clean Rothy's inserts goes beyond washing them. Proactive steps can help keep them fresh longer. This means fewer deep cleans and a more pleasant shoe experience. I found these habits make a big difference for my own Rothy's.

One simple tip is to rotate your Rothy's shoes. Do not wear the same pair every single day. This gives your inserts time to air out naturally between wears. Airing helps reduce moisture buildup. Moisture often contributes to odor-causing bacteria.

Wearing socks with your Rothy's also helps. Socks absorb sweat and protect the inserts. This creates a barrier between your foot and the insert. You can also use shoe deodorizers or odor-absorbing packets. Place these inside your shoes overnight. They draw out moisture and neutralize smells. ## FAQ Section

### Can I put Rothy's inserts in the dryer?
No, you should never put Rothy's inserts in a machine dryer. The high heat can damage the material, causing them to shrink or warp. Always air dry your Rothy's inserts flat on a clean surface to maintain their shape and integrity.

### How often should I clean Rothy's inserts?
Clean your Rothy's inserts as needed, typically every few weeks or once a month, depending on how often you wear your shoes. If they start to smell or show visible dirt, it is time for a wash. Regular cleaning helps prevent odor buildup.

### What if my Rothy's inserts still smell after cleaning?
If inserts still smell after cleaning, ensure they are completely dry before reuse. Dampness causes odor. You can also try a vinegar rinse (1 part white vinegar to 2 parts water) before washing. Baking soda sprinkled on dry inserts overnight can also absorb lingering odors.

### Can I bleach Rothy's inserts?
No, do not use bleach on Rothy's inserts. Bleach can weaken the fabric and cause discoloration, especially on colored inserts. Stick to mild, eco-friendly detergents for cleaning. Bleach is too harsh for the recycled plastic yarn.

### Are all Rothy's inserts washable?
Yes, Rothy's designs all their shoe inserts to be machine washable. This makes cleaning simple and convenient. Always follow the washing instructions provided by Rothy's or detailed in this guide to ensure proper care.
