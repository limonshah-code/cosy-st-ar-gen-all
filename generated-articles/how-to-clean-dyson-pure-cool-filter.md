## Keep Your Air Clean: How To Clean Dyson Pure Cool Filter

A clean home feels great. Fresh, pure air makes your living space even better. Your Dyson Pure Cool machine works hard to remove pollutants from the air you breathe. It traps dust, allergens, and odors, making your home a healthier place.

But what happens when your air purifier's filter gets full? Its performance drops, and your air quality suffers. Many people wonder *how to clean Dyson Pure Cool filter* to keep their device working perfectly. Understanding proper filter care is key to maintaining clean air and extending the life of your Dyson machine. This article will guide you through everything you need to know about keeping your Dyson Pure Cool filter in top condition, from understanding filter types to step-by-step maintenance.

### Takeaway

*   Dyson Pure Cool filters are generally sealed units. You replace them, not wash them.
*   Regularly wipe the exterior of your Dyson Pure Cool machine. This improves airflow.
*   Replace internal filters every 6-12 months, depending on use and air quality.
*   Always use genuine Dyson replacement filters. This ensures performance.
*   Reset your machine's filter life indicator after replacement.

### Clear Answer

To "clean" your Dyson Pure Cool filter, you mostly focus on external casing cleaning and internal filter replacement. Wipe the device exterior with a damp cloth. Gently vacuum the filter housing. For the main internal HEPA and activated carbon filters, you must replace them with new, genuine Dyson filters. These sealed units are not washable.
