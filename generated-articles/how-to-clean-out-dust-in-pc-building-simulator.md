### Opening the PC Case and Initial Inspection

First, you must open the PC case. This usually involves removing side panels. Click on the screws holding the panels in place. Then, remove the panel itself. Now you can see the internal components. Take a moment to look inside.

Scan for any visible dust. Pay close attention to fan blades. Check heatsinks on the CPU and GPU. Also, look at the power supply. Dust loves to collect in these areas. An initial visual check is always the first step.

### Systematically Cleaning Components

Once the case is open, begin cleaning. Start with the largest, most visible dust areas. Use your compressed air can. Sweep it across the components. I like to work from top to bottom. This way, dust falls down and is easier to remove.

Clean the CPU cooler first. Then move to the GPU fans and heatsink. Do not forget the case fans. Even small fans on the motherboard can gather dust. Be thorough in your cleaning.

*   **CPU Cooler:** Point the can at the heatsink fins. Spray until clean.
*   **GPU:** Clean the fans and the heatsink shroud.
*   **Case Fans:** Spray both sides of the fan blades.
*   **Power Supply:** If accessible, clean the fan intake.
*   **Motherboard:** Lightly spray across the mainboard to clear surface dust.
