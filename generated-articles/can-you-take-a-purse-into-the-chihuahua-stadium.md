## Prohibited Items Beyond Bag Size

Weapons of any kind are strictly forbidden. This includes firearms, knives, and any object that could be used to cause harm. Security screenings are thorough to prevent such items from entering. Other common prohibited items include large banners, signs, or flags. These can obstruct views for other spectators. Professional cameras with detachable lenses and tripods are often disallowed. Any item deemed disruptive or dangerous by security staff will be confiscated. This can include noisemakers like air horns.

Understanding this list helps you pack smart. You can avoid unnecessary delays or the frustration of having items confiscated at the gate. Always err on the side of caution. ## After the Game: Caring for Your Stadium Purse

After an exciting game or concert at Chihuahua Stadium, your purse might need a little attention. Stadium environments can be crowded, dusty, or even spill-prone. A quick clean-up helps keep your bag looking good and ready for your next adventure. Taking good care of your accessories ensures they last longer.

First, empty your purse completely. Remove any tickets, receipts, or forgotten items. Check all pockets and compartments. Sometimes, a crumb or a loose piece of paper can stay hidden. Give the inside a good shake to get rid of any dust or debris. You might find a stray peanut shell or a piece of confetti from the celebration.

For clear plastic or vinyl bags, a simple wipe-down often does the trick. Use a damp cloth with a mild soap solution. Gently wipe both the inside and outside surfaces. This removes dirt, fingerprints, and any light stains. Avoid harsh chemicals that could damage the plastic. Let the bag air dry completely before storing it. This prevents any mildew or lingering odors.

If you brought a small fabric clutch, check for any visible marks. For minor spots, a spot cleaner designed for fabric might work. Always test a small, inconspicuous area first. Some fabrics might not react well to water or cleaning solutions. If your fabric purse is washable, you might consider a deeper clean.
