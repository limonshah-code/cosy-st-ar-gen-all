## Preparing Your Area for Bissell Little Green Cleaning

Before you begin cleaning with your Bissell Little Green Carpet Cleaner, proper preparation of the area is crucial. This step saves time and helps you achieve better results. It also prevents spreading dirt or damaging your carpets. Taking a few minutes now will make the entire cleaning process smoother.

First, remove any loose dirt, debris, or pet hair from the area you plan to clean. A quick vacuuming pass is often enough. This step prevents the Bissell Little Green from pushing surface grime deeper into the carpet fibers. It also protects your machine from clogging with larger particles. Your carpet cleaner is a spot cleaner, not a vacuum.

Next, identify the specific stain or soiled area you want to address. If it is a fresh spill, blot up as much liquid as possible with a clean, dry cloth. Press firmly but do not rub, as rubbing can spread the stain. For older, dried stains, you might want to gently scrape off any crusted residue. This pre-treatment step helps the cleaning solution work more effectively.

### Spot Testing and Pre-Treating

Before applying any cleaning solution to a visible area, always perform a spot test. Choose an inconspicuous area of the carpet or upholstery. This could be behind a sofa, inside a closet, or a hidden corner. Apply a small amount of the cleaning solution to the test spot. Wait a few minutes to observe any color changes or damage.

If the fabric color remains stable and no damage occurs, you can proceed with confidence. For particularly tough or set-in stains, pre-treating can make a big difference. Spray a small amount of Bissell pre-treatment solution directly onto the stain. Let it sit for the recommended time, usually a few minutes, as per the product instructions. This helps to break down the stain before you even start the main cleaning process.

Always ensure the area is well-ventilated during and after cleaning. This helps with drying and disperses any cleaning solution odors. Move furniture away from the cleaning zone to avoid accidental splashes or moisture damage. Protecting surrounding areas with old towels or plastic sheeting is also a good idea. Taking these precautions ensures a safe and effective cleaning experience.

## Filling and Priming Your Bissell Little Green Cleaner

Properly filling your Bissell Little Green Carpet Cleaner is essential for effective cleaning. This step ensures you have the right mix of water and cleaning solution. It also prepares the machine for powerful spot removal. Following these instructions will help you avoid common mistakes and get the most out of your cleaner.

First, locate the clean water tank on your Bissell Little Green. This tank is usually clearly marked and easy to remove. Press the release latch or lift the tank straight up, depending on your model. Once removed, carry the tank to a sink for filling. You will notice fill lines inside the tank, usually for water and cleaning formula.

Fill the clean water tank with hot tap water up to the designated water line. Avoid using boiling water, as it can damage your machine. Hot water helps activate the cleaning solution and lifts stains more effectively. Next, add the recommended amount of Bissell cleaning formula. Only use formulas specifically designed for portable carpet cleaners, especially Bissell-branded ones, to protect your machine and warranty. Using other detergents might create too much foam or leave residue.

### Priming the Machine for Use

After filling the tank, firmly replace it on your Bissell Little Green machine. Ensure it clicks into place securely. This prevents leaks and ensures proper flow during cleaning. Next, plug your machine into a grounded electrical outlet. Always ensure your hands are dry when handling the power cord and plug.

Now, you need to prime the machine. Turn on the power switch. The machine will make a low humming sound. Hold the cleaning tool over a sink or a bucket. Squeeze the spray trigger for about 15-30 seconds. You will see water and cleaning solution begin to spray from the nozzle. This indicates the pump is primed and the solution is flowing correctly. If you do not see spray after a short time, check that the tank is properly seated and the solution level is adequate.

Once the machine is primed, you are ready to tackle those stubborn stains. The proper mixture and priming ensure consistent cleaning power. This setup makes your Bissell Little Green an effective tool for maintaining a fresh and clean home. Always empty any unused solution from the clean water tank after each cleaning session.

## Mastering the Bissell Little Green Cleaning Technique

Using your Bissell Little Green Carpet Cleaner effectively involves more than just spraying and sucking. The right technique ensures thorough stain removal and prevents re-soiling. Mastering these steps will make your cleaning efforts highly successful. You will achieve cleaner carpets and upholstery with less effort.

Begin by positioning the cleaning tool directly over the stain or soiled area. Make sure the nozzle is flat against the surface you are cleaning. This allows for optimal spray distribution and suction. It also prevents oversaturation of the carpet. A good position is key to getting the job done right.

Now, activate the spray by pressing the trigger on the cleaning tool. As you spray, slowly pull the tool backwards over the stain. Do not just spray in one spot. This motion helps to distribute the cleaning solution evenly across the soiled area. Release the spray trigger as you reach the end of your backward pass.

### Effective Scrubbing and Suction

After spraying, it is time for scrubbing. With the spray trigger released, push the cleaning tool slowly forward and then pull it back over the stain. Use gentle, overlapping strokes. The bristles on the cleaning tool help to agitate the carpet fibers and lift the dirt. Avoid scrubbing too aggressively, as this can damage the carpet. Let the solution and the tool do the work.

Once you have scrubbed the area, focus on extraction. Make several slow, firm passes over the cleaned area with the tool, keeping the spray trigger released. This strong suction removes the dirty water and cleaning solution from the carpet. Continue until you see clear water being suctioned into the dirty tank. This step is crucial for two reasons: it prevents detergent residue from attracting new dirt, and it reduces drying time.

Repeat the spray, scrub, and suction process if the stain is still visible. For tough stains, you might need several passes. Always finish with extra suction passes to remove as much moisture as possible. Excess moisture can lead to longer drying times and potential mildew growth. It is important to remove as much liquid as you can. Learn more about [how long for carpet to dry after Bissell](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-long-for-carpet-to-dry-after-bissell) to ensure proper drying.

When cleaning upholstery, be careful not to oversaturate the fabric. Upholstery can take longer to dry than carpets. Use less spray and more suction passes on delicate fabrics. Always check the care tag of your upholstery before cleaning to ensure it is safe for wet cleaning. Proper technique makes all the difference in achieving a truly clean finish.

## Post-Cleaning Care: Emptying and Rinsing Your Machine

After a successful cleaning session, proper post-cleaning care for your Bissell Little Green Carpet Cleaner is just as important as the cleaning itself. This step ensures your machine stays in good working order. It also prevents odors and extends the lifespan of your appliance. Neglecting this maintenance can lead to residue buildup and decreased performance.

First, unplug your Bissell Little Green from the power outlet. Safety first. Then, locate the dirty water tank. This tank will contain all the grime and liquid pulled from your carpets. Carefully remove the dirty water tank from the machine. Most models have a handle or a clear release mechanism for easy removal.

Carry the dirty water tank to a utility sink or toilet. Pour out the dirty water and debris. You might be surprised by how dark and murky the water is. Rinse the dirty water tank thoroughly with clean water. Use warm water to help loosen any remaining residue or debris. You can also wipe down the inside of the tank with a paper towel or cloth. This removes any film that might develop. For a detailed guide on this specific task, check out [how to clean the dirty tank on Bissell Little Green](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-the-dirty-tank-on-bissell-little-green).

### Rinsing the Clean Tank and Hose

Now, address the clean water tank. If there is any unused cleaning solution left, empty it out. Do not store cleaning solution in the tank, as it can lead to clogs or deterioration of internal parts. Rinse the clean water tank with fresh water and allow it to air dry. This prevents any chemical residue from building up inside.

The hose and cleaning tools also need attention. Rinse the cleaning tool under running water to remove any trapped dirt or carpet fibers. You can disconnect it from the hose for easier rinsing. To flush the hose, you can fill the clean water tank with plain warm water (no solution). Reattach the empty dirty water tank. Then, turn on the machine and spray the plain water through the hose and tool into a sink or bucket for a minute or two. This flushes out any lingering detergent or debris.

After rinsing all components, ensure they are completely dry before reassembling or storing the machine. Air drying is best. You can leave the tanks open to allow air circulation. Storing a damp machine can lead to mildew growth and unpleasant odors. Once dry, reassemble your Bissell Little Green. Store it in a cool, dry place, ready for its next use. Proper post-cleaning care keeps your machine fresh and ready to tackle future spills.

Consistent cleaning of the machine parts prevents clogs, odors, and extends its lifespan. Taking a few minutes after each use will save you time and money in the long run.

After you have emptied and rinsed the dirty water tank and flushed the hose, turn your attention to the brush head. The brush head is where most of the scrubbing action happens. It can accumulate hair, carpet fibers, and stubborn dirt. To clean the brush head, disconnect the cleaning tool from the hose. Hold it under running water. Use your fingers or a small brush to dislodge any trapped debris from the bristles. Make sure all hair and dirt are removed. ## Troubleshooting Common Bissell Little Green Issues

Even with the best care, you might occasionally encounter an issue with your Bissell Little Green Carpet Cleaner. Most problems are minor and can be resolved with simple troubleshooting steps. Knowing how to quickly diagnose and fix these common issues saves you time and frustration. It also keeps your cleaning routine on track.

One frequent problem is the machine not spraying cleaning solution. First, check if the clean water tank is properly seated. Sometimes, it just needs a firm push to click into place. Ensure the tank contains enough solution. If it is low or empty, the pump cannot draw liquid. Also, check that you have primed the machine by squeezing the spray trigger for a few seconds. Air in the lines can stop the spray.

Another common issue is weak suction. If your Bissell Little Green is leaving too much water behind, check the dirty water tank. It might be full and needs to be emptied. A full dirty tank will prevent further suction. Also, inspect the suction nozzle on your cleaning tool. It could be clogged with debris, such as hair or carpet fibers. Clear any blockages you find. Ensure the cleaning tool is firmly attached to the hose. A loose connection can reduce suction power.

### Addressing Leaks and Odors

Leaking is another problem that can happen. If you notice water leaking from your machine, first check that both the clean and dirty water tanks are securely seated. Gaskets on the tanks or seals around the hose connection can sometimes cause leaks if they are not properly aligned. Make sure the caps on the tanks are tightened correctly. If a seal is damaged, you may need to replace it. Finding replacement parts might be necessary if seals or tanks are cracked, and you can find guidance on [Bissell Little Green parts](https://cosyhomecreation.com/bissell-little-green-parts-all-you-need-to-know) for such situations.

Unpleasant odors from the machine itself usually indicate a buildup of dirty water or cleaning solution residue. This is a sign that the machine needs a thorough cleaning. Empty and rinse both tanks immediately after each use. Flush the hose and cleaning tool with plain water. Allow all parts to air dry completely before storage. Regularly cleaning the dirty tank, brush head, and machine head prevents these odors from developing.

If your Bissell Little Green is still not working after these troubleshooting steps, consult your owner's manual. It provides specific troubleshooting guides for your model. Sometimes, a part may need replacement. Contacting Bissell customer support is also an option if the issue persists. Most problems are simple fixes, helping you get back to spotless cleaning quickly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use any cleaning solution in my Bissell Little Green?
No, you should only use Bissell-branded cleaning solutions designed for portable carpet cleaners. Other detergents can create too much foam. This foam might damage your machine or leave sticky residues on your carpet. Always check the label to ensure compatibility.

### How often should I clean my Bissell Little Green machine?
You should empty and rinse both tanks, flush the hose, and clean the cleaning tool after every use. A more thorough cleaning of the brush head and machine head is good after every few uses or as needed. This prevents odors and maintains performance.

### Why is my Bissell Little Green not spraying solution?
First, check if the clean water tank is correctly seated and contains enough solution. Ensure the machine is plugged in and turned on. Prime the pump by holding the spray trigger for 15-30 seconds. A blockage in the nozzle can also stop the spray.

### How long does it take for carpets to dry after using the Bissell Little Green?
Drying time typically ranges from 2-4 hours, but it depends on factors like carpet thickness, humidity, and ventilation. Thoroughly extracting dirty water significantly reduces drying time. Opening windows or using fans can also speed up the process.

### Can I use the Bissell Little Green on upholstery?
Yes, the Bissell Little Green is excellent for cleaning upholstery. Always perform a spot test on an inconspicuous area first. Avoid oversaturating the fabric. Use gentle strokes and extract thoroughly to prevent water marks and ensure quick drying.

### What should I do if my Little Green has weak suction?
Check if the dirty water tank is full and needs emptying. Inspect the cleaning tool's suction nozzle for any clogs from hair or debris. Ensure the hose is securely attached to both the machine and the cleaning tool. Loose connections can reduce suction power.
