## Caring for Your Maurice: Keeping Your Plush Pristine

Proper care ensures your Maurice the French Fry Squishmallow stays soft and huggable for years. Squishmallows are generally easy to clean, but gentle methods are important. You want to preserve their signature marshmallow-like feel. Harsh chemicals or aggressive washing can damage the plush fibers. I always recommend a careful approach.

For minor spots, use a damp cloth and mild soap. Gently dab the stained area, then pat dry with a clean towel. Avoid rubbing vigorously, as this can spread the stain or disrupt the fabric. Air dry your Squishmallow completely afterwards. This prevents any mildew or lingering dampness.

For a deeper clean, Squishmallows can be machine washed on a delicate cycle. Place Maurice inside a pillowcase or laundry bag to protect his delicate fabric. Use cold water and a small amount of mild detergent. Afterward, air dry your Squishmallow on a low-heat setting in a dryer or lay him flat. Tumble drying on high heat can melt the fibers. Following these steps will keep your Maurice looking fresh.
