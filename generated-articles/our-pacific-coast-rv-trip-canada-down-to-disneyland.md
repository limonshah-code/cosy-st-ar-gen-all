## Kicking Off in Canada: Vancouver's Coastal Charm

Our Pacific Coast RV trip began in beautiful Vancouver, Canada. This city offers a fantastic starting point. It has a vibrant atmosphere and stunning natural surroundings. We spent a few days exploring before heading south. We parked our RV at a nearby campground.

Vancouver offers many family-friendly activities. Stanley Park provides great walking and biking trails. The Vancouver Aquarium is a popular attraction. Granville Island Public Market has fresh food and unique shops. We enjoyed the diverse culinary scene.

### Exploring British Columbia's Best

Before leaving Canada, we explored more of British Columbia. The drive along the Sea-to-Sky Highway to Whistler is spectacular. It offers dramatic ocean and mountain views. If you have extra time, consider a ferry trip to Vancouver Island. It has charming towns and beautiful scenery.

We found RV-friendly campsites easily in British Columbia. Provincial parks offer great facilities. Reservations are often needed, especially in summer. We loved the fresh air and lush forests. British Columbia provides a wonderful introduction to the Pacific Northwest.

### Border Crossing Tips for RV Travelers

Crossing the Canada-USA border in an RV requires preparation. We made sure all our documents were in order. Passports are essential for everyone. We declared any food items we carried. Certain agricultural products are restricted. Check the latest regulations before you travel.

The process was smooth for us. Border agents may ask about your travel plans. They might inspect your RV. Keep your vehicle tidy to speed up inspection. We had a positive experience by being honest and prepared. Always have your vehicle registration and insurance ready.

## FAQ Section

### How long does an RV trip from Canada to Disneyland take?
An RV trip from Canada to Disneyland typically takes three to five weeks. This timeframe allows for comfortable travel days and sufficient time to explore major attractions. It also factors in rest days and unexpected stops. A faster trip can be done in two weeks, but it feels rushed.

### What are the best times of year for a Pacific Coast RV trip?
The best times are late spring (May-June) or early fall (September-October). The weather is generally pleasant, and crowds are smaller. Summer can be busy and hot, especially in Southern California. Winter brings more rain, particularly in the Pacific Northwest.

### Do I need special insurance for an RV trip across the border?
Yes, you need to ensure your RV insurance covers travel in both Canada and the USA. Check with your insurance provider before your trip. Some policies may require a rider or separate coverage for international travel. Always carry proof of insurance.

### What are popular stops between Vancouver and Disneyland?
Popular stops include Seattle, Portland, the Oregon Coast, Redwood National Park, San Francisco, Big Sur, and various beach towns in Southern California. National parks like Olympic and Crater Lake are also favorite detours. Choose stops that match your interests.

### How do I find RV-friendly campgrounds along the Pacific Coast?
Many resources help find campgrounds. Apps like Campendium, AllStays, and iOverlander are very useful. State parks and national parks offer great options. Private RV parks provide amenities. Always reserve popular sites in advance, especially during peak season.

### Can I travel with pets on this RV trip?
Yes, you can travel with pets. You must check specific border crossing requirements for pets from Canada to the USA. Most national parks have restrictions on where pets can go. Many RV parks welcome pets. Always keep your pet's vaccination records handy.
