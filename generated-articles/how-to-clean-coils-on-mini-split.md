## How to Clean Mini Split Coils for Peak Performance

Keeping your home comfortable means your mini split works its best. Many people forget one critical task: cleaning the coils. Dirty coils make your system work harder. This costs you money and impacts air quality. I learned this the hard way, dealing with a less efficient unit.

Cleaning the coils on a mini split is a maintenance step you cannot skip. It improves efficiency and extends your unit's life. This guide will walk you through the process. We will cover why it matters, what tools you need, and how to clean both indoor and outdoor coils. You will also learn when to clean and when to seek professional help. Let us make your mini split run like new.

### Takeaway

*   **Boost Efficiency:** Clean coils reduce energy use and lower utility bills.
*   **Improve Air Quality:** Remove dust, mold, and allergens for healthier air.
*   **Extend Lifespan:** Regular cleaning prevents strain on your mini split system.
*   **Follow Steps:** Always power off, gather tools, and clean gently.
*   **Know When to Call:** Seek pros for deep mold or complex issues.
