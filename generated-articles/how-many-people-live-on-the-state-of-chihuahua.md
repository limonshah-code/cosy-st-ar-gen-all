Manufacturing is a cornerstone of Chihuahua's economy. The maquiladora industry is particularly important. These factories assemble products for export. They are often located near the U.S. border. This industry provides many jobs.

The maquiladoras attract a large workforce. They pull people from across Mexico. This contributes significantly to the population growth in cities like Ciudad Juárez. The sector offers stable employment, which is appealing. It directly influences where many people choose to live.

#### Agriculture and Mining

Beyond manufacturing, agriculture and mining are traditional sectors. Chihuahua is a major agricultural producer. It grows crops like pecans, apples, and cotton. Ranching is also a significant activity. These sectors provide rural employment.

Mining has a long history in the state. Silver, lead, and zinc are important minerals. Mining operations create jobs in specific regions. ### FAQ Section

#### What is the population of Chihuahua City?

Chihuahua City is the state capital and its second-largest city. According to the 2020 INEGI Census, Chihuahua City had a population of 978,067 residents. This makes it a major urban center. It plays a significant role in the state's economy and administration.

#### What is the population of Ciudad Juárez?

Ciudad Juárez is Chihuahua's most populous city. It is a key border city. The 2020 INEGI Census recorded Ciudad Juárez with a population of 1,512,450 residents. Its large population is due to its industrial base and proximity to the U.S. border.

#### Is Chihuahua Mexico's most populated state?

No, Chihuahua is not Mexico's most populated state. While it is the largest state by land area, its population density is low. Mexico City and the State of Mexico have much larger populations. Chihuahua ranks among the top ten most populous Mexican states.

#### What factors attract people to live in Chihuahua?

People are drawn to Chihuahua by several factors. These include economic opportunities, especially in manufacturing and border trade. The state also offers a unique quality of life, with access to nature and cultural richness. Its educational and healthcare services also appeal to residents.

#### How often is population data updated for Mexican states?

Mexico's official population data comes from national censuses. These are conducted every ten years by INEGI. The latest full census was in 2020. However, INEGI also conducts intercensal surveys and provides annual population estimates. These estimates offer more current figures between full censuses.
