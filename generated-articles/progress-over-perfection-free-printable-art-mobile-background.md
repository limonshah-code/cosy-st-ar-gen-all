### How to Set Your New Mobile Background: A Simple Guide

Setting your new "Progress Over Perfection" free printable art mobile background is easy. It takes only a few steps. The process is similar for both iPhones and Android phones. I will walk you through each one. You will have your motivational art on your screen in no time.

First, ensure you have downloaded your chosen image. It should be saved in your phone's photos or gallery app. Most free printable art files come in common image formats like JPEG or PNG. These formats are universally compatible. Make sure you know where the file is located on your device.

The next steps depend on your phone's operating system. I will cover both popular options. You can follow the guide that fits your device. This process is very user-friendly.

#### iOS Setup

1.  **Open Photos App:** Locate the image in your iPhone's Photos app.
2.  **Tap Share Icon:** Tap the share icon, which looks like a square with an arrow pointing up.
3.  **Scroll Down:** Scroll down the options until you see "Use as Wallpaper." Tap on this option.
4.  **Adjust and Set:** You can pinch to zoom in or out. You can also drag the image to position it perfectly. Make sure the "Still" option is selected for a static background. Tap "Set."
5.  **Choose Location:** Select "Set Lock Screen," "Set Home Screen," or "Set Both." Choose "Set Both" for maximum impact.

Your new motivational background will now appear on your chosen screen.

#### Android Setup

1.  **Open Gallery/Photos App:** Go to your phone's gallery or photos app. Find the image you downloaded.
2.  **Tap Menu/Options:** Tap the three dots (menu icon) or the "More" option. This is usually in the top right or bottom corner.
3.  **Select "Set as wallpaper":** From the menu, choose "Set as wallpaper."
4.  **Choose Location:** You will likely see options like "Home screen," "Lock screen," or "Home and lock screens." Select your preferred location.
5.  **Adjust (if needed):** Some Android phones let you crop or adjust the image. Do this if necessary to fit your screen. Then confirm your choice.

Your Android phone now displays your inspiring new background. You can enjoy your custom motivational message all day long. This simple change brings a powerful daily reminder to your fingertips.
