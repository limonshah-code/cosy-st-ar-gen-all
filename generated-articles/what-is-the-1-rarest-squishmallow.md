### Preserving the Rarity: Care and Storage for Your Collection

Once you find a rare Squishmallow, proper care is essential. You want to protect your investment. Keep your Squishmallows clean. Dust and dirt can accumulate over time. Gently spot clean with a damp cloth if needed. Avoid harsh chemicals. These can damage the fabric or colors. A regular light dusting helps maintain their appearance.

Store your Squishmallows in a safe environment. Direct sunlight can fade colors. High humidity can lead to mold or mildew. Keep them in a cool, dry place. Display cases or shelves are good options. Handle your rare Squishmallows with care. Avoid excessive handling. The more they are touched, the more wear and tear they accumulate. Display them in a way that minimizes physical contact.
