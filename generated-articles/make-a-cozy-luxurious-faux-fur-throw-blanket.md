## Selecting the Perfect Faux Fur and Backing Fabric

The heart of a cozy, luxurious faux fur throw blanket lies in its fabric choices. Selecting the right faux fur and backing material is key. These decisions impact the blanket's look, feel, and durability. Take time to explore your options. You want fabrics that complement each other well.

### Understanding Faux Fur Types

Faux fur comes in many varieties. Each type offers a different texture and appearance. Long-pile faux fur is shaggy and plush. It mimics animal fur like wolf or fox. This type offers extreme softness and warmth. Short-pile faux fur has a smoother, denser feel. It often resembles mink or rabbit fur. It provides a sleek, sophisticated look. Shaggy faux fur adds a bohemian touch. It creates a relaxed, inviting vibe.

Consider the density and softness of the faux fur. High-quality faux fur has a thick, dense pile. It feels incredibly soft to the touch. It also sheds less. Look for fabrics that drape well. A good drape means the blanket will fall gracefully over furniture. It will not look stiff. Choose a color that complements your home decor. Neutrals like cream, grey, or brown offer versatility. Bold colors like emerald green or deep blue make a statement.

### Choosing a Complementary Backing

The backing fabric provides comfort and stability. It also adds to the overall luxurious feel. You have several excellent options for the backing.

*   **Minky Fabric:** This is a popular choice. Minky is super soft, plush, and often has a slight stretch. It feels wonderful against the skin. It adds extra warmth without much bulk.
*   **Fleece Fabric:** Fleece is soft, warm, and affordable. It comes in many colors. It is easy to work with. Anti-pill fleece is best as it resists pilling over time.
*   **Flannel Fabric:** Flannel is a brushed cotton fabric. It is soft, breathable, and warm. It works well if you want a lighter blanket. It offers a classic, cozy feel.
*   **Satin or Silk:** These options provide a cool, smooth feel. They add a touch of elegance. They can be slippery to sew. They might be better for an accent throw than a primary warmth blanket.

When selecting your backing, consider its weight and drape. It should match the faux fur reasonably well. A heavy faux fur paired with a very light backing might feel unbalanced. Ensure the backing color harmonizes with the faux fur. A contrasting color can create a stylish edge. A coordinating color offers a seamless look. For an extra layer of warmth and a truly luxurious experience, consider using two layers of faux fur, with the second layer serving as your backing. This creates an incredibly plush blanket.

Choosing the right fabrics sets the foundation for your luxurious creation. These choices ensure your **faux fur throw blanket** feels as good as it looks.

## Step-by-Step Guide to Making Your Faux Fur Throw

Creating your own **cozy luxurious faux fur throw blanket** is a rewarding project. Follow these steps carefully for the best results. I will walk you through the entire process.

### Preparing Your Fabrics and Cutting

1.  **Measure Your Desired Blanket Size:** Decide on the length and width of your finished blanket. A common throw size is 50 inches by 60 inches. You may want a different size for your space.
2.  **Lay Out Your Faux Fur:** Lay the faux fur fabric flat on a large surface. Make sure the fur pile runs in the direction you want. For most blankets, the pile runs downwards if draped over you. Smooth out any wrinkles.
3.  **Cut the Faux Fur:** Flip the faux fur over so the backing is facing up. Use a fabric marker to mark your dimensions. Add about 1 inch to each side for seam allowance.
    *   **Important Tip:** When cutting faux fur, cut only the fabric backing with sharp scissors or a utility knife. Do not cut through the fur pile. This minimizes shedding and keeps the edges neat. Gently separate the fur after cutting.
4.  **Cut the Backing Fabric:** Lay your backing fabric (minky, fleece, etc.) flat. Place your cut faux fur piece on top of it, matching the edges. Use the faux fur as a guide to cut the backing fabric to the exact same size.

### Joining the Layers

1.  **Pin the Fabrics Together:** Place the faux fur and backing fabric right sides together. The fur side of the faux fur should be touching the right side of the backing fabric. Align all edges carefully.
    *   **Pro Tip:** Push the fur pile inwards, away from the seam line, as you pin. This prevents fur from getting caught in your stitches. Use many fabric clips or pins. Place them every few inches around the entire perimeter.
2.  **Mark the Opening:** Along one side of the blanket, mark an opening of about 8-12 inches. This opening is where you will turn the blanket right side out later. Use a fabric marker or extra pins to clearly define this section.

### Sewing Your Blanket

1.  **Set Up Your Sewing Machine:** Attach a walking foot if you have one. Use a heavy-duty needle. Set your stitch length to slightly longer than normal (e.g., 3.0-3.5 mm). This helps with the thick fabric.
2.  **Start Sewing:** Begin sewing at one end of your marked opening. Backstitch to secure the thread. Sew around the entire perimeter of the blanket with a 1/2-inch to 5/8-inch seam allowance.
    *   **Remember:** Stop sewing when you reach the other end of your marked opening. Backstitch again. Make sure you leave the opening unsewn.
3.  **Trim Corners and Seams:** Clip the corners of the blanket at an angle. Cut close to the stitches but do not cut the stitches themselves. This reduces bulk in the corners. You can also trim the seam allowance down to 1/4 inch, especially if your faux fur is very bulky. This creates a neater finish once turned.

### The Finishing Touch

1.  **Turn Right Side Out:** Reach into the opening you left. Gently pull the entire blanket right side out through this hole. Take your time. Push out the corners carefully with a blunt object, like a chopstick.
2.  **Close the Opening:** Fold the raw edges of the opening inside, matching the seam line. You can hand-stitch this opening closed using a ladder stitch for an invisible finish. Alternatively, you can machine topstitch very close to the edge of the opening. This creates a neat, durable closure.
3.  **Fluff and Enjoy:** Use a hair comb or wire brush to gently comb any trapped fur out of the seams. Give your blanket a good shake. Your cozy, luxurious faux fur throw blanket is now ready!

## Expert Tips for Working with Faux Fur Fabric

Working with faux fur can seem daunting at first. It has unique properties that require specific techniques. With these expert tips, you will handle faux fur like a pro. Your **faux fur throw blanket** will look professional.

### Taming the Faux Fur Shed

Faux fur tends to shed, especially when cut. This is normal. There are ways to minimize it.

*   **Cut from the Back:** Always cut faux fur from the backing side. Use sharp scissors or a utility knife to cut only the backing fabric. This leaves the fur pile intact. It prevents excessive shedding along the cut edge.
*   **Shake and Vacuum:** After cutting your pieces, take them outside. Give them a good shake to release loose fibers. You can also use a vacuum cleaner with a brush attachment on a low setting. Gently vacuum the cut edges. This removes any lingering loose fur.
*   **Seal Edges (Optional):** For very high-shedding furs, some people choose to lightly apply a fray check liquid along the raw edges. Do this sparingly and test on a scrap first. This can help bind the fibers.

### Achieving Smooth Seams

Creating clean, smooth seams is crucial for a luxurious finish. Faux fur can be bulky and tricky to sew.

*   **Use a Walking Foot:** This sewing machine attachment is your best friend when sewing faux fur. It helps feed both the top and bottom layers of fabric evenly. This prevents one layer from stretching or shifting. It ensures your seams align perfectly.
*   **Longer Stitch Length:** A slightly longer stitch length (3.0-3.5mm) works better for faux fur. Shorter stitches can get lost in the pile. They can also create tension that causes puckering.
*   **Pin or Clip Liberally:** Use plenty of pins or fabric clips. Place them frequently along your seam lines. This keeps the layers from shifting. Remember to push the fur pile away from the seam line as you pin.
*   **Brush Out Seams:** After sewing each seam, gently brush the fur pile away from the stitching. Use a comb or a wire brush. This helps the stitches disappear into the fur. It creates an almost invisible seam.
*   **Trim Seam Allowance:** If your faux fur is very thick, trim your seam allowances down to 1/4 inch after sewing. This reduces bulk. It helps the blanket lie flatter. Be careful not to cut the stitches.

These techniques ensure your blanket looks beautiful and lasts a long time. They help you conquer any challenges posed by this lovely fabric. With patience and these tips, your **faux fur throw blanket** will be a showstopper.

## Caring for Your Luxurious Faux Fur Throw Blanket

Once you have created your beautiful, cozy, luxurious faux fur throw blanket, proper care is important. This ensures it stays soft, plush, and beautiful for many years. Faux fur requires gentle handling to maintain its texture and luster.

### Gentle Washing for Longevity

Faux fur can often be washed at home. However, you must be careful.

*   **Check Fabric Care Labels:** Always refer to the care instructions for your specific faux fur and backing fabric. This is your best guide.
*   **Machine Wash Cold, Gentle Cycle:** Place your faux fur blanket in a large mesh laundry bag or a duvet cover. This protects it from agitation. Use cold water and a mild detergent. Select the gentle or delicate cycle on your washing machine. Avoid using bleach or fabric softener. Bleach can damage the fibers. Fabric softener can leave a residue that makes the fur stiff.
    *   For more detailed advice on washing faux fur in your machine, you can check out this guide: [Can you wash faux fur in washing machine?](https://cosyhomecreation.com/can-you-wash-faux-fur-in-washing-machine)
*   **Air Dry is Best:** The heat of a dryer can melt or damage faux fur fibers. This causes matting and loss of softness. Instead, remove the blanket from the washer. Gently squeeze out excess water. Hang it to air dry on a sturdy line or lay it flat on a clean surface. Ensure good air circulation. It might take a day or two to dry completely.
*   **No Heat Drying:** If you must use a dryer, use a "no heat" or "air fluff" setting. Add a few dryer balls to help fluff the fur. Remove the blanket while it is still slightly damp. Finish air drying it. Never use high heat.

### Restoring Softness and Luster

Sometimes, faux fur can lose some of its fluffiness after washing or use. You can easily restore its luxurious feel.

*   **Brush Gently When Dry:** Once your blanket is completely dry, use a soft-bristled brush or a hair comb. Gently brush through the fur pile. This separates the fibers. It helps restore its original fluffiness and softness. Brush in the direction of the pile.
*   **Shake It Out:** A good shake after brushing can also help re-fluff the fur. It allows the fibers to settle naturally.
*   **Spot Cleaning for Small Spills:** For small spills, blot the area immediately with a clean cloth. Use a mild soap and water solution if needed. Rinse with a damp cloth. Avoid rubbing, which can damage the fur.
    *   If you have other faux fur items, like a chair, the cleaning principles are similar. Learn more about how to [clean a faux fur chair](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-faux-fur-chair).
*   **Professional Cleaning:** For very large or stubborn stains, consider professional dry cleaning. Always choose a dry cleaner experienced with faux fur. They use specialized techniques that protect the fabric.
    *   If you choose this option, it's helpful to know more about the costs involved: [How much to dry clean a blanket?](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-much-to-dry-clean-a-blanket)

By following these care instructions, your handmade faux fur throw blanket will remain a beautiful and cozy addition to your home for years to come.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Faux Fur Blankets

Making a faux fur blanket raises common questions. Here are answers to help you with your project and care.

### Can you wash a faux fur blanket in a washing machine?

Yes, you can often wash a faux fur blanket in a washing machine. Use cold water and a gentle cycle. Always use a mild detergent. Avoid fabric softener and bleach. Place the blanket in a large mesh bag or a duvet cover. This protects the fur. Air drying is best for faux fur. High heat can damage the fibers.

### What kind of needle do I need for faux fur?

You need a strong needle when sewing faux fur. A heavy-duty needle works best. Look for denim or leather needles, typically size 16 or 18. These needles can handle the thickness and density of faux fur fabric. They resist breaking or bending during sewing. This ensures clean, consistent stitches.

### How much faux fur fabric do I need for a throw blanket?

For a standard throw blanket (around 50x60 inches), you generally need about 1.5 to 2 yards of 60-inch wide faux fur fabric. You will need the same amount for your backing fabric. Always buy a little extra. This allows for trimming or minor mistakes. Check the fabric's width when purchasing.

### Is it hard to sew faux fur?

Sewing faux fur is not difficult, but it requires specific techniques. The main challenges are its bulkiness and shedding. Use sharp tools and a walking foot on your sewing machine. Push the fur pile away from seams. These methods make sewing faux fur manageable. With patience, you can achieve excellent results.
