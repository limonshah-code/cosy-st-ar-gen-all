## Health Considerations and Lifespan: Potential Costs

Teacup Chihuahuas are beloved for their miniature size. However, their small stature can predispose them to specific health issues. Understanding these potential health concerns is crucial for any prospective owner. These conditions can significantly impact the long-term cost of owning a Teacup Chihuahua.

One common concern is hypoglycemia, or low blood sugar. This is especially true for puppies and very small adults. It requires careful feeding schedules and prompt attention if symptoms appear. Patellar luxation, a slipping kneecap, is another genetic predisposition in Chihuahuas. This condition might require surgery, which can be expensive. Hydrocephalus, a build-up of fluid in the brain, is also more common in Teacup Chihuahuas. This severe condition often requires lifelong medication or complex surgical interventions.

### Impact of Health Issues on Vet Bills

Addressing these health issues can lead to significant veterinary expenses. Diagnostic tests, specialized medications, and potential surgeries add up quickly. For example, a patellar luxation surgery might cost $1,500 to $3,000 per knee. Managing hydrocephalus can involve ongoing medication costing hundreds of dollars annually. Dental disease is also prevalent in Chihuahuas. Professional dental cleanings under anesthesia are often required. These procedures can range from $300 to $700 or more, depending on the severity and location.

It is important to discuss potential health problems with your breeder. A responsible breeder will be transparent about their dogs' health history. They should also perform health screenings on the parent dogs. This helps minimize the risk of inheriting certain conditions. Being prepared for these potential costs is part of being a responsible Teacup Chihuahua owner.
