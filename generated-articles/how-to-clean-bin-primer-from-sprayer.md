## How to Clean BIN Primer from Your Sprayer Easily

Finishing a painting project brings a great feeling of accomplishment. However, the job is not fully done until your tools are clean. If you used BIN primer, you know how quickly it dries and how tough it can be. Proper sprayer cleaning is essential to keep your equipment working. This guide helps you tackle the challenge of cleaning BIN primer from your sprayer. We will cover necessary tools, detailed steps, and tips for future use. Get ready to learn simple ways to maintain your valuable painting sprayer.

### Takeaway

*   Use denatured alcohol as the primary solvent.
*   Clean your sprayer immediately after using BIN primer.
*   Disassemble sprayer components for a thorough clean.
*   Flush the sprayer thoroughly with solvent and then water.
*   Always wear personal protective equipment.

Cleaning BIN primer from your sprayer requires immediate action and the correct solvent. Flush the sprayer with denatured alcohol until clear, then disassemble parts like the tip, filter, and gun. Soak these components in fresh denatured alcohol. Scrub any remaining residue before reassembling and flushing with water for final rinse.

### Understanding BIN Primer's Unique Challenge

BIN primer is an excellent product for many painting tasks. It blocks stains and seals odors very well. However, this primer is shellac-based. This means it dries extremely fast. This quick-drying nature helps you work efficiently on walls and ceilings. It also presents a challenge for cleaning your equipment.

Once BIN primer dries, it becomes very hard and sticky. It adheres strongly to surfaces inside your sprayer. This can quickly clog nozzles, filters, and hoses. Dried primer reduces sprayer performance. It can also damage your equipment permanently if not removed. Understanding this unique challenge is the first step toward effective cleaning.

Unlike water-based or oil-based paints, BIN primer needs a specific type of solvent. Water does not dissolve shellac. Common paint thinners may not work effectively either. Denatured alcohol is the recommended cleaner for shellac-based products. It breaks down the tough shellac resins. This makes cleaning much easier and helps preserve your sprayer. ### Essential Tools and Solvents for Cleaning Your Sprayer

Cleaning BIN primer from your sprayer needs specific items. Having everything ready before you start saves time. It also makes the cleaning process smoother. Gathering your supplies ensures you can work without interruptions. This preparation helps achieve a truly clean sprayer.

First, you need the right solvent. Denatured alcohol is the best choice for BIN primer. Buy enough to flush and soak your sprayer parts. You will need a few gallons, depending on your sprayer size. Never substitute with water or wrong thinners for shellac.

Next, gather cleaning tools. You will need several empty buckets for flushing and soaking. A stiff brush, like a toothbrush or a specialized sprayer brush kit, helps remove dried primer. Rags or paper towels are good for wiping. Finally, safety gear is crucial. Wear gloves, safety glasses, and a respirator. These items protect you from strong fumes and skin contact.

### Step-by-Step Guide to Cleaning BIN Primer from Your Sprayer

Cleaning BIN primer from your sprayer requires a careful approach. Following these steps ensures you remove all residue. A clean sprayer performs better and lasts longer. Let's get your sprayer spotless and ready for its next job. This process is thorough, but worth the effort.

#### Step 1: Prepare for Cleaning and Initial Flush

First, turn off your sprayer. Release any pressure in the system. Remove the remaining BIN primer from the paint cup or hopper. Pour it back into its original can if usable. You can also dispose of it properly if not.

Now, pour denatured alcohol into the sprayer's cup or bucket. Start with about one quart. Turn the sprayer on to a low-pressure setting. Spray the alcohol into an empty waste bucket. Keep spraying until the liquid coming out runs clear. This flushes the main lines and pump. It removes most of the liquid primer.

#### Step 2: Disassemble and Soak Components

After the initial flush, turn off the sprayer again. Disassemble the important parts. These include the spray tip, tip guard, filter, and gun filter. Also, remove the hose if it detaches easily. Place all these small parts into a bucket filled with fresh denatured alcohol. Let them soak for at least 15-30 minutes. Soaking helps soften any dried BIN primer.

#### Step 3: Scrub and Clean Individual Parts

While parts soak, clean the main sprayer unit. Use a rag soaked in denatured alcohol to wipe down the exterior. For the internal components that do not detach, like the pump intake tube, use a stiff brush. Scrub any visible primer residue. Pay close attention to threads and connection points.

Take the soaked parts out of the alcohol. Use your stiff brushes to thoroughly scrub each piece. Make sure to clean inside the tip, around the filter mesh, and inside the tip guard. For stubborn clogs, soak the part longer. It is important to remove all traces of the BIN primer. This is similar to [how to clean oil-based paint from a sprayer](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-oil-based-paint-from-a-sprayer) in terms of component cleaning, though with a different solvent.

#### Step 4: Final Flush and Reassembly

Once all parts are clean, reassemble your sprayer. Reinstall the filters, tip guard, and spray tip. Pour more fresh denatured alcohol into the sprayer. Flush the system again. Spray until the alcohol coming out is completely clear. This removes any loosened particles.

Finally, flush the system with water. This is a crucial step for preventing future issues. Pump clean water through the sprayer. Continue until only clear water comes out. This step washes away any residual denatured alcohol. This makes sure your sprayer is ready for future use with any material.

### Deep Cleaning Specific Sprayer Components

A truly clean sprayer means every component is free of residue. BIN primer is known for sticking to tiny spaces. Focusing on each part ensures a complete clean. Let's break down how to deep clean specific components. This detailed attention prevents future blockages and poor spray patterns.

#### H3: Cleaning the Spray Tip and Tip Guard

The spray tip is vital for a good spray pattern. BIN primer can quickly clog its small opening. After soaking, use a small needle or a tip cleaning tool to clear the hole. Push it gently through the tip to remove any softened primer. Also, scrub the outside and inside of the tip guard. Make sure all primer is gone from the guard's crevices. A clean tip means an even coat every time.

#### H3: Maintaining the Sprayer Filters

Most sprayers have one or more filters. A main filter is often in the gun handle, and a smaller one might be at the pump intake. These filters catch debris. BIN primer can leave a film on the filter mesh. Soak filters in denatured alcohol for a long time. Then, scrub them with a stiff brush. Hold them up to a light to check for blockage. Ensure no primer particles remain. Blocked filters restrict flow and cause pressure issues.

#### H3: Flushing the Hose and Gun Body

The sprayer hose can hold a lot of primer. After flushing with alcohol, check the hose for flexibility. If it feels stiff, run more alcohol through it. You can also disconnect it and manually flush it. For the gun body, remove any filters and attachments. Wipe down the trigger area and fluid passages with alcohol-soaked rags. Use small brushes for tight spots. Ensure no primer builds up around the trigger mechanism. Proper care here extends the life of your entire sprayer. ### Preventing Future BIN Primer Clogs and Issues

Cleaning your sprayer is important. Preventing clogs in the first place is even better. A few simple habits can save you a lot of cleaning time. These practices extend the life of your equipment. They also ensure your sprayer is always ready for its next job. Let's look at easy ways to keep your sprayer in top condition when using BIN primer.

One key rule is to clean immediately. BIN primer dries very fast. Do not let it sit in your sprayer, even for a short break. As soon as you finish spraying, start the cleaning process. This prevents the primer from hardening inside. Quick action makes removal much easier.

Always pre-strain your BIN primer. Even new cans can have small lumps or dried bits. Pour the primer through a fine mesh paint strainer before putting it in your sprayer. This prevents larger particles from entering the system. Fewer particles mean fewer clogs in your tip and filters. This simple step can save you major headaches down the line.

Finally, proper storage matters. Once your sprayer is clean and dry, store it correctly. Follow your sprayer's manual for specific storage advice. Some manufacturers recommend storing with a pump protector fluid. This lubricates internal parts. It also prevents any remaining moisture from causing rust or damage. These simple steps keep your sprayer performing its best.

### Troubleshooting Common Cleaning Challenges

Sometimes, cleaning BIN primer from a sprayer is not straightforward. You might encounter dried primer or stubborn clogs. These challenges can be frustrating. However, specific methods can help you overcome them. Knowing what to do in tough situations saves your sprayer. Let's look at common problems and their solutions.

#### H3: Dealing with Dried BIN Primer

If BIN primer dries inside your sprayer, it becomes very hard. A standard flush might not work. In this case, extended soaking is your best friend. Disassemble the affected parts. Place them in a bucket of fresh denatured alcohol. Let them soak for several hours, or even overnight. The alcohol needs time to break down the hardened shellac. Check periodically and scrub with a stiff brush. Patience is key when dealing with dried BIN. This is especially true when you need to [clean Zinsser BIN off brushes](https://cosyhomecreation.com/how-to-clean-zinsser-bin-off-brushes) where it has dried.

#### H3: Removing Stubborn Clogs

Stubborn clogs often happen in the spray tip or filters. If soaking and scrubbing do not clear them, try a small, stiff wire. A guitar string or a thin brass wire can work. Gently push it through the clogged opening. Be very careful not to scratch or damage the precision parts. For filters, try back-flushing them under a faucet with strong water pressure. Always follow this with a denatured alcohol rinse.

If an entire hose or pump is clogged, you might need a stronger approach. Some people use an air compressor to force air through lines. Do this with extreme caution. Make sure all connections are secure. Always wear eye protection. If all else fails, consult your sprayer's manual or a professional. Sometimes, parts replacement is the only solution for severe clogs.

### Safety Precautions When Cleaning Primers

Cleaning a paint sprayer involves handling strong chemicals. Your safety is very important. Always take precautions to protect yourself. Ignoring safety steps can lead to health problems or injury. Let's review the essential safety measures for cleaning BIN primer from your sprayer.

First, ensure good ventilation. Denatured alcohol produces strong fumes. Work in an open area, like outside, or in a well-ventilated garage. Open windows and doors. Use fans to help circulate air. Good airflow reduces the amount of fumes you inhale. This protects your respiratory system.

Second, wear personal protective equipment (PPE). This includes safety glasses or goggles. They protect your eyes from splashes and fumes. Wear chemical-resistant gloves to protect your skin. Denatured alcohol can dry and irritate skin. A respirator mask with appropriate filters is also crucial. It provides direct protection from inhaling harmful vapors. Make sure your respirator fits well.

Third, handle solvents carefully. Denatured alcohol is flammable. Keep it away from open flames, sparks, and heat sources. Store it in its original container, in a cool, dry place. Dispose of used solvent properly. Check local regulations for chemical waste disposal. Never pour it down drains. Treat all cleaning chemicals with respect. Your health depends on it.

### FAQ Section

#### Q1: What solvent cleans BIN primer best?
Denatured alcohol is the best solvent for cleaning BIN primer. BIN primer is shellac-based, and denatured alcohol effectively dissolves shellac. Using the correct solvent ensures thorough cleaning and prevents damage to your sprayer. Always use fresh denatured alcohol for flushing and soaking parts.

#### Q2: Can I use water to clean BIN primer?
No, you cannot use water to clean BIN primer. BIN primer is shellac-based and is not water-soluble. Water will not dissolve the primer and can even cause it to gum up. Always use denatured alcohol for primary cleaning, followed by a water rinse to remove alcohol residue.

#### Q3: How quickly should I clean my sprayer after using BIN primer?
You should clean your sprayer immediately after using BIN primer. BIN primer dries very quickly, often within minutes. Delaying cleanup allows the primer to harden inside the sprayer, leading to stubborn clogs and potential damage. Start the cleaning process as soon as you finish spraying.

#### Q4: What if BIN primer dries inside my sprayer?
If BIN primer dries inside your sprayer, you will need to disassemble the affected parts. Soak them in fresh denatured alcohol for several hours or overnight. This allows the alcohol to soften the dried shellac. Then, carefully scrub the parts with a stiff brush to remove the residue.

#### Q5: Can I use universal cleaner?
It is not recommended to use a "universal cleaner" for BIN primer. Universal cleaners may not contain the specific chemicals needed to dissolve shellac. Always refer to the primer's manufacturer recommendations or use denatured alcohol. Using the wrong cleaner can be ineffective or even harm your sprayer.

#### Q6: How often should I deep clean my sprayer?
You should deep clean your sprayer every time you use BIN primer. Because BIN primer dries so quickly and is so adhesive, a thorough deep clean after each use is essential. This prevents build-up and ensures your sprayer remains in optimal working condition for future projects.
