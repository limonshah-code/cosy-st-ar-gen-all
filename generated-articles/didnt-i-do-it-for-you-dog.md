### Reading Canine Body Language Cues

Dogs use their entire body to convey messages. A relaxed dog has soft eyes and a loose posture. A stressed dog might yawn excessively or lick their lips. My dog shows he wants attention by pawing at my leg.

Their tail position is also a key indicator. A high, fast wag often means excitement. A low, slow wag can mean uncertainty. Understanding these signs helps avoid misunderstandings. It makes our interactions smoother.

Their ears and eyes also tell a story. Forward ears show interest. Pinned-back ears suggest fear or aggression. Wide eyes can signal fear or anxiety. Learning these subtle cues is important for clear communication.
